# Contributing

Most pages in this repository are generated first-party publisher material.
Contributions should be limited to verifiable factual corrections,
accessibility fixes, or defects in the public tooling.

1. Keep a change narrowly scoped and explain the evidence for it.
2. Do not add tracking parameters, affiliate links, rankings, endorsements,
   copied reviews, or third-party promotional claims.
3. Do not create additional Discussions or comments. The one official
   Discussion is updated by automation and remains locked.
4. Do not include private user data, credentials, or copyrighted material you
   do not have permission to contribute.

Product-support requests belong in the routes described in
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md), not in a pull request.
