Short answer

A US green card (permanent resident) photo must be 2×2 inches (51×51 mm) on a plain white or off-white background, with the head 1 inch to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm) from chin to crown. Aim for a recent colour photo, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows. Green card and most USCIS applications use the 2×2 inch format.

Snapport lets you take it at home, set a compliant background and crop to the exact spec, so a self-taken photo still meets the requirement. Always confirm the current official rule before you submit.

What to look for before choosing

  • Exact spec: 2×2 inches (51×51 mm) (the #1 rejection reason is the wrong size/crop).
  • Background: a plain white or off-white background with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the head 1 inch to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm) from chin to crown.
  • Neutral expression, eyes open, no glasses glare or head covering.
  • A recent, high-resolution colour photo; verify the latest official rule before submitting.

A practical decision process

  1. Stand against a plain, evenly lit wall.
  2. Take the shot head-on with Snapport.
  3. Set the background to plain white.
  4. Crop/export to 2×2 inches (51×51 mm).
  5. Save at full quality to upload or print.
