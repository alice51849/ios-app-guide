Short answer

A Italian CV follows local hiring conventions: often a photo and personal details, and frequently the Europass template in traditional sectors. Typical length is 1–2 pages. Photo: Often expected. Matching these expectations matters as much as the content.

CV Desk helps you build to the right structure on your iPhone, keep the layout ATS-readable, and export a clean PDF without a watermark. Tailor the wording to each job before you send it.

What to look for before choosing

  • Correct format for a Italian CV: often a photo and personal details, and frequently the Europass template in traditional sectors.
  • Length: 1–2 pages.
  • Photo: Often expected.
  • ATS-readable layout (single column, standard fonts, no text in images).
  • Clean PDF export with no watermark; tailor keywords to each job posting.

A practical decision process

  1. Pick a Italian CV-appropriate template.
  2. Fill in reverse-chronological experience with measurable results.
  3. Apply the local photo rule (Often expected).
  4. Mirror keywords from the target job description for ATS.
  5. Export as PDF and check it opens cleanly on desktop.
