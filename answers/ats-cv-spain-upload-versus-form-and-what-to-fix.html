When you attach a CV, an applicant tracking system reads it before a person does. One column, headings such as work experience, education and languages, one date format and a PDF with real, selectable text decide whether it reads correctly.

First, tell the application types apart. If the portal asks you to fill in fields one by one, your CV's layout does not affect that reading. If instead you attach the file — on a company's careers site, in an international application, or through an upload form — the system parses the document as it is.

The most common failure is a two-column layout. The parser reads top to bottom and left to right, so a sidebar with languages and skills gets mixed into your job entries. The second is creative headings: "My path" may not be recognised as work experience. The third is a PDF made as an image, which arrives with no text.

Before sending, tailor the CV to each offer using terms from the ad that genuinely describe what you have done. CV Desk gives a 0–100 ATS score with tappable fixes, exports a single-column PDF with selectable text or an editable Word file, and keeps a tailored resume for every offer. The score is an on-device estimate; results vary by employer system.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the application uses a form or a file upload
  • Whether the CV is one column, with no sidebar
  • Whether headings are standard: work experience, education, languages
  • Whether all dates use the same format
  • Whether the PDF text can be selected

The steps that actually work

  1. Check whether the portal asks you to fill in fields or attach a file.
  2. If attaching, move everything into one column: dates, role and company one under another.
  3. Use standard headings instead of creative titles.
  4. Add terms from the offer only where they describe what you actually did.
  5. Select a line in the exported PDF to confirm it is real text.

FAQ

Do all companies in Spain use an ATS?

Not all, but when you attach a file on an application portal a system commonly reads it first. If you only fill in a form, layout does not affect that reading.

Can I include a photo in an ATS CV?

You can, as long as it does not force a two-column layout. In CV Desk the photo is optional and off by default.

Where does CV Desk store my resume?

On your iPhone. No account is needed and the app works in airplane mode.
