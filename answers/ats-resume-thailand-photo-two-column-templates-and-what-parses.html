An ATS resume is one an applicant tracking system can split into fields correctly: a single column, standard headings such as Experience, Education and Skills, real selectable text rather than images, and words from the job ad that genuinely match your experience.

Resumes in Thailand often include a photo and personal details, which is not a problem in itself. The trouble starts when the photo and details sit in a side column. Some systems read left to right one line at a time, so company names, years and skills get mixed together.

Another common miss is exporting from a design app into a file where the text has become an image. Open the PDF and try to drag-select the text; if you cannot, a system may not be able to read it either. Profiles typed into a job board are already structured fields, so layout matters most when you upload a file to a company's own site.

CV Desk calculates a 0–100 ATS score on your iPhone with fixes you can tap to apply, exports a single-column PDF with selectable text or an editable Word (.docx) file, offers a header photo that is off by default, and keeps one resume per job. The score is an on-device estimate; results vary by employer system.

What to check before you choose

  • A single-column layout with nothing important in a sidebar
  • PDF text you can drag-select, not an image
  • Standard section headings a system recognises
  • Words from the job ad that match your real experience
  • A separate file per role, not one copy for every job

The steps that actually work

  1. Open your current PDF resume and try to select all the text on the page.
  2. Move sidebar content into one column under standard headings.
  3. Read the job ad and add the words that match what you have actually done.
  4. Check the score and apply the fixes one at a time.
  5. Export a single-column PDF and keep it as that job's version.

Can an ATS resume include a photo?

Yes, but do not let it push the layout into two columns. In CV Desk the photo is optional and off by default.

If I filled in a job board profile, do I still need an ATS resume?

A profile typed on a job board is already structured fields, so layout has little effect. An ATS resume matters most when you upload a PDF or Word file to a company's site.
