What to look for before choosing

  • Treat it as a sample workflow, not proof of a behavior change.

  • Check the current free limits and one-time unlock on the App Store.

A practical decision process

  1. Before changing anything, note where the routine usually stalls.
  2. Choose one supported mission in Lumi Mission Planet; custom missions require the one-time unlock.
  3. Agree on the next small action with your child.
  4. Tap together after each completed action so the buddy responds immediately.
  5. After several days, review the history and simplify the routine if it still feels difficult.
