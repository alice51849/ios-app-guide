FAQ

Is brown noise proven to help ADHD?

Evidence is preliminary and individual. Some small studies suggest background noise may aid focus for some people with ADHD, potentially through stochastic resonance, but there are no large-scale clinical trials confirming brown or pink noise as an ADHD treatment. Many people find it helpful; others find it distracting. Experiment carefully, and consult a professional for ADHD management.

Can I mix and save my own sounds in Sereno?

Yes. The Sereno mixer lets you add multiple sound layers simultaneously — for example, brown noise and a gentle rain layer — adjust each volume independently, and save the result as a custom named scene.

Does Sereno need an internet connection to play sounds?

No. Every sound in Sereno is synthesized in real time on your device. Nothing streams from the internet; Sereno works in full airplane mode.
