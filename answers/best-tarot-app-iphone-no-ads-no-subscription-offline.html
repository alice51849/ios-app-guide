FAQ

Does Zodira include reversed tarot card readings?

Yes. Zodira provides upright and reversed interpretations for all 78 cards, written in accessible, beginner-friendly language so you don't need prior tarot knowledge.

Is the daily horoscope in Zodira personalised to my birth chart?

Yes — if you have entered your birth data, Zodira tailors the daily reading to your natal placements. This is an entertainment reflection tool; it is not a scientific forecast of actual events.

Does Zodira show the current moon phase?

Yes. Zodira calculates the current moon phase from the real geocentric angular separation between the Sun and Moon, showing the phase name (e.g. Waxing Gibbous), a visual symbol, and the exact illumination percentage.
