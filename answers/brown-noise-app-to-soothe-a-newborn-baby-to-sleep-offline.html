Short answer

In the first three months — the “fourth trimester” — newborns are soothed by the steady, low, whooshing sound they heard in the womb, which is why a continuous brown-noise or soft white-noise “whoosh” often calms crying and colic faster than silence. Sereno plays exactly these steady sounds — brown, pink and white noise plus a gentle whoosh — fully offline, with a one-time purchase and no account, so you can start it in seconds in a dark room. It is a comfort tool, not a medical device: follow your pediatrician's advice for a colicky or unsettled baby.

FAQ

Is white noise safe for a newborn?

Pediatric guidance (AAP) is to keep the volume below about 50 dB — no louder than a soft shower — place the device at least 2 metres (7 feet) from the crib, never inside it, and use it to settle the baby rather than running it loudly all night. Sereno has volume control and a timer to help you stay within these limits.

Brown noise or white noise for a baby?

Many parents find brown or pink noise calms a newborn better because it is lower and warmer — closer to the muffled whoosh of the womb — while plain white noise can sound harsh. Sereno offers brown, pink and white so you can try each at a low volume and see what settles your baby.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Sereno is a one-time purchase, works offline and needs no account, so there is no free trial to cancel and nothing to pay every month.
