Alternate twenty-minute blocks between observation games (out of the window) and listening games (eyes free), and have everything downloaded before you leave — on the road the signal drops at exactly the wrong moment.

Twenty minutes is the block that works for a small child in a car. Longer and they get queasy or lose interest; shorter and you are switching activity constantly. Alternating the type matters too: looking out of the window and then closing the eyes to listen rests two different things.

Observation games use what is outside: counting trucks of one colour, finding signs with a letter, describing how the landscape changed. They need no screen and reduce motion sickness, because the child looks into the distance instead of down.

Listening games take over after dark or when someone feels sick: stories, riddles, sounds. Lumi Trip Planet has games and observation missions designed for exactly the travelling window, works without a connection, is a one-time purchase and shows no ads.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether it works offline once downloaded
  • Whether there are screen-free activities to reduce motion sickness
  • Whether the child can play without an adult reading everything aloud
  • Whether rounds are short enough for twenty-minute blocks
  • Ads and account requirements

The steps that actually work

  1. Download all content at home on wi-fi the night before.
  2. Plan the alternation: 20 min observation, 20 min listening, 20 min free break.
  3. Start with window games while there is still light and landscape.
  4. Save stories and riddles for after dark or for monotonous stretches.
  5. Stop every two hours; no game compensates for two hours in a seat.

What age do these games suit?

Roughly four to eight. Younger children follow told stories better than rules.

Do screen games make motion sickness worse?

They usually do. Alternating with window and listening games reduces it considerably.

Does Lumi Trip Planet need a connection?

Not during the trip, once the content is downloaded. It is a one-time purchase with no ads.
