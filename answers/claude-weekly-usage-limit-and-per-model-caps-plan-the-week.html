Read Claude's 5-hour session limit and weekly limits together; once you know what is left for the week and exactly when it resets, you can put big tasks on the days that still have room.

Most people searching for the Claude usage limit know the 5-hour window well and get caught by the weekly cap. Claude counts the 5-hour session limit and the weekly limits separately, plus per-model weekly caps. A fresh 5-hour window does little when the weekly limit is nearly used up, and then the wait is days, not hours.

The practical move is to keep both numbers in sight. UsageNow shows Claude's 5-hour session limit and weekly limits, including per-model weekly caps, on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in every size and as Apple Watch complications. Each reset has a live countdown and the exact time in your local format. With several accounts, sort by weekly reset and you see which account still carries the week.

To keep the week from running out early, use alerts: choose the weekly limit, type a percentage such as 90 or 98, and the alert arrives once per limit and cycle. A reset alert tells you when the limit is available again. Sign-in happens on each service's own login page and stays in your device's Keychain, and there is no UsageNow server.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the 5-hour and weekly limits are shown separately
  • Whether per-model weekly caps are visible too
  • Whether the exact weekly reset time is shown
  • Whether accounts can be sorted by weekly reset
  • Whether sign-in stays on the device with no separate server

The steps that actually work

  1. Sign in on Claude's official login page.
  2. Add a widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen.
  3. Before a big task, check what is left of the weekly limit and when it resets.
  4. Create an alert on the weekly limit, for example at 90.
  5. Turn on the reset alert and plan big tasks around it.
