The first thing to check is whether it can count days since, not just days left. A couple's "day 312" or the days since you quit smoking or started exercising are count-ups, not countdowns. CountDaysNow splits upcoming, today and past events into three clear views with exact day counts, and includes a calculator to add or subtract days or compare two dates.

The second is visibility. A D-day you only see inside the app is soon forgotten. Put the most important date on a Lock Screen or Home Screen widget, and keep an active countdown like an exam or a discharge date running live in the Dynamic Island, so you see it many times a day without trying. The third is alerts. Reminders 7, 3 and 1 day before, on the day, at day 100 and on every anniversary give you time to prepare gifts or bookings. Set yearly dates as repeating events so you never re-enter them, and show the lunar date beside the Gregorian one for parents' birthdays and other days observed by the lunar calendar.

Students facing the college entrance exam can pin the exam D-day to the Lock Screen and add mock exams and application deadlines. What to check before you choose

  • Whether it counts both days left (D-) and days since (D+)
  • Whether it supports Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and the Dynamic Island
  • Whether 100-day, yearly and 7-days-before alerts arrive automatically
  • Whether it can show lunar dates alongside
  • Whether yearly dates can repeat automatically
  • Whether it works without ads or a recurring subscription

The steps that actually work

  1. Add the one date you most want to see (the day you started dating, an exam, a discharge date).
  2. Check that past dates show as D+ and upcoming ones as D-.
  3. Put that date on a Lock Screen or Home Screen widget.
  4. Make birthdays and anniversaries repeating events and turn on the lunar date where needed.
  5. Turn on 7-days-before and same-day alerts and prepare gifts or bookings when they arrive.

FAQ

Can it count how many days since we started dating?

Yes. Past dates show the days since as D+, and you can get alerts at day 100 and every anniversary.

Can my partner and I see the same D-day?

With Lifetime Pro you can share a chosen D-day with another CountDaysNow user and update it together. The invited person sees and edits only the visible fields such as title and date.

How do I keep track of lunar birthdays?

Each event can show the lunar date as a second calendar beside the Gregorian date, so lunar birthdays and anniversaries don't get mixed up.
