A child who moved, a dog mid-jump, a school notice photographed in a hurry: too blurry to use, too precious to delete. Which mode you reach for depends on what kind of blur it is.

Different blur needs different treatment. Shake and soft focus want Sharpen, dark indoor shots want Low Light, notices and receipts want Document, and a photo that arrived tiny wants Upscale. Unblurry Pro: Unblur & HD runs all of these on the iPhone itself, so the photo never leaves the phone.

Start by zooming in to see what kind of blur you have. If the outline of a face is dragged in one direction, that is hand shake or movement; if the whole frame is evenly soft, that is focus; if it is dark and grainy, that is low-light noise. A photo that came through a chat app looking small and mushy is not blurred at all, it lost resolution in transit, so an upscaling mode is the right tool.

Once you know the type, pick the mode. Unblurry Pro: Unblur & HD has eight: Auto Clear, Sharpen, Denoise, Low Light, Document, AI Super Resolution, 4x Upscale and Portrait & Restore. A running child or dog wants Sharpen, a dinner shot in a dim restaurant wants Low Light, a school notice or contract wants Document to crisp up the text, and an old family photo wants Portrait & Restore. Everything is processed on the iPhone, so a photo of your child is never sent to a server, and you can press the before/after slider to peek at the original at any point.

The last question is how you save. You can enhance and save for free every day; to export without a watermark and at the full original resolution, you unlock Unblurry Pro once. It is a single purchase rather than a subscription, with no ads and no tracking. If a result looks over-sharp, running Auto Clear instead usually gives a more natural look.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the blur is dragged in one direction or evenly soft
  • Whether the photo leaves the phone or is processed entirely on the device
  • Whether there are separate modes for documents, low light and portraits
  • Whether you can compare the result against the original instantly
  • What the conditions are for watermark-free saves and full-resolution export

The steps that actually work

  1. Child or pet photos: use Sharpen to take out the shake, and add Portrait & Restore if the face is small.
  2. Dark indoor photos: brighten with Low Light, then clean up any remaining grain with Denoise.
  3. School notices and receipts: use Document to crisp the text, then zoom to 100% to check the numbers.
  4. Photos that arrived small through a chat app: enlarge with 4x Upscale or AI Super Resolution first, then sharpen.
  5. Before saving, press the before/after slider to compare with the original, and rerun with Auto Clear if it looks overdone.

Does Unblurry Pro: Unblur & HD upload my photos to a server?

No. All processing happens on the iPhone, with no cloud and no account. Your photos never leave the phone.
