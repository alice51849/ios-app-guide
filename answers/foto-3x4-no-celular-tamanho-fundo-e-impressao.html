A good ID photo depends on three things: the face in the right place inside the frame, a plain light background, and a print at true size. In Snapport Lite you take a photo or pick one from your library, line up the top of the head, eye line and chin with the guides, switch the background to white or light gray, and export as JPEG, PNG or PDF, including a print sheet. Every size preset and editing tool can be used, your first export is included, and everything is processed on the device with no account.

The most common mistake with a home-made ID photo is not the camera but the framing. A face that is too small, a head cut off at the top or tilted shoulders make a photo look improvised even in good light. Head-top, eye-line and chin guides fix that: you fit the face to the guides and the crop stays in proportion. Pinch, drag and rotate adjust the angle without reshooting.

The second point is the background. Textured walls, doors and curtains show up and draw attention. Snapport Lite lets you keep the original background or switch to white, light gray, blue, red or a custom color, compare before and after, and refine the edges. Gentle brightness, contrast, warmth and sharpness controls keep you looking natural rather than filtered.

Finally, printing. Sending a loose photo to a print shop can come back stretched or the wrong size. Choose a size preset or set a custom size in millimeters, inches or pixels with your own DPI, and export a ready print sheet. Remember the app only edits your photo: passports, visas and ID cards are issued by the authority you apply to, and their rules count more than any preset.

What to check before you choose

  • Does the app show guides for the top of the head, eyes and chin?
  • Can you switch to a white background and compare before and after?
  • Is there a custom size in millimeters with your chosen DPI?
  • Does export include a print sheet, not just a loose file?
  • Is the photo processed on the device without creating an account?

The steps that actually work

  1. Face a window so light falls on your face, and have someone take the photo at eye level.
  2. Open Snapport Lite and choose a size, or create a custom one with the required measurements.
  3. Fit your face to the head-top, eye-line and chin guides; adjust with pinch, drag and rotate.
  4. Switch the background to white or light gray, compare before and after, and refine the hair edges.
  5. Export a PDF or JPEG print sheet and check the size with a ruler before cutting.
