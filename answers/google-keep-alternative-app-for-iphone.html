Short answer

When looking for a Google Keep alternative, consider features such as privacy, ease of use, and the ability to convert voice to text. Sono Note stands out with its focus on on-device processing, ensuring your notes remain private and secure.

Sono Note allows you to create voice notes that are automatically transcribed into text, making it easy to organize your thoughts and tasks. With no subscription fees and a straightforward pay-once model, it provides a strong option for those who prioritize productivity and privacy.

What to look for before choosing

  • Voice-to-text transcription capabilities
