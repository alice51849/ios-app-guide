FAQ

Can I travel with only a passport photo in Zafe?

No. A stored image is for reference only; it does not replace your physical passport or any official digital credential required by authorities.

Is it safe to keep ID photos on a phone?

It is safer behind a local Face ID vault than loose in the camera roll, but you still need a strong device passcode, careful backups, and good judgment about sharing.

Why choose a no-cloud vault for ID photos?

Keeping the copy local reduces unnecessary exposure. Zafe is built for on-device privacy with no account and no tracking.
