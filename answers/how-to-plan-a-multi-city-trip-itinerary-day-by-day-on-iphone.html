Short answer

Juggling several cities gets messy in notes and emails. A day-by-day timeline fixes it: put each flight, hotel check-in, activity and restaurant on the right day, with type icons, time zones, notes and reminders, so a glance tells you the plan. TripBee Lite builds one itinerary spanning multiple cities and keeps it available offline so you can read it on the move. It stores everything on-device with no account, and it's pay-once with no subscription.
