FAQ

Is 25 minutes the optimal interval length, or can I use a different timer?

Attention research shows degradation begins after 20–40 minutes of continuous work, so 25 minutes is a reasonable default — but individual capacity varies. Some people do better with 50-minute blocks and longer breaks. Experiment with what leaves you feeling recharged rather than cut off mid-flow.

Why is blocking apps more effective than just silencing notifications?

Muting stops audio alerts, but the app icon is still visible and one tap away — low-friction access keeps the habit loop alive. Blocking adds a meaningful barrier (a delay, PIN, or countdown) that interrupts the automatic reach-and-tap behaviour, which is the root of most distraction.

Does background music help or hurt focus while studying?

It depends on the task and the music. Research generally shows lyric-free, moderate-tempo music has minimal impact on straightforward tasks but can impair reading comprehension and memory encoding for complex material. Ambient or low-frequency constant noise (like brown noise) is often a safer background choice for deep study.
