How to write a resume with little or no work experience

Crafting a resume with minimal work experience is achievable with the right tools.

Short answer

When writing a resume with little or no work experience, focus on your skills, education, and any relevant volunteer work or internships. Highlight transferable skills that relate to the job you're applying for, and consider using a clear, professional format to make your resume stand out.
