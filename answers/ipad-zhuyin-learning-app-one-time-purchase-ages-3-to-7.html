What to check before you choose

  • Whether one purchase unlocks every symbol and level
  • Ads, in-app purchase pop-ups, outside links or social features
  • Coverage of symbols, blending, tones and stroke order
  • Big buttons and clear voice prompts a child can use alone
  • Whether sign-up or internet is required, and where progress is stored

The steps that actually work

  1. Open it on the iPad yourself first and confirm there are no ads or pop-ups.
  2. Start with recognizing the 37 symbols, one game at a time.
  3. Add blending once the symbols are familiar, then tones with Tone Roller Coaster.
  4. Use Magic Tracing to follow the strokes and learn the shapes.
  5. Check the parents page weekly and leave weak spots to weak spot review.
