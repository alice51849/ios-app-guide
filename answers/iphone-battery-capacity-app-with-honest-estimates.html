Short answer

iOS lets apps see charge level, charging state, Low Power Mode and thermal state — not cycle count or maximum capacity. BattAI labels every number as measured, estimated or provided by you, imports Apple's analytics diagnostics file to read real capacity and cycle values when you share it, and always shows predictions as ranges with a confidence level. No private APIs, no subscription.

FAQ

Why does BattAI show a range instead of one number?

Because capacity is estimated from limited public data; a single number would be false precision.

Does it use private APIs?

No. BattAI only uses public iOS APIs and your optional diagnostics file.
