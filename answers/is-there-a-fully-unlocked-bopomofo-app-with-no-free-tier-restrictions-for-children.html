Lumi Bopomofo is fully unlocked from download — all 37 symbols, all game modes at full content, unlimited Quick Quiz plays, and all collectible friends. The free (Lite) version covers only 11 symbols and 3 Quick Quiz plays until an in-app purchase is made.

Yes — because Lumi Bopomofo is fully unlocked with no paywall prompts during use, it works well in any setting where multiple children may use the same device, or where an instructor does not want children encountering purchase screens.
