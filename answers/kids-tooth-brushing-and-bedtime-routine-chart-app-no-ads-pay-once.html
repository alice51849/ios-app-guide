Short answer

A visual bedtime or brushing routine works best when it is predictable, short and positively reinforced. Lumi Mission Planet includes tooth brushing and sleep time as core missions, lets children tap each small win, and celebrates mission completion with Lumi and collectible progress. It is a family routine helper, not a sleep-training or dental-care substitute; parents still supervise brushing and bedtime, while the app provides an ad-free, pay-once, no-account structure that keeps progress on the device.
