A passport photo comes down to four things: the size, how much of the frame the face fills, the background, and the print size. Snapport offers common templates such as 35×45 mm and US 2×2, head-top, eye-line and chin guides for face position, background replacement to white, and 4×6, A4 or Letter print sheets with several copies and cut guides. Everything is processed on the iPhone and photos are never uploaded. Requirements vary by country, agency and application type and Snapport does not guarantee acceptance, so always check the official requirements before submitting.

Home-made passport photos tend to be rejected for the same reasons: the face is too small or cropped too tight, the background has shadows, or the print comes out at the wrong ratio. Start by facing a window with even light, and have someone else take the photo at eye level. Check for glare on glasses or hair over the eyes at this stage.

Snapport runs Apple Vision face detection on the device and shows head-top, eye-line and chin guides. Pinch, drag and rotate to fine-tune, auto-center the face, and reset anytime. Replace the background with white, gray, blue or red, or keep the original, and review the on-device person segmentation before and after. Brightness, contrast, warmth, exposure and sharpness are light adjustments that keep identity intact.

Last comes printing. Export JPEG, PNG or PDF and lay several copies onto a 4×6, A4 or Letter print sheet, then cut along the guides. Snapport is a single purchase with no subscription, in-app purchases, ads, tracking or account, and it uses the network only for an optional template update check that you start yourself.

What to check before you choose

  • Are there size templates such as 35×45 mm plus custom sizes?
  • Do head-top, eye-line and chin guides help place the face?
  • Can the background be switched to white and compared before and after?
  • Can you make a 4×6 or A4 print sheet with cut guides?
  • Is the photo processed on the device without uploads?

The steps that actually work

  1. Stand facing a window with a light wall behind you and have someone take a straight photo at eye level.
  2. In Snapport, choose the passport template for your country, or set a custom size.
  3. Align with the head-top, eye-line and chin guides using pinch, drag and rotate.
  4. Switch the background to white and compare before and after around the hair.
  5. Export a 4×6 or A4 print sheet, print it, cut along the guides and compare with the official rules.
