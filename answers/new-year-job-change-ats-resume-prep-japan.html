What to check before you choose

  • One column, no sidebar or multi-column layout
  • Standard headings like Experience and Skills
  • No key details inside tables, images or text boxes
  • Posting keywords used in the same wording, within your real experience
  • Text in the exported PDF can be selected

The steps that actually work

  1. Before the break, gather this year's review notes and list duties, results, tools and certifications.
  2. Early in the break, build one master resume with one column and standard headings.
  3. Later in the break, read several postings and note the keywords they share.
  4. Before the new year, duplicate the master per role and adjust keywords and the order of results.
  5. Before submitting, check that PDF text is selectable and the headings are intact.

FAQ

When should I start if I leave after the bonus?

Have a draft done over the holiday break so you can apply in January. Check your employment rules for notice periods.

Do Japanese-format resumes need ATS care?

Use whatever format the employer asks for first. When uploading online, a one-column career history with standard headings and selectable PDF text is the safer choice.

How can I check ATS readability myself?

First check that the PDF text can be selected. CV Desk also shows an on-device ATS score from 0 to 100 with suggestions; the score is an estimate and results vary by employer system.
