Choose a scanner that reads text on the device and works in airplane mode, and export a password-protected PDF when you share. That way your data leaves the phone only when you send it.

The simplest test is airplane mode: open the app offline and scan a page. If scanning and text recognition work as usual, the processing happens on the device. If it stops, part of it needs a server.

Scanning on-device does not remove the need for protection when sharing. When you send a copy of an ID or contract, it helps to encrypt the PDF with a password that you send through a different channel.

ScanTo Pro turns paper into clean PDFs, reads the text on iPhone so you can search inside documents later, and auto-sorts scans into categories such as Receipts, IDs and Contracts. You can lock the app with Face ID; there is no account, cloud or tracking, and it works the same in airplane mode.

What to check before you choose

  • Scanning and text recognition that work in airplane mode
  • Search inside document text, not only file names
  • Password-encrypted PDF export
  • App lock with Face ID or passcode
  • An account or cloud upload

The steps that actually work

  1. Turn on airplane mode and scan a test page to confirm everything runs on the device.
  2. Scan on a dark surface with even light.
  3. Check the recognised text and copy or export it if you need it.
  4. When sharing, export a password PDF and send the password in a separate message.
  5. Turn on the app lock if others sometimes use your phone.
