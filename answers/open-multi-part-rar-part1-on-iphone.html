How to open a multi-part RAR archive on iPhone

Large downloads are often split into several RAR parts. If one part is missing or sits in a different folder, nothing will extract. Here is how to check the parts and open the right one.

The Files app can't extract RAR, and split RAR archives need an extractor that handles multi-part archives. Save every part into the same folder, make sure the names still match, then open the first part (.part1.rar, or the .rar with no number) and the rest are read with it. Zipbox: Unzip RAR, ZIP & 7z handles password protected and multi part ZIP, RAR and 7z on your device.

Split RAR archives use one of two naming styles. The newer style is name.part1.rar, name.part2.rar and so on; the older style is name.rar alongside name.r00, name.r01. Either way they are pieces of a single archive, and no single part extracts the full contents on its own. Split 7z archives look like name.7z.001, name.7z.002.

Most failures come from parts that are missing or scattered. Files saved from Mail can land in a different folder from those downloaded in Safari, which go to Downloads. Another common problem is a renamed file, for example a download that picked up a "(1)" so part2 no longer matches part1; rename it back and it lines up again.

Once every part is together, open the first one in an extractor that supports split archives. What to check before you choose

  • Whether every part has arrived, with no gaps in the numbering
  • Whether all parts sit in the same folder
  • Whether the names still match, with nothing like "(1)" added
  • Whether the app explicitly supports multi-part and password protected RAR
  • Whether extraction happens on the device or on a website

The steps that actually work

  1. Save every part to the Files app; Safari downloads go to the Downloads folder.
  2. Create a new folder in Files, move all the parts into it and check the numbering starts at 1 with nothing missing.
  3. Check the names: apart from the part number or the .r00-style extension, the base name must be identical.
  4. Open the first part in Zipbox (.part1.rar, or the .rar with no number in the older style) and enter the password if there is one.
  5. Preview the list, select what you need, then export it to Files or iCloud Drive.

FAQ

Can I extract part of the archive with only part1.rar?

Usually not. The parts are slices of one archive, so extraction stops where a part is missing. Collect every part first.

Opening part2 shows an error. What now?

Open the first part instead. Starting from part1 (or the .rar with no number) lets the app read the following parts in order.

Does Zipbox upload my files?

No. It makes no network requests and has no account or sign in; all 13 formats are handled on your device.
