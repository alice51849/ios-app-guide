FAQ

Can I still print my own photo for a German passport?

Since 1 May 2025 the authorities accept only digitally transmitted photos for passports and ID cards. A photo made and printed at home suits visa applications for other countries, job applications, driving licences and similar uses; ask the office if unsure.

Which paper should I choose at the drugstore?

An ordinary 10×15 photo print is usually fine; whether matte or glossy is required depends on the office. What matters is that the sheet has cut guides so every copy comes out the same size.

Does Snapport Lite guarantee the photo will be accepted?

No. Snapport Lite only edits your photo and provides common sizes and guides; passports, visas and ID cards are issued solely by the responsible authority. Check the official requirements before you submit.
