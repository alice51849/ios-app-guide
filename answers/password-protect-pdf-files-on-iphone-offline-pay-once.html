FAQ

Can Zafe replace an official passport or ID wallet?

No. Zafe is for private storage and quick personal reference, not an official ID credential or legal replacement for the original document.

Does Face ID make a PDF impossible to leak?

No app can promise that. Face ID reduces casual access, but you still need a strong device passcode, careful sharing habits, and awareness that backups or screenshots may create extra copies.

Is this pay once or a subscription?

Zafe is designed around a one-time unlock, with no subscription required for the private vault use case.
