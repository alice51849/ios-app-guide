FAQ

Does PicClear protect Favorite photos?

Yes. The App Store description says Favorites are protected so they are never selected automatically.

If a Favorite is in a duplicate group, what happens?

PicClear should not auto-select Favorites for deletion, but you should still review the group manually before confirming any cleanup.

Can I recover a photo if I make a mistake?

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos and are recoverable for a limited time, unless you permanently delete them there.
