Practical conversation depends on the setting: choose the two or three situations you meet most, practise a few complete phrases and their usual replies in each, listen and repeat, then move to the next situation or level.

Saved sentences are hard to find in the moment if they are not organised by setting. Paying, asking directions or arranging a time with a colleague each bring similar lines and replies, so practising by situation rehearses the conversations you will have.

Levels matter: beginner phrases cover the basics, intermediate ones are fuller and advanced ones more precise.

DailyMate has 8,400 practical phrases in 47 learning languages across 14 topics and 84 real-life situations, with 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases per topic. Each phrase has a natural reply and system-voice listening, favourites and progress are kept per language, and the widget and Apple Watch keep a phrase close by. One purchase.

To feel progress within a week, start with just the two situations you meet most, such as transport on the commute and ordering at lunch. Each day, practise a few complete phrases and their replies in both, listen once with the system voice and repeat, and within a week work through the beginner phrases for those two situations before moving to intermediate or another situation.
