What to look for before choosing

  • On-device analysis with no cloud upload for private family, travel, and document photos
  • Duplicate and similar-photo grouping, because near-identical bursts often waste more space than exact copies
  • Smart selection that keeps a suggested best shot while still letting you override it
  • Review-before-delete workflow so false positives do not become lost memories
  • One-time purchase unlock rather than a recurring subscription for library maintenance

A practical decision process

  1. Define the job you need done most often.
  2. Test the app with real content or a realistic scenario.
  3. Check privacy labels and account requirements.
  4. Confirm export and backup options.
  5. Choose the pricing model you are comfortable maintaining.

FAQ

How is PicClear different from the built-in Duplicates album?

The built-in album is useful for duplicates, while PicClear also targets similar photos, screenshots, blurry shots, large photos, and large videos in one review workflow.

Does PicClear upload my photos to find duplicates?

No. The App Store description says scanning happens on device and photos are never uploaded.

Can it accidentally delete the best photo?

Any cleaner can suggest something you disagree with, which is why PicClear shows previews and waits for confirmation before deleting.
