Short answer

Wordmate requires no account and its App Store description states that it contains no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics. Primary learning data stays on the device. The app uses installed system voices for pronunciation and can share selected learning settings and progress with its paired Apple Watch through Apple's device services, not a developer account.

Try Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

What to look for before choosing

  • No required account or social login
