Instead of fighting yourself every time, decide in advance which apps can't open during which periods. Set a focus session for study, deep work and bedtime; the apps you choose are blocked during the session and return automatically when it ends. Add a calm cooldown before stopping early, and use streaks and reclaimed hours as positive feedback.

The hard part isn't knowing you should scroll less. It's the reflex when you pick the phone up: unlock, open short videos, swipe. Relying on willpower means making the decision again every time, dozens of times a day, and losing when you're tired. Deciding in advance, so the chosen apps simply won't open for a while, turns dozens of decisions into one.

Which sessions are easiest to keep? Start with the one you most want to change. Students can use Study Mode, Pomodoro-style cycles with breaks, one class long. Office workers can protect the most important morning block for deep work. Poor sleepers can start with Sleep Wind Down, locking short-video and social apps an hour before bed, and people who scroll the moment they wake can use Morning Reset.

The second key is a threshold for stopping early. A tool you can never stop gets uninstalled; one you can stop with a tap does nothing. It works better to allow stopping, but only after a cooldown and a confirmation, so the urge passes before you decide. Last is visible progress: focused time today, weekly charts, streaks and total hours reclaimed keep people going better than a raw screen-time number.

LockHour Pro is designed this way: start a session with one tap, chosen apps are shielded immediately and unlock automatically when time is up; modes include Quick Focus, Deep Work, Study Mode, Sleep Wind Down, Morning Reset and Custom; Hard Mode adds a cooldown and confirmation before stopping early; and you get today's focused time, weekly charts, streaks, Home and Lock Screen widgets and a Dynamic Island countdown. No account, no ads, no tracking, and data never leaves the device. One purchase unlocks everything, with no subscription.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether it locks only the apps you choose and unlocks them automatically
  • Whether there are modes for study, deep work and bedtime
  • Whether stopping early has a cooldown instead of a one-tap exit
  • Whether you can see focused time, streaks and reclaimed hours
  • Whether it needs an account or uploads your data
  • Whether it's a one-time purchase or a monthly subscription

The steps that actually work

  1. Write down the two or three apps you can't put down (short video, social, games).
  2. Pick one period to change first: study, a morning work block or the hour before bed.
  3. Start the matching focus mode, add those apps to the block list and run the first session.
  4. Turn on Hard Mode so stopping early takes a cooldown and confirmation.
  5. Put the widget on your Home Screen and check today's focused time and streak each night.

FAQ

How is it different from built-in Screen Time?

Screen Time limits a daily total, and one tap extends it. LockHour Pro blocks chosen apps during the focus session you start, unlocks them automatically at the end and adds a cooldown before stopping early, which suits fixed periods like study and bedtime.

Can I still take calls and read messages while apps are locked?

Only the apps you choose are blocked. Everything else works as usual, so you can lock short-video and social apps and keep messaging.

How long should I lock at first?

Start with one class or one Pomodoro and lengthen it after a few days in a row. For bedtime, start 30 to 60 minutes before you go to bed.
