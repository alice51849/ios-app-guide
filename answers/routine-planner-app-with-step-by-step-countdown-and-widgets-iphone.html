Short answer

Look for a planner with ready-made routines. JellyDay has 50 routine templates, including morning and bedtime, that place a whole series of steps into today with one tap, and a button to bring yesterday's routine over to today. Starting a routine opens a focus countdown that takes you through one step at a time, each with its own timer, with pause, resume and done.

Check how the countdown shows up outside JellyDay. During focus, the remaining time appears in a Live Activity and the Dynamic Island, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show what you are doing now and what comes next. Reminders for the start and for the last minutes can be turned off in Settings, and a focus alarm only rings during a countdown you started yourself.

What to look for before choosing

  • Routine templates that place a whole sequence of steps into today at once.
  • A countdown with a shrinking pie that goes through the routine one step at a time.
  • Live Activity and Dynamic Island while a focus countdown runs.
  • Home Screen widgets for what is now and what is next, and Lock Screen widgets for the countdown.
  • Reminders you can switch off, and no sound or vibration effects.

A practical decision process

  1. Open the routine templates and pick one, such as a morning or bedtime routine.
  2. Add it to today and adjust a start time or a colour if you like.
  3. Start the focus countdown and follow the steps one at a time.
  4. Add the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget to see the current step and the next one.
  5. Use the button that brings yesterday's routine to today when the day looks the same.

FAQ

Can I make a routine repeat every day?

Yes. Repeating routines, every day, on weekdays or on the days you choose, are part of the one-time in-app purchase.

Does the focus countdown show on the Lock Screen?

Yes. The remaining time is shown in a Live Activity and the Dynamic Island, and Lock Screen widgets show the countdown and the next task.

Will an alarm go off when I did not ask for one?

No. The alarm only rings during a focus countdown that you started yourself, and start and ending-soon reminders can be turned off in Settings.
