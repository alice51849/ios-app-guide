Short answer

Most shift workers end up rebuilding their alarms every time a new rota comes out, and one missed edit means a late start or a 5 a.m. wake-up on a rest day. A better alarm works the ring days out from a shift or alternating-week pattern you enter once, and lets you add extra working days such as overtime, night shifts or on-call days on top.

Check that it shows the coming week in advance, so you can confirm the plan before bed, and that it schedules real system alarms through Apple's AlarmKit rather than notifications. Public holidays still matter on a rota: it helps when each alarm can follow its own holiday calendar, and when a date with unknown holiday data is treated as a workday so the alarm still rings.

EasyAM is the app this guide covers: EasyAM decides for you, day by day: it rings on workdays and stays quiet on public holidays and your days off. Free to start · One-time unlock · No subscription. Check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing.

What to look for before choosing

  • A shift or alternating-week pattern entered once, not weekly edits.
  • Extra working days such as overtime, night shifts or on-call days, with your own labels.
  • A different ring time for one day or several in a row without touching the rest.
  • A preview of the next seven days so you can check which mornings ring.
  • Real system alarms through AlarmKit, with unknown holiday dates treated as workdays.

A practical decision process

  1. Set your wake-up time, country and region, then enter your shift or alternating-week pattern once.
  2. Add the overtime, night shifts or on-call days that are not part of the pattern.
  3. Add your time off and confirm those mornings stay quiet in the seven-day preview.
  4. Open the calendar and compare the next two weeks with your rota.
  5. Keep a backup alarm for anything critical, as with any alarm app.

The download includes one saved alarm. Shift and alternating-week patterns, unlimited alarms, a holiday calendar for each alarm and more clock themes come with a one-time in-app purchase.

Can I add night shifts and on-call days?

Yes. Add them as extra working days, as single days or date ranges, each with your own label and icon.

Can I change the time for just one shift?

Yes. Give one day, or several in a row, its own ring time; the other workdays keep your usual time.
