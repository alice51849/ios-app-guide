Short answer

A South African CV follows local hiring conventions: no photo, ID number, race or marital status (POPIA and Employment Equity Act make these legally sensitive); British English; references 'available on request'. Typical length is 2 pages (up to 3 for senior or academic roles). Photo: No — legally sensitive under POPIA. Matching these expectations matters as much as the content.
