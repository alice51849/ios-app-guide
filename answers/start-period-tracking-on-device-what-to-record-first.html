Record start date, end date and flow, for three or four consecutive cycles — only then do the average and range mean anything.

The value of tracking comes from continuity, not from the number of fields. A few fields you actually complete every month beat a detailed setup that lasts three weeks. The goal of the first three months is one thing: your own cycle-length range.

After the third or fourth cycle, add one field at a time — cramps or mood, for example. One at a time is what lets you see whether the new field relates to the cycle at all; five at once and nothing is attributable.

Also consider where the data lives. Cycle data is unusually sensitive, and keeping it on the device with no account carries less risk. Cyca is built that way: on-device data, one-time purchase, no subscription. Apps like this provide records and estimates, not a medical diagnosis — see a clinician about anything unusual.

What to check before you choose

  • How many fields are on by default and whether they can be turned off
  • Whether predictions use your own records or assume a fixed 28 days
  • Whether data stays on the device and whether an account is required
  • Whether your own records can be exported
  • Whether there is a subscription

The steps that actually work

  1. Record the start date on day one rather than filling it in afterwards.
  2. Enable three fields only: start, end, flow.
  3. Log three or four consecutive cycles without changing the field setup.
  4. After the fourth cycle, look at the shortest and longest cycle length — the range is more useful than the average.
  5. Add one field at a time after that, and watch two cycles before keeping it.

FAQ

How many months before it is accurate?

At least three or four cycles. An average built on fewer is dominated by a single unusual month.

Is tracking useful with irregular cycles?

More useful, in fact. An irregular cycle is exactly the case where your own range beats a 28-day default.

Does Cyca need an account?

No. Data stays on the device, and it is a one-time purchase with no subscription.
