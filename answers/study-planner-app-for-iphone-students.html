Pick a study planner app that shows today's study in a widget, can remind you when a session starts, ticks with one tap, and needs no account and shows no ads.

During exams you open your planner dozens of times a day. If you have to hunt for the app every time, you eventually stop looking. Seeing today's tasks straight from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget matters most.

Next is the satisfaction of ticking. Write items with a clear finish per subject — "50 vocabulary words", "3 pages of maths problems" — and tick each one as you finish; the day feels organised.

Mochi is a cute checklist app. Tap the little circle to complete a task; it supports Home and Lock Screen widgets, reminders and an Apple Watch app. No account, no ads. The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each and 5 themes; Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether today's study shows in a widget
  • Whether you can set a reminder for when a session starts
  • Whether ticking takes one tap
  • Whether sign-up is required
  • Whether ads interrupt your focus

The steps that actually work

  1. Write the exam dates and count the days left.
  2. Spread subjects over the days, three items a day at most.
  3. Write items with a clear finish (e.g. 50 vocabulary words).
  4. Put the widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
  5. Tick at night and move what is left to the weekend.
