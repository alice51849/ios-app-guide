Unlike photos, a screenshot's value is the text and links inside it. The fix is recognising that text, sorting by purpose (shopping, tickets, chats, maps), acting on what it records (open the link, track the parcel, add a reminder) and clearing it when done. Manual albums do not survive a month.

The built-in Screenshots album only gathers them in one place, still ordered by time. You are looking for the one with the order number from last month, not the screenshot from 3 October. So the first step is making the text searchable: with OCR, the order number, shop name and address inside a screenshot become keywords you can type.

The second step is categories and actions. ShotInbox AI: Sort Screenshots reads screenshot text with on-device Apple Vision and Natural Language, sorts into Shopping, Receipts, Travel, Tickets, Maps & Places, Chats & Social, Work, Study, Recipes & Food, Inspiration, QR & Codes, Errors & Tech and Sensitive, lets you search text, categories and links, lists detected events and reminders by time, and acts directly: Open in Maps, Open Link, Copy Text, Track Package, Add Reminder, Add to Calendar. The latest 50 screenshots include the complete core workflow for free, and Lifetime Pro is one purchase with no subscription.

The third step is clearing. Most screenshots are disposable: a pickup slip after collection, a promo code after use. A sorting tool should never delete on its own, and Photos asks for final confirmation; that confirmation is protection, not friction.

What to check before you choose

  • OCR, so you can search by the words inside a screenshot
  • Automatic categories by content, or dragging each one into albums yourself
  • Actions straight from the screenshot: open link, track package, add reminder
  • Recognition on the phone, or screenshots uploaded somewhere
  • No auto-delete, with a confirmation before anything is removed

The steps that actually work

  1. Skip manual albums; run the whole batch through a tool that recognises screenshot text.
  2. Search for something you remember (shop, order, place) to confirm it is actually findable.
  3. Check that tickets, receipts and chats landed in separate categories and correct the misses.
  4. Push anything time-bound (pickup, payment, event) into Reminders or Calendar.
  5. Clear finished screenshots on the spot; Photos asks once more, then it is really gone.

FAQ

Doesn't Photos already have a Screenshots album?

It does, but it only groups them by time. It cannot search the text inside, and it will not separate tickets from receipts or chats.

My screenshots include IDs and private chats. Will a sorting tool upload them?

Depends on the tool. Choose one that recognises everything on the device and uploads nothing, so sensitive content never leaves the phone.
