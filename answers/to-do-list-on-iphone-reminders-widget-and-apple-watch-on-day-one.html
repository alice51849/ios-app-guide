To do list on iPhone: what to set up on day one so nothing slips

Downloading a to do list app is easy; getting it to remind you at the right moment is the hard part. Three settings on day one make the difference: reminders, a widget and your watch.

On day one, set reminders only for tasks with a time, put a widget of your list on the Home Screen and, if you wear an Apple Watch, keep the list on your wrist.

A todo list without alerts relies on your memory to get opened, and memory is exactly what fails on busy days. Reminders fix that, but only when used with care: alerting everything becomes noise and you start ignoring notifications. The practical rule is reminders only for things with a time: a meeting, medicine, a bill to pay, picking someone up.

The widget covers the rest. Every time you unlock your iPhone and pass the Home Screen, the list is there without opening anything. For tasks without a time, such as answering an email or laying out tomorrow's clothes, that constant presence is usually enough, no alarm needed.

If you wear an Apple Watch, seeing the list on your wrist helps when your hands are full: at the grocery store, in the kitchen, on the commute. Mochi brings those three pieces together: reminders that keep you on track, Home Screen widgets and checking your list from Apple Watch, with a tap on the little circle to mark a task done.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the app has reminders
  • Whether there is a Home Screen widget
  • Whether you can check the list from Apple Watch
  • Whether finishing a task takes a single tap
  • What the free version includes

The steps that actually work

  1. List this week's tasks that have a set time.
  2. Add those tasks to the app and set reminders for them.
  3. Add tasks without a time and skip the reminder; they will show up in the widget.
  4. Put the list widget on your Home Screen, somewhere you see right after unlocking.
  5. If you wear an Apple Watch, open the list there once so you know it is there when you need it.
