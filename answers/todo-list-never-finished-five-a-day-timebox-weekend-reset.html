What to do when your to-do list never gets finished: five a day, time boxes, a weekend reset

A list that never empties is rarely a speed problem. It was overfilled before the day began, and the completion rate only comes back once the volume comes down.

Keep five items for today, give each a 25- or 50-minute time box, and spend a fixed 15 minutes every Sunday deleting or shrinking anything that has not moved. Do all three and the list starts emptying within a week.

Most to-do lists hold three kinds of things at once: what must happen today, what you would like to do this week, and ideas for someday. Mixed together, your eye lands first on the items you cannot do, the whole list starts to feel like a reproach, and you stop opening it. Split it into a today list and a later list. Today holds five at most; everything else goes to later.

Next, give each item a time box. "Write the report" expands forever; "Report: outline only, 25 min" ends. At work, 25 minutes fits the gap between meetings; for students, 50 minutes matches a class period. When the timer ends, stop, and whatever is left becomes tomorrow's first item instead of tonight's overtime.

Last is the weekend reset: 15 minutes on Sunday going through the later list. Anything untouched for two weeks gets deleted or shrunk to one step that fits a 25-minute box. A list you can read in one glance is a list you keep opening. Mochi is built for exactly this kind of short list: write the task, tap the little circle to finish, set a reminder, and keep today's five on your Home Screen with a widget. Free to start, no ads.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether you can keep a today list and a later list separately instead of one pile
  • How many taps adding an item takes; past three, busy days skip it
  • Whether reminders can be set for the time you actually sit down to work
  • Whether today's list is visible on the Home Screen without opening the app
  • Ads, and whether an account is required before you can write anything

The steps that actually work

  1. Dump everything from notes, sticky pads and your head into the later list. Do not filter yet.
  2. Each morning (or the night before) move five items into today, in order, with the one you keep putting off first.
  3. Write a time box after each: "25 min" or "50 min". Start the timer when you start; when it ends, tick it or send it back to later.
  4. Check once at 4 pm: how many are left? More than two means one moves to tomorrow now, so you do not carry an unfinished list home.
  5. Sunday evening, 15 minutes on the later list: delete what has not moved in two weeks, split anything too big into one 25-minute step.

FAQ

What if I finish all five with time to spare?

Pull one more from later, but never pre-load it into today. An early empty list is the point; it makes you open the app tomorrow. Starting with eight is what drives completion down.

The time box ended and the task is half done. Now what?

Stop. Write the remaining part as a new item and make it tomorrow's first. If the same task fails three days running, it is too big and needs splitting again.
