Three things in the week before: cut to two stops a day, prepare at least two hours of offline activity, and pack in three groups — everything else can be adjusted on the ground.

First, cut the itinerary. A preschooler holds up for about two stops a day; the third is usually spending the adult's patience. Rank the places you want and treat the top two as the plan and the rest as options.

Second, prepare the gaps. Flights, drives and waiting for food regularly add up to more than two hours, and that is exactly where things fall apart. Whatever you prepare must not need a connection — planes and mountain roads have none. Lumi Trip Planet is built for that window: travel games and observation missions, one-time purchase, no ads.

Third, pack in three groups: carry-on (medicine, one full change of clothes, activities), checked, and a backup at home. The carry-on group is the one that matters, because it has to survive every surprise — delays, slow baggage, a spilled drink.

What to check before you choose

  • How many stops a day, and whether the third can be dropped
  • Whether the gap activities need a connection
  • Whether the carry-on group holds one complete change of clothes
  • Whether the activities carry ads or need an account
  • Whether the child can run it without constant adult help

The steps that actually work

  1. Seven days out: rank the places and keep two per day.
  2. Five days out: estimate total travel and waiting time, and prepare that much offline activity.
  3. Three days out: pack in three groups; the carry-on holds a full change of clothes.
  4. One day out: confirm offline content is already downloaded, not queued for the airport.
  5. On the day: let the child move before the first stop that requires sitting still.

FAQ

How many sights per day is realistic?

Two for preschoolers, three for primary age. Beyond that you are moving, not playing.

Do the activities have to work offline?

The moment you need them is usually the moment there is no signal — planes, tunnels, mountains. Offline is the baseline requirement.

Does Lumi Trip Planet show ads?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no ads and kid-safe content.
