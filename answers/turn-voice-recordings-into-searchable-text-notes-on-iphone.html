A recording is only findable once it is text: record on the iPhone, get a summary and key points right there, then export as text or Markdown into the notes tool you already search. Sono Note runs the whole flow on the device with no account.

Start by deciding what the recording is for. A three-hour lecture, a client meeting and an idea that arrives while walking need three different kinds of text: a lecture needs key points and content you can search, a meeting needs action items and owners, a walking thought needs one clean paragraph. Decide which one you want first, or you will end up with a wall of raw transcript nobody reads.

Second, make transcription and tidying one action instead of two. Sono Note hands you a finished note the moment you stop: a summary, key points, action items and a follow-up email ready to send, with the original recording kept inside the note so you can replay a passage instead of the whole thing. There are ready templates for classes, client calls, interviews, doctor visits and ideas, and everything happens on the iPhone with no account, so it keeps working in airplane mode or a subway tunnel.

Third, make the text findable. Export the note as text or Markdown and put it where you already search, whether that is Notes or the company drive, so next week a keyword brings back the deadline someone mentioned. The free tier gives 3 notes a day at up to 3 minutes each, enough to test how well your speaking style transcribes; longer recordings and PDF export come with Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether you get a raw transcript or a tidied summary with key points
  • Whether recording and transcription both stay on the phone
  • Whether it still records with no signal, on the subway or underground
  • Whether notes export as text or Markdown into the tool you search
  • Whether the free tier is enough to judge accuracy, and whether paying is a subscription or one unlock

The steps that actually work

  1. Before recording, choose the purpose: lecture, meeting or walking idea, which decides whether you want a summary or the full content.
  2. Put the phone close to the speaker; one voice and a quiet room transcribe cleanest.
  3. Read the summary and key points first and check proper nouns; replay the original audio inside the note wherever you are unsure.
  4. Tick the action items into your to-do list, then export the note as text or Markdown to the place you search every day.
  5. Once a week, delete recordings you no longer need from the one place that lists them, so what remains is what you actually look for.

FAQ

Will a three-hour lecture transcribe accurately?

Accuracy depends on the audio: close to the speaker, one voice, quiet room. Check proper nouns before relying on the text. The free tier allows 3 minutes per note; long lectures need Pro's recordings of up to 3 hours.

Can I search Sono Note's text in another app?

Yes. Export as text or Markdown (PDF with Pro), drop it into your notes app or cloud drive, and that tool's search finds the keywords.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is processed on the iPhone, it works in airplane mode, and your recordings and notes never leave the device.
