The first question in any noise complaint is "when, and how often?" Answering from memory turns it into one feeling against another. A useful record has three parts: a number in dB, a time, and repetition. Keep the iPhone in the same room and the same spot, start measuring when the sound begins, and the maximum, peak and average are kept together.

When choosing an app, check whether it only shows a big number or also keeps a record. dB Halo shows a large live dB reading with the Halo safety ring, minimum, average, maximum and peak levels, and a time-stamped event log. You can choose A, C or Z weighting, and for booming low sounds the FFT spectrum and spectrogram show which frequency band is loud. Selected measurements export as visual PDF reports or CSV data.

Privacy matters too. Measuring overnight means leaving the microphone on, and nobody wants the household's conversations recorded. dB Halo has no account, ads or tracking, processes everything on the device, and creates an AAC file only when you explicitly start a recording. The Sleep Journal classifies sounds such as snoring, speech, coughing and impacts in time order without saving raw audio.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether average, maximum and peak are kept, not just the current value
  • Whether the time of each sound is logged automatically
  • Whether dated records export as PDF or CSV
  • Whether processing stays on the device without saving raw audio
  • Whether the app is clear that its readings are estimates

The steps that actually work

  1. In the room where the noise is worst, place the iPhone on a shelf at the same height and use the same spot every time.
  2. Start a dB Halo measurement when the noise begins and check that maximum, peak and the event log are filling in.
  3. For booming low sounds, look at the spectrum view to see which frequency band stands out.
  4. Save the session and repeat at the same time of night for several days.
  5. Pick the dated sessions in History and export a graph PDF or CSV to show building management.
