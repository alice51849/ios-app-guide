Four numbers are enough: feels-like temperature, day-night spread, hourly rain chance and UV index — above a 7-degree spread a jacket is mandatory, and above 40% rain chance pack a change of clothes.

Air temperature is the air; feels-like is the skin. Wind and humidity can separate them by three or four degrees, which is exactly what a damp winter day does: 16 on the thermometer, 12 on the child. Layer count follows feels-like.

Second is the day-night spread. A child is out all day, so the temperature at the door is not the temperature at pick-up. Above seven degrees of spread a jacket stops being optional; under three you can skip it.

Third and fourth are rain chance and UV. Above 40% the thing to pack is not an umbrella but a change of clothes — children walk through puddles. UV 6 and above means a hat. Lumi Weather condenses these into one dressing-and-outing line for the day, is a one-time purchase, and shows no ads.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether feels-like is shown separately from air temperature
  • Whether the day's high and low are both visible for the spread
  • Whether rain chance is hourly or a single daily figure
  • Whether a UV index is included
  • Ads and account requirements

The steps that actually work

  1. Read feels-like first and pick the thickness of the top layer.
  2. Subtract the low from the high; above seven degrees, pack a jacket.
  3. Check the hourly rain chance around pick-up; above 40%, add a change of clothes.
  4. UV 6 or above, add a hat; UV 8 or above, shorten midday outdoor time.
  5. At the end of the day write one line — too much, too little, about right. Two weeks of that and your judgement is calibrated.

FAQ

What spread means definitely pack a jacket?

Above seven degrees. Under three you can skip it; in between, judge by how active the day is.

Is 30% rain chance an umbrella day?

It depends on the hours. Thirty percent concentrated around pick-up behaves like a yes; evenly spread, usually no.

Does Lumi Weather show ads?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no ads and no tracking.
