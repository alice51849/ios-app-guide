# Aim990 Plus — English Exam Practice Offline | iOS App

Get comfortable working against the clock Aim990 Plus is a dedicated listening and reading trainer for serious adult learners. It gives every session a practical rhythm: open with an 8-question mixed Warm-up, complete a 20-question Pressure Set within 15 minutes, then use Mistake Replay to work through errors while they are still fresh. Plenty of original material Train with 630 original questions, organised as 90 items in each of Parts 1–7. That includes 90 different photographs for the visual questions. All material was written for this app and is not represented as official exam content. Mix focused drills with longer runs Choose mini, half or full mock formats to suit the time available. Dictation lets you slow down and catch what you missed; speed drills tighten the clock when you are ready to push. Use your results constructively Weakness analytics show which areas deserve another look, without predicting or guaranteeing a score.
