Aim990 Plus — Offline Exam Pressure Trainer | iOS App

Train for clear decisions under exam pressure Aim990 Plus is a focused listening and reading trainer for serious adult learners. It turns preparation into a repeatable loop: begin with an 8-question mixed Warm-up, move into a 20-question Pressure Set with a 15-minute limit, then use Mistake Replay to revisit what went wrong. A complete original question bank Work through 630 original questions, organized as 90 items in each of Parts 1–7. The visual material includes 90 distinct photos. Questions are created for this app and are not presented as official exam content. Practice for the way you need to improve Choose mini, half, or full mock formats when you want a longer test session. Use dictation to catch details, speed drills to make decisions under tighter timing, and device speech for additional audio practice. A 150-entry vocabulary collection helps you review useful language alongside the question bank. Turn mistakes into a plan Weakness analytics highlight areas that deserve more attention. Your progress and profile keep sessions connected, while Mistake Replay gives missed questions another purposeful pass instead of leaving them behind. Private by design The app works offline and keeps its core experience on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account, server connection, advertising, tracking, or analytics.
