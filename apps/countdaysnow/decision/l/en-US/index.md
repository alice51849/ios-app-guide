# CountDaysNow: Event Counter — Countdown Plan: Work Backwards | iOS App

Plan backwards from the dates that matter. Countdown Plan gives any event its own preparation steps, such as renew the passport 30 days before a trip or order the cake 7 days before a birthday, and shows what is late, due today, due in the next 7 days and done across all your events. Each step is due a set number of days before its event. Change the event date and every step moves with it; a repeating event starts each new cycle with a fresh plan. Countdown Plan is free, works on-device and needs no account. Daily Focus saves one private Since / Today / Ahead choice each day and preserves that dated trail as milestones change. No ads, no subscription, no account. Apple Watch stays useful offline. • Milestone alerts and 15 calendars A gentle nudge arrives 7, 3 and 1 day before, on the day itself, at day 100 and on every anniversary.
