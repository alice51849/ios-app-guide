DailyMate Privacy Policy

Effective July 13, 2026

Privacy at a glance

DailyMate does not require an account and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social-network SDKs. We do not collect or sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your selected languages, topic, difficulty level, learning progress, daily goal, voice preference, and saved-phrase library are stored locally on your device. A limited copy of the phrases needed by the Home Screen widget is stored in DailyMate's private App Group container. Selected learning settings, saved phrases, and progress may also sync locally between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity service. DailyMate does not send this data to a developer server.

App purchase and reviews

DailyMate is sold as a paid app through the App Store. Apple processes the transaction, and DailyMate does not receive your payment-card details. If the app requests an App Store rating, the request is handled by Apple's system interface. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Speech and network access

Pronunciation uses voices installed and provided by the operating system. DailyMate does not upload phrases, saved items, or learning progress to our servers. Core learning features work without an account.

Data retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on the device until you remove it or delete DailyMate. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. Your App Store purchase history remains with Apple so you can download the app again through your Apple Account.

Children

DailyMate is a general language-learning app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users because the app has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking system.
