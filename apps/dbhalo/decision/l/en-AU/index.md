# dB Halo: Sound Meter — Noise Dose, Sleep & Spectrum | iOS App

Check a busy worksite, aircon, backyard tools or noise around a coastal holiday home. dB Halo gives you a clear, private view of the sound around you while processing stays on your devices. The large Halo safety ring shows live dB, minimum, average, maximum and peak readings, plus a time-stamped event log. Select A, C or Z weighting, connect an external microphone and save multiple calibration profiles. Analyse one live session in three synchronised views: FFT Spectrum, RTA octave bands and Spectrogram. Noise Dose estimates daily exposure against NIOSH or OSHA criteria and shows the remaining safer time. Use it as a practical guide around worksite noise, not as a work health and safety assessment. The on-device Sleep Journal recognises and classifies snoring, breathing, speech, coughing, alarms, animal sounds, impacts, ambient sound and crying, logging each event without retaining raw microphone audio. Microphone audio is not saved by default; an AAC file is created only when you explicitly start a recording. History keeps every saved session organised and ready when you need it. Export selected measurements as visual PDF reports or CSV data, export explicitly recorded AAC files and burn dB overlays onto photos and videos. Export environmental audio levels to Apple Health, check Home and Lock Screen widgets, use App Intents and Shortcuts and take standalone measurements on Apple Watch. No account, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything is processed on your devices. Each general feature that produces a result includes three free uses. Spectrum, Noise Dose and Sleep Journal each include one. A use counts only after a result is created; cancellations, denied permissions and failures do not count, and existing results remain readable. Lifetime Pro is one non-consumable in-app purchase: pay once, own it for good, with no subscription. Readings are estimates. dB Halo is not a certified professional sound level meter and does not replace medical advice or a work health and safety assessment.
