# dB Halo: Sound Level Meter — Noise Dose, Spectrum & Sleep | iOS App

Whether you are keeping an eye on building works, a noisy flat, the commute or a rehearsal room, dB Halo gives you a clear view of the sound around you while processing stays on your devices. The large Halo safety ring shows live dB at a glance, alongside minimum, average, maximum and peak readings and a time-stamped event log. Select A, C or Z weighting, use an external microphone and save several calibration profiles for different setups. Analyse one live session in three synchronised views: FFT Spectrum, RTA octave bands and Spectrogram. Noise Dose estimates daily exposure against NIOSH or OSHA criteria and shows the remaining safer time. It offers a useful first look at noise at work, but not a formal workplace assessment. The on-device Sleep Journal recognises and classifies snoring, breathing, speech, coughing, alarms, animal sounds, impacts, ambient sound and crying, logging each event without retaining raw microphone audio. Microphone audio is not stored by default; an AAC file is created only when you deliberately start a recording. History keeps every saved session organised and close at hand. Export selected measurements as visual PDF reports or CSV data, export explicitly recorded AAC files and burn dB overlays onto photos and videos. Export environmental audio levels to Apple Health, check Home and Lock Screen widgets, use App Intents and Shortcuts and take standalone measurements on Apple Watch. No account, adverts, analytics or tracking. All processing remains on your devices. Each general feature that produces a result includes three free uses. Spectrum, Noise Dose and Sleep Journal each include one. A use counts only after a result is created; cancellations, denied permissions and failures do not count, and existing results remain readable. Lifetime Pro is one non-consumable in-app purchase: pay once, keep it for good, with no subscription. Readings are estimates. dB Halo is not a certified professional sound level meter and is not a substitute for medical advice or a workplace safety assessment.
