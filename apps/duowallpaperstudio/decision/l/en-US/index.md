# Foldable Wallpaper Studio — Dual Screen Pairs & Widgets | iOS App

Wallpapers made in pairs for the two screens of iPhone Duo. Close it: a close-up on the cover screen. Open it: the whole scene on the inner screen, both cut from the same original artwork. 300+ original pairs in 14 styles · 5 widgets drawn with your wallpaper · your own photos as pairs · no ads, no account, no data collected. Most wallpapers fit one screen, but a foldable has two, so Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes them in pairs: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen, cut from the same original artwork at the exact pixel sizes of iPhone Duo. WHY YOU WILL LOVE IT Every set is a true pair, composed for both screens 300+ original sets in many styles at launch Closed and open previews before you save Recolor a set and add one short line of text Turn your own photo into a pair, with the focus where you want it Widgets in the same colors as your wallpaper No ads, no account, no tracking EVERYDAY IDEAS A quiet night sky for work, a cute 3D scene for the weekend, or a pure black glow that looks great on OLED. Or a travel photo: the close-up on the cover screen, the full landscape inside. Add a matching clock, calendar or text card, and your Home Screen and Lock Screen look like one design. 300+ SETS IN MANY STYLES Japanese-style illustrated scenery: summer clouds, seaside towns and starry skies Warm lantern lanes and stone paths Golden autumn and snowy seasonal moments Fantasy realms with floating islands and glowing light Cute clay and 3D Rainy windows and city lights at night Natural wonders and auroras Classic art styles: oil painting, woodblock print and watercolor Flowers and blossoms Calm minimal textures and solid colors Pure black glow for OLED Vivid satin, foil and metal textures Macaron pastel colors All artwork is original, with no characters or works from other brands. FEATURES Closed and open previews in the app, on any iPhone or iPad Color swatches and one line of your own text, with both previews redrawn instantly In the Create tab, make a pair from your own photo and drag to set the focus. The system photo picker is used, so the app never reads your whole library Pixel-exact export: 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen, saved as two images to Photos with add-only access. On other models, iOS scales the image Five widgets: My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card, all drawn with the wallpaper you choose, in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes.
