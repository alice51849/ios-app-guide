# EasyAM: Workday Alarm Clock — Bank holiday? It stays quiet | iOS App

Will your alarm go off tomorrow, and should it? EasyAM works it out for you: it rings on working days and stays quiet on bank holidays and your days off, so you can stop switching alarms off and on again. EasyAM opens on your next seven days, or 14 on iPad: which mornings will ring, and when. Why you'll love it Bank holidays for your country or region are skipped automatically. Add annual leave, a trip or a sick day, and those mornings stay quiet. Give one day, or a few in a row, its own ring time; every other day stays the same. Fancy a lie-in? From the day before, tap “Sleep in” to silence just the next ring while your routine stays as it is. Holiday data for 67 countries and regions is built in, works offline and can be updated. Real iOS system alarms, with Alarms Ready shown only after iOS has confirmed the schedule. Made for real weeks Office workers: the bank holiday Monday stays quiet and Tuesday rings as normal, without you lifting a finger. Teachers and students: add half-term or study leave as days off. Shift workers: add overtime, night shifts or on-call days as extra working days; repeating rotas use the shift patterns in the in-app purchase. Working abroad or relocating? Change your country or region at any time. Features One alarm with a weekly repeat or a one-off time, a label and snooze from 1 to 60 minutes. Days off and extra working days as single days or date ranges, each with your own label and icon; a day off can have its own wake-up alarm, and a whole range can be edited or removed in one go. Up to 12 alarm categories with your own name, colour and one of 59 icons, plus batch editing for several alarms at once. Calendar tab with a month view of bank holidays, make-up working days and your days off. Make-up working days ring where a country has them, and missing or out-of-date holiday data counts as a working day, so the alarm rings. Holiday data covers 2026 to 2028, including state or provincial holidays for 15 countries. 101 clock themes in ten collections, from flip cards and glowing digits to analogue watch dials and departure boards. Standard is included and every theme can be previewed full screen. With an analogue dial theme, the Home Screen widget becomes a round clock face. Layouts for iPhone and iPad, 12 or 24-hour time, light or dark appearance, and 50 languages that follow your system language. Widgets and Apple Watch The Next Alarm widget shows your next alarm and holiday status in small, medium and large, plus extra large on iPad; the medium and large sizes let you sleep in tomorrow with a tap. Lock Screen widgets come in circular, rectangular and inline styles, and the small widget also appears in StandBy. While an alarm rings or snoozes, it shows as a Live Activity and in the Dynamic Island. Your theme also colours the Lock Screen widgets, the Live Activity and the Apple Watch clock. The Apple Watch app installs with the iPhone app and shows your next alarm and the coming seven days. Ask to sleep in tomorrow from your wrist, and the watch shows it as done once your iPhone has confirmed it with iOS. Complications and a Smart Stack card are included; the watch works with its paired iPhone, which stays the alarm source. Privacy No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your alarms and settings stay on your device; the app only goes online to download public holiday data. Purchases Unlimited alarms and alarm duplication, shift and alternating-week patterns, a holiday calendar for each alarm and 100 more clock themes are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase.
