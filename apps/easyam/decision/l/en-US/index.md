# EasyAM: Holiday Alarm Clock — Rings for work, skips days off | iOS App

Will your alarm ring tomorrow, and should it? EasyAM decides for you, day by day: it rings on workdays and stays quiet on public holidays and your days off. Open the app and your next seven days are right there, showing which mornings ring and at what time. No more turning alarms off before a long weekend and back on after it. What changes with EasyAM Public and regional holidays are skipped automatically. Vacations, sick days and trips stay quiet once you add them as time off. Make-up working days ring as usual in places that have them. Leaving early one day? Change the time for just that day; the rest stay the same. Want a slow start tomorrow? One tap skips only the next ring and keeps your routine. Your next alarm is on your widgets, Lock Screen and Apple Watch. Made for real routines Office workers: long weekends take care of themselves, with nothing to switch off the night before. Students and teachers: ring on class days and add school breaks as time off. Shift workers: set a shift or alternating-week pattern once, available with the in-app purchase. Working abroad or moving: change your country or region anytime, with 67 countries and regions built in. Features One alarm with weekly repeat or a one-time ring, a label and snooze from 1 to 60 minutes Seven-day preview in the app, 14 days in the wide iPad layout Holiday data for 67 countries and regions for 2026 to 2028, usable offline, with newer data files to download Days with unknown or outdated holiday data count as workdays, so the alarm rings Time off for a single day or a date range, with your own label and icon and an optional wake-up alarm Extra working days such as overtime, business trips, night shifts or on-call days, also with labels and icons Edit or remove a whole date range at once Different ring times on chosen days, for one day or several in a row Up to 12 alarm categories with your own name, color and one of 59 icons Batch editing for several alarms Calendar tab with public holidays, make-up working days and your days off Real iOS system alarms, marked ready only after iOS confirms the schedule, plus a schedule health screen A Live Activity and Dynamic Island view while an alarm rings or snoozes 101 clock themes in ten collections, from flip cards and glowing digits to analog watch dials and departure boards, each with a full-screen preview iPhone and iPad layouts, 50 languages, 12-hour or 24-hour time, light and dark appearance Widgets and Apple Watch The Next Alarm widget comes in small, medium and large sizes, plus extra large on iPad, and shows your next alarm with holiday and schedule status; medium and large add a one-tap button to sleep in tomorrow. Lock Screen widgets come in three styles, and the small widget also appears in StandBy. Choose an analog dial theme and the Home Screen widget becomes a round clock face. The Apple Watch app installs with the iPhone app and shows your next alarm and the coming seven days, synced from your iPhone. You can skip tomorrow's ring from your wrist too; it shows done once your iPhone confirms it with iOS. Complications and a Smart Stack card are included. Your iPhone schedules the alarms, so the watch app works with your paired iPhone. Privacy No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your alarms and settings stay on your device; the only network use is downloading public holiday data files. Unlimited alarms, shift and alternating-week patterns, a holiday calendar for each alarm and 100 more clock themes are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. Support Questions or ideas?
