JellyDay

Visual Daily Planner

Your whole day as soft pastel blocks you can see at a glance. Plan it in seconds, follow it at a glance, and watch a gentle countdown show the time left.

See your whole dayDrag tasks onto today's timeline. Longer tasks are taller blocks, and a bright line marks "now", so what you are doing and what comes next are easy to spot.
Ready-made routinesStart from routines such as morning, work from home, weekend cleaning and bedtime. Bring yesterday's routine over to today with one tap.
Watch time passA shrinking pie shows how much time is left. A Live Activity and the Dynamic Island keep the countdown in view while you focus.
Running behind?Push the rest of your day back 15 minutes with one tap, so the whole plan moves with you.
Cute jellyCute jelly characters and stickers make every task friendlier, and a done task gives a little wobble.
Private by designNo account, no ads, no tracking, and JellyDay collects no data. Your plans stay on your device; optional iCloud sync uses your own private iCloud.

JellyDay offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks extras such as routines that repeat automatically, all routines plus your own, and iCloud sync. There is no subscription.

JellyDay is a planning tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
