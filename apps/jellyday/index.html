JellyDay

Visual Daily Planner

Your whole day as soft pastel blocks you can see at a glance. Plan it in seconds, follow it at a glance, and watch a gentle countdown show the time left.

Download on the App Store Google PlayGet it on Google Play
See your whole dayAdd tasks to today's timeline, and press and hold a block to drag it to a new time. Longer tasks are taller blocks, and a bright line marks "now", so what you are doing and what comes next are easy to spot.
Ready-made routinesStart from 50 ready-made routines, such as morning and bedtime. Bring yesterday's routine over to today with one tap.
Watch time passA shrinking pie shows how much time is left. A Live Activity and the Dynamic Island keep the countdown in view while you focus.
Running behind?Push the rest of your day back 15 minutes with one tap, so the whole plan moves with you.
Cute jellyCute jelly characters and stickers make every task friendlier, and a done task gives a little wobble.
Private by designNo account, no ads, no tracking, and JellyDay collects no data. Your plans are stored only on your device.

JellyDay offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks extras such as routines that repeat automatically, all 50 routines, all 60 stickers, and more widgets. There is no subscription.
