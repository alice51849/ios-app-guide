JellyDay Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Privacy at a glance

JellyDay does not require an account and does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social-network SDKs. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your tasks, routines, stickers, completed marks, and app settings are stored in the app's container on your device. The widgets, the Live Activity, and the Apple Watch app read a copy of your plan on your own devices. Nothing is sent to a developer server, and JellyDay does not connect to a server operated by the developer. Your data is stored only on your own devices and is not uploaded to any cloud service.

Apple Watch

When you use the watch app, your plan is copied from your iPhone to your Apple Watch through Apple's system connection between your own devices.

Notifications and focus alarm

Reminders are optional and local: they are scheduled on your device and are not sent through a server. On iOS 26 and later, JellyDay can use Apple's AlarmKit so a focus countdown rings when it ends, only if you give permission. You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

The one-time in-app purchase is processed by Apple. JellyDay does not receive your payment-card details. Apple provides a verified entitlement so the purchase can be restored, refunded, or revoked under App Store policies. Family Sharing shares the purchase, not your plans. There is no subscription.

Ratings

After you complete a few tasks, JellyDay may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple handles your response; JellyDay does not receive it or your identity.

Children

JellyDay does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. It is a planning tool for people of any age, and it is not a medical device.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete a task or routine or delete the app. Purchase history remains with Apple so an eligible purchase can be restored.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hourstag.app@gmail.com.
