JellyDay Support

Answers to common questions about JellyDay. If you cannot find what you need, write to us at the address at the bottom of this page.

How do I plan my day?

Open the Today tab and add tasks to the timeline. Longer tasks become taller blocks, and a bright line shows where "now" is. To change a time, press and hold a block and drag it; it moves in 5-minute steps. Mark a task done when you finish it. You can plan as many tasks as you like.

What are routines?

JellyDay has 50 ready-made routines, such as morning and bedtime, and 3 of them are free. Open the Routines tab, pick one, and add it to today. Routine mode walks you through each step, one countdown at a time. Repeating a routine automatically and using all 50 routines are part of the one-time purchase.

I am running behind. Can I move my day?

Yes. You can push the rest of your day back by 15 minutes with one tap, and bring yesterday's routine over to today with one tap.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Reminders are optional. In JellyDay Settings, check that the reminders for when a block starts and before it ends are turned on, then make sure notifications are allowed for JellyDay in iPhone Settings > Notifications. A Focus mode can also silence notifications. JellyDay schedules reminders on your device for about the next 48 hours.

How do I add a widget?

Press and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap the + button, and search for JellyDay. Widgets show the plan stored on your device. If a widget looks out of date, open JellyDay once.

The focus countdown and Live Activity

Start a focus countdown to follow the time left as a shrinking pie. If you do not see it on the Lock Screen or in the Dynamic Island, check that Live Activities are allowed for JellyDay in iPhone Settings. On iOS 26 and later, JellyDay can also ring when a focus countdown ends, even in Silent or Focus mode, if you allow it when asked. A regular notification is scheduled as a backup.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes, together with your iPhone. The JellyDay watch app shows what is happening now, what comes next, and the countdown. You can start or pause the countdown and mark a task done, and you can add a JellyDay complication to your watch face. Plans are copied from your iPhone to your watch, so open JellyDay on your iPhone if the watch looks out of date.

How does the purchase work?

JellyDay has one in-app purchase, paid once and processed by Apple, with no subscription. To get it back on a new device or after reinstalling, open Settings in JellyDay and choose Restore purchase, using the same Apple Account. Family Sharing shares the purchase, while each person's plans stay their own. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Plans live on your device, so deleting the app removes them.

Using JellyDay on Android

The Android version (Google Play) has the same day timeline, routines, focus countdown, reminders and stickers. While you focus, the countdown stays in your notifications, and a notification tells you when it is up. Apple Watch, Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities are iPhone features and are not part of the Android version. On tablets and unfolded foldable phones, JellyDay uses a two-column layout.

Widgets and reminders on Android

To add a widget, press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets and choose JellyDay. The Now widget is included; the large Timeline widget comes with the one-time purchase. If reminders do not appear, check that notifications are allowed for JellyDay in Android Settings > Apps > JellyDay > Notifications; battery saving can also delay them.

Purchases on Android

On Android the one-time purchase is processed by Google Play, with no subscription. To get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings in JellyDay and choose Restore purchase with the same Google account. Refund requests are handled by Google Play. To delete your data, clear JellyDay's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
