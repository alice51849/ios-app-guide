JellyDay

A visual daily planner: your whole day on one timeline

JellyDay turns today into colored blocks on a single timeline. The longer a task takes, the taller its block, and a bright line shows where you are right now, so what's now, what's next and how much time is left are clear at a glance. A routine timer then walks you through your morning or bedtime one step at a time.

See how long everything takes, not just what's on your plate

A list tells you what to do, but not how long each thing takes or whether there's any room left. In JellyDay, a half-hour task and a two-hour task sit side by side on the same timeline, so you can see how full your day already is before you add more.

Blocks sized by timeGive each task a name, sticker, start time, color and a length from 5 minutes to 2 hours. Add as many as you like.
A line for right nowThe now line moves down the timeline as the day goes on, so you always know where you are and whether there's time for the next thing.
Drag to rescheduleLong-press any block and drag it to a new time in 5-minute steps.
Plan laterNot sure when yet? Save a task to Plan later and put it on the timeline when you're ready.

A routine timer that walks you through each step

Add a ready-made routine, such as morning or bedtime, to today with one tap. JellyDay then takes you through it one step at a time, and each step gets its own countdown, so you don't have to remember what comes next.

For focused work or study, start the focus timer for a block. A shrinking pie shows the minutes left, with the time spelled out in plain words, and you can pause, resume or mark it done. The countdown stays in your notifications while you focus, and a notification lets you know when it's up.

When the day doesn't go as planned

Running late? From a task's menu, push it and the rest of today's unfinished tasks back 15 minutes, or move it to tomorrow.

Missed a few? The Now card moves everything you missed to tomorrow in one tap, or rebuilds the rest of today starting now. And if yesterday worked well, bring yesterday's schedule over to today with one tap.

Now and next, without opening the app

The Now widget for your home screen, in small and medium sizes, shows what you're doing and what's next. Reminders can let you know when a task starts and when it's about to end, and you can turn them off in settings.

On tablets and unfolded foldable phones, JellyDay uses a two-column layout. It's available in 50 languages and follows your device language.

Your plans stay on your device

The app needs no account or sign-up, and has no ads and no tracking. JellyDay does not collect any data, and the app makes no network connections of its own.
