Visual daily planner apps for iPhone, compared honestly (JellyDay vs Structured, Tiimo, Routinery, TimeBloc)

I make JellyDay. It came out in November 2026, so it is much newer than the apps below. This page compares them using their US App Store listings, read on November 26, 2026, and says where each one is better than mine. Ratings and prices change, so check each store page before you decide.

The short version

  • Want your calendar and reminders on the same timeline, synced to a Mac? Structured or Tiimo. JellyDay does not connect to calendars.
  • Want routines that talk you through each step out loud? Routinery.
  • Want a timeline planner with built-in statistics? TimeBloc.
  • Want a cute, offline timeline with no account, paid for once instead of by subscription? That is what JellyDay is for.

Where the others are better than JellyDay

  • Track record. JellyDay had no US ratings when I checked. Structured alone has about 169,000. If you want an app with a long track record and many reviews, start there.
  • Calendars. Structured (Pro), Tiimo and TimeBloc pull in your existing calendar. JellyDay's description does not mention calendar sync, so meetings have to be added by hand.
  • Mac and other devices. Structured and Routinery run on Mac too. JellyDay works on iPhone and iPad, and its description says plans are stored on your device; it does not mention sync.
  • Voice guidance. Routinery reads each routine step aloud. JellyDay shows a countdown but does not describe voice prompts.
  • AI help. Structured and Tiimo can draft a schedule with AI. JellyDay has no AI features.
  • Statistics. TimeBloc shows your progress over time. JellyDay does not list statistics.

Where JellyDay may fit better

  • Pay once. "Some features are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase." The others in this table sell mainly subscriptions (two also list a lifetime option).
  • See how full your day is. "every task is a soft pastel block, and the taller the block, the longer it takes. A bright line shows where you are right now."
  • Running late. "Push a task back 15 minutes and every unfinished task after it moves too; finished ones stay put." Leftover tasks can be started "all from now, or move them all to tomorrow, with one tap."
  • Countdown you can see. "A shrinking pie shows the minutes left, and you can pause or finish from the Live Activity without opening the app."
  • Private and offline. "No account and no sign-up. No ads and no tracking. JellyDay does not collect any data, and the app makes no network connections of its own."
  • Cute. "Cute jelly characters and stickers that make each task easy to spot."

Who should not use JellyDay

  • Anyone whose day is mostly calendar meetings: pick a planner that syncs your calendar.
  • Anyone who plans on a Mac or needs the same plan on several devices.
  • Anyone who wants AI to build the schedule for them.
  • Anyone who wants an app with a long track record and many reviews.
