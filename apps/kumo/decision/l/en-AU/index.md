# Kumo: GLP-1 & Weight Tracker — Fasting timer & injection log | iOS App

One bad weigh-in shouldn't ruin your week, so Kumo shows your 7-day average instead of daily noise. Logging takes about 10 seconds a day, and it works just as well whether or not you're on GLP-1. Built for iPhone Duo: open it up and Kumo fills the whole inner screen in two roomy columns. WHAT MAKES KUMO DIFFERENT • No account, no ads, no tracking MADE FOR REAL LIFE Taking GLP-1? Note each injection date and site. Not taking it? Leave that card off. WEIGHT AND TRENDS • Kilograms, pounds or stone, with daily dots and a 7-day average line, plus the last 30 days of trend and history • 3-month and all-time trends and older history (Kumo Full) • This week's average vs last week, plus non-scale wins like looser jeans BODY STATS AND GOAL ESTIMATE • Waist, hips, chest, thigh, upper arm, neck and more, each with its own trend (1 free, all with Kumo Full) • An estimated goal date at your current pace (reference only) • Before-and-after photos, with faces blurred by default (Kumo Full) FASTING TIMER • 12:12, 14:10 and 16:8; 18:6, 20:4, custom and fasting charts with Kumo Full • One-tap start you can adjust later • Time left on Apple Watch; live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with Kumo Full • A reminder to eat a proper meal when time's up. Kumo never pushes longer fasts; the limit is 23 hours • If you're pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, or have a chronic condition or a history of eating issues, talk to your GP before fasting INJECTION CARD (OPTIONAL) • Log the date, site and your own notes, with reminders on the schedule you set • Next-injection countdown, map of recent sites and how-you-feel check-ins • Records only: no calculations, no dose suggestions or dose logging, no interpretation WATER AND PROTEIN • Tap +1 glass of water or +10, 20 or 30 g of protein; rings show today's progress • See today's steps from the Health app (shown only, never stored) A CLOUD THAT GROWS • Grows in 5 stages with the days you log, not your weight • Basic cloud free; outfits and weather themes (sunny, rainbow, rain, snow, starry night) with Kumo Full APPLE WATCH, WIDGETS AND SIRI • Turn the Digital Crown and tap once to log, even without your phone, plus watch face complications, including fasting • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, all free: logged today, streak, weekly average, your cloud, plus a quick-log widget • A Control Centre button • Ask Siri to log weight or water, or open Today WEEKLY CARD AND EXPORT • Each week, a card shows your change and curve, never your weight. Share in one tap • CSV and PDF monthly report export (Kumo Full) FREE Weight log, 7-day average, 30-day trend and history; 1 module of your choice (fasting 12:12, 14:10 or 16:8, injection card, or water and protein); 1 body stat; 1 reminder; Apple Watch logging and watch face complications; all Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; the basic cloud; no account; your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, synced between iPhone and Apple Watch; Health app sync is optional. KUMO FULL Several modules at once and all body stats; 18:6, 20:4, custom fasting and fasting charts; Lock Screen and Dynamic Island live countdown; 3-month and all-time trends and history beyond 30 days; before-and-after photos; CSV and PDF export; multiple and water reminders; outfits and weather. Kumo Full is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings. Kumo is for personal tracking only and does not provide health advice. Always follow your GP's guidance.
