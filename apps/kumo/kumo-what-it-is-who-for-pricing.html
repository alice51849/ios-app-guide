What Kumo is

Kumo is a weight log for iPhone and Apple Watch, also on Google Play for Android. The scale jumps around every day, so Kumo turns a 10-second weigh-in into a calm 7-day average, with or without GLP-1 shots. A cute cloud grows every day you show up.

  • Weight and trends: opens to today with your last weight filled in; adjust by 0.1. Daily dots and a 7-day average line; pounds, kilograms or stones; weekly average vs. last week.
  • Body stats: waist, body composition, hips, chest, thigh, upper arm and neck, each with a trend, and an estimated goal date for reference only.
  • Fasting timer: 12:12, 14:10 and 16:8 with a one-tap start and an editable start time; Kumo caps fasts at 23 hours and reminds you to eat a proper meal.
  • Shot card (optional): log the date, site and your own notes, with reminders on the schedule you set, a next-shot countdown, a map of recent sites and how-you-feel check-ins.
  • Water and protein: tap +1 glass of water or +10, 20 or 30 g of protein.
  • Apple Watch, widgets and Siri: twist the Digital Crown and tap to log, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center button, and Siri to log weight or water.

Who Kumo is for

  • People who weigh in often and get discouraged by daily ups and downs, and would rather watch a 7-day average.
  • People on GLP-1 shots who want to log each shot and see their weight trend in one place.
  • People not on shots who just want to weigh in and watch the trend; the shot card stays off.
  • People doing 16:8 who want a one-tap start and the time left on Apple Watch.
  • People who do not want an account: Kumo has no account, no ads and no tracking.

Fasting is not for everyone. The listing asks people who are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, or have eating problems or a chronic condition to talk to a health professional before fasting.

How pricing works

Kumo is free to download. Without a purchase you get the weight log with the 7-day average and the last 30 days of trend and history; one module of your choice (fasting 12:12, 14:10 or 16:8, the shot card, or water and protein); one body stat; one reminder; Apple Watch logging and watch face complications; all Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; and the basic cloud.

Kumo Full adds several modules at once and all body stats; 18:6, 20:4, custom fasting and fasting charts; a live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; 3-month and all-time trends and history beyond 30 days; before and after photos; CSV and PDF export; multiple and water reminders; and outfits and weather themes.

Kumo Full is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time in-app purchase. The current price for your region is shown on the App Store page. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled in your Apple Account settings.

How Kumo differs

  • About 10 seconds a day: it opens to today with your last weight already filled in.
  • Trend first: the 7-day average line sits next to the daily dots.
  • Gentle streaks: “Didn't weigh today” still keeps your streak.
  • A cloud that grows with the days you log, not your weight, with weather themes in Kumo Full.
  • Modules only when you want them: shots, fasting, water and protein are optional.
  • Records only: the shot card does no calculations, no dose tracking or suggestions and no interpretation.
  • Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking; on iPhone, your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, synced between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Shareable without the number: a weekly card shows your change, never your weight.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kumo free?

Kumo is free to download, and the weight log, 7-day average, one module, one body stat, one reminder, Apple Watch logging and all widgets work without a purchase. Kumo Full is a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time in-app purchase; see the App Store page for the price in your region.

Does Kumo need GLP-1 shots?

No. The shot card is optional. Kumo works the same for people who just weigh in or do intermittent fasting.

Does Kumo calculate or suggest doses?

No. The shot card records only: no calculations, no dose tracking or suggestions, no interpretation.

Do I need an account?

No. Kumo has no account, no ads and no tracking. On iPhone, your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Is Kumo on Android?

Yes. Kumo is also on Google Play; the link is on this page. Features can differ between iPhone and Android; the support page lists the differences.

Kumo is for personal tracking only and does not provide health advice. Always follow your health professional's guidance.
