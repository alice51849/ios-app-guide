Kumo Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 (Android section added November 6, 2026)

Privacy at a glance

Kumo does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. The developer never receives your records. There is no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Where your records are kept

Your weight entries, daily check-ins, notes, reminders, and settings are saved on your own device. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, the same records are also kept in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account so your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch show the same data. That iCloud data is protected by Apple and is not accessible to the developer. You can turn sync off at any time. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you turn it on, Kumo writes the weight and waist measurements you log to the Health app. Kumo reads today's step count from Health only to show it on your Today screen; your steps are displayed only and are never saved in Kumo, synced, or uploaded. If you also turn on “Add weights from Apple Health”, Kumo reads body weight that your smart scale or other apps saved to Health, and adds the weights you confirm (or, if you choose “Add automatically”, all new ones) to your Kumo log. Those weights are then stored like the weights you type: on your device and, if sync is on, in your own private iCloud. They are never uploaded anywhere else and never written back to Health. Kumo does not read any other Health data. Health data is never used for advertising.

Photos

Progress photos are optional. Photos you pick are stored only inside Kumo on your device (and in your own iCloud if sync is on). Kumo never uploads them anywhere. Sharing a photo or a weekly card happens only when you tap Share and choose where to send it.

Reading your scale with the camera is optional. When you tap the camera button while logging your weight, Kumo uses the camera to read the number on your scale's display. The reading happens entirely on your device; no picture is taken, saved or uploaded, and nothing is logged until you confirm the number.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Kumo asks for notification permission only after you choose to turn reminders on.

Purchases

Some features are unlocked with an in-app purchase or subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; Kumo never receives your payment details.

Android (Google Play)

The Android version follows the same promise: Kumo does not collect, share, or sell any personal data, and the developer never receives your records. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

Your weight entries, daily check-ins, notes, reminders, photos, and settings are saved only on your phone. Kumo for Android has no cloud sync. If you export a backup file from Settings, the file goes where you choose to save or send it, and only you control it. Clearing Kumo's storage or uninstalling the app removes the data from the phone.

Health Connect is optional. If you turn it on, Android asks you to allow Kumo to read your body weight, and Kumo adds the weights you confirm to your log. Kumo reads no other Health Connect data, and the weights are stored only on your phone and never uploaded. You can remove access at any time in Android Settings, under Health Connect, then App permissions.

Progress photos and reading your scale with the camera are optional. Photos stay inside Kumo on your phone; blurring faces in a photo and reading the number on your scale happen entirely on your device, and nothing is uploaded. Reminders and the notification shown during a fast are local notifications on your phone.

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; Kumo never receives your payment details. Kumo for Android uses Google Play Billing for purchases and Google ML Kit models that run on your device; neither sends your records to the developer.

Not medical advice

Kumo is a personal log. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or calculate doses.

Children

Kumo is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
