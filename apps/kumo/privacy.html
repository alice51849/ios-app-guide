Kumo Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 (Android section added November 6, 2026; updated November 16, 2026: face data, iCloud sync, Apple Health, notifications, sharing and exports)

Privacy at a glance

Kumo does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. The developer never receives your records. There is no account or sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. The iOS app contains no third-party SDKs; the Android app uses only the Google components listed in the Android section.

Where your records are kept

Your entries (weight, waist and other body measurements, fasting sessions, shot dates and injection sites, water, protein and mood check-ins, notes), progress photos, reminders, and settings are saved on your own device. If your device is signed in to iCloud, Kumo automatically keeps the same records in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, so your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch show the same data; Kumo's Settings shows whether iCloud is available. There is no separate sync switch inside Kumo: to stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Kumo in the iOS Settings app, or sign out of iCloud. That iCloud data is protected by Apple and is not accessible to the developer.

Widgets, controls and Apple Watch complications read a small summary (recent weights, streak, next shot date, fasting start time, water and mood) that Kumo keeps in its app group container on your device; Siri shortcuts and Live Activities use your records on the device. None of this is uploaded. After an app update, Kumo also keeps up to three automatic snapshot files of your records, including progress photos, on your device as a safety copy.

Exports (a backup file, a CSV file, or a monthly PDF report) are created only when you ask, and go only where you choose to save or send them.

Deleting the app removes the data on that device, but not the copy in your iCloud. Delete all data in Kumo's Settings erases your records on this device and in your iCloud, including the automatic snapshots. It does not remove weights or waist measurements Kumo already wrote to the Health app; you can delete those in Health.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional, and each Health feature has its own switch. With “Write to Apple Health” on, Kumo writes the weight and waist measurements you log to the Health app. With “Connect Apple Health” on the Today screen, Kumo reads today's step count only to show it there; your steps are never saved in Kumo, synced, or uploaded. With “Add weights from Apple Health” on, Kumo reads body weight that your smart scale or other apps saved to Health and adds the weights you confirm (or, if you choose “Add automatically”, all new ones) to your Kumo log. Those weights are then stored like the weights you type: on your device and, while iCloud sync is active, in your own private iCloud. They are never uploaded anywhere else and never written back to Health. So that it does not ask twice, Kumo keeps on this device a list of the Health entries it has already added or you declined; this list is not synced or uploaded. Kumo does not read any other Health data. Health data is never used for advertising.

Photos

Progress photos are optional. Photos you pick are stored only inside Kumo on your device (and in your own iCloud while iCloud sync is active); they are not added to your photo library, and Kumo never uploads them anywhere.

Reading your scale with the camera is optional. When you tap the camera button while logging your weight, Kumo uses the camera to read the number on your scale's display. The reading happens entirely on your device; no picture is taken, saved or uploaded, and nothing is logged until you confirm the number.

Face data (iOS app)

Section updated 2026-11-16. This section applies to the Kumo iOS app (iPhone and iPad) on the App Store; the Android version is described in the Android section of this policy.

1. What face data the app uses

Kumo uses face data only for the optional before-and-after progress photos (a Kumo Full feature). When it shows or shares a progress photo with "Blur face" turned on (the default), Kumo uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to find the rectangle around each face in that photo so it can blur that area. This rectangle is the only face data Kumo uses. Kumo does not perform facial recognition or identification, does not create a face template, faceprint or any other biometric identifier, does not compare faces, and does not use Face ID or TrueDepth depth data.

2. How face data is used

The rectangle is used only to blur faces in the before-and-after view, the month thumbnails and the share card you create. It is not used for advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Sharing and third parties

Face data is never uploaded, sold, shared with, or disclosed to the developer or any third party. Kumo has no developer server and no third-party SDKs, ads, analytics or tracking. A share card is sent only when you tap Share and choose where to send it; with Blur face on, it contains the blurred photos.

4. Where face data is stored

Face rectangles are computed in your device's memory each time a photo is prepared for display and are never written to storage, synced or uploaded. What Kumo stores is the progress photo itself, as you picked it (resized, not blurred), and whether Blur face is on for it: inside Kumo on your device; in your own private iCloud database while iCloud syncing is active for Kumo (the developer cannot access it); in up to three automatic snapshot files that Kumo keeps on your device after app updates; and in a backup file only if you export one.

5. How long face data is retained

Face rectangles are discarded as soon as the blurred image has been drawn; none are kept after you leave the screen or quit the app. Progress photos stay until you delete them. The developer never receives or retains any face data.

6. Deletion

Because face rectangles are never saved, there is no stored face data to delete. You can delete a progress photo in Kumo; a copy already inside an automatic on-device snapshot stays until that snapshot is replaced by newer ones after later app updates, or until you use Delete all data. Delete all data in Settings removes all photos and the automatic snapshots, and deletions also apply to your private iCloud copy while syncing is active. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

Notifications

Reminders and fasting alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. Kumo asks for notification permission only when you accept its offer to turn on reminders or when you start a fasting timer.

Sharing

Nothing is shared automatically. A weekly card or a before-and-after card is sent only when you tap Share and choose where to send it. The cards show Kumo's web address, and the weekly card's share message includes a link to download Kumo (open.cait518.cc/kumo); Kumo itself sends no data when you share.

Purchases

Some features are unlocked with an in-app purchase or subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; Kumo never receives your payment details.

Android (Google Play)

The Android version follows the same promise: Kumo does not collect, share, or sell any personal data, and the developer never receives your records. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. The Android app uses only these Google components: Google Play Billing for purchases, Google Play In-App Review for the system rating prompt, Health Connect, and Google ML Kit models that run on your device for reading your scale and blurring faces. Google Play services may download those models and handles its own diagnostics under Google's privacy policy; Kumo sends none of your records to Google or to the developer.

Your weight entries, daily check-ins, notes, reminders, photos, and settings are saved only on your phone. Kumo for Android has no cloud sync. If you export a backup file from Settings, the file goes where you choose to save or send it, and only you control it. Clearing Kumo's storage or uninstalling the app removes the data from the phone.

Health Connect is optional. If you turn it on, Android asks you to allow Kumo to read your body weight, and Kumo adds the weights you confirm (or, if you choose “Add automatically”, all new ones) to your log. Kumo reads no other Health Connect data and writes nothing to Health Connect, and the weights are stored only on your phone and never uploaded. You can remove access at any time in Android Settings, under Health Connect, then App permissions.

Progress photos and reading your scale with the camera are optional. You can pick a progress photo or take one with your camera app; photos stay inside Kumo on your phone. Blurring faces in a photo and reading the number on your scale happen entirely on your device, and nothing is uploaded. Reminders and the notification shown during a fast are local notifications on your phone.

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; Kumo never receives your payment details.

Not medical advice

Kumo is a personal log. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or calculate doses.

Children

Kumo is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
