What makes an injection tracker useful

Most people start with a notes app or a calendar, and it works until you can't remember whether last week was the left side or the right. A shot log earns its place on your phone when it does a few small things well:

  • The date, so the next one is never a guess.
  • The site, so you can see where you went recently at a glance.
  • A line of notes in your own words, for anything you want to remember or mention later.
  • A reminder on your schedule, not one the app picks for you.

It should also stay out of the way. Logging a shot should take seconds, not a form.

How Kumo's shot card works

The shot card is optional. Turn it on if you're on GLP-1 shots; leave it off and Kumo stays a simple weight log.

  • Log the date, site and your own notes for each shot.
  • Get reminders on the schedule you set, plus a countdown to the next shot.
  • See a map of recent sites and add how-you-feel check-ins.

It is a record, nothing more: no calculations, no dose tracking or suggestions, and no interpretation of what you log. Decisions about your shots stay between you and your care team.

In the free version, the shot card can be your one module (the others are a fasting timer, or water and protein), with 1 reminder. Running several modules at once and multiple reminders come with Kumo Full.

Your weight trend in the same app

The scale jumps around every day, so Kumo turns a 10-second weigh-in into a 7-day average. It opens to today with your last weight filled in; adjust by 0.1, in pounds, kilograms or stones. You get daily dots and a 7-day average line with the last 30 days of trend and history, and a weekly average compared with last week. "Didn't weigh today" still keeps your streak, and a small cloud grows with the days you show up, not with your weight.

Who it fits, and who it doesn't

A good fit if you want shots and weight in one place, you like a quick tap over a long entry, and you'd rather not make an account: there are no ads and no tracking, and your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Not the right tool if you need dose tracking, dose math or an app that interprets what you log. Kumo deliberately does none of that.

FAQ

What does Kumo's shot card log?

The date, the injection site and your own notes. It also shows a map of recent sites and lets you add how-you-feel check-ins.

Can it track doses?

No. The shot card is a record only: no calculations, no dose tracking or suggestions, and no interpretation.

Will it remind me when my next shot is due?

Yes. You set the schedule, Kumo reminds you on it and shows a countdown to the next shot. The free version includes 1 reminder.

Do I need to be on shots to use Kumo?

No. Kumo works with or without GLP-1 shots. If you are not on shots, just weigh in and watch the trend.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no ads and no tracking, and your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Kumo is for personal tracking only and is not medical advice. Talk to your clinician about your shots and always follow their guidance.
