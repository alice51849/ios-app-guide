Kumo Support

Updated November 6, 2026

Contact

Email hourstag.app@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

iCloud sync

Turn on iCloud sync in Kumo Settings on each device and sign in to the same Apple Account. New entries can take a minute to appear on another device.

Apple Watch

Open Kumo on your Apple Watch and turn the Digital Crown to set your weight, then tap the log button. The watch app works on its own and syncs through your iCloud.

Apple Health

Kumo writes weight and waist measurements to the Health app and reads only today's step count to show it (not saved). To change access, open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps, then Kumo.

Purchases

To restore a purchase, open Settings in Kumo and tap Restore purchases. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

Android (Google Play)

Kumo for Android is distributed through Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alice51849.Kumo

What is different on Android?

Kumo on Android has weight logging, the 7-day average trend, your cloud pet with outfits and weather themes, the fasting, shot log, water and protein modules, body measurements, reminders, before and after photos, PDF monthly reports and CSV export, themes, a Home screen widget, an ongoing notification during a fast, and app shortcuts. iCloud sync, the Apple Watch app, Lock Screen widgets, Siri, and the Apple Health app are available only on Apple devices.

Can I move my data between phones?

Open Settings and choose backup to export a backup file you can keep or send to yourself, and use import on the new phone. Kumo on Android does not sync through the cloud, so keep the backup file somewhere you trust.

How do I use Health Connect?

Health Connect is optional. If you turn it on in Kumo Settings, Android asks you to allow Kumo to read your body weight, and Kumo then adds the weights you confirm to your log. Kumo reads nothing else from Health Connect. To change access later, open Android Settings, then Health Connect, then App permissions, then Kumo.

How do I add the widget on Android?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home screen, tap Widgets, find Kumo, and drag the widget onto the screen.

What can I use for free, and how do purchases work on Android?

The free version works right away with weight logging, one body measurement, your cloud, and one module of your choice. Kumo Full unlocks everything and is available as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time purchase. Purchases belong to the Google account that made them. If a purchase does not appear, check your connection, open Google Play once with the same account, and reopen Kumo. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Why did a reminder not appear on Android?

Allow notifications for Kumo in Android Settings and make sure the reminder is turned on in Kumo. Battery saver or Do Not Disturb can delay reminders.

How do I remove all my data on Android?

Clear Kumo's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Records exist only on your phone, so this removes them for good unless you kept a backup file.
