Kumo Support

Updated November 5, 2026

Contact

Email hourstag.app@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

iCloud sync

Turn on iCloud sync in Kumo Settings on each device and sign in to the same Apple Account. New entries can take a minute to appear on another device.

Apple Watch

Open Kumo on your Apple Watch and turn the Digital Crown to set your weight, then tap the log button. The watch app works on its own and syncs through your iCloud.

Apple Health

Kumo only writes weight and waist measurements to the Health app. To change access, open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps, then Kumo.

Purchases

To restore a purchase, open Settings in Kumo and tap Restore purchases. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

客服（繁體中文）

有問題請寫信到 hourstag.app@gmail.com，並附上裝置型號與 iOS 版本。iCloud 同步：每台裝置都在 Kumo 設定中打開同步，並登入同一個 Apple 帳號。手錶：轉動數位錶冠設定體重，再按「記錄」。健康 App：Kumo 只寫入體重與腰圍。還原購買：Kumo 設定 →「還原購買」；訂閱可在 iPhone 設定 → Apple 帳號 → 訂閱 管理或取消。
