# LED Moving Text — Cue flows and marquee signs | iOS App

Cue Flow handles moments that need more than one sign. Put one message on each line, then step backward or forward live, or choose a 2–20 second timer with optional repeat. Event crews, speakers and queues get a prepared run of messages without leaving the full-screen board. Type a message and hold up your phone. From the back of an arena, across an arrivals hall or over a busy counter, people read it in one glance. It opens straight onto a live canvas. The preview is the editor, so the words, the speed, the size and the colour change the moment you do. One tap takes it full screen, where you tap to show or hide the controls, use the clearly labelled pause button to stop the motion, swipe to change speed and direction, pinch to resize and turn the letters sideways when you need every inch of height. It is made for the moments when your voice will not carry. Cheer for a song or a shift change without shouting yourself hoarse. Meet someone at arrivals and let them spot you first. Hold up a pickup message at the curb. Ask for a quieter table or a slower sentence in places where speaking is hard. Leave a short notice on a desk while you step away. The one-time unlock adds unlimited saved boards, over one hundred typefaces, twenty animation effects including outline, shadow, glow, blink, pulse, wave, typewriter and dot matrix, the other fifty-nine ready-made boards across six everyday situations, page, slide-in, bounce, sway and drift motion, up and down travel, rainbow and gradient lettering, the rest of the LED frames and their colours, letter spacing and repeat gap, photo and aurora backgrounds, beat-reactive motion, a countdown, and MP4 and GIF export. Everything runs on the device. No account, no cloud, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Your messages, your boards and any photo you pick stay on your iPhone.
