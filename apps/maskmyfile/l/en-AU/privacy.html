Mask My File Privacy Policy

Mask My File is made to protect files without ever collecting them. No account is required, and the app carries no advertising, tracking, analytics or content-upload service.

Processing stays on your device

Apple Vision and the embedded Tesseract OCR engine scan the content you pick locally. Masking, redaction, metadata removal, export and verification all run on the device as well.

Face regions, never identity profiles

Face detection only marks image regions for you to review. Mask My File never identifies people, builds a biometric or identity template, uploads face data, shares it or keeps separate face data.

Purchases are the only network call

Apple processes the optional lifetime purchase and restore through StoreKit. Mask My File uses no VPN, NetworkExtension, proxy, tunnel, DNS filter or network routing at all.

You control what stays

Originals are never overwritten. Temporary workspace files, saved projects, Placeholder Vault mappings and Private Session data stay only as each feature explains. Delete them in the app or remove the app to clear its local container.
