Protect a file in three clear steps

Reach for Mask My File before sending a rental application, a health-card copy, a client file, a screenshot, or anything carrying details others do not need to see.

1

Select a photo, PDF, document, structured-data file, or pasted text. Scanning begins locally on your device right away.

2

Review each finding, add anything that was missed or outline an exact area, then choose a protection style the file type supports.

3

Create the protected copy, and share it only after Private Ready confirms the new output reopened and passed its check.

What does Private Ready mean?

It means the freshly protected copy was rendered, reopened, and checked for a valid file structure. It never replaces your own final review before you share.
