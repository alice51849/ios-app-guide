Meow Party: 2 Player Cat Games

What is Meow Party?

Meow Party: 2 Player Cat Games is a collection of 200 original cute cat minigames, starring the cats of the Meow Squad. Rounds take about 30 to 120 seconds with gradually rising difficulty, and many games have levels. There are four families of 50 games each: party, fold puzzles, arcade and rhythm, and cozy creations (cooking, dress-up, gardening, simple management and wordless puzzles).

Who is it for?

People who want quick games on one phone: most of the 200 games can be played solo, and 54 let two players battle or team up face to face on the same phone, which suits friends, couples and families at a cafe table, on the couch or on a long train ride. Cute computer rivals let you practice most of the two-player games on your own.

Does it need a special device?

No. Every game is fully playable on a regular iPhone. Each fold move has an on-screen alternative: a hold-to-close button or a two-finger pinch.

How much does it cost?

It is a one-time purchase on the App Store: no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription, and all 200 games are included. Apple's storefront lookup on 2026-11-15 showed NT$190 in Taiwan, US$5.99 in the United States, ₩9,900 in Korea and ¥1,000 in Japan; prices can differ by country.

How is it different from similar minigame collections?

Meow Party is paid once with nothing more to buy: no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. It plays fully offline, needs no account, collects no data and does not use the camera, location, contacts or photos; progress stays on your device. The interface comes in 50 languages and follows your system language.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. Meow Party is also available for Android on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alice51849.FoldParty
