Meow Party Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Meow Party: 2 Player Cat Games does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device, using the standard local storage provided by iOS (UserDefaults). This data is never sent to the developer or to any other party. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Device sensors and permissions

Meow Party does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or Bluetooth, and it does not ask for any of these permissions.

An optional setting lets a face-down device count as a closed fold. To do this the app reads only the motion sensor's gravity values on your device, which needs no permission. These readings are used instantly to detect orientation, are not stored, and never leave your device.

Purchases

Meow Party is a one-time paid app on the App Store. It has no in-app purchases and no advertising. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, and the app never receives your payment details.

Children

Meow Party is not directed at children, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hourstag.app@gmail.com.
