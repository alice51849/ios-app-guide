Meow Party Support

Meow Party: 2 Player Cat Games is a collection of 200 original, cute cat mini-games for one or two players on one device. It is more fun on a foldable iPhone, and everything is fully playable on any iPhone or iPad.

Common questions

How do I fold on a regular iPhone or iPad?

You do not need a foldable device. Press and hold the on-screen Close button to fold, or pinch two fingers toward the middle of the screen. Let go to open again.

How does two-player mode work?

Two players share one device and sit face to face. On a foldable iPhone each player gets one half of the screen. On a regular iPhone or iPad, the screen is shared in the same way, so both players can play at once.

What does the face-down option do?

It is an optional setting that lets a device lying face down count as closed. It only reads the motion sensor's gravity values on your device. No permission is needed, and nothing leaves your device.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Meow Party has no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app also removes your progress and settings.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The app works fully offline and never connects to the internet.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Meow Party has no advertising and no in-app purchases.
