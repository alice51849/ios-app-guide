# Metro Time Helper: Taipei MRT — When to Leave & Train Times | iOS App

Built for the Taipei MRT in Taiwan: tell Metro Time Helper what time you need to be there, and it works out the latest time to head into the station. Its standout feature is 50 languages, so it suits locals in Taiwan on the daily commute just as well as travellers from around the world riding the Taipei MRT for the first time, each in a language they know. Use it on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Most transport apps tell you when you will arrive. Metro Time Helper tells you what you really want to know before a meeting, a dinner booking or the last train back to your hotel: when should I head off? It works backwards from your arrival time, transfers and all, so you are not left guessing. Three modes in one app Switch any time with the glass button in the top left corner. Everyday MRT Choose your starting station, your destination and when you need to arrive. You get the latest time to enter the station, a step-by-step timeline for each leg and transfer, first and last train warnings, and a reminder when it is time to go. Heading off right now instead? Switch to Leave now and see when you will get there. Tap any station on the built-in zoomable MRT map to set it as your start or destination, and your trip is drawn on the map. Choose Standard estimates (waiting half the time between trains) or Cautious estimates (the full time between trains), and adjust your station entry buffer. Xiaobitan branch Qizhang to Xiaobitan on the official fixed timetable: a countdown to the next train, full timetables for today, weekdays and weekends, departure reminders 1 to 30 minutes ahead, and the official timetable PDF. Trains on this branch only run about every 12 to 16 minutes, and most trip planners overlook it. Everyday trip planning also uses the same official timetable whenever your route takes in this branch. Meihe City (美河市) in Xindian sits right next to Xiaobitan Station, so with a departure reminder set, residents do not have to hang around on the platform. Beitou to Xinbeitou branch The short line out to the Xinbeitou hot spring area, with the same countdown, full timetables, reminders and official PDF. Widget and Live Activities One smart Metro Time widget follows the app's current mode, has a button to switch modes and shows when it last updated. Once you set Remind me, a Live Activity counts down to your time to head off or your next departure on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (iPhone only). Apple Watch A standalone Watch app with all three modes, station selection, countdowns and watch face complications. Lines covered Wenhu, Tamsui-Xinyi (including the Xinbeitou branch), Songshan-Xindian (including the Xiaobitan branch), Zhonghe-Xinlu, Bannan, stage one of the Circular line, and the New Taipei Metro Sanying line (Dingpu to Yingge, with transfers to the Bannan line at Dingpu). The Airport MRT and light rail are not included. The official Taipei MRT network map is also in the app, as a zoomable vector map in Chinese or English (needs a connection). Right at home on iPhone and iPad A polished native Liquid Glass design, 50 languages you can switch inside the app, and colour themes to suit you. Private by design No sign-up, no personal data collected, no ads and no third-party analytics. Everything is worked out on your device and notifications are scheduled locally. Try everything first Use every feature for 24 hours from when you start. After that, a single one-off unlock keeps the full app yours for good. No subscription and no auto-renewal. The price is shown on the App Store. Good to know Times are estimates based on official train frequencies and travel times between stations. They are not live train arrivals. Metro Time Helper is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation.
