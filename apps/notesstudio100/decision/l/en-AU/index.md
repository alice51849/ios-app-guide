# 100 Notes Studio: Pen & Paper — Handwriting, PDF & Study | iOS App

100 Notes Studio brings handwriting, typed text, PDF markup, notebook organisation and study tools into a calm workspace that also works offline. WRITE NATURALLY Choose from 29 different pens and brushes, including fountain, calligraphy and technical pens, watercolour, charcoal, mechanical pencil, 2B/4B/6B graphite, coloured pencil, marker and oil pastel. Each tool has its own texture and response. A pressure-sensitive stylus can use pressure, tilt, palm rejection and supported gestures. Pixel, whole-stroke and precision erasers are joined by lasso selection for moving, scaling, copying or deleting strokes, a ruler, eight shape tools and a laser pointer. 100 CAREFULLY DESIGNED STYLES Each style has its own cover, paper structure, colour palette, dividers, stamps or stickers, toolbar accent and widget appearance. The collection includes minimal and linen books, Japanese grids, dot-grid planning, two-column study layouts, and pages for academic work, engineering, music, meetings, research, travel, wellness and recipes. Styles can be previewed before unlocking. TOOLS FOR NOTES AND STUDY Fixed pages or an infinite canvas; A4, A5, Letter, screen or custom sizes; portrait or landscape Rich text with bold, italic, underline, fonts, colours, lists, tasks, tables, quotes, code and links Import and mark up PDFs with highlighting, underlining, strike-through, text notes, handwriting, rotation, cropping, insertion, extraction, merging and export Audio recordings stay attached to the page where they were made On-device recognition of handwriting and PDF content, with search across titles, text, handwriting and attachments Study mode with linked flashcards, cloze cards and FSRS scheduling Stickers, washi tape and an automatically numbered table of contents Nested folders, tags, pins, favourites, archive, a recoverable bin and version history Home-screen and lock-screen widgets, quick automations and a smartwatch companion for voice, text and checklist notes Ink layers with opacity, visibility and locking, plus a reference workspace for a PDF or image beside the page PRIVACY AND OFFLINE WORK Core work does not require an account or network connection. The app is designed without ads, third-party tracking, analytics or external AI processing. Handwriting recognition and search run on your device. Protect the app or individual notebooks with device biometrics or a passcode. CORE TOOLS AND OPTIONAL UNLOCK The core editor includes 29 tools, PDF markup, audio recordings attached to the page where they were made, handwriting search and study tools, with 1 active notebook, unlimited pages and 2 of 100 included styles. An optional one-time unlock adds more notebooks, the ability to apply 100 styles, vector PDF export, restorable version history and encrypted backup. A focused workspace for handwriting, documents and organised study.
