# 100 Notes Studio: Pen & Paper — Handwriting & PDF Workspace | iOS App

100 Notes Studio is a private, offline workspace for handwriting, typed notes, PDF markup, page-attached audio recordings and study. WRITE WITH CHARACTER 29 distinct pens and brushes — fountain pen, calligraphy, fude brush, technical pen, watercolour wash, charcoal, mechanical pencil (2B/4B/6B) and more — each with its own texture and stroke response. Pressure and tilt shape supported strokes, while palm rejection helps prevent stray marks. Pixel, whole-stroke and precision erasers handle corrections. Use the lasso to move, scale, copy or delete strokes, plus a ruler, eight shape tools and a laser pointer for presentations. 100 DISTINCT NOTEBOOK STYLES Each Studio Style has its own cover, paper structure, colour palette, dividers, stamps or stickers, toolbar accent and widget appearance. The collection includes minimal, linen, Japanese grid, academic, engineering, music, meeting, research, planner, travel, wellness, recipe, creative and dark-paper systems. Browse and preview all 100 styles before choosing one. MORE THAN A CANVAS Fixed pages or an infinite canvas, with A4, A5, Letter, screen and custom sizes in portrait or landscape Formatted text with bold, italic, underline, fonts, colours, lists, checklists, tables and links PDF import and markup with highlights, underlines, strike-through, text notes, handwriting, rotation, cropping, page insertion, extraction, merging and export Audio recordings attached to the page you choose On-device handwriting and PDF text recognition, with search across titles, body text, handwriting and attachments Study tools with linked flashcards, cloze cards and FSRS spaced repetition Stickers, washi tape and an auto-numbered table of contents Nested folders, tags, pins, favourites, archives, recoverable trash and restorable page history Ink layers with opacity, visibility and locking, plus a reference view that keeps a PDF or image beside the page Widgets and quick capture for faster access PRIVATE BY DESIGN No account, ads, third-party tracking or analytics, and no external AI. Editing, search and study work offline. Notes stay on your device unless you choose to export or back them up. Lock the workspace or an individual notebook with device authentication. FREE CORE, OPTIONAL ONE-TIME UPGRADE The free version includes the core editor, 29 writing tools, PDF markup, page audio attachments, handwriting search and study tools, with 1 active notebook, unlimited pages and 2 of the 100 styles. An optional one-time upgrade adds unlimited notebooks, all 100 styles, vector-ink PDF export, restorable page history and encrypted backup. A focused home for handwriting, documents and study.
