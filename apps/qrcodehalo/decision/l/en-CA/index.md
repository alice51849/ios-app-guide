# QR Code Halo: Scan & Create — Review risk before action | iOS App

QR Code Halo follows a clear local workflow: inspect what was decoded, decide from transparent findings, and act only when the available action makes sense. A scan never opens content automatically. Local Payload Security Inspector Breaks the full payload into scheme, host, path, query and fragment on the device. It provides deterministic Safe, Caution or Blocked findings, the reason for each finding and an action disposition. It does not consult network reputation services or hide uncertainty behind a fuzzy score. QR Verification Save a result as a local reference, then compare a later scan by its complete SHA-256 fingerprint, QR format, content type, URL components, security level and action disposition. Batch Risk & Difference Audit Groups exact duplicates and matching hosts across a batch, then checks lookalike hosts within a deliberately bounded comparison. You can read the batch summary, each item's risk and every complete payload without losing detail, and full audit reading stays available.
