# QR Code Halo: Scan & Create — Inspect before you open | iOS App

QR Code Halo puts inspection before interaction. Read the decoded structure, weigh visible evidence and only then choose what to do. Recognition alone never triggers an external action. Local Payload Security Inspector Examines the entire payload locally and lays out scheme, host, path, query and fragment. Its fixed rules produce Safe, Caution or Blocked findings, transparent reasons and an action disposition. It neither calls an online reputation service nor substitutes a vague numerical score for evidence. QR Verification Save a result as a local reference, then compare a later scan by its complete SHA-256 fingerprint, QR format, content type, URL components, security level and action disposition. Batch Risk & Difference Audit Sorts a batch into exact duplicate groups and host groups, then makes a bounded comparison of potentially confusable lookalike hosts. The overview, individual risk and full decoded content remain visible together, with unrestricted reading of the complete audit.
