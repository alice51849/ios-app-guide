Save it now. Actually read it later. You send yourself links all day. A recipe from a mate, a jacket you liked, a video you promised to watch, a thread you meant to reread. They scatter across chats, notes and screenshots, and you never see them again. SaveTag gives all of them one home, and it does the filing for you. Save from anywhere • Share any link into SaveTag from any app with a share sheet • Copy a link and quick-save it straight from the clipboard • Title, site and preview are pulled in for you Tags that write themselves • Every save is sorted the moment it lands, on your device • Precise topics, not one big pile: shopping, food, recipes, travel, places, learning, work, tech, finance, health, style, home, news, pets, parenting, gaming, design, sports, photography, music and inspiration • Add your own custom tags when you want your own system Search that understands what you meant Look for the idea, not the exact words you typed.
