# Studydown: Face-Down Timer — Face-down activity time ledger | iOS App

See where your effort goes, one activity at a time. Studydown records the time you spend working on a project, reading or practising a skill when you put your phone face down. Choose an activity, tap to begin, then leave your phone still on a table with the screen facing down. Keep the app open while recording. Lifting your phone pauses the count after your selected grace period. Time is no longer added when you leave the app. • Organize activities with your own names and colours. Add missed entries or correct existing records. • Check today's and this week's totals, consecutive days and the breakdown by activity. • Plan weekly goals and deadlines. Set reminders for each day or every few hours. • Keep a CSV copy of your records. Pro adds full JSON backup and restore. Try up to 3 activities with a cumulative allowance of 5 recorded minutes; it does not renew daily. Sessions already in progress are preserved in full. One Pro purchase unlocks unlimited activities and recording, detailed statistics, goals, deadlines, widgets, Apple Watch and backups. No subscription. Timing and records work on your device, with no account or ads. Sharing exported files is your choice. Purchases and restoring purchases use Apple's services. Every face-down session keeps a Focus Signature: phone pickups and average uninterrupted time. Give each deadline an effort target to see logged Activity time and the daily pace still required.
