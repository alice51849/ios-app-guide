TripBee Cute: Itinerary Maker
Press & media

TripBee Cute turns your whole trip into one cute illustrated booklet, with the countdown, flights, day plans and packing list on a single page.

把整趟旅行收進一本可愛的插畫手冊：出發倒數、航班、每日行程和行李清單都在同一頁。

Key features

  • Each trip is a little booklet with its own illustrated cover
  • Countdown to departure, destination weather and local time
  • Boarding-pass style flight cards with times, terminals and booking codes
  • Day plans you can name, decorate with stickers, check off and reorder, with a color for every day and a date filter
  • Downtime slots and a rainy-day plan you can copy from the main plan
  • Photos, tickets and notes on each plan, with directions in your maps app
  • Split shared costs, settle up and see who owes whom, with live exchange rates across currencies
  • Packing list and trip notes
  • CSV template import, and a web booklet anyone can open in a browser or chat app, even offline
  • Home Screen widgets that show your next trip on its cover art
  • Dark Mode and 50 languages
  • No account, no ads, no tracking; booklets stay on your device
  • Cute Pass is a one-time in-app purchase that adds unlimited booklets, more covers and more
