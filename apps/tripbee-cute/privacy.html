TripBee Cute Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Privacy at a glance

TripBee Cute does not require an account and does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social-network SDKs. We do not collect or sell personal data.

This policy covers TripBee Cute for iPhone and iPad (App Store) and for Android (Google Play). The sections marked iPhone and iPad describe the Apple version; the sections marked Android describe the Google Play version. Both versions work without an account and keep your booklets on your device.

Data stored on your device (iPhone and iPad)

Your booklets, flights, day plans, notes, packing lists, cover choices, app settings, and attached tickets or photos are stored in the app's local container on your device. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Apple Watch app read a copy of your upcoming trip on your own devices; nothing is sent to a developer server.

iCloud sync (iPhone and iPad)

iCloud sync is optional and off by default. If you turn it on (Cute Pass), your booklets are stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access. The developer cannot read this data. Turning sync off stops further uploads.

Weather, maps, and sharing (iPhone and iPad)

To show destination weather, Apple's geocoding service receives the destination text to resolve its coordinates. TripBee Cute then sends the resolved latitude and longitude and the trip dates directly to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. No account identifier is attached, and the developer does not keep a server-side copy. Opening directions hands the selected place to Apple Maps or another map app you choose. A web booklet is a file created on your device and is shared only when you choose to share it through Apple's system share sheet.

Photos and notifications (iPhone and iPad)

Photo access is used only for images you select to attach to a booklet. Notifications are optional and local: they are scheduled on your device for reminders you enable. Selected photos and reminder details are not sent to a developer server.

Purchases (iPhone and iPad)

Cute Pass is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. TripBee Cute does not receive your payment-card details. Apple provides a verified entitlement so the purchase can be restored, refunded, or revoked under App Store policies.

Retention and deletion (iPhone and iPad)

Local data remains until you delete a booklet, use Reset all data in Settings, or delete the app. Data synced to your private iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud. Purchase history remains with Apple so an eligible purchase can be restored.

Data stored on your device (Android)

On Android, your booklets, flights, day plans, notes, packing lists, cover choices, app settings, and attached tickets or photos are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The Home screen widget reads a copy of your upcoming trip on your own device. Nothing is sent to a developer server, and the app turns off Android cloud backup, so this data is not copied to a Google account backup by the app. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync, and TripBee Cute does not include iCloud sync, friend editing, or an Apple Watch app on Android.

Network use (Android)

The internet permission is used only to show destination weather. To do this the app resolves the place you entered to coordinates using the geocoder built into your device, then sends those coordinates and the trip dates directly to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. The request is about the destination you typed, not your current location, and carries no account name or device identifier; the developer does not keep a server-side copy. The device geocoder is an Android system service, so your device vendor's location service may process the place name according to its own policy. Nothing else is uploaded automatically: a web booklet, trip file, or backup file is created on your device and leaves it only when you choose to share it with another app, and opening directions or the support and privacy pages hands a link to your maps app or browser.

Permissions (Android)

TripBee Cute asks for these Android permissions: Internet (destination weather, as described above), Notifications (to show the reminders you set; Android 13 and later ask the first time you enable a reminder), and Receive boot completed (to schedule your reminders again after the phone restarts). It does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access. Photos and files are added through the system photo picker and file picker, so the app only receives the items you select to attach to a booklet. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and may arrive a few minutes late.

Purchases (Android)

Cute Pass is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. TripBee Cute does not receive your payment-card details. Google Play tells the app whether the purchase is active so it can be restored, refunded, or revoked under Google Play policies. There is no subscription.

No ads or analytics (Android)

The Android version contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, does not use an advertising ID, and does not sell or share personal data.

Retention and deletion (Android)

Local data remains until you delete a booklet, choose Reset all data in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Purchase history remains with Google Play so an eligible purchase can be restored.
