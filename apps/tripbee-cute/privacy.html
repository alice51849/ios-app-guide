TripBee Cute Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Privacy at a glance

TripBee Cute does not require an account and does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social-network SDKs. We do not collect or sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your booklets, flights, day plans, notes, packing lists, cover choices, app settings, and attached tickets or photos are stored in the app's local container on your device. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Apple Watch app read a copy of your upcoming trip on your own devices; nothing is sent to a developer server.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional and off by default. If you turn it on (Cute Pass), your booklets are stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access. The developer cannot read this data. Turning sync off stops further uploads.

Weather, maps, and sharing

To show destination weather, Apple's geocoding service receives the destination text to resolve its coordinates. TripBee Cute then sends the resolved latitude and longitude and the trip dates directly to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. No account identifier is attached, and the developer does not keep a server-side copy. Opening directions hands the selected place to Apple Maps or another map app you choose. A web booklet is a file created on your device and is shared only when you choose to share it through Apple's system share sheet.

Photos and notifications

Photo access is used only for images you select to attach to a booklet. Notifications are optional and local: they are scheduled on your device for reminders you enable. Selected photos and reminder details are not sent to a developer server.

Purchases

Cute Pass is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. TripBee Cute does not receive your payment-card details. Apple provides a verified entitlement so the purchase can be restored, refunded, or revoked under App Store policies.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete a booklet, use Reset all data in Settings, or delete the app. Data synced to your private iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud. Purchase history remains with Apple so an eligible purchase can be restored.
