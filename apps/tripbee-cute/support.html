TripBee Cute Support

TripBee Cute works without an account on iPhone, iPad, and Android. Use the steps below for the most common questions; the Android section further down covers Google Play.

Common questions

What can I use for free?

You can keep one booklet at a time with the six standard covers and the full booklet page, including flights, day plans, the packing list, reminders, and web booklet sharing. Delete it whenever you want to start a different booklet.

What does Cute Pass unlock?

Cute Pass lets you keep several booklets at once and unlocks 50 more illustrated covers, plus smart packing lists, backup and restore, iCloud sync, and sending a booklet with its photos. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings, find Cute Pass, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. On Android, see the Android section below.

How do I share a web booklet?

Open a booklet, tap the ••• menu, choose Web booklet, and tap Share web booklet. Your travel buddies can open the page in any browser or chat app, even offline.

How do I add the widget or use Apple Watch?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then add the TripBee Cute widget. On Apple Watch, install TripBee Cute from the Watch app on your iPhone to see the countdown and your next plan.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Confirm that notifications are allowed for TripBee Cute in iPhone Settings and that the plan has a time and a reminder enabled. Focus modes can delay how notifications appear.

How do I remove all local data?

Open Settings and choose Reset all data. This removes every local booklet and attachment after confirmation.

Android (Google Play)

Get TripBee Cute for Android from Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alice51849.TripBeeCute

What is different on Android?

The Android version has the illustrated booklets, countdown, flight cards, day plans, packing lists, web booklet, CSV import, weather, reminders, and Home screen widgets. iCloud sync, planning with friends through iCloud, the Apple Watch app, and Lock Screen widgets are available only on Apple devices.

What can I use for free, and what does Cute Pass unlock on Android?

You can keep one booklet at a time with the six standard covers and the full booklet page, including flights, day plans, the packing list, reminders, CSV import, and web booklet sharing. Cute Pass is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It lets you keep several booklets at once and unlocks 50 more covers, smart packing lists, backup export and import, and sending a trip file with its photos.

How do I restore my purchase on Android?

Open Settings, find Cute Pass, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Google account that made the original purchase in Google Play. If it does not appear right away, check your connection and open Google Play once, then try again.

How do I add the widget on Android?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home screen, tap Widgets, find TripBee Cute, and drag the small, medium, or large widget onto the screen. The widget shows your next trip with its cover and countdown.

How do I back up or move my booklets on Android?

Open Settings and choose backup to export a backup file you can keep or send to yourself, and use import to bring it back later. You can replace everything or merge in only what is new. To send one trip to another phone, open the booklet, choose Share, and send the web booklet or the trip file. Backup export and trip files with photos use Cute Pass.

Why did a reminder not appear on Android?

Allow notifications for TripBee Cute in Android Settings, and make sure the plan has a time and a reminder turned on. Reminders are scheduled in a battery-friendly way and can arrive a few minutes late; battery saver or Do Not Disturb can delay them further.

How do I remove all local data on Android?

Open Settings in the app and choose Reset all data, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
