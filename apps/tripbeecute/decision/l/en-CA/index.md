# TripBee Cute: Travel Journal — Vacation Countdown & Planner | iOS App

Stop digging through emails, screenshots and group chats: TripBee Cute turns every vacation into one cute illustrated booklet, with the countdown, flights, day plans and packing list together on one page. Each trip sits on your bookshelf as a little booklet with its own illustrated cover. Open it and scroll: the cover, the countdown to departure, your flights, each day's plans, then everything to pack and remember. Why it makes trips easier See the whole trip at a glance, top to bottom on one page. Feel the excitement build with a countdown and the weather where you are going. Tell the days apart at once: each day has its own colour, and you can filter to a single date. Keep tickets, photos and notes right next to each plan. Pack without juggling apps: your packing list lives in the same booklet. Plan together: friends can edit the same booklet with you through iCloud (Cute Pass). Share a web booklet that opens in any browser or chat app, even offline. Made for every kind of trip A road trip to the Rockies, a winter getaway down south, a cottage weekend, a cruise or a big trip overseas with friends. Build the booklet before you go, check the countdown on your wrist and your next plan on your Home Screen, and keep the whole trip in your pocket while you travel. Add photos and notes along the way and the booklet becomes your travel journal. If it rains, switch to the rainy-day plan you prepared. Already have your plans in a spreadsheet? Export the CSV template, fill it in yourself or ask any AI assistant you like to do it, then import the file and a new booklet appears on your shelf. Everything inside 57 illustrated covers of cities, landmarks, seasons and travel moods: 6 included, 51 more with Cute Pass. Countdown to departure, plus weather and local time at your destination. Flight cards styled like boarding passes, with times, terminals and confirmation codes. Day plans you can name, decorate with stickers, check off and reorder, with gaps for downtime. A rainy-day plan for any day, which can start as a copy of the main plan. A colour for each day, and a date filter to show one day or all of them. Photos, tickets and notes on every plan, with directions in your maps app. A packing list you build yourself, plus smart packing lists based on who is travelling, the season and the number of days (Cute Pass). Trip notes and gentle reminders. Plan with friends: invite them through iCloud to edit the same booklet, and changes sync to each other's devices. Inviting needs Cute Pass, friends need TripBee Cute too, and nobody has to sign up. iCloud sync between your own devices (Cute Pass). A single backup file of all your booklets and photos, which you can import to replace everything or to merge, adding only what is new and deleting nothing (Cute Pass). CSV import that turns a filled-in template into a booklet. Share a web booklet whose countdown keeps running, or a trip file with photos and tickets (Cute Pass). Trip files from TripBee and TripBee Lite open too. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with your cover art and next plan. An Apple Watch app with the countdown and your next plan. Dark Mode, iPad and 50 languages that follow your device or can be changed in the app. Privacy No account, no ads and no tracking. Your booklets stay on your device, and are stored in your own private iCloud only if you turn on sync. Weather comes from Open-Meteo using only your destination. Cute Pass Without a purchase you can keep one booklet at a time, with the 6 included covers, CSV import, the web booklet, plans, flights, the packing list, widgets and the Apple Watch app. Some features are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase (Cute Pass): as many booklets as you like, 51 more covers, smart packing lists, iCloud sync and backups, trip files with photos and tickets, and planning with friends.
