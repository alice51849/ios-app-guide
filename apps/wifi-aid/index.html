Designed to avoid false alarms

Conservative by design.

  • Confirmed: DNS is blamed only when names repeatedly fail while direct-IP access works.
  • Confirmed: A sign-in portal needs both an interception signal and blocked secure baselines.
  • Confirmed: One slow or failed request never creates a high-confidence verdict.
  • Honest: When the checks disagree, WiFi Aid says it needs more evidence.
Private by default

Your network history stays with you.

  • No account, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • Diagnostic history is stored only on your device.
  • Location permission is optional and used only to show the current Wi-Fi identity.
  • Shared reports omit Wi-Fi names, BSSIDs, local addresses, and raw endpoints.
