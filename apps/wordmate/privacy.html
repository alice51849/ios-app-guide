Wordmate Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Wordmate does not require an account and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social-network SDKs. We do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your selected languages, topic, level, learning progress, daily goal, voice preference, and saved-word library are stored locally on your device. A limited copy of the words needed by the Home Screen widget is stored in Wordmate's private App Group container. Selected learning settings, saved words, and progress may also sync locally between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity service. The Home Screen widget and Apple Watch sync are iOS-only features; the Android app has neither, so on Android this data stays only in the app's own storage. If Android system backup is turned on for your Google Account, Android may include this local data in your own device backup, which we cannot access. Wordmate does not send this data to a developer server.

Purchases and reviews

Wordmate is a paid download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions: on iPhone and iPad it is sold through the App Store and Apple processes the purchase; on Android it is sold through Google Play and Google processes the purchase. Wordmate does not receive your payment-card details. If you request a rating, the request is handled by the system interface (Apple's rating prompt on iOS, Google Play's in-app review on Android). Apple's and Google's processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

Speech and network access

Pronunciation uses voices installed and provided by the operating system. Wordmate does not upload vocabulary, saved words, or learning progress to our servers. Core lessons work without creating an account. On Android the app requests no runtime permissions and makes no network requests of its own; only Google Play's own services, such as the optional rating prompt, go online.

Data retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on the device until you remove it or delete Wordmate. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to the operating system's behavior on iOS or Android. App Store purchase history remains with Apple and may be used to redownload the paid app with the same Apple Account; on Android, Google Play keeps the purchase in your Google Account so you can reinstall it.

Children

Wordmate is a general vocabulary-learning app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users because the app has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking system.
