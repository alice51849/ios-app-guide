Wordmate Support

Wordmate is designed to work without an account. Most questions can be resolved directly in the app using the steps below.

Common questions

How do I change the language I am learning?

Open the Learn tab and use the language selector. This changes vocabulary content and pronunciation without changing the app interface language.

How do I change the app interface language?

Open Settings, choose App language, then select a language or Follow System.

Why is a pronunciation voice unavailable?

Wordmate only uses a system voice that matches the selected language, so it never substitutes an incorrect language. Install the matching Apple voice in iPhone Settings, then reopen Wordmate and choose it under Settings > Voice.

Why does the widget speaker open Wordmate?

iOS widgets cannot reliably complete text-to-speech after their extension closes. Wordmate opens briefly and plays the selected system voice from the full app for reliable pronunciation.

How do I add a word to My Library?

Tap the round star on a study card, or tap the bookmark in the widget. Saved words are automatically grouped by topic and level.

How do I restore Wordmate Pro?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original one-time purchase.
