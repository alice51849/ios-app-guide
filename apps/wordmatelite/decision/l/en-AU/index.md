# Wordmate Lite: Learn Words — Five a day, one quiet minute | iOS App

Five words, one minute, and a trail you can actually see behind you. Most vocabulary tools make you build decks before you learn a thing, then leave you with a review pile that never shrinks. Wordmate Lite goes the other way. Open it, walk a five word pebble trail, and that is the day done. The last screen shows the exact five words you just picked up, so a spare minute leaves something real behind. It is made for adults who have started a language a few times and let it drop: the long commute, the trip to Japan or Bali you booked months ago, the uni subject you are keeping on top of, the language spoken at your partner's family table, the work vocabulary you are expected to already know. There are 44 learning languages and 14 life paths, including travel, business, exams, daily life, dining, shopping, interviews, health, technology, nature, entertainment and social chat, each split into small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level. Tap a word to hear it read aloud by the system voice, and choose which voice reads each language. Save the words worth keeping, grouped by topic and level, and come back to them any time. Set a daily goal of 5, 10, 15 or 20 words, then watch it add up: how many days in a row you have kept going, your best run, today's words, progress in every language, and a small ring filling on each topic and unit. All of it works offline. No account, no sign in, no personal data collected, no third party advertising and no tracking. What you learn stays on your device, which matters on a long flight, out past the last tower, or anywhere the reception drops out. You can start without paying anything: one learning language and one path at beginner level stay open for good, and every other path stays visible so you know what is there. A single one time purchase unlocks all 44 languages, all 14 paths, every level, unlimited learning and an unlimited saved word list. Buy it once, keep it for good, no subscription.
