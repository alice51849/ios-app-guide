# Wordmate Lite: Vocabulary — One minute, five new words | iOS App

Five words. One minute. A trail you can actually see behind you. Most vocabulary tools ask you to build decks before you learn anything, then hand you a review queue that only gets longer. Wordmate Lite works the other way around. You open it, walk a five word pebble trail, and the day is done. The closing screen shows the exact five words you just learned, so a spare minute leaves something behind. It is made for adults who have restarted a language more than once: keeping your French alive after immersion, holding a conversation with the other side of the family, prepping for a trip, building the English you need for work and study, or learning the vocabulary your job assumes you already have. There are 44 learning languages and 14 life paths, including travel, business, exams, daily life, dining, shopping, interviews, health, technology, nature, entertainment and social talk, each divided into small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level. Tap a word to hear it read aloud by the system voice, and choose which voice reads each language. Save the words worth keeping, grouped by topic and level, and return to them whenever you like. Set a daily goal of 5, 10, 15 or 20 words, then watch it add up: how many days in a row you have kept going, your best run, today's words, progress in each language, and a small ring filling on every topic and unit. Everything works offline. No account, no sign in, no personal data collected, no third party advertising and no tracking. What you learn stays on your device, which counts on a long flight, on the subway, or out where the signal disappears. You can start without paying anything: one learning language and one path at beginner level stay open for good, and every other path stays visible so you always know what is waiting. A single one time purchase unlocks all 44 languages, all 14 paths, every level, unlimited learning and an unlimited saved word list. Pay once and it stays yours, with no subscription.
