# Wordmate Lite: Vocabulary — Five new words in a minute | iOS App

Five words, one minute, and a path you can see behind you. Most vocabulary tools want you to build decks first and revise forever afterwards. Wordmate Lite turns that around. You open it, walk a five word pebble trail, and you are finished for the day. The closing screen shows the exact five words you have just learnt, so the minute is never wasted. It suits adults who have started a language three or four times and let it slide: commuters on the train, anyone revising for an exam, people brushing up before a holiday, professionals who need the vocabulary of their own trade, and anyone who wants a run of days they can keep even when they are shattered. There are 44 languages to learn and 14 everyday paths, among them travel, business, exams, daily life, dining, shopping, interviews, health, technology, nature, entertainment and social chat, each organised into small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level. Tap a word to hear it spoken by the system voice, and choose which voice reads each language. Save the words worth keeping, sorted by topic and level, and go back to them whenever you like. Set a daily goal of 5, 10, 15 or 20 words, then watch it build: how many days in a row you have kept going, your best run, today's total, progress in each language and a small ring filling on every topic and unit. It all works offline. No account, no sign in, no personal data collected, no third party advertising and nothing tracked. Whatever you learn stays on your own device, on the Tube, on a delayed train, or anywhere with no signal at all. You can start without paying: one learning language and one path at beginner level stay open for good, and every other path stays visible so you can see what is waiting. A single one off purchase unlocks all 44 languages, all 14 paths, every level, unlimited learning and an unlimited saved word list. Pay once and it is yours to keep, with no subscription.
