# Wordmate Lite: Vocabulary — Learn 5 words a day, offline | iOS App

Five words. One minute. A trail you can actually see. Most vocabulary tools ask you to build decks before you learn anything, then bury you under a review queue that only grows. Wordmate Lite works the other way around. You open it, walk a five word pebble trail, and you are done for the day. The closing screen shows the exact five words you just learned, so one quiet minute always leaves something behind. It is built for adults who have started a language three times and stopped three times. Commuters with one free subway stop. Travelers packing next week. Students grinding through exam prep. Professionals who need the vocabulary of their own field. Anyone who wants a streak they can still keep on a bad day. Inside there are 44 learning languages and 14 life paths, including travel, business, exams, daily life, dining, shopping, interviews, health, tech, nature, entertainment and social talk, each broken into small units at beginner, intermediate and advanced level. Tap any word to hear it read aloud by the system voice, and pick the voice you prefer for each language. Save the words worth keeping, grouped by topic and level, and come back to them whenever you want. Set a daily goal of 5, 10, 15 or 20 words, then watch the count rise: how many days in a row you have kept going, your best run, today's words, progress in every language, and a small ring filling on each topic and unit. Everything runs offline. No account, no sign in, no personal data collected, no third party advertising, no tracking. What you learn stays on your device, on a plane, on a subway platform, in a waiting room with no signal. You can start without paying anything: one learning language and one path at beginner level stay open for good, and every other path stays visible so you always know what is there. A single one time purchase unlocks all 44 languages, all 14 paths, every level, unlimited learning and an unlimited saved word list. Buy it once and it stays yours. There is no subscription.
