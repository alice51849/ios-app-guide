Short answer

To understand where your money went, record each completed expense and convert it into the work time required to earn that amount. Reviewing money and time together makes category patterns concrete without bank syncing.

HoursTag is built for exactly this, with Pay once, Private, No tracking. Test it on a real example before relying on it, and check the current App Store listing for pricing.

What to look for before choosing

  • Manual expense records converted into work hours.
  • Hourly or monthly income setup using your own numbers.
  • Spending history with categories and time-based breakdowns.
  • Goals and wishlist progress expressed in work time.
  • On-device data, no account, and a one-time purchase.

A practical decision process

  1. Set your hourly or monthly take-home income.
  2. Log a completed expense in HoursTag.
  3. Choose its category and Need, Want, or Impulse tag.
  4. Review history and breakdowns in both money and work time.
  5. Track a goal to see the work time behind future progress.

FAQ

Does it import transactions from my bank?

No. You enter expenses yourself, and HoursTag keeps the records on your device without an account.

Is it a checkout blocker?

No. HoursTag records and reframes spending; it does not block stores, cards, or purchases.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase.
