إجابة مختصرة

For multi-country backpacking, the practical need is simple: log each expense offline in the currency you actually paid, then see whether today's spending is still within the daily limit. G+Money Lite supports trips or events, category filters, a daily limit, instant home-currency totals and 50+ currencies with live or manual rates. It will not plan routes, book hostels or guarantee exact bank conversions; it is a focused travel-money log for keeping your own spending visible across borders.

Try G+Money Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

ما الذي تبحث عنه قبل الاختيار

  • Works offline for hostel basements, trains, border crossings and no-roaming days
  • 50+ currencies with manual rates for eurozone and non-euro countries
  • Daily limit view to preserve a buffer for transport, emergencies and rate changes

  • Pay once, no account and no subscription, so the app does not become another recurring travel cost

الأسئلة الشائعة

Can I track a trip that crosses several currencies?

Yes. G+Money Lite is built around a currency converter plus expense tracker, and the App Store description lists 50+ currencies with home-currency totals.

Are offline rates always current?

No. Offline conversion uses the rates available or manually entered at the time. Update live rates when you have internet, and treat totals as planning estimates.

Is it useful after the trip?

Yes. You can filter by trip, category and date, then export text or CSV if you want to review what the backpacking route actually cost.
