إجابة مختصرة

Mochi's widget is genuinely interactive: you add it to your Lock Screen or Home Screen and the task circles respond to taps, marking items done with real-time updates. The widget automatically matches your active skin — so your Lock Screen looks intentional rather than generic. You can point different widget placements at different lists, which is useful if you want groceries on your Lock Screen and a work checklist on your Home Screen.

Try Mochi on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

الأسئلة الشائعة

Which iPhones support Mochi's interactive widget?

Interactive widgets require iOS 17 or later, supported on iPhone XS (A12) and newer.

Does Mochi work on Apple Watch?

Yes — the app includes Apple Watch support (via MochiWatchBridge) so you can view and check off tasks from your wrist, in addition to the iPhone widgets.

If I check something off on the widget, does it sync to the main app?

Yes. The widget shares state with the main app via an App Group — changes appear immediately when you open Mochi, and vice versa.
