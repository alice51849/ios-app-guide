We develop both Lumi Bopomofo apps. On the Taiwan App Store, Lumi Bopomofo was listed as free and Lumi Bopomofo Pro as a NT$290 paid download when checked on 17 July 2026. The free app is the sensible first step when you still need to see whether the activities suit your child.
