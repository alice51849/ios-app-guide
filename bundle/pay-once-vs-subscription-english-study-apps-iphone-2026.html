Frequently asked questions

Is a buy-once TOEIC app good enough to improve my score?

For learners who already have basic English skills and want test-format practice, a structured buy-once TOEIC app like Aim990 is effective. For beginners needing broad vocabulary and grammar building first, a broader app may help initially.

What is the cheapest way to prepare for TOEIC on iPhone?

Aim990 provides structured L&R practice as a one-time purchase, making it one of the most cost-effective TOEIC preparation options compared to monthly language learning subscriptions.

Can I improve my TOEIC score without a subscription?

Yes. TOEIC preparation is structured enough that a focused buy-once app with daily practice plans is sufficient for most score improvement goals.
