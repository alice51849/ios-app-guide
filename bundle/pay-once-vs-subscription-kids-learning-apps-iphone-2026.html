Frequently asked questions

Are subscription kids learning apps better than buy-once apps?

Subscription platforms like ABCmouse offer breadth — hundreds of activities across subjects. Buy-once apps focus on one skill area deeply. If you need broad coverage, a subscription may be worth it. For a specific skill like Zhuyin or phonics, a focused buy-once app is often more effective and much cheaper.

Do buy-once kids apps receive content updates?

Yes. Buy-once kids apps receive updates alongside subscription apps. Developers have a strong incentive to maintain App Store ratings, which means keeping apps updated.

What is the total cost to buy all Lumi learning apps vs. ABCmouse for 5 years?

The Lumi series covers Zhuyin, phonics and maths as individual one-time purchases. Combined, the total is a fraction of ABCmouse's 5-year subscription cost of approximately $780.
