Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to buy privacy apps once instead of subscribing?

In most cases, yes. A single subscription privacy app at $3–5/month costs $180–300 over five years. Buying a comparable buy-once app typically costs $2–5 once, with no renewal.

Are buy-once privacy apps less feature-rich than subscription apps?

For the core functions — photo vaulting, document scanning, data redaction and cycle tracking — buy-once apps cover what most users need. Subscription apps sometimes add cloud backup as a differentiator, but for privacy-focused users, cloud backup is often undesirable.

Which buy-once app replaces a private photo vault subscription?

Zafe: Private Photo Vault is a one-time purchase. It uses Face ID and stores photos in an encrypted vault on your device, with no cloud account required.

Do buy-once apps receive updates?

Buy-once apps on the App Store receive updates like any other app. The developer decides whether a major version upgrade requires a new purchase — check the App Store listing for the current version's included features.
