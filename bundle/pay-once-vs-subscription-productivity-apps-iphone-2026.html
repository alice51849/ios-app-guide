Frequently asked questions

How much can you save by switching from subscription to buy-once productivity apps?

Using four buy-once apps instead of four subscription equivalents at an average of $5/month each saves roughly $240–600 over five years, depending on the specific apps.

Do one-time purchase apps get updated regularly?

App Store buy-once apps receive updates at the developer's discretion. Most developers release regular updates because the App Store review process favours actively maintained apps.

Is a subscription productivity app ever worth the extra cost?

Subscriptions make sense when an app requires significant server infrastructure — cloud sync across many devices, AI processing, or real-time collaboration. For solo use cases like voice notes, habit tracking or resume editing, the server cost is minimal, so a buy-once model is more consumer-friendly.
