A practical workflow

  1. Set your hourly or monthly income in HoursTag. Check that the income assumptions reflect the comparison you want to make.
  2. Enter the proposed price and read the work-time equivalent. Treat it as one perspective on the decision, not an instruction to buy or avoid something.
  3. Label the item Need, Want, or Impulse and review the history later. A goal or wishlist can help you compare several purchases in the same unit.

Know the limits

  • HoursTag does not measure actual hours worked or replace payroll, a timesheet, or tax calculations.
  • The result depends on your income inputs and cannot decide whether an expense is necessary for you.

When a simpler option is enough

A basic calculator can divide a price by an hourly amount. HoursTag is relevant when you also want tags, history, and goals around that comparison.

Before you decide

Does it automatically discover my income?

No. You provide the income assumptions. That makes the result a personal comparison rather than a statement about your real payroll.

Does this link open HoursTag Lite?

No. It opens the original HoursTag: Hours to Buy. This guide does not describe the separate Lite app's free allowance or workflow.
