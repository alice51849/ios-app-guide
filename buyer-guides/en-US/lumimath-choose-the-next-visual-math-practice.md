## A practical workflow

1. Begin with an available visual question together. Ask the child to explain the picture or pattern rather than rushing to tap an answer.

2. Use practice at the child's pace when a timed activity is not a good fit. Stop or change topic if the session becomes frustrating.

3. Review the progress view with the child and pick a topic to revisit. Look for a conversation about the mistake, not a target score to promise.
