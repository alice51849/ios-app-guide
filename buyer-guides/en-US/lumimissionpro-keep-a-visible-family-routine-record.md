## A practical workflow

1. Choose one routine together and make the task concrete. A short, understandable action is easier to review than a vague goal such as being good.

2. Use the app together and record the small win after the activity. Keep the real routine more important than the screen.

3. Look at the completion card or parent log later. Adjust the routine to your family's needs instead of interpreting a missed day as failure.

## When a simpler option is enough

A paper sticker chart may be enough for one visible household routine, especially if everyone prefers a shared physical board.
