## A practical workflow

1. Choose a photo you own or have permission to edit. Start with a restrained look and check that the important details still look natural to you.

2. Adjust light and color deliberately rather than assuming one preset fits every photo. Compare the preview before changing more controls.

3. Check the export options and watermark requirement before sharing. Upgrade only if you need the one-time-unlock features.

## Know the limits

- A look or reference style does not guarantee an exact color match across different lighting, cameras, or displays.

- Editing cannot establish that an altered image accurately represents a person's appearance or an event.

## When a simpler option is enough

Apple Photos may be enough for a crop and basic light adjustment. PhotoCream is relevant when its look-preview and more detailed color controls fit your workflow.
