FAQ

How do I study for the TOEIC test?

Practice daily, focus on your weakest sections, and do timed drills to build pace; track progress over a plan.

Can an app guarantee a TOEIC score?

No — no app can guarantee a score. TOEIC is a registered trademark of ETS; this is an independent study aid, not endorsed by ETS.

How long does TOEIC prep take?

It varies by starting level and study time; steady daily practice over several weeks is a common approach.
