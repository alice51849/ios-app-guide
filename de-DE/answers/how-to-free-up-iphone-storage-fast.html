The fastest wins are deleting large 4K videos and then immediately emptying the Recently Deleted album (Photos > Utilities > Recently Deleted > Delete All), because items there still count against storage for up to 30 days. FAQ

Does offloading an app delete my data?

No — 'Offload App' (Settings > General > iPhone Storage > [app] > Offload App) removes only the app binary and frees its storage while preserving your documents and data. Reinstalling the app restores access to that data.

What is the 'Review Large Attachments' recommendation in iPhone Storage?

iOS surfaces your largest individual files — videos, photos, and attachments received in Messages — sorted by size, so you can quickly spot and delete the biggest space hogs. Find it in Settings > General > iPhone Storage at the top under Recommendations, or by tapping into the Photos or Messages app entry.

Can I free up space without deleting anything?

Somewhat — enabling iCloud Photos Optimize Storage and offloading unused apps both reduce device footprint without truly deleting content. But if you're near capacity, some manual deletion of videos, duplicate photos, or Messages attachments is usually necessary.
