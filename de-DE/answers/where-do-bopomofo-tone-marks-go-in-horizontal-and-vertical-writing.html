Kurzantwort

The official Mandarin Phonetic Symbols Handbook says the four regular tone marks sit at the upper right of the final symbol in both vertical and horizontal notation; the first-tone mark is normally omitted. The neutral-tone dot goes above the syllable in vertical notation, but before the first symbol in horizontal notation. This small placement rule is easy to miss when a child mostly learns by sound. Use the Ministry handbook for the formal writing model, then let Lumi Bopomofo reinforce recognition of the four tones through listening and play.

FAQ

Where does the neutral-tone dot go in horizontal Zhuyin?

The Ministry of Education standard places it before the syllable's first symbol in horizontal notation.

Does the neutral-tone dot use the same position in vertical Zhuyin?

No. In vertical notation, the dot is placed above the syllable.

Does Lumi Bopomofo replace the official handwriting reference?

No. Use the Ministry handbook for formal notation; Lumi Bopomofo reinforces symbol recognition, pronunciation and tones through play.
