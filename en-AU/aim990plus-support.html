Aim990 Plus Support Centre

Start here for straightforward help with languages, sound, mistake practice, offline access and progress saved on your device.

Popular support topics

How can I switch the app to another language?

Head to Profile > Study > Language and pick the language you would like.

How do I fix missing English speech?

Aim990 Plus speaks with the iOS en-US system voice. Turn up the media volume first. If you still hear nothing, open Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English, install or select a voice, then quit and reopen the app.

Where do I go to practise my mistakes?

Use the Replay tab, or tap Mistake Replay on Today, to revisit questions you missed.

Can I use the main features offline?

Yes. Core content and progress are available offline, and your study records stay on the device.

Does resetting progress remove the app?

No. Profile > Reset progress permanently removes your answers, mistakes, statistics and daily progress, but it does not uninstall Aim990 Plus.

How can I get Aim990 Plus back on my device?

Because Aim990 Plus is a paid App Store download, it does not have an in-app purchase restore button. Open your purchased apps with the same Apple Account to download it again.

Need another hand?

Send an email to hourstag.app@gmail.com with your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, selected app language and a description of what went wrong.
