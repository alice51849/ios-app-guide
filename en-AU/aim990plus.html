What is Aim990 Plus?

Aim990 Plus is an iOS app. English Exam Practice Offline

Get comfortable working against the clock

Aim990 Plus is a dedicated listening and reading trainer for serious adult learners. It gives every session a practical rhythm: open with an 8-question mixed Warm-up, complete a 20-question Pressure Set within 15 minutes, then use Mistake Replay to work through errors while they are still fresh.

Plenty of original material

Train with 630 original questions, organised as 90 items in each of Parts 1–7. That includes 90 different photographs for the visual questions. All material was written for this app and is not represented as official exam content.

Mix focused drills with longer runs

Choose mini, half or full mock formats to suit the time available. Dictation lets you slow down and catch what you missed; speed drills tighten the clock when you are ready to push. Device speech provides extra listening support, while 150 vocabulary entries make targeted word review easy to fit into a study routine.

Use your results constructively

Weakness analytics show which areas deserve another look, without predicting or guaranteeing a score. Progress and profile views help you see the work already completed. Mistake Replay closes the loop by returning missed questions to your next focused review.

Made for private offline study

Everything works offline on iPhone and iPad. There is no account, server, advertising, tracking or analytics. The paid download includes every feature from the start, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paywalls.
