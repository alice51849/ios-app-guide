An airport pickup sign works when the name is the only line, in large static high-contrast type, held above head height; on an iPhone that means landscape or letters turned ninety degrees, full brightness and a charged battery.

An airport pickup sign has one job: the arriving passenger should recognise their name before they start scanning the hall. So the sign carries only what they will recognise instantly, surname or company, no greeting, no flight number. Fewer characters means bigger type. Paper loses in practice because it creases, turns grey against backlight and gets covered when you wave; a bright screen stays readable in a dim hall and behind the barrier.

Three things decide readability. First, contrast: light type on a dark background carries further than black on white. Second, size: a short name should fill the full width, and if the phone is held upright, letters turned ninety degrees use the full height. Third, stillness: a name reads better static, while scrolling text is for longer lines such as the exit or the meeting point. Set brightness to maximum and switch off auto-lock, or the sign goes dark in the middle of the wait.

You can build such a sign in any notes app, but you lose a borderless full screen, text size by gesture and a quick switch between a static name and a scrolling line. LED Moving Text opens straight onto a live canvas where the preview is the editor: the words, the speed, the size and the colour change the moment you do, one tap takes it full screen, a pinch resizes the letters, and the sideways ninety-degree mode plus still and scrolling motion are part of the free tier.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the name stands still, or scrolls and is only half readable to someone walking past
  • Whether the type fills the full width or, held upright, the full height
  • Light type on a dark background for backlight and the barrier
  • Whether size and speed change right on the sign without opening a menu
  • Whether it works without internet or an account, even in a dead zone in the hall

The steps that actually work

  1. Check the arrival time and exit just before leaving; delays change the meeting point.
  2. Charge the iPhone fully, set brightness to maximum and auto-lock to Never.
  3. Type only the surname or company and choose light type on a dark background.
  4. Hold the screen in landscape or use the sideways mode so the name fills the whole area.
  5. Hold the sign above head height at the barrier, where arrivals look straight ahead.

FAQ

Paper or screen for an airport pickup sign?

Screen, if it is bright and large enough. Paper creases, greys out in backlight and cannot be enlarged on the spot; a screen adjusts in seconds but needs a charged battery.

Should the name scroll or stand still?

Stand still. A name has to be grasped in one glance; scrolling suits a longer note such as the exit or meeting point, not the name itself.

Does LED Moving Text need internet or an account?

No. Everything runs on the device; there is no account, no cloud, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and your messages and boards stay on your iPhone.
