Short answer

Claude counts paid-plan usage in a 5-hour session window and in weekly limits, and some plans also have weekly caps for individual models. Claude's own usage page shows these numbers when you open it; a tracker's job is to keep them in view, with a countdown to each reset, so you can decide before a task whether to start it now or after the next reset.

Check three things before you install one: that it shows the session limit, the weekly limits and any per-model weekly cap separately; that it warns you at a percentage you choose rather than only at 100 percent; and where your Claude sign-in goes. The safest pattern is signing in on Claude's own login page with the session kept in the device Keychain and no extra server in between.

UsageNow is the app this guide covers: UsageNow shows how much of each AI plan you have left and exactly when it resets, right on your Home Screen, and warns you before you run out. What to look for before choosing

  • The 5-hour session limit, the weekly limits and per-model weekly caps shown separately.
  • A live countdown and the exact reset time for each limit, in your local format.
  • Alerts at a percentage you choose, sent once per limit and cycle, plus an optional reset alert.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Apple Watch complications, so you do not have to open the app.
  • Pro and Max, work and personal accounts side by side, with sign-in on Claude's own login page kept in the Keychain.

A practical decision process

  1. Sign in with your Claude account and compare the numbers with Claude's own usage page.
  2. Set an alert at 90 percent on the limit you hit most often and check that it arrives once in that cycle.
  3. Add a Lock Screen or Home Screen widget so the remaining share is visible without opening anything.
  4. If you use more than one Claude account, sort them by 5-hour or weekly reset to see which frees up first.
  5. Use the free 24-hour trial to test every feature, widgets included, before paying for anything.

Which Claude limits does it show?

The 5-hour session limit and the weekly limits, including per-model weekly caps, each with a live countdown and the exact reset time.
