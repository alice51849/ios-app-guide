Short answer

Look for a tracker that only records what you tell it. Kumo's optional shot card logs the date, the injection site and your own notes, reminds you on the schedule you set, counts down to the next shot and shows a map of the sites you used most recently, with a quick check-in of how you feel. It does not record doses and does not calculate, interpret or suggest anything; your doctor's guidance stays the reference for that.

Then check the weight side. Logging should take about ten seconds: Kumo opens on today, carries over your last weight and lets you adjust it by 0.1. A 7-day average line smooths out the daily ups and downs, this week's average is compared with last week's, and non-scale wins such as looser jeans count too. Your records stay on your device and in your own iCloud, with no account and no ads.

What to look for before choosing

  • A shot card for the date, injection site and your notes, with reminders on your own schedule.
  • A countdown to the next shot and a map of recently used injection sites.
  • Weight logged in about ten seconds, with a 7-day average trend line.
  • Body measurements such as waist and hips, each with its own trend: one is free, all of them come with Kumo Full.
  • No account and no ads, with data on your device and in your own iCloud.

A practical decision process

  1. Log today's weight; the app starts from your last entry, so a 0.1 adjustment is often enough.
  2. Switch on the shot card in Settings and set your own reminder schedule.
  3. After each shot, record the date, the site and a note about how you feel.
  4. Read the 7-day average and the week-over-week comparison instead of single days.
  5. Export all records as a CSV file whenever you want your own copy.

FAQ

Does it calculate or suggest my dose?

No. The shot card only records the date, the injection site, your notes and how you feel, and reminds you on the schedule you set. It does not record doses and does not calculate, interpret or suggest anything; follow your doctor's guidance.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no advertising. Your records stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and sync between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Can I log from my Apple Watch?

Yes. Turn the Digital Crown and tap once to log your weight, even when your phone is not nearby.
