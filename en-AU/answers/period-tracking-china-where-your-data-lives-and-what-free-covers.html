When choosing a period tracking app, ask three things first: does it need an account, do your logs leave the phone, and can the free version keep logging periods long term. Once those are clear, compare predictions, calendar and reminders; what you really cannot do without is the years of records you build up.

Periods, symptoms and mood together are very personal health information. Some apps require sign-up and sync logs to a server by default; others run entirely on the phone. People use both, but you should know which one you are using before you start logging.

The second thing is what free actually includes. Some apps let you use everything at first and ask for a subscription later; others keep the free part available for logging and charge only for advanced features. Checking up front means you will not be forced to choose after months of records.

Cyca needs no account and has no server; data stays on your iPhone and it works offline. Logging today and your periods is free; a one-time Cyca Pro unlock adds future predictions (next period, fertile window and ovulation), personal rhythm, smart planning, full calendar history and deeper personalisation, with no subscription. Cyca offers guidance for health and planning and is neither a medical device nor a method of contraception.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether an account is required before you can log
  • Whether logs are uploaded to a server or the cloud
  • Whether the free version keeps logging periods rather than expiring
  • Whether advanced features are a subscription or a one-time purchase
  • Whether logging works without a connection

The steps that actually work

  1. Before downloading, read the privacy notes: account or not, and where data is stored.
  2. Start by logging only the first and last day of each period for a few cycles.
  3. Add cramps, flow and mood if useful; each takes seconds.
  4. After two or three cycles, check whether predictions match what actually happens.
  5. If cycles are clearly irregular or you feel unwell, follow a doctor's advice.
