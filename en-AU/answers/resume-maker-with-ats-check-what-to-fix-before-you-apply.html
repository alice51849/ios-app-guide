Keep the resume in one column with real, selectable text, use standard headings such as Experience, Education and Skills, and use the keywords from the job ad as written. Then check the result before you send it.

Applicant tracking systems turn your file into text and sort it into fields. Tables, multiple columns and text inside images can get scrambled in that conversion, so a company name lands in Skills or a work date disappears.

Keywords do not mean stuffing the resume with jargon. It means using the same wording as the ad: if it asks for project management, do not only write task coordination. Tailor the resume for each role instead of sending one version everywhere.

CV Desk builds the resume on iPhone and gives an on-device ATS estimate from 0 to 100 with clear fixes, then exports a single-column PDF with selectable text and an editable Word file. You can keep several resumes, one per job. Your resume never leaves the device.

What to check before you choose

  • PDF export with selectable text, not an image
  • One-column layout with standard headings
  • A check that explains what to fix, not just a number
  • An editable Word version when you need it
  • A watermark or surprise subscription at export

The steps that actually work

  1. Copy the job ad and pick its six to eight most important keywords.
  2. Write experience as actions and measurable results under standard headings.
  3. Remove tables, columns and text inside images.
  4. Run the check and apply the suggested fixes one by one.
  5. Export the PDF and confirm you can select and copy the text before sending.

FAQ

Is the app's ATS score the same as the employer's?

No. It is an on-device estimate to improve parse safety and keyword coverage; each employer uses its own system.

Should I add a photo?

The header photo is optional and off by default. Follow what the ad or country you apply in expects.

Is my resume uploaded?

No. It stays on your device and no account is needed.
