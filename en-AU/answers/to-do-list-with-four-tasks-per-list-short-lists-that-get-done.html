Keep at most 4 tasks per list and only two lists: "Today" and "Later" (or "Shopping"). Put today's list in a Home Screen widget, add a reminder only to things with a fixed time, and tick each task as soon as it is done.

A fifteen-line to-do list makes you feel organised in the morning and like a failure in the evening. You read what is left, not what is done. With four tasks, the list fits on one screen, you can see the end, and each tick is a quarter of the day: that is what makes you open the app again tomorrow.

Four is not a magic number, but it is roughly what a normal day absorbs on top of work or classes: a call to make, a form to hand in, an errand, one thing for yourself. If a task does not fit, it is usually too big. "Prepare for the exam" becomes "reread chapter 3"; "tidy the flat" becomes "empty the dishwasher".

The second list is the reserve. Anything that crosses your mind and is not for today goes there. In the evening, move one or two tasks at most into "Today". That avoids the classic trap: a single list where urgent things and someday things mix until you no longer dare open it. If you prefer, the second list can be your shopping list.

A to-do list only works if you see it. On iPhone that means a Home Screen widget rather than an app tucked into a folder, and, if you wear an Apple Watch, the list on your wrist so you can tick without taking out your phone on the train or in the supermarket. Keep reminders for tasks with a time: a reminder on everything ends up ignored.

What to check before you choose

  • Is today's list visible in a Home Screen widget?
  • How many taps does it take to add a task, and to tick it?
  • Can you tick tasks from an Apple Watch?
  • Can reminders be set task by task, or does everything notify?
  • What exactly does the free version include, in lists and tasks?
  • Is the upgrade an in-app purchase clearly stated on the App Store page?

The steps that actually work

  1. Empty your head into a "Later" list: everything, unsorted.
  2. Create a "Today" list and pick at most 4 tasks for it.
  3. Cut every vague task down until it fits in under an hour.
  4. Add the widget to your Home Screen, and open the app on your Apple Watch if you have one.
  5. Add reminders only to fixed-time tasks: appointments, calls, deadlines.
  6. In the evening, tick, delete what no longer makes sense, and move 1 or 2 tasks up from "Later".

FAQ

Isn't 4 tasks too few?

For one day, rarely. If you finish all 4, move one up from the "Later" list. That is more motivating than starting with 12 and carrying 8 over.

Is Mochi's free version enough?

For this method, yes: 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, 5 themes and 1 emoji pack. Premium, an in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks and all 101 themes.

Can I tick from my Apple Watch?

Yes, Mochi has an Apple Watch app, as well as Home Screen widgets.

Is "to do list" the same as "task list"?

Yes. Many people search in English on the App Store; the method is the same: a short, visible list you tick.
