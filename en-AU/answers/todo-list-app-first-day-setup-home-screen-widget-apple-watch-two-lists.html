On the day you download it, do three things: put today's list in a Home Screen widget, have it on your Apple Watch if you wear one, and add reminders only to fixed-time tasks. Use two lists, "Today" and "Shopping" (or "Later"), with at most 4 items each.

The most common reason a to-do list app gets abandoned is that you stop seeing it. Once it is in a folder or on the second page, remembering to open it becomes a task in itself. A Home Screen widget puts today's items where you look right after unlocking, so a glance on the train or in a checkout queue tells you what is left.

The second reason is length. Twelve items in the morning and three ticked by the evening looks like failure, so you do not open it the next day. Keep each list to 4 items: for office workers, "answer one important email, send one report, make one call, one thing for yourself"; for students, "review one chapter, finish one assignment, fill in one form, exercise 20 minutes". Anything that does not fit goes in the other list.

How to split the two lists? The most practical pair is "Today" and "Shopping": things to buy around big sales and items to pick up on the way home go to Shopping, and Today holds only what needs doing. Or use "Today" and "Later": anything that is not for today goes to Later, and each evening you move one or two items to tomorrow. Keeping them apart stops Today from filling up with errands.

Use reminders sparingly. A reminder on every item means a dozen alerts a day, and soon you swipe them all away. Add them only to things with a fixed time: when to leave, when a payment is due, when to call. Leave the rest to the widget. If you wear an Apple Watch, ticking on your wrist while walking or shopping is much faster than taking out your phone.

What to check before you choose

  • Can today's list sit in a Home Screen widget so you see it without opening the app?
  • Is there an Apple Watch app, and can you tick items on it?
  • Can reminders be set per item rather than for the whole list?
  • How many taps to add an item, and to tick one?
  • How many lists and items does the free version allow?
  • Is payment an in-app purchase, clearly stated on the App Store page?

The steps that actually work

  1. After downloading, create two lists: "Today" and "Shopping" (or "Later").
  2. Put only 4 items in Today; split big ones, e.g. "prepare the report" becomes "outline the report".
  3. Long-press an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, search for Mochi and place it on your first page.
  4. If you have an Apple Watch, open Mochi on the watch and check that your list shows up.
  5. Add reminders only to fixed-time tasks: leaving, paying, calling, handing in homework.
  6. Before bed, spend 2 minutes: tick, delete what is no longer needed, and move one or two items from Later into tomorrow.
