Year-end deep cleaning checklist: split it by room, then by day

Deep cleaning stalls because of planning, not motivation. Leave everything for the last days of December and it collides with shopping, travel and guests.

List every job room by room, break each into a task that takes 30 minutes or less, and spread them across December — one small job on weekdays, big jobs like the oven hood and windows on weekends, the entryway last.

Start the list in mid-December, not the week of Christmas. Weekdays get small jobs you can finish in 30 minutes (one cupboard, one bathroom drawer), weekends get the jobs that need time and daylight, such as windows, the range hood filter or the fridge. Put outdoor jobs on the warmest afternoons you can.

Split the list by room first — kitchen, bathroom, living room, entryway — and then into concrete actions: "soak the stove grates" instead of "clean the kitchen". You can tick the first one after 30 minutes; the second never feels finished. Order each room top to bottom and back to front so you don't re-dirty what you just cleaned.

Keeping one list per room on your phone lets you look at only today's job. What to check before you choose

  • Jobs split by room and by concrete action
  • Each job small enough for 30 minutes
  • Windows and outdoor jobs on warm afternoons
  • Cleaning supplies and trash bags bought first
  • Your area's last bulky-trash pickup of the year checked

The steps that actually work

  1. Mid-December: write down every spot you want to clean, room by room.
  2. Break each into 30-minute jobs and sort them by how long they take.
  3. Check the last trash and recycling pickup of the year, and sort what you'll throw out first.
  4. Assign one small job per weekday and the big jobs (hood, windows, fridge) to weekends.
  5. Leave only the entryway and bathroom finish for the last days before the holiday.

FAQ

When should I start year-end cleaning?

About two weeks before the holiday. At 30 minutes a day that covers most homes without a marathon weekend.

Which order is most efficient?

Top to bottom, back to front: lights and hood first, floors and the entryway last, so dust doesn't fall onto finished areas.

Can I build this list with Mochi's free version?

The free version gives you 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each. For more rooms, Premium (an in-app purchase) unlocks unlimited lists and tasks.
