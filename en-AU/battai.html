What is BattAI: Battery health score?

BattAI: Battery health score is an iOS app. Charge Alerts & 80% Forecast

BattAI

• BattAI uses five values from public iOS APIs: battery level, charge state, Low Power Mode, whole-device thermal state, and time. Other results are calculated from those readings or added by you. Each detail shows its source and limits.

• BattAI has no account, telemetry, ads, or network requests. Data stays on this device, except for a minimal feature-limited snapshot sent directly to your paired Apple Watch. Sampling is limited, analysis runs at most every five minutes, estimates use ranges, and missing data stays missing.

Privacy

• No ads, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, or internet requests. Data stays here, except for a minimal snapshot sent directly to your paired Apple Watch.

• Public iOS APIs provide battery level, charge state, Low Power Mode, whole-device thermal state, and time. BattAI labels every other result as estimated or provided by you.

• The fixed rules follow established lithium-ion guidance on deep discharge, long periods at high charge, heat, and charge volume. Apple’s 80% maximum-capacity service benchmark is kept separate from BattAI coaching bands. No result promises extra battery life.
