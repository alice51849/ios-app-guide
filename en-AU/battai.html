BattAI

• BattAI uses five values from public iOS APIs: battery level, charge state, Low Power Mode, whole-device thermal state, and time. Other results are calculated from those readings or added by you. Each detail shows its source and limits.

• BattAI has no account, telemetry, ads, or network requests. Data stays on this device, except for a minimal feature-limited snapshot sent directly to your paired Apple Watch. Sampling is limited, analysis runs at most every five minutes, estimates use ranges, and missing data stays missing.
