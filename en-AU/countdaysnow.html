What is CountDaysNow: Event Counter?

CountDaysNow: Event Counter is an iOS app. Countdown Plan: Work Backwards

Plan backwards from the dates that matter. Countdown Plan gives any event its own preparation steps, such as renew the passport 30 days before a trip or order the cake 7 days before a birthday, and shows what is late, due today, due in the next 7 days and done across all your events.

Each step is due a set number of days before its event. Change the event date and every step moves with it; a repeating event starts each new cycle with a fresh plan. Countdown Plan is free, works on-device and needs no account.

Daily Focus saves one private Since / Today / Ahead choice each day and preserves that dated trail as milestones change.

No ads, no subscription, no account. One purchase unlocks everything for good.

• Ahead · Today · Since

CountDaysNow keeps future, current and past events in three clear views, with precise day counts and a date calculator for adding, subtracting or comparing dates.

• Events, categories and archive

Create and edit events, organize them into categories, search quickly and archive milestones without losing their history.

• Recurrence, reminders and Calendar

Set repeating events, including an optional end date, schedule reminders and import only the Calendar events you choose.

• Widgets, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

Put any event on Home Screen widgets in every size, including iPad Extra Large, keep a live countdown running in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and let the Next 3 widget show your three closest dates at once. Apple Watch stays useful offline.

• Milestone alerts and 15 calendars

A gentle nudge arrives 7, 3 and 1 day before, on the day itself, at day 100 and on every anniversary. Any event can show a second calendar beside the Gregorian date: Chinese lunar, Japanese, Hebrew, Persian, Indian, Islamic, Buddhist and more.

Events stay in step across your iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud, a single countdown can be shared with other CountDaysNow users and updated together, and a local snapshot is written before every update so nothing goes missing.

How do iCloud sync and shared countdowns work?

With Lifetime Pro, optional iCloud sync keeps events and categories up to date across your devices through your private Apple CloudKit database. Share Countdown sends only the selected event’s title, note, target date, calendar system, time zone, all-day setting, recurrence, symbol and color to the CountDaysNow users you invite; reminders, categories, the Calendar source identifier, favorite and archive status, photo backgrounds and every other event remain private. Sync also carries the technical event identifier, created and updated timestamps, and conflict-resolution metadata; invitees can view and edit only the user-visible fields, not this technical metadata. The owner can stop sharing or delete the shared countdown, and participants can leave it.

CountDaysNow uses no accounts, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking tools, and sends no data to developer servers.
