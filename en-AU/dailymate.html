What is DailyMate: Everyday Phrases?

DailyMate: Everyday Phrases is an iOS app. Real Sentences for Daily Life

Turn spare moments into useful language by learning complete sentences, not disconnected words. DailyMate includes 8,400 practical phrases across 47 learning languages, 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations. Buy once for the complete app. No subscription.

LANGUAGE THAT FITS REAL LIFE

Build up phrases for the situations that make up an ordinary day or a trip overseas.

• 8,400 complete phrases

• 47 learning languages

• 14 everyday topics and 84 situations

• Every topic has 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases

• Listening with system voices suited to the chosen language

• Saved phrases and separate progress for each learning language

THREE LEVELS, TOPIC BY TOPIC

Start with the essentials, add richer intermediate wording, then move into precise advanced expressions. There are 200 different phrases at each level in every topic.

LISTEN, SAVE AND COME BACK

Hear complete phrases with the matching system voice. Save the ones you will want again and pick up from the progress kept for that language.

HANDY ON THE HOME SCREEN AND WATCH

Use the interactive Home Screen Widget to see a phrase, advance or save it. Apple Watch puts phrases, system-voice listening, saved items and progress on your wrist.

BUY ONCE. NO SUBSCRIPTION.
