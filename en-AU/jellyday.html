What is JellyDay: Visual Daily Planner?

JellyDay: Visual Daily Planner is an iOS app. Cute Routine Schedule & Timer

See your day as a timeline: every task in JellyDay is a soft pastel block, and the taller the block, the longer it takes. A bright line marks right now, so what's now, what's next and how much time is left are clear at a glance.

Running late? Push the rest of today back from a task's menu, then let a gentle countdown carry you through each block or routine, step by step.

HOW JELLYDAY WORKS

Taller block, longer task: see how much of today is already taken before you add more.

Push a task back 15 minutes and every unfinished task after it moves too; finished ones stay put.

Tasks left unfinished? Start them all from now or move them all to tomorrow with one tap.

Add a morning or bedtime routine with one tap, then run through it step by step, each step with its own countdown.

A shrinking pie shows the minutes left, and you can pause or finish from the Live Activity without opening the app.

See what's now and what's next on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

MADE FOR REAL DAYS

Time-block your working day, set out a study day like a timetable with a countdown for each block, and end the evening with a bedtime routine.

EVERYTHING IN JELLYDAY

Today's timeline: every task is a pastel block sized by how long it takes, with a line for right now

As many tasks as you like, each with a name, sticker, start time, colour and a length from 5 minutes to 2 hours

Not sure when yet? Save it to your inbox and schedule it later

Long-press any block and drag it to a new time in 5-minute steps

From a task's menu, push it and the rest of today's unfinished tasks back 15 minutes, or move it to tomorrow

When tasks run past their time, a short summary lets you start them all from now or move them all to tomorrow

Bring yesterday's schedule over to today with one tap

Ready-made routine templates, such as morning and bedtime, add every step to today with one tap

Focus countdown with a shrinking pie and the time left in plain words, plus pause, resume and done

Routines walk you through each step, one countdown at a time

Live Activity and Dynamic Island keep the countdown in view while you focus, with buttons to pause or finish

Reminders when a task starts and when it is about to end, which you can turn off in the app's settings

On iOS 26 or later, an alarm lets you know when a focus countdown you started is up

Now widget for your Home Screen in small and medium sizes, showing what you're doing and what's next

Lock Screen widgets in circular, rectangular and inline styles for the countdown and what's next

Companion Apple Watch app, used with your iPhone: see what's now and what's next, follow the countdown, start and pause, mark a task done, plus watch face complications

Cute jelly characters and stickers that make each task easy to spot

Works on iPhone and iPad, in 50 languages that follow your device language

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account and no sign-up. No ads and no tracking. JellyDay does not collect any data, and the app makes no network connections of its own. Your plans are stored on your device, and purchases are handled by the App Store.

IN-APP PURCHASE

The features above come with the app. Some features are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase: routines that repeat every day, on weekdays or on the days you choose; all 50 routine templates; all 60 stickers; large and extra large timeline widgets that show half or all of your day; a big StandBy countdown widget; and tap-to-complete on small and medium Home Screen widgets. Family Sharing is supported: the purchase is shared, but your plans are not.
