What is Mochi DoneStamp: Last Time?

Mochi DoneStamp: Last Time is an iOS app. Remember when you did it

When did you last change the bed linen, service the air con, check the smoke alarms, call Mum, back up your travel photos or renew the rego?

Mochi DoneStamp remembers the moments that do not belong on a rigid to-do list. Finish something, tap once, and the exact date and time become part of a calm, searchable history. Add an approximate or exact rhythm when it helps; the next due time always restarts from when you actually finished.

• One-tap logging with instant Undo

• Backdate, edit or delete any occurrence

• Flexible and exact rhythms from minutes to years

• Local reminders with Done and Snooze actions

• Interactive Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

• Siri and Shortcuts

• Optional notes and compressed photos

• Interval insights, full backup and single-file JSON export

• Light and Dark Mode, iPad, VoiceOver, RTL and Dynamic Type
