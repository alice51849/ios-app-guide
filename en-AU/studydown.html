What is Studydown: Face-Down Timer?

Studydown: Face-Down Timer is an iOS app. Face-down activity time ledger

Make room for what matters, then see the time you put into it. Studydown turns a phone placed face down into a record of your projects, reading and everyday interests.

Choose an activity, tap to start, and rest your phone face down on a steady surface. Keep Studydown open while timing. Picking it up pauses the count after your chosen grace period; leaving the app stops time from being added.

• Name and colour your activities, arrange them your way, and add or correct entries manually.

• Review today and this week, your consecutive days and the time spent on each activity.

• Set weekly goals and deadlines. Choose a daily reminder or one every few hours.

• Save your records as CSV. Pro also includes JSON backup and restore.

Start with up to 3 activities and a total allowance of 5 recorded minutes, not a daily reset. A session already underway is kept in full. A single Pro purchase unlocks unlimited activities and recording, advanced statistics, goals, deadlines, widgets, Apple Watch and full backups. No subscription.

Timing and records stay on your device, without an account or ads. You choose whether to share exported files. Purchases and purchase restoration use Apple services.

Every face-down session keeps a Focus Signature: phone pickups and average uninterrupted time. Give each deadline an effort target to see logged Activity time and the daily pace still required.
