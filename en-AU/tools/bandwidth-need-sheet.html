Formula and limits

Total per row = devices × per-device figure. Needed = sum of the rows. Headroom = plan − needed; under 20% of the plan is tight, below zero is not enough. Figures are added in tenths of a Mbps, so totals never drift.

Purchase 1: 1 × 15.0 ↓ / 0.5 ↑ = 15.0 ↓ / 0.5 ↑
Purchase 2: 2 × 3.0 ↓ / 3.0 ↑ = 6.0 ↓ / 6.0 ↑
Purchase 3: 4 × 1.0 ↓ / 0.5 ↑ = 4.0 ↓ / 2.0 ↑
Needed download = 25.0; Needed upload = 8.5
Headroom ↓ / ↑ = 100.0 − 25.0 = 75.0 ↓; 20.0 − 8.5 = 11.5 ↑
Status = Fits

Per-device figures are approximate planning values from the services' own guidance (Netflix, Zoom, console makers, FCC). This sheet plans; it does not measure your line.

  • Streaming (SD): 3.0 ↓ / 0.5 ↑ Mbps
  • Streaming (HD): 5.0 ↓ / 0.5 ↑ Mbps
  • Streaming (4K): 15.0 ↓ / 0.5 ↑ Mbps
  • Video calls: 3.0 ↓ / 3.0 ↑ Mbps
  • Online gaming: 3.0 ↓ / 1.0 ↑ Mbps
  • Cloud backup: 1.0 ↓ / 5.0 ↑ Mbps
  • Browsing and messaging: 1.0 ↓ / 0.5 ↑ Mbps
  • Smart home devices: 0.5 ↓ / 0.5 ↑ Mbps

Plan speeds from 0.5 to 10000 Mbps with at most one decimal, 1 to 50 devices per row, up to 20 rows. Wi-Fi coverage, latency and your provider's real delivered speed are outside this sheet.

  • Plan download (Mbps) / Plan upload (Mbps): 0.5 ≤ x ≤ 10000 Mbps
  • Devices: 1 ≤ n ≤ 50
  • Purchase: 1 ≤ n ≤ 20
