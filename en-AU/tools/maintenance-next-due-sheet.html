Formula and limits

Next due = last done + interval. Month intervals keep the same day of the month and fall back to the last day when it does not exist, including leap years. Days left = next due − today. Overdue below 0 days; due soon from 0 to 7.

2026-06-01 + 3 months = 2026-09-01; 2026-09-01 − 2026-09-05 = -4
2026-08-31 + 1 week = 2026-09-07; 2026-09-07 − 2026-09-05 = 2
2025-11-30 + 12 months = 2026-11-30; 2026-11-30 − 2026-09-05 = 86

Intervals from 1 to 3650 days, weeks or months. Dates between 1900-01-01 and 2999-12-31. Up to 30 tasks. No reminders, notifications or calendar sync; nothing leaves this page.

  • Interval: 1 ≤ x ≤ 3650
  • Last done: 1900-01-01 ≤ x ≤ 2999-12-31
  • Task: 1 ≤ n ≤ 30
