What is TripBee Pro: Trip Planner?

Plan every trip in one beautiful, private, offline app.

TripBee Pro turns messy travel plans into a clear day-by-day timeline. Add flights, hotels, restaurants and activities, get a smart packing list for the whole family, and open turn-by-turn directions with a single tap — no account, no internet needed.

• One-tap Apple Maps directions from any starting point to any place
