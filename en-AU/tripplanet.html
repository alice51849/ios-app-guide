What is Lumi Trip Planet: World Travel?

Lumi Trip Planet: World Travel is an iOS app. Family travel adventure

Turn every family trip into a tiny space adventure.

Trip Planet helps your family turn travel moments into magical missions everyone can share. Create a trip, choose smart Mission Packs, and watch your journey appear as a glowing constellation across a cinematic nebula. Instead of another checklist, everyone gets small real-world quests: try a new food, be brave at the airport, say thank you, spot three colors at a museum, take a memory photo, or help pack the hotel bag. Tiny missions. Big smiles.

WHY FAMILIES LOVE IT

- Movie-grade animated space backgrounds, or your own trip photo

- Constellation adventure map with a premium game feel

- Mission Packs for flight days, hotels, food, museums, kindness, bravery and memories

- Dazzling reward unlocks and a collectible gallery

- Travel Passport stamps for completed trips

- Auto-generated Trip Story Cards to share as keepsakes

- iCloud sharing for the whole family

- Multiple travelers and premium theme worlds with Plus

SAFE BY DESIGN

Trip Planet has no ads, no third-party tracking and no external servers. Purchases and sharing sit behind a parental gate, and your family's names and photos stay private on-device and in your own iCloud.

PAY ONCE. ADVENTURE OFTEN.

Trip Planet is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Unlock it once and make every holiday, road trip, flight and weekend away feel like a story your family created together.
