Privacy Policy — Aim990 Plus

Effective July 19, 2026

Privacy commitment

Aim990 Plus does not provide accounts or include ads, analytics, tracking or social SDKs. We do not collect or sell personal information.

Local learning records

Goals, settings, answers, mistakes, statistics, mock-test records and progress are stored locally on your device. The app does not use a developer-operated server.

iOS voice resources

The English voice is supplied by the iOS system. Apple may manage voice assets subject to its terms. Aim990 Plus does not send question content or progress to the developer.

Reminder choices

Optional study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification server is involved.

Payment information

Aim990 Plus is sold as a paid App Store download and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple manages the transaction; the app and developer never see your payment card number.

Data removal

Reset progress deletes study progress from the device. Removing the app deletes its local data according to iOS behaviour. Apple continues to hold your purchase history.

General adult education

Aim990 Plus is a general education product for adults. It does not collect personal information from any user.

Questions about this policy

Contact hourstag.app@gmail.com with any privacy questions.
