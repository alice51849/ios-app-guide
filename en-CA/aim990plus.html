What is Aim990 Plus?

Aim990 Plus is an iOS app. Offline English Test Practice

Build accuracy when the clock is running

Aim990 Plus is an independent listening and reading trainer designed for serious adult learners. Its core routine keeps preparation focused: begin with an 8-question mixed Warm-up, move to a 20-question Pressure Set with a 15-minute limit, then revisit errors in Mistake Replay.

A broad bank of original questions

The app includes 630 original questions, organized as 90 items in each of Parts 1–7. Visual practice uses 90 distinct photographs. The content was made for this app and is never presented as official exam material.

Work at the right length and pace

Use mini, half, or full mock formats for anything from a compact session to a complete run. Dictation helps uncover listening details, speed drills develop decisions under tighter timing, and device speech offers flexible audio support. Review 150 vocabulary entries whenever a shorter language session fits better.

Focus on useful next steps

Weakness analytics identify areas that may benefit from more attention without promising a score. Progress and profile views keep a clear record on your device. Mistake Replay makes that record practical by bringing missed questions back for focused review.

Complete and private from day one

Aim990 Plus runs offline on iPhone and iPad. There is no sign-in, server, advertising, tracking, or analytics. One paid download provides all features, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paywalls.
