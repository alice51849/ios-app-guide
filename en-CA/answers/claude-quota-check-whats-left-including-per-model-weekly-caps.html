Your Claude quota has to be read as the 5-hour session limit, the weekly limit and the per-model weekly caps together; keep what is left and the reset time for each on a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget and checking takes one glance.

Most people searching for their Claude quota want to know how much is left and how long it will last. Claude splits it into a 5-hour session limit and a weekly limit, and each model also has its own weekly cap; watch only one and another often stops you first. Read all three together to know whether the next piece of work fits.

UsageNow shows Claude's 5-hour limit, weekly limit and per-model weekly caps on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, with a live countdown to every reset and the exact reset time in your local format. Tap the widget to refresh when you want the latest numbers; Apple Watch complications show them too.

If you would rather not keep looking, set your own alerts: pick the 5-hour limit, the weekly limit or all limits and type a percentage such as 90 or 98; each fires once per limit and cycle. Turn on the reset alert to know the moment a limit is back. Sign-in happens on Claude's official login page and stays in the device's Keychain; there is no UsageNow server.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the 5-hour and weekly limits are shown separately
  • Whether per-model weekly caps are visible
  • Whether there is a reset countdown and an exact reset time
  • Whether you can see it on the Home Screen or Lock Screen without opening a page
  • Whether sign-in stays on the device

The steps that actually work

  1. Sign in on Claude's official login page.
  2. Add a widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen in the size you want.
  3. Look at the 5-hour limit, the weekly limit and the per-model weekly caps to see which has the least left.
  4. Check that one's reset countdown and plan the next piece of work around it.
  5. Set an alert at 90 or 98, then turn on the reset alert.
