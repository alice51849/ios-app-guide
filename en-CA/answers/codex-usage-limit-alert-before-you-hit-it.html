The Codex usage limit has to be watched as a 5-hour window and a weekly window, and an alert on each at a percentage you care about, such as 90 or 98, lets you wrap up before you hit it.

Most people searching for the Codex usage limit have been stopped partway through a long task. ChatGPT Codex counts a 5-hour window and a weekly window separately, and the weekly one can run out while the 5-hour one still has room, so watching only one of them means you notice too late.

In UsageNow you pick the 5-hour limit, the weekly limit or all limits, type a percentage such as 90 or 98, and the alert is set. Add as many as you like; each alert fires once per limit and cycle, so your Lock Screen does not fill up. Turn on the reset alert and you know the moment a limit is back.

When an alert arrives, checking what is left takes a glance at a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget or an Apple Watch complication, with a countdown to the reset and the exact reset time in your local format. Claude, GitHub Copilot and Gemini limits sit on the same screen. Sign-in happens on each service's official login page and stays in the device's Keychain.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the 5-hour and weekly windows are shown separately
  • Whether you can set alerts at a percentage you choose
  • Whether the same limit avoids alerting you over and over
  • Whether it also tells you when a limit is back
  • Whether sign-in stays on the device instead of passing through another server

The steps that actually work

  1. Sign in on ChatGPT's official login page.
  2. Pick the 5-hour limit, the weekly limit or all limits.
  3. Type the percentage where you want to wrap up, such as 90 or 98, to create the alert.
  4. Turn on the reset alert so you can pick up again the moment a limit is back.
  5. Put a widget on the Home Screen or Lock Screen and check what is left and the reset time when an alert arrives.
