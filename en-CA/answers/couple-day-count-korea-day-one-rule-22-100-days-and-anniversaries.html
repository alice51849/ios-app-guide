In Korea, couple days are counted with the first day as day 1. So day 100 is the start date plus 99 days, and day N is the start date plus N−1 days. Anniversaries such as the first and second year fall on the same calendar date each year, not after a fixed number of days.

Calendar arithmetic gives the difference between two dates, so the start date itself is day 0. The couple-anniversary custom counts the start date as day 1, so you add one to the calendar difference. For example, if you started dating on 7 November 2026, day 22 is 28 November, day 100 is 14 February 2027, day 200 is 25 May, day 300 is 2 September and day 1,000 is 2 August 2029. The first anniversary is 7 November 2027.

Counting by hand, the different lengths of each month make it easy to slip by a day at month boundaries. CountDaysNow splits past, today and upcoming events into three views with exact day counts, and its calculator adds, subtracts and compares dates, so you can check the start date plus 99 days right away. Check once at the start whether the number shown counts the first day as day 0 or day 1.

Remembering is harder than calculating. FAQ

How many days after the start date is day 100?

Counting the start date as day 1, day 100 is 99 days later. A calendar difference gives 99, so add one in your head.

Is the first anniversary day 365?

No. The first anniversary is the same date the next year. Counting the start as day 1, that is usually day 366, or day 367 if a leap day falls in between.

Can my partner and I see the same countdown?

In CountDaysNow, one countdown can be updated together with another CountDaysNow user, and you can also send it as a share card.
