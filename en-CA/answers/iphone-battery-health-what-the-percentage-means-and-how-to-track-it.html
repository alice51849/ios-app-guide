iPhone battery health mainly comes down to Maximum Capacity in Settings › Battery: how much charge the battery holds now compared with when it was new. The lower it is, the shorter a full charge lasts; near 80% it is worth watching. One reading is not enough; log it over time to see how fast it is falling.

Maximum Capacity drops as the battery ages chemically: charge cycles, long stretches at full charge and heat while charging all wear it down. Apple says iPhone batteries are designed to keep about 80% of their original capacity after a certain number of complete charge cycles (around 500 for iPhone 14 and earlier, around 1000 from iPhone 15). So 85% on a six-month-old phone means something different from 85% on a three-year-old one.

Settings only shows today's Maximum Capacity, not how it changes. BattAI keeps a record of charge level, charging and heat while you use your phone, and turns battery status into a 0–100 health score in five grades from excellent to needs service, listing what shapes it: capacity 40%, cycle count 25%, device age 10%, heat exposure 10% and charging habits 15%. You enter Maximum Capacity after checking Settings › Battery, or import it from a system Analytics file; BattAI cannot read that value itself.

Your Maximum Capacity readings become a trend chart with a 95% confidence band, and once readings are at least 30 days apart it shows monthly capacity change. Every charge gets a charge care score, and charging habits are rated on depth of discharge, charge limit, overnight charging, heat while charging and charging frequency, with suggestions such as using 80–90% when it suits you. Scores and estimates are calculated references, not official or manufacturer values.

What to check before you choose

  • Do you know your current Maximum Capacity, and when you last checked it?
  • Is it falling slowly, or faster over the last few months?
  • Does your phone often get hot while charging, or sit at 100% overnight?
  • How much charge do you lose per hour, and is one time of day worse?
  • When you sell or replace the phone, can you show a clear battery report?

The steps that actually work

  1. Open Settings › Battery and note Maximum Capacity.
  2. Enter it in BattAI, or import Maximum Capacity and cycle count from an Analytics file.
  3. Check what lowers your health score and deal with the biggest factor first.
  4. Adjust charging habits as suggested, for example an 80–90% limit when it suits you.
  5. Enter Maximum Capacity again after 30 days to see the monthly change.

FAQ

At what battery health should I replace the battery?

Near 80% is worth watching, but also consider how often you charge per day and whether the phone shuts down unexpectedly. Logging the trend for a while gives a better answer.

Can BattAI read Maximum Capacity directly?

No. Public system interfaces only provide charge level, charging state and the phone's thermal state; Maximum Capacity and cycle count come from what you enter after checking Settings or import from an Analytics file.

Does charging to 100% every time hurt the battery?

Long periods at full charge and heat while charging both speed up ageing. BattAI suggests changes from your charging history, such as an 80–90% limit when it suits you.
