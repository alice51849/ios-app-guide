The simplest method is an app that lets you log every payment in the local currency and see it converted to your home currency on the spot, plus a daily cap and categories set before you leave. Card charges vary with the rate and fees, so treat the log as a guide.

Holiday trips land in an expensive season for flights and hotels, and spending on the ground also rises with gifts and New Year events. Local price tags do not match your sense of value at home, so small payments add up before you notice you are over budget. Setting a daily cap before departure and logging by category — food, transport, shopping, sightseeing — prevents most overspending.

Expect logged amounts and actual charges to differ. The conversion rate in an app is a reference; your card issuer's rate, fees and exchange-counter rates are different. Log cash and card separately and note the rate when you exchange cash, so the statement is easy to reconcile later. Banks and exchange counters may keep shorter hours over the holidays, so get cash early.

What to check before you choose

  • Enter in local currency and see your home currency immediately
  • Logging that works offline, with no signal
  • A daily limit that shows which days ran over
  • Review by category and by trip
  • No account, with data kept on the device

The steps that actually work

  1. Before leaving, set a total budget and divide it by the number of days for a daily cap.
  2. Fetch rates before departure so logging works on the plane or underground.
  3. Log every payment in local currency with a category, and note whether it was cash or card.
  4. Each night, compare the day's total with the cap and adjust the next day.
  5. Back home, compare the log with your card statement to see rate and fee differences.

FAQ

App or notebook for travel money?

Whichever you will keep using, but converting currencies by hand is tedious, so an app that converts as you type is easier during the trip.

Why does the app's converted amount differ from my card charge?

The app uses a mid-market reference rate, while your card issuer applies its own rate and fees. Use the log for budgeting and the statement for the final figure.

Does G+Money Lite work without a signal?

Yes. Fetch the latest rates while online and it logs and converts offline with your saved rates.
