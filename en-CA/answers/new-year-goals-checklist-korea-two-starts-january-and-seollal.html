New Year goals checklist: plan a five-week first round, not a whole year

"Exercise", "learn a language", "save money" — the same goals every year, and by mid-January the planner is blank. Usually it's not willpower; it's that nothing on the list can be ticked today.

Give each goal one action that takes about 10 minutes and can be checked off today, keep at most three on the daily checklist, and treat January 1 to Lunar New Year (February 6, 2027) as a first round you review and restart.

A year is too long to steer by. Cutting it into rounds — January 1 to Lunar New Year is about five weeks in 2027 — lets you look at how often you actually ticked each action and adjust before the habit quietly dies. If January went badly, the second new year is a natural restart rather than a failure.

Checklist items must be actions, not outcomes. "Lose weight" can't be ticked; "take the stairs after work" can be ticked tonight. If you can't answer yes or no at 10 p.m., rewrite it. Add a minimum version (three words of vocabulary, a three-minute stretch) for late-work days so the streak doesn't break.

Checklists last when they're visible and quick to tick. The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each — enough for a "today's three" list and a review list.

What to check before you choose

  • One tickable action per goal
  • Three or fewer daily items
  • A minimum version for busy days
  • A fixed reminder time
  • A review date set at the end of the first round

The steps that actually work

  1. By December 31, write down every goal without filtering.
  2. Pick three and move the rest to an "after round one" list.
  3. Turn each into a 10-minute action plus a minimum version.
  4. From January 1, put them on your daily checklist with a reminder at the same time each day.
  5. Around February 6, review five weeks of ticks: grow what worked, shrink what didn't, start round two.

FAQ

How many goals should go on the daily list?

Three or fewer. You can hold more goals, but more daily items tends to turn the checklist into pressure.

I already fell off in January. Now what?

Use Lunar New Year as a second start and make the action smaller instead of restarting at full size.

Is Mochi's free version enough?

It includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, which fits a three-action daily list and a review list. Premium unlocks unlimited lists and tasks.
