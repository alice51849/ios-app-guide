Period tracking works when you record the first and last day of every period without gaps, plus a few quick notes such as flow, cramps and mood. The more cycles you log, the closer predictions of your next period, fertile window and ovulation day get to reality.

Every cycle is different and can shift with stress, sleep or travel. In the first two or three cycles you track, predictions still lean on general averages more than on your own data. The key is not logging as much detail as possible but recording the start and end date every time, because that is what cycle length is calculated from.

Cyca lets you log your period and estimates your next period, fertile window and ovulation day, all on one calm calendar that adjusts every time you log. Cramps, flow and moods take seconds to log, so a daily entry never feels like a chore.

A Daily Body Forecast reads where you are in your cycle and suggests how your energy, mood and skin may be today, and you can turn on reminders before your next period and fertile window, plus a daily logging reminder. All data stays on the device, with no account, no cloud and no tracking, and the app works offline.

What to check before you choose

  • Do you record the first and last day of every period?
  • Are your symptom notes short enough to log every day?
  • Do predictions adjust every time you add new data?
  • Is there a reminder before your period so you can prepare?
  • Does your cycle data stay on your device, or go to a cloud?

The steps that actually work

  1. Log the first day of your period as soon as it starts.
  2. Note flow, cramps and mood briefly each day.
  3. Log the last day when your period ends.
  4. Turn on the reminder before your next period and the daily logging reminder.
  5. After two or three cycles, check the calendar to see how long and how regular your cycle is.

FAQ

How many cycles until predictions are accurate?

The more the better. Early predictions lean on general averages; once you have several cycles of your own, Cyca adapts to your real rhythm every time you log.

Can I use Cyca as contraception?

No. Cyca provides wellness and planning estimates and is not a medical device or a form of contraception.

Is my period data sent anywhere?

No. Cyca has no account, no cloud and no tracking; your data stays on your device and the app works offline.
