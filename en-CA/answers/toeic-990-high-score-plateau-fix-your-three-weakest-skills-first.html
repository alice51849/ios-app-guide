To push toward 990, stop drilling at random: find your three weakest skills with a short diagnosis, decide which to fix first, drill one a day, then recheck the same skills on Day 30 next to your Day 1 record to see whether the time went to the right place.

Most people searching for TOEIC 990 are past the basics and stuck at a high score, unsure where the remaining points go. At this stage another set of mock tests rarely moves the score; the drag is usually a few skills, such as grammar speed, pacing on long reading passages, or one type of listening question.

Aim990 starts with a diagnosis of about 20 grammar, listening and reading questions in about 8 minutes, estimates your practice band, and finds your three weakest skills. It writes them up as a prescription: which to fix first, why you lose points, and roughly how long it takes. Each day you drill one prescribed skill through diagnose, repair and verify stages instead of endless random sets.

On Day 30 you retest the same skills and compare side by side with Day 1; the learning evidence log records accuracy and pace for each skill. An emergency mode covers 1, 3, 7 and 14 days before the test. There is no account and no sign-in, your records stay on your device, and daily practice works offline. The band shown is an unofficial estimate, not a score guarantee.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether it diagnoses first and tells you what to fix first
  • Whether practice follows your own answer history instead of random questions
  • Whether accuracy and pace are logged per skill
  • Whether you can retest the same skills after a set number of days and compare with your start
  • Whether there is a condensed plan for the last days before the test

The steps that actually work

  1. Take the roughly 8-minute diagnosis and see your three weakest skills.
  2. Follow the prescription order and drill only the first skill each day.
  3. Take each skill through diagnose, repair and verify before moving on.
  4. Check the weakness radar to see whether your top three repair targets change.
  5. Retest the same skills on Day 30 and compare side by side with Day 1.

FAQ

Do more mock tests help when I am stuck at a high score?

Near the top, more sets rarely move the score; finding the few skills that keep losing points and fixing them is more likely to show a difference.

How long should I practise each day?

Aim990 drills one prescribed skill a day through diagnose, repair and verify stages, and practice works offline, so a commute or lunch break is enough.
