Travel English: practice replies first, phrases second

Vietnamese travelers searching for travel English rarely lack vocabulary. What they dread is the moment an airport officer or receptionist asks something and they don't know how to answer.

Instead of memorizing loose phrases, practice pairs: the question people usually ask and a natural reply. Focus only on airports, hotels, restaurants, directions and shopping, and say them aloud for ten minutes a day in the week before your trip. Listen with the device voice and slow it down to train your ear first.

On a trip you mostly answer rather than start conversations: "How long are you staying?" at immigration, "Do you have a reservation?" at the hotel, "For here or to go?" at a café. Memorizing "I'd like to check in" from a phrasebook isn't enough, because if you can't answer the next question the conversation stops. Practicing one exchange at a time, question and reply on the same card, is the closest to real life.

Don't take on too much. For a first trip abroad, five situations are enough: airport, hotel, restaurant, directions and shopping. Day one, airport and hotel; day two, restaurants; day three, directions and shopping; from day four, repeat what still trips you up. Ten minutes at a time, listen to the other person's line and say your reply aloud. After a week, when you hear the same question abroad, you respond before your head starts translating.

If you worry about listening, slow it down, listen a few times, then return to normal speed. The same works for Japan, Korea or Thailand: practicing just how to reply with "thank you" or "this one, please" in the local language makes people warm up to you right away.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether questions and replies come in pairs, not loose phrases
  • Whether you can practice by situation: airport, hotel, restaurant
  • Whether you can hear pronunciation and slow it down
  • Whether it has your destination's language, not just English
  • Whether there's a free part to try before the trip
  • Whether it works without an account or ads

The steps that actually work

  1. Choose your destination's language and open the Traveler topic at Beginner level.
  2. Start with airport and hotel, saying each question-and-reply pair aloud.
  3. Slow down any line you can't catch, listen three times, then go back to normal speed.
  4. From day two add restaurants, directions and shopping, ten minutes a day.
  5. The day before you fly, practice only the cards you still can't answer.
