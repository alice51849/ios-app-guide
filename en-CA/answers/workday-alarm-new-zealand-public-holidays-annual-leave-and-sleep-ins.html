A workday alarm is one alarm that knows your calendar: it rings on work days, skips public holidays for your country and your own days off, and still rings on the first work day back. Choose your country or region, confirm in the calendar which public holidays it will skip, add annual leave as days off, and check the next seven days before bed.

People searching for a workday alarm in New Zealand usually have the same problem: the alarm repeats Monday to Friday, but the year does not. Waitangi Day, Anzac Day, Matariki and the Christmas and New Year break fall on different weekdays each year, and every one of them means remembering to turn the alarm off the night before and back on the night after.

EasyAM skips public holidays for your country or region automatically, using built-in holiday data for 67 countries and regions that works offline and can be updated. Add annual leave, a trip or a sick day as a single day or a date range and those mornings stay quiet; the first work day back rings as usual. The app opens on your next seven days, so you can see which mornings will ring and when.

It uses real iOS system alarms and only shows Alarms Ready after iOS has confirmed the schedule. If holiday data is missing or out of date, that day counts as a work day, so the alarm rings rather than leaving you asleep. Check the Calendar tab month view: if a holiday you observe, such as your regional anniversary day, is not listed, add it yourself as a day off.

What to check before you choose

  • Does it skip public holidays for your country or region without you editing anything?
  • Can you add annual leave or a trip as a date range, not one day at a time?
  • Does it still ring on the first work day after a long weekend?
  • Can you see the coming week's mornings at a glance before bed?
  • What happens if the holiday data is missing: does it ring or stay silent?

The steps that actually work

  1. Choose your country or region, then open the Calendar tab to see which public holidays it will skip.
  2. Create one alarm with a weekly repeat for your work days and your usual wake-up time.
  3. Add your annual leave, and any holiday you observe that the calendar does not list, as days off.
  4. Give an early shift or a flight day its own ring time; the other days stay the same.
  5. Before bed, check the next seven days and tap Sleep in if you only want to silence tomorrow.

FAQ

Will it ring on Waitangi Day or Matariki?

Public holidays for the country or region you choose are skipped automatically. Check the Calendar tab month view to confirm which days are listed, and add any that are missing as days off.

What if I just want one extra hour tomorrow?

From the day before, tap Sleep in to silence only the next ring; your routine stays as it is.

Does it need the internet?

Holiday data is built in and works offline. The app only goes online to download public holiday data updates.

Is it a real alarm?

Yes. EasyAM schedules real iOS system alarms and shows Alarms Ready only after iOS has confirmed them.
