What is UsageNow: AI Usage Tracker?

UsageNow: AI Usage Tracker is an iOS app. Claude, Codex & Copilot Limits

Hit your AI limit halfway through a task? UsageNow shows how much of each AI plan you have left and exactly when it resets, right on your Home Screen, and warns you before you run out. Built for developers who code with AI agents every day.

EVERY LIMIT AT A GLANCE

Claude: the 5-hour session and weekly limits, including per-model weekly caps

ChatGPT Codex: the 5-hour and weekly windows

GitHub Copilot: premium requests, chat and code completions

Gemini: daily request quotas for each model

A live countdown to every reset, plus the exact reset time in your local format

ALERTS BEFORE YOU HIT THE WALL

Make your own alerts: choose the 5-hour limit, the weekly limit or all limits, then type the percentage that matters to you, like 90 or 98. Add as many as you like.

Each alert comes once per limit and cycle, so your lock screen stays quiet. Turn on a reset alert to know the moment a limit is back.

WIDGETS THAT DO MORE

Flip through all of your accounts right on the widget

Tap a widget to refresh it whenever you want the latest numbers

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in every size, plus Apple Watch complications

ALL YOUR ACCOUNTS, IN YOUR ORDER

Work and personal, Pro and Max, side by side. Sort by 5-hour reset, weekly reset, highest or lowest usage, name or service, or drag accounts into your own order.

EVERYWHERE YOU WORK

iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

You sign in on each service's own login page. Your sign-in stays in your device's Keychain, and UsageNow talks only to the services themselves. There's no UsageNow server, no account to create and no tracking.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE

Fully translated into 50 languages, with dates and times written the way your region writes them. Pick a language in Settings anytime.

TRY EVERYTHING FIRST

Start a free 24-hour trial in the app and use every feature, widgets included; nothing is charged. Before and after the trial, one account's current usage is free in the app, and UsageNow Pro unlocks more accounts, widgets, Apple Watch and alerts with a single one-time purchase. No subscription.
