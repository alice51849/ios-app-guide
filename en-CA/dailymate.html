What is DailyMate: Practical Phrases?

DailyMate: Practical Phrases is an iOS app. Useful Sentences for Real Life

Learn language through complete sentences that belong in real life, not through isolated vocabulary. DailyMate gathers 8,400 practical phrases in 47 learning languages, organized into 14 everyday topics and 84 situations. One purchase gives you the complete app. No subscription.

FULL PHRASES, USEFUL CONTEXT

Move through language you can use across everyday plans, travel and work.

• 8,400 complete, practical phrases

• 47 learning languages

• 14 topics and 84 real-life situations

• Per topic: 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases

• Listening with matching system voices

• Saved phrases and separate progress for each learning language

THREE LEVELS FOR EVERY TOPIC

Begin with essential wording, grow into more detailed intermediate phrases, then work through precise advanced expressions. Every level contains 200 phrases within each topic.

LISTEN AND SAVE WHAT MATTERS

Hear full phrases with a system voice matched to the language. Save useful lines and return to that language’s progress.

FROM HOME SCREEN TO APPLE WATCH

The interactive Home Screen Widget lets you view, advance or save a phrase. Apple Watch keeps phrases, system-voice listening, saved items and progress close at hand.

ONE PURCHASE. NO SUBSCRIPTION.

DailyMate is a paid app. Buy it once for the complete experience on iPhone, iPad, the interactive Widget and Apple Watch. No subscription.
