What is DailyMate Lite: Speak Today?

DailyMate Lite: Speak Today is an iOS app. 50 Languages, Real Dialogues

Be ready when it is your turn to speak. DailyMate Lite turns everyday situations into short cue-and-reply practice in 50 languages. Start free with the Travel topic at Beginner level, then unlock the full library with one purchase. No subscription.

PRACTISE COMPLETE EXCHANGES

Each card keeps a useful cue with a natural reply, so you learn what to say and how the conversation can continue—not just an isolated word.

• 8,400 original cue-and-reply pairs

• 50 learning languages

• 14 topics and 84 real-life situations

• Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced levels

• Pronunciation with matching on-device system voices

• Separate progress, streaks, and saved phrases for every language

START FREE

Choose any learning language and practise the complete Travel topic at Beginner level for free. Other topics and levels stay visible through genuine previews.

ONE-TIME LIFETIME UNLOCK

One purchase unlocks every topic, all three levels, the complete 8,400-pair library, unlimited saved phrases, interactive widgets, Apple Watch and App Intents. No subscription.

BUILT FOR EVERY SCREEN

Enjoy a native, adaptive experience on iPhone and iPad, keep the next cue on your Home Screen and continue in the dedicated Apple Watch app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, third-party ads, analytics, or tracking. Core learning data stays on your device, and pronunciation uses installed system voices.

LEARN AT YOUR PACE

Practise before travelling, between classes or meetings, while waiting for coffee, or whenever a real conversation is close. Switch language, topic, or level without losing your place.
