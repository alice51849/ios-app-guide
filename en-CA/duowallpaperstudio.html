What is Foldable Wallpaper Studio?

Foldable Wallpaper Studio is an iOS app. Cute Aesthetic Foldable Pairs

One wallpaper rarely fits both screens of a foldable, so Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes pairs: a close-up for the tall cover screen and the full panorama for the wide inner screen, cut from the same scene. Every pair is designed for iPhone Duo and saved at exact pixel sizes, so each image fits its screen exactly.

Why you will love it

A close-up outside and the whole view inside, from one scene

300+ original pairs in many styles

See each pair closed and open before you save

Recolour with swatches and add a short line of text, with both previews updating instantly

Turn your own photo into a pair and drag to set the focal point

Widgets that take their colours from your wallpaper

No ads and no account

When to use it

Use it when you set up a new foldable and want both screens to feel like one design, when the season changes and you want a new mood, when a favourite travel photo deserves both screens with the subject exactly where you want it, or when your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets should match the wallpaper you just saved.

What is inside

Many styles: Japanese-style illustrated scenery: summer clouds, seaside towns and starry skies; warm lantern lanes and stone paths; golden autumn and snowy seasonal moments; fantasy realms with floating islands and glowing light; cute clay and 3D; rainy windows and city lights at night; natural wonders and auroras; classic art styles: oil painting, woodblock print and watercolour; flowers and blossoms; calm minimal textures and solid colours; pure black glow for OLED; vivid satin, foil and metal textures; macaron pastel colours.

Closed and open preview that works on any iPhone or iPad.

Colour swatches and one short line of text.

A Create tab for pairs from your own photos. You pick images with the system photo picker, so the app never reads your whole library.

Exact pixel export in HD: 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen, saved as two images to Photos with add-only access. On other models, iOS scales the images.

Five widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen: My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card, all drawn with the wallpaper you choose. Battery shows only this device's charge level and charging state.

A short guide in the Settings tab for setting the cover and inner screens. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so the last step takes a few taps in Settings.

Privacy and purchases

No ads, no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The only network connection is for in-app purchases.

You can try every feature, and 5 pairs are ready to use. Images saved from those pairs and from your own photos carry a small watermark. Duo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks all the other launch pairs, removes the watermark and adds extra widget styles, such as more clock and battery designs and the colours of the Duo Pro pairs.
