What is JellyDay: Visual Daily Planner?

JellyDay: Visual Daily Planner is an iOS app. Cute Routine & Time Blocking

See your day as a timeline: every task in JellyDay is a soft pastel block, and the taller the block, the longer it takes. A 'now' line shows where you are in your day, so what you're doing now, what's next and how much time is left are clear at a glance.

Running late? Push the rest of today back from a block's menu, then let a gentle countdown walk you through each block or routine, step by step.

HOW JELLYDAY WORKS

Taller block, longer task: see how much of today is already taken before you add more.

Push a task back 15 minutes and every unfinished task after it moves too; finished ones stay put.

Left a few tasks undone? Start them all from now or move them all to tomorrow with one tap.

Add a morning or bedtime routine with one tap, then go through it step by step, each step with its own countdown.

A shrinking pie shows the minutes left, and you can pause or finish from the Live Activity without opening the app.

See what's now and what's next on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Block out focus time for work or study, keep a work-from-home day on track, and end it with your bedtime routine.

YOUR DAY ON A TIMELINE

Every task is a pastel jelly block, and a 'now' line marks the current moment.

Add as many tasks as you like.

Give each task a name, a sticker, a start time, a length from 5 minutes to 2 hours and a colour.

Not sure when yet? Keep it in your inbox and schedule it later.

Long-press a block and drag it to a new time, in 5-minute steps.

From a block's menu, push it and every unfinished task after it back 15 minutes, or move it to tomorrow.

When tasks run past their time, a short summary lets you start them all from now or move them all to tomorrow.

Bring yesterday's routine over to today in one tap.

READY-MADE ROUTINES

Add a whole routine, such as morning or bedtime, to today in one tap.

Routines walk you through one step at a time, with a countdown for each step.

FOCUS COUNTDOWN

A shrinking pie shows how much time is left, and a short sentence tells you the minutes remaining.

Pause, resume or finish whenever you like.

Live Activity and Dynamic Island keep the countdown in view while you focus, with buttons to pause or finish.

Get a reminder when a task starts and when it's almost over. You can turn reminders off in Settings.

On iOS 26 or later, an alarm lets you know when a focus countdown you started ends.

WIDGETS

The Now widget for your Home Screen, in small and medium sizes, shows what you're doing and what's next.

Lock Screen widgets in circular, rectangular and inline styles show your countdown and your next task.

The Apple Watch app works with your iPhone to show what's now, what's next and the countdown, and lets you start, pause and mark a task done. It also offers watch face complications.

LITTLE JOYS

Cute jelly characters and stickers for your tasks.

Available in 50 languages, following your device language. Works on iPhone and iPad.

PRIVACY

No account and no ads. JellyDay doesn't track you or collect your data, and your plans are stored on your device.
