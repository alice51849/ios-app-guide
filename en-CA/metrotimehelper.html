What is Metro Time Helper: Taipei MRT?

Metro Time Helper: Taipei MRT is an iOS app. When to Leave for the Subway

Built for the Taipei MRT in Taiwan: tell Metro Time Helper when you need to be there, and it shows you the latest time to walk into the station.

Its standout feature is 50 languages, so it works just as well for locals in Taiwan on their daily commute as for travellers from around the world riding the Taipei MRT for the first time, each in a language they know. Use it on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Most transit apps answer one question: when will I get there? Metro Time Helper answers the one you really have before a meeting, a dinner reservation or the last train of the night: when do I need to leave? It works backward from your arrival time, transfers included, so you are never left guessing.

Three modes in one app

Switch anytime with the glass button in the top left corner.

Everyday MRT

Choose your starting station, your destination and the time you need to arrive. You get the latest time to enter the station, a step-by-step timeline for each leg and transfer, first and last train warnings, and a reminder when it is time to head out. Leaving right now? Switch to Leave now and see when you will arrive. Tap any station on the built-in zoomable MRT map to set it as your start or destination, and your route is drawn right on the map. Pick Standard estimates (half the time between trains) or Cautious estimates (the full time between trains), and adjust your station entry buffer.

Xiaobitan branch

Qizhang to Xiaobitan on the official fixed schedule: a countdown to the next train, complete schedules for today, weekdays and weekends, departure reminders 1 to 30 minutes ahead, and the official schedule PDF. Trains on this branch come only about every 12 to 16 minutes, and most transit apps overlook it. Everyday trip planning also uses the same official schedule whenever your route includes this branch. Meihe City (美河市) in Xindian sits right next to Xiaobitan Station, so with a departure reminder set, residents do not have to wait around on the platform.

Beitou to Xinbeitou branch

The short line to the Xinbeitou hot spring area, with the same countdown, complete schedules, reminders and official PDF.

Widget and Live Activities

One smart Metro Time widget follows the app's current mode, has a button to switch modes and shows when it last updated. Once you set Remind me, a Live Activity counts down to your time to head out or your next departure on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (iPhone only).

Apple Watch

A standalone Watch app with all three modes, station selection, countdowns and watch face complications.

Lines covered

Wenhu, Tamsui-Xinyi (including the Xinbeitou branch), Songshan-Xindian (including the Xiaobitan branch), Zhonghe-Xinlu, Bannan, phase one of the Circular line, and the New Taipei Metro Sanying line (Dingpu to Yingge, with transfers to the Bannan line at Dingpu). The Airport MRT and light rail are not included. The official Taipei MRT network map is also in the app, as a zoomable vector map in Chinese or English (needs a connection).

Made for iPhone and iPad

A polished native Liquid Glass design, 50 languages you can switch inside the app, and colour themes to make it yours.

Private by design

No sign-up, no personal data collection, no ads and no third-party analytics. Everything is calculated on your device and notifications are scheduled locally.

Buy once, everything included

Every feature is available from the very first launch. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and nothing renews automatically.

Good to know

Times are estimates based on official published train frequencies and travel times between stations. They are not real-time arrivals.

Metro Time Helper is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Taipei Rapid Transit Corporation. It uses publicly available official data such as timetables, travel times and train frequencies.
