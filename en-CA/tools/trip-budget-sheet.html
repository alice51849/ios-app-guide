Formula and limits

Left to spend = total budget − fixed costs. Per day = left to spend ÷ days. Per person per day = per day ÷ travelers. Each category = per day × its share. Amounts are added in whole cents; daily figures are only rounded when shown.

Fixed costs = 1750.50
Left to spend = 3000.00 − 1750.50 = 1249.50
Per day = 1249.50 ÷ 5 = 249.90
Per person per day = 249.90 ÷ 2 = 124.95
Food = 249.90 × 40% = 99.96
Transport = 249.90 × 20% = 49.98
Tickets = 249.90 × 25% = 62.48
Shopping = 249.90 × 15% = 37.49

Amounts from 0 to 100,000,000 with at most two decimals, 1 to 120 days, 1 to 20 travelers, up to 15 fixed costs, four shares that add up to exactly 100. Same currency throughout, no exchange rates, no price or booking advice.

  • Total budget / Amount: 0 ≤ x ≤ 100,000,000
  • Days: 1 ≤ d ≤ 120
  • Travelers: 1 ≤ p ≤ 20
  • Share: Σ = 100%
  • Fixed costs: 0 ≤ n ≤ 15
