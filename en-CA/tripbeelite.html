What is TripBee Lite: Trip Planner?

TripBee Lite: Trip Planner is an iOS app. One Journey, Simply Organized

Focus on the journey ahead instead of managing an archive. TripBee Lite is a calm, polished planner for one active trip.

Build a clear day-by-day timeline, collect flights and stays, and keep notes, reminders and ticket references ready when you need them.

The free app stores one journey at a time. Change it freely or replace it when your next trip takes centre stage.

One non-consumable Premium purchase unlocks unlimited saved journeys, sharing, complete backup and restore, and the smart packing list for the lifetime of the app. No subscription.

No account is required. There are no ads, analytics or tracking; your plans remain yours.
