What is Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages?

Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages is an iOS app. Vocabulary Flashcards & Widget

Language learning should work across a busy day. Wordmate brings 44 languages, 14 practical topics and three levels to your pocket and your wrist, with an example sentence for every English word and spoken pronunciation. Learn from the interactive Home Screen widget or the watch app. No subscription, no in-app purchases.

ONE APP STORE PURCHASE. EVERYTHING INCLUDED.

Wordmate is a paid download. Purchase it once on the App Store and all 44 languages, every learning level and every feature are included from day one, including the interactive widget and Apple Watch experience. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid upgrades.

LEARN USEFUL WORDS IN CONTEXT

Move through 14 practical themes and 84 focused units. Natural example sentences show how words fit into everyday language, while text-to-speech (TTS) on iPhone and iPad lets you hear pronunciation in the language you are studying.

KEEP EACH LANGUAGE ON TRACK

• Set your interface and learning language separately

• Keep independent progress for every language you study

• Organize important words in your favourites

• Build consistency with progress tracking and a learning streak

• View a word, advance or save it from the interactive Home Screen widget

• Learn on Apple Watch, hear the system voice, save favourites and check your progress

Practise on your commute, between classes, during a coffee run or before a weekend away. Choose a theme, complete a unit and resume exactly where you stopped.

PRIVACY FROM THE START

No account is required. There are no third-party ads and no tracking or analytics. Your primary learning data remains on your device.
