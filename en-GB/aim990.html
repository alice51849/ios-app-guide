What is Aim990: English Revision Coach?

Aim990: English Revision Coach is an iOS app. TOEIC L&R weak spot repair

Aim990 is a diagnostic English coach for TOEIC Listening & Reading-style revision. It is not a question dump: every session you open is prescribed by your own answer evidence.

HOW AIM990 WORKS

1. 8-minute diagnosis — around 20 grammar, listening and reading tasks estimate your practice band and pinpoint your three weakest skills.

2. Personal prescription — those skills become a written plan: what to repair first, why it is costing you marks, and how long it should take.

3. Focused practice — every day you practise one prescribed skill in three phases: diagnose, repair, verify. No endless random sets.

4. Day 30 recheck — the same skills are measured again, so you can compare Day 1 evidence with Day 30 evidence and see what has genuinely improved.

INSIDE YOUR COACH

- Evidence ledger recording accuracy and pace for every skill

- Weakness radar with your top three repair targets

- Grammar speed drills with 15-second clues

- Reading time trainer for long passages

- Listening dictation and shadowing with on-device speech

- Spaced-repetition vocabulary with meanings in your language

- Practice mock with an estimated practice band

- Panic Mode for the final 1, 3, 7 or 14 days

- Apple Watch companion for quick reviews

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE

No account, no login, no adverts, no tracking. Your evidence ledger stays on your device and daily practice works offline.
