Help with Aim990 Plus

Use these answers to sort out language choices, speech, mistake review, offline use and locally saved progress.

Help topics

Where can I change the display language?

Go to Profile > Study > Language and select your preferred language.

What should I do if the English voice is silent?

The app uses the iOS en-US system voice. Raise the media volume first. If that does not help, open Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English, install or choose a voice, and then reopen Aim990 Plus.

How can I practise questions I got wrong?

Visit the Replay tab, or select Mistake Replay on Today, to work through your incorrect answers again.

Which parts are available offline?

The core learning content and progress tracking work offline. Your study information is kept on the device.

What happens when I reset my progress?

Choosing Profile > Reset progress permanently erases answers, mistakes, statistics and daily progress. The app itself stays installed.

Can I download the app again after deleting it?

Yes. As this is a paid App Store download rather than an in-app purchase, there is no restore button. Sign in with the same Apple Account and re-download Aim990 Plus from your purchased apps.

Ask for help

Write to hourstag.app@gmail.com with your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the app language you use and a clear account of the problem.
