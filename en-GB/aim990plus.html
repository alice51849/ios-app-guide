Build a dependable routine under time pressure

Aim990 Plus is an independent listening and reading trainer for committed adult learners. Each session follows a clear loop: start with an 8-question mixed Warm-up, tackle a 20-question Pressure Set in 15 minutes, then return to errors through Mistake Replay.

Original material across every part

The app contains 630 original questions, organised as 90 items in each of Parts 1–7, including 90 different photographs. The material was created for this app and is not described as official exam content.

Revise with purpose

Select mini, half or full mock formats for progressively longer sessions. Dictation helps you notice missed detail, while speed drills develop quicker decisions against the clock. Device speech adds flexible audio work, and a collection of 150 vocabulary entries supports regular language review.

Know what to revisit

Weakness analytics reveal areas that need more attention without making score promises. Progress and profile views give you a useful record of completed work. Mistake Replay then turns that insight into action by bringing missed questions back for another attempt.

Private, complete and offline

Aim990 Plus works offline on iPhone and iPad. It has no login, server, adverts, tracking or analytics. A single paid download includes the entire experience, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paywalls.
