FAQ

Can I see all projects together?

Yes. Cross-project tag analysis totals income, expenses and net by tag.

Is bank sync included?

No. MoneyTag: Income & Expense is manual and on-device by design, which keeps it private and offline.

Does MoneyTag work offline, and when does it use the network?

Ledger data stays on the device, and saved or manual exchange rates work offline. Automatic rate updates contact Frankfurter or ExchangeRate-API; their Cloudflare infrastructure may process connection, usage and diagnostic data for functionality and analytics, as disclosed in the app's privacy information.

MoneyTag

Projects, transactions, amounts, notes, tags, categories and settings stay in MoneyTag’s on-device storage and are not sent to Frankfurter / ExchangeRate-API. With iCloud Sync on, one copy also lives in your own iCloud. When automatic rates are on, MoneyTag sends the three-letter base-currency code and the necessary network request to api.frankfurter.dev / open.er-api.com. Frankfurter / ExchangeRate-API’s Cloudflare infrastructure may process the IP address, approximate region, interaction data and diagnostics. This processing is for app functionality and analytics, may be linked to you, is not used for tracking, and involves no advertising. Turn off automatic rates and use a manual rate to avoid these requests. Reference rates are provided by Frankfurter or ExchangeRate-API; for informational use.
