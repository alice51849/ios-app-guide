An ATS resume: what the system reads when you upload a file

Not every application goes through an ATS. The difference is whether you fill in fields on a platform or upload your resume as a file.

When you upload a resume file, the employer's applicant tracking system breaks it into text before a person sees it. A single column, standard headings such as experience, education and skills, one date format and a PDF with selectable text decide whether your experience and keywords are read correctly.

First, tell the application types apart. An online resume filled in field by field on a job bank is already structured data, so layout does not affect how it is read. The case to worry about is uploading a file: foreign companies, tech firms and corporate careers pages often ask for a PDF or Word file that a system then parses.

The most common problem is a two-column design. The parser reads top to bottom and left to right, so a sidebar with languages and skills gets mixed into your work history. The second is creative headings, such as calling work experience 'my journey', which the system may not recognise. The third is a PDF made of images, which arrives as a file with no text.

Before you apply, add the words from the job description that genuinely match your experience, and keep a separate copy for each job. CV Desk calculates a 0–100 ATS score with tappable fixes, exports a single-column PDF with selectable text or an editable Word file, and keeps one resume per job. The score is an on-device estimate; results vary by employer system.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether this application uses platform fields or a file upload
  • Whether the resume is one column with no sidebar
  • Whether headings use standard names such as experience, education and skills
  • Whether all dates use the same format
  • Whether the text in the PDF can be selected

The steps that actually work

  1. Check whether the application page asks you to fill in fields or upload a file.
  2. If uploading, switch to one column: date, title and company stacked top to bottom.
  3. Replace creative headings with standard ones.
  4. Add keywords from the job description only where you really did the work.
  5. In the exported PDF, select a line to confirm it is real text, not an image.

FAQ

Do I need an ATS resume for job banks too?

An online resume filled in on a platform is structured fields, so layout does not affect reading. An ATS-friendly layout matters most when you upload a PDF or Word file to a company's site.

Can an ATS resume include a photo?

Yes, but do not let it push you into a two-column layout. In CV Desk the photo is optional and off by default.

Where is my resume uploaded?

It is not uploaded. CV Desk keeps your resume on your iPhone, needs no account and works in airplane mode.
