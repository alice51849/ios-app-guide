Running a flip clock on iPhone or iPad all day: what to set first

A flip-style clock looks good and reads well from across the room. If it stays on all day, though, a few settings matter.

Choose whether the screen stays on only while charging or with a battery floor, and on OLED screens use gentle pixel shifting and a dim night face. That keeps the load on the screen and battery low.

iPhones and iPads normally turn the screen off after a while. To use one as a clock, it helps to have a rule such as staying on while charging, or returning to normal when the battery drops below a set level.

Showing the same digits for hours on an OLED iPhone raises burn-in concerns. Shifting the display slightly over time, softening details when idle and using a dim red face on black at night reduce the load, and the night face also helps with brightness in the bedroom.

What to check before you choose

  • Staying on only while charging, or with a battery floor
  • Burn-in care for OLED screens
  • A night face that is not glaring in the bedroom
  • Portrait, landscape and large iPad layouts
  • Account, ads and network use

The steps that actually work

  1. Choose the device and its spot, and check the charging cable reaches.
  2. Set Keep Awake to charging only, or always on with a battery floor.
  3. On an OLED iPhone, turn on Panel Care.
  4. In the bedroom, set the night schedule and the dim red face.
  5. Pick the date or calendar view for each face and check it reads from a distance.
