Household chore log: record only what you forget, and count from the day you did it

Nobody forgets the dishes. What gets forgotten are the jobs done a few times a year, until the AC starts to smell or the filtered water tastes off and you cannot remember when you last dealt with it.

A household chore log should only hold chores done weeks or months apart that are easy to forget, such as cleaning the AC filter, changing the water filter and washing sheets and curtains. Log the date the moment you finish, and set a rhythm so the next date counts from the day you actually did it, not the day you planned.

People looking for a chore log have often tried a notebook or notes app and quit within two weeks because they were recording everything. Daily chores do not need logging. The ones worth it are the long-gap jobs. In a hot climate the AC runs almost all year, so its filter clogs faster than you expect; in the rainy season curtains and sheets get damp quickly; and water filters have a working life of their own.

The key is logging when you finish, not reconstructing it later. In Mochi DoneStamp, tap once when a chore is done and it records the exact time, so you always see how long it has been. If you forgot to tap on the day, you can backdate, edit or delete any occurrence, and a wrong tap can be undone right away.

After a while, set a rhythm for the repeating ones. Rhythms can be flexible or exact, from minutes to years, and the next date always counts from the day you actually did it. When it is due, a local reminder arrives with Done and Snooze actions. Add an optional note or photo, such as the filter model, and use interval insights to see how many days apart you really do it.

What to check before you choose

  • Can you log a chore in one tap when you finish?
  • Can you add or edit a past date for the day you forgot?
  • Does the next date count from when you did it or from the plan?
  • Is there a Home or Lock Screen widget showing how long it has been?
  • Does the data stay on the device with no account needed?

The steps that actually work

  1. Pick 3–5 chores that are done far apart and easy to forget.
  2. Next time you finish one, tap to log it right away.
  3. If you remember roughly when you last did it, backdate that as a starting point.
  4. Set a rhythm for each chore and turn on reminders.
  5. Put a widget on the Home Screen to see which chores are coming due.

FAQ

Should I log every chore?

No. You remember the daily ones anyway. Logging only chores done weeks or months apart keeps the habit going much longer.

Why should the next date count from when I actually did it?

If you did it three days late, the next one should shift too; otherwise reminders come too early and get ignored.

I forgot to log it on the day. Can I fix that later?

Yes. In Mochi DoneStamp you can backdate, edit or delete any occurrence at any time.
