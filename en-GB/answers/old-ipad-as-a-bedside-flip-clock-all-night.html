A bedside clock from an old iPad or iPhone: what to set up

The device you no longer use has everything needed: a big screen, a charging port and a spot on the nightstand. The job is to stop it switching off and stop it dazzling you.

Plug the device in, pick a clock app that keeps the screen on while charging, switches to a dark face at night and protects OLED panels, then lower the brightness to the lowest level you can still read from the pillow.

The first obstacle is auto-lock: iOS turns the screen off after a few minutes, and setting it to Never in Display & Brightness keeps an unplugged old device awake until the battery dies in a few hours. Better to use a clock that understands charging: on while the cable is in, or above a battery floor you set.

The second point is the night itself. A white face at 3 a.m. wakes you, and on an OLED panel digits that never move all night eventually leave marks. Stay Clock: Flip Desk Clock turns your iPhone or iPad into an always-on clock: Keep Awake while charging with a battery floor or the system setting, a night schedule that switches to a dim red face on true black, and gentle pixel shifting to spare OLED screens. One complete face is included free, with no account, ads or tracking; an optional Lifetime purchase adds 100 more faces, with no subscription.

Finally, think about placement and sound. In landscape an iPad reads from across the bedroom; in portrait an iPhone fits a narrow shelf. The hourly chime can stay off at night, and a simple alarm saves opening another app in the morning. Wipe the screen before you set the device down: a fingerprint stands out on a black background.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether the screen stays on while charging without using the Never auto-lock setting
  • Whether there is a dark, red or low-light night face
  • Whether the OLED panel is protected by pixel shifting
  • Whether the app works without an account or ads
  • Whether the layout follows portrait and landscape on iPad

The steps that actually work

  1. Install the app on the unused device and plug it into its bedside charger.
  2. Turn on Keep Awake while charging, or set a battery floor.
  3. Schedule the night mode so the face turns dim red at bedtime.
  4. Lower the brightness to the level you can still read from the pillow, then check with the lights off.
  5. If the screen is OLED, leave pixel shifting on and choose a true-black background.

FAQ

Is it a problem to leave an iPad plugged in all night?

Devices manage charging themselves; what matters is a cable in good condition and not covering the device. A clock that follows charging also avoids draining the battery when you unplug it.

How do I keep the clock from marking an OLED screen?

Choose a true-black background, a low-light night face and an app that shifts the pixels slightly. Avoid fixed white elements all night long.

Does Stay Clock: Flip Desk Clock work on an old iPad in landscape?

The app has iPhone and iPad layouts for every screen size, in portrait and landscape, and the time stays readable from a distance. Check your model's compatibility on the App Store page; one complete face is included free.
