For 35×45 mm photos you want an app with the right template, guides for head and eyes, a background tool and a print sheet, bought once rather than by subscription. Some countries now take passport photos only digitally from approved sources, so check what your application accepts before you print.

Start with the application itself. Some authorities have moved to digital-only passport photos taken at the office or by an approved photographer; Germany did so for passports and ID cards in May 2025. Many other applications, such as visa applications at embassies and ID cards issued by universities, clubs or employers, still ask for a printed 35×45 mm photo. Always check the requirements of the office you apply to.

With the app, the payment model decides what it costs you over time. A subscription keeps running even if you need one photo a year, and you have to remember to cancel it. An app you buy once is still there when a child needs a new photo or the next trip needs a visa. Also check whether in-app purchases appear later and whether your photos are uploaded to a server.

Snapport is a one-time purchase: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking, no account. What to check before you choose

  • Whether your application takes a printed photo or a digitally submitted one
  • One-time purchase or subscription, and whether in-app purchases appear later
  • A 35×45 mm template with guides for head, eyes and chin
  • A 4×6 print sheet with cut guides so several photos fit on one print
  • Whether photos stay on the device or are uploaded

The steps that actually work

  1. Ask the office you are applying to whether a printed 35×45 mm photo is accepted.
  2. Stand in even light in front of a plain, light wall and have someone take the photo at eye level.
  3. Choose the 35×45 mm template and line up head, eyes and chin with the guides.
  4. Set the background to the required colour and check the result in the preview.
  5. Export a 4×6 print sheet, print it as a normal photo print and cut along the guides.
