Visible, satisfying to tick, and small enough to finish each day: a to-do list that does all three gets opened daily. The number of features matters far less.

The first condition is visibility. An app buried in a folder on page three gets forgotten, however good it is. Put today's list in a Home Screen widget and you see it every time you unlock your phone, which is several reminders a day without relying on memory.

The second is a finish that feels like something. Crossing out a line, tapping a circle, watching progress move: these small returns are why people come back. Looks count too. On a pastel Home Screen, a grey spreadsheet-style app sticks out, and a theme you like is a reason to keep the widget there.

The third is a small daily list. A list that only grows turns from a tool into a source of guilt. Keep four or five things you will really do today and park the rest on a second list for the weekend. Mochi is built around that idea: tap the little circle to check off, 101 themes, Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch; the free version has 2 lists of up to 4 tasks each.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether today's list can live in a Home Screen widget
  • Whether ticking something off gives clear feedback (a line, progress)
  • Whether you can change the look to one you like
  • Whether timed tasks can get a reminder
  • The free limits, and whether paying is one-time or a subscription

The steps that actually work

  1. Write down everything from sticky notes, notes apps and your head. Do not filter yet.
  2. Pick the 4 things you will really do today for a Today list; everything else goes on a second list.
  3. Put the Today list in a Home Screen widget and pick a theme that matches your wallpaper.
  4. Turn on reminders only for timed tasks: a deadline, a bill, a call.
  5. Each night, cross off what is done and decide what carries over; after a week, count the days you opened it.

FAQ

How many tasks should a day hold?

Start with four. Finishing four feels better than crossing four out of twenty, and it makes opening the list tomorrow easier.

Do I need reminders on everything?

No. Put reminders only on tasks with a time. Reminders on everything get ignored.

Is Mochi a subscription?

No. It is free to use; Premium is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.
