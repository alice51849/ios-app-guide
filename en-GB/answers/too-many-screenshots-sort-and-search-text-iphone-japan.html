What to check before you choose

  • Are screenshots sorted by content automatically?
  • Can you search the text inside images?
  • Can you jump from a screenshot to Maps, a link or Calendar?
  • Does it avoid deleting anything without your final confirmation?
  • Does reading stay on the device without uploads?

The steps that actually work

  1. Give ShotInbox access to your photo library and let it load your screenshots.
  2. Skim the categories it sorted them into: receipts, tickets, travel and so on.
  3. To find one, search a word or shop name that appeared in the screenshot.
  4. From the result, open the link, open it in Maps or add it to Calendar.
  5. Select screenshots you no longer need and check them in the Photos confirmation before deleting.
