What is DailyMate: Everyday Phrases?

DailyMate: Everyday Phrases is an iOS app. Learn Spanish, French, German

Know the words but still freeze mid-conversation? DailyMate teaches complete sentences and the natural replies that follow, so you learn what people really say rather than lists of single words. Choose from 8,400 practical phrases in 47 learning languages, organised into 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations, from greetings, food and shopping to work, health and travel. A Home Screen widget and a watch app keep one phrase in front of you all day. One purchase. No subscription.

PHRASES MADE FOR REAL SITUATIONS

Learn complete expressions you can recognise and use in daily life.

• 8,400 practical phrases

• 47 learning languages

• 14 topics and 84 real-life situations

• In each topic: 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases

• Listening through system voices matched to your chosen language

• Favourites and progress kept separately for each learning language

A CLEAR ROUTE THROUGH THREE LEVELS

Begin with everyday essentials, develop fuller intermediate expressions, then explore precise advanced language. Each topic contains a distinct set of 200 phrases at every level.

LISTEN AND KEEP THE USEFUL ONES

Hear each complete phrase through the appropriate system voice. Save the phrases worth revisiting and return to the progress for whichever language you are learning.

PRACTISE BEYOND THE APP

The interactive Home Screen Widget lets you view a phrase, move on or save it. Apple Watch keeps phrases, system-voice listening, favourites and progress close by.

ONE PURCHASE. NO SUBSCRIPTION.

DailyMate is a paid app. A single purchase includes the complete experience on iPhone, iPad, the interactive Widget and Apple Watch. No subscription.
