What is Meow Party: 200 Cat Mini Games?

Meow Party: 200 Cat Mini Games is an iOS app. 2 Player Fun for Foldables

Most games ignore the fold, but Meow Party makes it part of the game: 200 cute cat mini games, including 54 for two players face to face on one phone, all with no internet, no ads and no sign-up needed. Optimised for the iPhone Duo foldable screen, it is even more fun when you fold, and every game is fully playable on any iPhone too.

The crease becomes the centre line, the net or a mirror, with one side for each player

200 original mini games, 50 in each of four families

54 games for two players face to face on one phone, in versus or co-op

Most games work solo, and most of the two-player games include a cute computer opponent

Quick rounds of about 30 to 120 seconds, with gradually rising difficulty and levels in many games

Every player character is a cute cat from the Meow Squad, even in two-player games

Plays fully offline, with no ads and no account

Decide who makes the tea with a quick match across the kitchen table. Fill a few spare minutes on the train, in a queue or while the kettle boils. Set the phone between you and a friend, partner or flatmate, take a side each and settle it in one quick round. Playing alone? Unwind on the sofa with a cosy cooking or gardening game, or practise against the computer before your next rematch.

What's inside

Party: 50 lively party games made for quick laughs

Fold puzzles: 50 puzzles where folding and unfolding is how you solve them

Arcade and rhythm: 50 games of fast reactions, rhythm and one-thumb skill

Cosy creations: 50 gentle games of cooking, dress-up, gardening, simple management and wordless brain teasers

Two players: 54 games built for face-to-face play on one phone, and most games also work solo

On a foldable: depending on the game, close the phone to peek at a secret on the outer screen and open it to play, close it as your move, or unfold to zoom a tiny world out into a wide arena

On any iPhone: every fold move has an on-screen alternative, a press-and-hold close button or a two-finger pinch

A gradually rising difficulty, and many games have levels

Bunnies, bears, chicks, penguins, foxes, frogs, little mochi and more join in as friends, customers, rivals and props

Interface in 50 languages that follows your system language

Privacy and purchases

Meow Party works fully offline, needs no account and collects no data. Your progress is saved on your device, and the app does not use your camera, location, contacts or photos. No ads. No in-app purchases. All 200 games are included.
