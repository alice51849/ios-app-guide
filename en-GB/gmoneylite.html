What is G+Money Lite: Trip Spending?

G+Money Lite: Trip Spending is an iOS app. Travel Budget & Currency

Optimised for iPhone Duo. Built with the latest SDK, the app fills the entire inner display and automatically adjusts its layout whether the device is folded or open.

Know how much you can still spend today, wherever you travel. Log each purchase in the local currency, see it in your home currency and taken off today's budget. It turns red when you go over your daily limit.

No connection? Keep logging with your last saved rates.

Check today's spending on the Home Screen widget or your Apple Watch.

Convert

• The latest rates are fetched automatically when you open the app (about every 6 hours), or you can enter your own. Offline, your saved rates are used.

• Mid-market reference rate. Cards and cash may differ.

Spending

• Daily limit (optional): see each day's spending next to your limit.

• See where your money goes by category, keep each trip separate and filter your records.

• Backup & restore: Includes expenses, rates, categories and settings.

• Widget · Apple Watch

Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking. Rates use a public exchange-rate service.

Without a purchase: rates, budget and summary, 1 trip and 3 expenses.
