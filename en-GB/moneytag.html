What exactly is a project?

A project is a self-contained set of books: its own transactions, its own currency and its own bottom line. MoneyTag totals up the income, takes off the expenses and shows the net result for that project alone.

Does MoneyTag convert between currencies?

Each project has its own base currency. On the Home screen, choose the input currency directly. MoneyTag uses public exchange rates refreshed about every 6 hours, or you can set a manual rate and later reset to automatic. The converted amount is saved in the project’s base currency, and saved rates remain available offline. Reference rates are provided by Frankfurter or ExchangeRate-API; for informational use.

How do tags work?

Tags are central to MoneyTag, not an afterthought. Tag any income or expense, and the Tags screen totals that same tag across all of your projects at once — income, expenses and the net for the tag. Cross-project tag analysis is part of Lifetime Pro.

What do the Apple Watch app and the widgets show?

The Watch app lets you add a transaction from your wrist and see the current project's income, expenses and net. The widgets show that same position on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen. Projects, transactions, amounts, notes, tags, categories and settings stay in MoneyTag’s on-device storage and are not sent to Frankfurter / ExchangeRate-API. With iCloud Sync on, one copy also lives in your own iCloud.
