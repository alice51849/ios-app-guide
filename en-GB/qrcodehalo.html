What is QR Code Halo: Scan & Create?

QR Code Halo: Scan & Create is an iOS app. Inspect before you open

QR Code Halo puts inspection before interaction. Read the decoded structure, weigh visible evidence and only then choose what to do. Recognition alone never triggers an external action.

Local Payload Security Inspector

Examines the entire payload locally and lays out scheme, host, path, query and fragment. Its fixed rules produce Safe, Caution or Blocked findings, transparent reasons and an action disposition. It neither calls an online reputation service nor substitutes a vague numerical score for evidence.

QR Verification

Save a result as a local reference, then compare a later scan by its complete SHA-256 fingerprint, QR format, content type, URL components, security level and action disposition.

Batch Risk & Difference Audit

Sorts a batch into exact duplicate groups and host groups, then makes a bounded comparison of potentially confusable lookalike hosts. The overview, individual risk and full decoded content remain visible together, with unrestricted reading of the complete audit.

A local workflow for deliberate opening

What QR Code Halo does

Scan with the camera or recognise codes in photos, several codes in one frame

Safe preview for every result: full content, link scheme and host, and suspicious format hints

20 barcode formats: QR, Micro QR, Aztec, Data Matrix, PDF417, Micro PDF417, EAN-8, EAN-13, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 39, Code 93, Code 128, Interleaved 2 of 5, ITF-14, Codabar, GS1 DataBar and MSI Plessey

Batch scanning with duplicate suppression, zoom and torch

Create text, link, Wi-Fi, contact, email, phone, SMS, location and calendar event QR codes

Unlimited history with no item cap: favourites, search, filters and sorting by type, date, format, tag or category

Automatic on-device categorisation with manual override, and history export as CSV

Backup and restore preview the real counts, size and date range before you decide

Lifetime Pro: Unlimited scans, save a scanned batch to history and open its results, batch creation and batch CSV, commit a backup or a restore, high resolution PNG, PDF and SVG export, and custom Aurora styles

Privacy mode with automatic clearing, save to Photos, print, Share Extension and Siri Shortcuts
