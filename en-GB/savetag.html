What is SaveTag: AI Social Bookmarks?

SaveTag: AI Social Bookmarks is an iOS app. Save links, read them later

Save it now. Actually read it later.

You send yourself links all day. A recipe from a friend, a jacket you liked, a video you promised to watch, a thread you meant to reread. They scatter across chats, notes and screenshots, and you never see them again.

SaveTag gives all of them one home, and it does the filing for you.

Save from anywhere

• Share any link into SaveTag from any app with a share sheet

• Copy a link and quick-save it straight from the clipboard

• Title, site and preview are pulled in for you

Tags that write themselves

• Every save is sorted the moment it lands, on your device

• Precise topics, not one big pile: shopping, food, recipes, travel, places, learning, work, tech, finance, health, style, home, news, pets, parenting, gaming, design, sports, photography, music and inspiration

• Add your own custom tags when you want your own system

Search that understands what you meant

Look for the idea, not the exact words you typed. Search runs across titles, notes, tags and sources, so a vague memory is enough to find something again.

Rediscover, your daily ritual

Saving is easy. Coming back is the hard part. Rediscover surfaces a small handful of unread saves each day, with a goal ring and a streak, so your list finally shrinks instead of growing.

Built to stay on your iPhone

No account. No cloud. No adverts. No tracking. Your saves never leave your device. Tagging runs on Apple's on-device text intelligence, reading your saves locally to file them. It is not a chatbot and it never writes content for you.

Free and Pro

Free gives you every feature with your latest 5 saves. SaveTag Pro is a one-time purchase, unlocked for life: unlimited saves, custom tags, Markdown export and backup, and Family Sharing. No subscription, ever.

Also included

• Home Screen widget showing today's Rediscover

• Siri shortcuts to save the link in front of you

• Markdown export, so your saves are never locked in

• 50 languages

Made for people who save more than they read. Shoppers building a wishlist, cooks collecting recipes, travellers planning a trip, parents keeping advice, designers keeping references, and anyone whose saved tab has quietly become a black hole.
