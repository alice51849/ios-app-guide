Meeting notes to one-page outline

Turn messy meeting notes into a one-page outline: one headline, three to twelve key points, one next action and an optional metric, then download it as a sheet you can paste into a slide or a summary.

Key points

Optional: one number that proves the point, for example a rate or a count.

Formula and limits

The sheet keeps your words as they are: headline, then points in the order you typed them, then the next action, then the metric. It counts the points and trims surrounding spaces; nothing is rewritten, summarised or scored.

Headline ≤ 80 · Point × 3 ≤ 160 · Next action ≤ 160 · Metric ≤ 60
Section: Headline → Point 1…3 → Next action → Metric

Headline up to 80 characters, 3 to 12 points of up to 160 characters each, next action up to 160 characters, metric up to 60 characters. Every line must be single-line text. The sheet does not read files, recordings or calendars.

  • Headline: ≤ 80
  • Point: 3 ≤ n ≤ 12, ≤ 160
  • Next action: ≤ 160
  • Metric: ≤ 60
