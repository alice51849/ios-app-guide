Formula and limits

Profit = income total − expense total. Margin = profit ÷ income total. Net per hour = profit ÷ hours spent. Amounts are added in whole cents, so totals never drift.

Income total = 1500.00
Expense total = 400.39
Profit = 1500.00 − 400.39 = 1099.61
Margin = 1099.61 ÷ 1500.00 = 73.3%
Net per hour = 1099.61 ÷ 12.5 = 87.97

Amounts from 0 to 100,000,000 with at most two decimals, up to 20 income and 20 expense rows, hours from 0.25 to 2000 in quarter-hour steps. Same currency, no exchange rates, no tax or accounting advice.

  • Amount: 0 ≤ x ≤ 100,000,000
  • Hours spent: 0.25 ≤ h ≤ 2000
  • Income / Expense: 1 ≤ n ≤ 20
