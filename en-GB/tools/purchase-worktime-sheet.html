Formula and limits

Total = quantity × final unit price. Work hours = total ÷ hourly income. Workdays = work hours ÷ hours per workday.

1 × 120 + 2 × 45 + 3 × 30 = 300.00
300.00 ÷ 20 = 15.00
15.00 ÷ 8 ≈ 1.88

Include tax in your prices. No currency conversion, payroll calculation, savings prediction or buying advice. Display rounds to two decimals; totals use unrounded values.

  • Take-home income per hour: 0 < x ≤ 100000000
  • Final unit price: 0 ≤ x ≤ 100000000
  • Hours per workday: 0 < x ≤ 24
  • Quantity: 1 ≤ x ≤ 999
