Formula and limits

Running total = this item's minutes plus everything above it. Day = running total ÷ daily minutes, rounded up. Finished on = start date + day − 1. Spare minutes = days × daily minutes − total minutes.

1. Saved item 1: 12 ÷ 20 → Day 1 = 2026-09-07
2. Saved item 2: 47 ÷ 20 → Day 3 = 2026-09-09
3. Saved item 3: 55 ÷ 20 → Day 3 = 2026-09-09
4. Saved item 4: 95 ÷ 20 → Day 5 = 2026-09-11
Total minutes = 95
Days needed = 5 → 2026-09-11
Spare on last day = 5 × 20 − 95 = 5

The order you type is the order you read: nothing is reordered, skipped or merged. One session a day of exactly your budget, with no days off, no links opened or fetched, and no reading speed guessed for you.

  • Minutes per day: 5 ≤ m ≤ 600
  • Reading minutes: 1 ≤ m ≤ 600
  • Starting on: YYYY-MM-DD
  • Saved item: 1 ≤ n ≤ 30
