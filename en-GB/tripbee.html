What is TripBee Pro: Trip Planner?

TripBee Pro: Trip Planner is an iOS app. Group Travel & Packing List

Plan offline, sync privately with iCloud and invite others to edit a shared trip. One-time purchase, no ads and no tracking.

• Day-by-day itinerary with times, type icons, time zones, notes and reminders

• Live Day X of Y progress and a countdown before you fly

• Smart packing list by days, season and each family member (with children's ages) — plus a Beach kit

• One-tap Apple Maps directions from any starting point to any place

• Weather-aware planning with rainy-day backup ideas

• Share a gorgeous trip or day poster with friends and socials

• CSV import and full local Backup & Restore

Core planning works offline. With iCloud enabled, Apple CloudKit stores and syncs trips, notes, packing lists, photos, tickets and attachments in your private iCloud account. People you invite can read and edit a shared trip. Apple handles storage, sync and notifications; the developer has no travel-data server and cannot access this content.

Optimised for iPhone Duo.
