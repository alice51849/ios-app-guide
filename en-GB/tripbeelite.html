What is TripBee Lite: Travel Plan?

TripBee Lite: Travel Plan is an iOS app. One Journey, Neatly Organised

Keep the journey ahead beautifully organised. TripBee Lite is a calm, polished travel planner centred on the one active trip that matters now.

Arrange each day on a clear timeline, gather flight and hotel details, and keep notes, reminders and ticket references within easy reach.

The free app stores one journey at a time. Edit it freely or replace it when the next holiday begins.

One non-consumable Premium purchase unlocks unlimited saved journeys, sharing, full backup and restore, and the smart packing list for the lifetime of the app. No subscription.

No account is needed. There are no adverts, analytics or tracking; your plans remain under your control.
