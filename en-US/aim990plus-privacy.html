Aim990 Plus Privacy Policy

Effective July 19, 2026

Overview

Aim990 Plus has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or social SDKs. We do not collect or sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your goals, settings, answers, mistakes, statistics, mock-test activity, and progress are stored locally on your device. Aim990 Plus has no developer-operated server.

System speech

English audio uses an iOS system voice. Apple may manage voice assets under its own terms. Aim990 Plus does not send questions or progress to the developer.

Optional reminders

If you enable study reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Aim990 Plus does not use a push notification server.

Purchase

Aim990 Plus is a paid App Store download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple processes the payment; the app and developer cannot see your card number.

Deletion and retention

Reset progress deletes your local study progress. Deleting the app removes its local data according to iOS behavior. Your purchase history remains with Apple.

Adult education

Aim990 Plus is a general adult education app. It does not collect personal information from any user.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy, contact hourstag.app@gmail.com.
