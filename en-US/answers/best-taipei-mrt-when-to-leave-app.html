Short answer

Look for a planner that starts from the time you need to be there and works backward through every leg and transfer, so it can tell you the latest time to enter the station. A step-by-step timeline of each leg and transfer makes it easy to see where the minutes go, and a first and last train warning matters most late at night.

Check how the times are worked out. Estimates built from official headways and travel times between stations are not live arrivals, so a choice between a standard estimate and a more cautious one, plus an adjustable buffer for getting into the station, lets you decide how much margin you want. A reminder when it is time to go saves you from watching the clock.

Metro Time Helper is the app this guide covers: Built for the Taipei MRT in Taiwan: tell Metro Time Helper when you need to be there, and it tells you the latest time to walk into the station. What to look for before choosing

  • Planning backward from your arrival time, transfers included.
  • The latest time to enter the station and a timeline for every leg.
  • First and last train warnings and a reminder when it is time to go.
  • Standard or Cautious estimates and an adjustable station entry buffer.
  • A zoomable MRT map to pick the start and destination.

A practical decision process

  1. Choose your starting station and your destination, or tap them on the map.
  2. Enter the time you need to arrive.
  3. Read the latest time to enter the station and the timeline for each transfer.
  4. Pick Standard or Cautious estimates and set your station entry buffer.
  5. Tap Remind me so you get a notification when it is time to leave.

FAQ

Are these live train arrivals?

No. Times are estimates based on official published headways and travel times between stations.

Does it need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no ads and no personal data collection; everything is calculated on your device and notifications are scheduled locally.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every feature can be used for 24 hours from the moment you start, then a single one-time unlock keeps the full app; nothing renews automatically.
