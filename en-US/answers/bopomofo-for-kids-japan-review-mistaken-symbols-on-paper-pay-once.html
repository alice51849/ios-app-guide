If your child keeps forgetting bopomofo, stop repeating all 37 symbols; review only recently mistaken symbols in the app each day, and once or twice a week print just those symbols for tracing and a short test. For pairs with similar sounds, such as ㄓ and ㄗ, ㄕ and ㄙ, or ㄣ and ㄥ, add side-by-side listening practice.

With only 37 symbols, bopomofo is often treated as done once everything has been learned. In practice, pairs with similar sounds keep getting mixed up. The retroflex ㄓ, ㄔ, ㄕ against the tongue-tip ㄗ, ㄘ, ㄙ, and the finals ㄣ and ㄥ, are sounds Japanese lacks, so children growing up in Japan confuse them easily. Reviewing everything evenly spends time on known symbols and thins out practice on the weak ones.

In Lumi Bopomofo Pro, today's review automatically brings back symbols that have not stuck yet. From the parent page, you can also print learned or recently mistaken symbols as tracing-plus-test sheets. The point is combining on-screen listening and choosing with writing in stroke order on paper, using the same weak symbols.

The printed sheets also work as a check before a weekend Mandarin class or before seeing grandparents. Circle the symbols written correctly and put the mistaken ones on next week's sheet again; that way the parent never has to make up questions. The app is fully unlocked with one purchase, with no ads and no in-app purchases, so a child can use it alone safely.

The steps that actually work

  1. For about a week, do only the app's daily review for 5–10 minutes and watch which symbols your child gets wrong.
  2. Check the recently mistaken symbols on the parent page.
  3. Print a tracing-plus-test sheet with only those symbols and write them on paper once or twice a week.
  4. For similar-sounding pairs such as ㄓ and ㄗ, ㄕ and ㄙ, ㄣ and ㄥ, listen side by side before writing.
  5. Circle the symbols written correctly and include the mistaken ones in next week's sheet too.

Why does my child forget bopomofo after learning all of it?

Do the printed sheets need to be done every day?

No. A few minutes of the app's daily review, plus a sheet of weak symbols once or twice a week, is an easy pace to keep.
