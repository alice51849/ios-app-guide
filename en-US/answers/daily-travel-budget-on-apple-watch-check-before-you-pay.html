Split the trip budget into a daily limit, log each purchase right after paying, and keep today's budget on your watch or a widget so one glance is enough.

A whole-trip budget is hard to feel. On day two the big number still looks safe even if you have already overspent. A daily limit gives one small number that is easy to compare with the price in front of you.

The issue is timing. In a queue at the counter or a food stall, pulling out the phone, opening an app and finding a report feels too slow. That is why a budget on the wrist or the Home Screen gets checked more often.

G+Money Lite lets you set an optional daily limit and see how each day tracks against it. Purchases are logged in the local currency and converted to your home currency, with a widget and Apple Watch for quick checks. Everything stays on the device, with no account.

What to check before you choose

  • A daily limit you can set, not only a trip total
  • A glance view on Apple Watch or a widget, without opening the app
  • Logging in the local currency with the home-currency value
  • Logging that takes seconds right after paying
  • Account, ads and subscription

The steps that actually work

  1. Subtract tickets and lodging you already paid, then divide the rest by the number of days.
  2. Set that figure as the trip's daily limit.
  3. Add the widget to your Home Screen or check it on Apple Watch.
  4. Log each purchase right after you pay.
  5. If one day goes over, trim a little over the next two days rather than all at once.

FAQ

Do I have to set a daily limit?

No. The daily limit is optional; you can still log and see summaries without it.

Can I check without opening my phone?

G+Money Lite supports Apple Watch and a widget for quick checks.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Data stays on the device, with no account and no tracking. Only exchange rates are fetched from a public rate service.
