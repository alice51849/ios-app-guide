  • Conversation-focused listening practice for main idea, detail and speaker purpose
  • Dictation-style work to catch connected speech instead of guessing from keywords

FAQ

What is TOEIC Part 3?

Part 3 contains conversations followed by questions, so learners must catch context, details and intent while listening.

Is dictation useful for TOEIC listening?

Dictation can help train attention to connected speech and missed details, though improvement depends on consistent practice.

Does Aim990 work without internet?

Yes. Aim990 is described as offline-capable with progress kept privately on device.
