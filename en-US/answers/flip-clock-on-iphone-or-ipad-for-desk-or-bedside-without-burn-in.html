Flip clock on iPhone or iPad: the settings that actually matter

A full-screen flip clock on a charging phone looks great the first night. By the second, the screen has switched itself off, lights up the bedroom, or you start worrying about burn-in.

To use an iPhone or iPad as a flip clock, get three things right: the screen has to stay on (at least while charging, or Auto-Lock turns it off), at night you need a dark face, ideally dim red on pure black, and on an OLED screen you should avoid a completely static image for hours, using a slight pixel shift.

A flip clock borrows the split-flap look of old alarm clocks and station boards: big digits readable from across the room that flip over each minute. On iPhone, StandBy already shows a clock when the phone is charging, on its side and locked, but depending on the model the screen may turn off after a while. For a permanent display on a desk or shelf you need an app that keeps the screen on without switching off Auto-Lock for the whole phone.

In the bedroom, brightness matters more than style. A white face on a light background lights the room; a dim red face on pure black stays readable and disturbs far less. On OLED screens pure black really turns pixels off, but a motionless digit shown all night, every night, raises the risk of burn-in; a subtle pixel shift and softened details reduce it. Think about the battery too: a threshold above which the screen stays on avoids draining a phone that is not properly plugged in.

What to check before you choose

  • Can the screen stay on while charging without changing Auto-Lock?
  • Is there a dark night face, dim red on pure black?
  • Is there OLED protection such as a subtle pixel shift?
  • Are the digits readable from a distance in portrait and landscape?
  • Can you set 12- or 24-hour format, language and time zone?

The steps that actually work

  1. Pick the device: an iPhone charging on a dock or an old iPad on a shelf
  2. Set screen keep-on: while charging or above a battery threshold
  3. Choose a flip face readable from where you will sit or lie
  4. Schedule the switch to the dim red face on black for the night
  5. Add the date, an hourly chime or a simple alarm if you want them

FAQ

Is StandBy enough as a flip clock?

StandBy shows a clock while the iPhone is charging, on its side and locked, but depending on the model the screen may turn off after a while. For a permanent display or an iPad, a clock app that keeps the screen on is more reliable.

Does leaving the clock on all night damage an OLED screen?

A strictly static image for hours, night after night, raises the risk of burn-in. A pure black background, low brightness and a slight pixel shift reduce that risk a lot.

Can an old iPad be used as a flip clock?

Yes, it is a good use: plugged in on a shelf it shows the time in large digits. Stay Clock is built for iPhone and iPad.
