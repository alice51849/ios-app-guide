Short answer

Lumi Weather gives simple tips on the Today screen for when to pack an umbrella or raincoat, when a hat and sunscreen make sense, and when a light jacket will help against the wind. Where minute-by-minute precipitation data is available, it lets you know when rain is about to start or ease, and it gives a heads-up when rain looks likely tomorrow. The Get Ready checklist (part of the one-time unlock) turns the conditions into a short list of what to wear and what to bring, from sunglasses to rain boots, and you can share it in a tap.

Try Lumi Weather on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

FAQ

Is the Get Ready checklist free?

The Today screen and its tips work right away. The Get Ready checklist is part of the one-time in-app unlock, which also adds the hourly view, the 7-day forecast, multiple saved places and the hours ahead on Apple Watch. There is no subscription.

Will I get a reminder?

You can set a daily reminder for the time that suits your morning.

Does the rain alert work everywhere?

Minute-by-minute precipitation data is only available in some regions; where it is not, you still get the forecast and the umbrella tip.
