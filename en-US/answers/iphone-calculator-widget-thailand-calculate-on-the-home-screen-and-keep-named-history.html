A good calculator widget lets you tap a calculation in the Home Screen widget without opening the app, keeps the keys readable in every theme, and saves the result in history you can name and search, rather than being a shortcut that opens the app or shows only the last number.

Many calculator widgets are just shortcut buttons that open the app anyway; others only show the latest result. Before choosing, check whether you can really type numbers in the widget and whether what you calculate there ends up in history.

The second thing is readability. A pretty theme whose keys blend into the background makes mistakes easy on a small widget. Try the widget on the wallpaper you actually use before you decide.

CalDaily has a Home Screen widget that calculates without opening the app and follows the theme you chose in the app. Each result is saved with a name and a type, grouped by day, filtered by tool and exportable to CSV. Eight tools include tip and bill split, discount and tax, and trip fuel cost. One of the 100 built-in themes is free; a single one-time purchase unlocks the remaining themes and unlimited use of every advanced tool, with no subscription.

What to check before you choose

  • You can really calculate in the widget, not just open the app
  • Keys stay readable on the wallpaper you use
  • Results go into history you can name and search
  • Data stays on the device with no account
  • Clear pricing with no monthly fee

The steps that actually work

  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap add widget and choose CalDaily.
  2. Try a quick sum in the widget, such as dinner split three ways.
  3. Open the app and name the results you want to keep for later.
  4. Pick a theme whose keys read clearly on your wallpaper.
  5. Use the bill split or discount tool and set its defaults once.
