Shoot facing a bright window against a plain wall with the phone at eye level, then crop to the required size and head proportion (35×45 mm is common for many passports). Specs differ and change, so always check the issuing authority's current requirements before you apply.

Year-end departures bunch up between late December and early January. Some destinations require a minimum validity left on your passport, and by the time you notice, renewal may not be back in time. Processing takes days and offices get busy before the holidays, so having photos ready and applying in November takes the stress out. Check the processing time with your passport office.

The three usual problems with DIY photos are shadows, tilt and head size. Facing a window in daylight keeps shadows off your face, and a white or pale plain wall works as a background. Hold the phone at eye level a little way back; stepping back and cropping later distorts the face less than zooming in. Check that glasses glare or hair is not covering your eyes or eyebrows.

Snapport Lite handles the finishing. You can switch the background to white and lay several copies out on a 4×6 in, A4 or Letter sheet with cut guides. Photos are processed on the device, and acceptance is not guaranteed, so check the official requirements before submitting.

What to check before you choose

  • The photo size and head-size rules of the authority you apply to
  • How recently the photo must have been taken
  • A plain background with no shadows on the face or wall
  • Facing forward, with eyes, eyebrows and face outline not hidden by hair or glasses
  • Whether you submit on paper or online, which changes the file or print format

The steps that actually work

  1. Check your passport's expiry date and the validity your destination requires.
  2. In daylight, face a window, stand against a plain wall and take a few shots with the phone at eye level.
  3. In a cropping app, choose your authority's size and place head top, eyes and chin where the spec requires.
  4. Adjust the background if needed, lay copies out on a sheet with cut guides, export and print.
  5. Compare with the authority's current requirements before you take it to your application.

FAQ

Can I use a phone photo for a passport application?

Self-taken photos may be usable if they meet the size and shooting rules, but the issuing authority decides. Check its current requirements before you submit.

When should I apply to travel over the year-end holidays?

Processing takes time and offices get busy in December, so applying in November is safer. Ask your passport office for its current processing time.
