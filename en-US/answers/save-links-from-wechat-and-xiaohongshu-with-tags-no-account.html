Links disappear because they are scattered across each app's own favourites and were saved without tags. The fix is one collection that needs no account, tags written automatically on save, and a read-later rhythm that clears a few items a day. SaveTag: AI Social Bookmarks works exactly that way: one tap from the share sheet or clipboard, tags written on the device, no sign-up.

First, drop the habit of saving wherever you happen to be reading. WeChat articles, Xiaohongshu notes and browser pages each have their own favourites, and three months later you cannot remember which app holds that recipe. Change the rule: anything worth keeping, from any app, goes to the same place. In a WeChat article use the top-right menu and Copy Link, in a Xiaohongshu note use Copy Link from its share options, and in the browser use the share sheet directly.

Second, let the tags write themselves. Manual filing never survives a week, so choose a tool that files each save the moment it lands. SaveTag: AI Social Bookmarks takes a share from any app with a share sheet and also quick-saves straight from the clipboard after you copy a URL; title, source site and preview come in automatically, and each save is sorted on the device into precise topics such as shopping, recipes, travel, parenting and finance, which you can correct or extend with custom tags. When you look for something later, search runs across titles, notes, tags and sources, so a vague memory is enough.

Third, actually come back. A read-later pile turns into a black hole when the list has no exit. SaveTag's Rediscover surfaces a small handful of unread saves each day, with a goal ring and a streak, and you tick each one off as you read it, so the list shrinks instead of growing. Everything stays on the iPhone: no account, no cloud, no ads. The free tier has every feature and keeps your latest 5 saves; SaveTag Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited saves, custom tags and Markdown export, with no subscription.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether WeChat and Xiaohongshu links can come in via Copy Link or the share sheet
  • Whether tags are written on save or left for you to file by hand
  • Whether search finds things by rough meaning rather than exact title
  • Whether something brings unread saves back to you every day
  • Whether an account is required and whether your saves are uploaded

The steps that actually work

  1. WeChat article: open the top-right menu, choose Copy Link, then open the collection app and quick-save from the clipboard.
  2. Xiaohongshu note: tap share, choose Copy Link, and save from the clipboard the same way; title and preview fill in automatically.
  3. Browser page: tap the share button and pick the collection app from the share sheet; you return to the page as soon as it is saved.
  4. After saving, glance at the automatic tag, correct any that landed in the wrong topic, and add a custom tag if you want one.
  5. Each evening open Rediscover, read the handful it surfaces and tick them off, so the list genuinely gets shorter.

FAQ

Can WeChat articles be shared straight in?

WeChat articles usually offer Copy Link in the top-right menu; copy it, open SaveTag and quick-save from the clipboard. Apps with a normal share sheet can share in with one tap.

How accurate are the automatic tags in SaveTag: AI Social Bookmarks?

Tagging runs on Apple's on-device text intelligence, reading the save locally and filing it into precise topics such as shopping, recipes or travel; any save can be moved to another topic or given a custom tag.

What does keeping 5 saves on the free tier mean?

Free has every feature but keeps only your latest 5 saves. When you want a permanent collection, SaveTag Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited saves, with no subscription.
