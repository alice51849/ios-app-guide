After each service, tap once on your phone to record the date, adding a note or a photo of the receipt if useful. For items with a regular interval, such as engine or gear oil, set an approximate rhythm, and the next due time counts from the day you actually serviced it. Doing it early or late won't throw every later date off.

What people forget isn't that maintenance is due, it's the last date. Engine oil is often changed every 1,000 to 1,500 km or every two to three months, gear oil, air filters, brake pads and tires each have their own intervals, and then there's the car, AC filters, the water heater and water-filter cartridges. Memory can't hold it all. Treat each service as an event, record the exact time when it's done, and checking the history is faster than digging for receipts.

How do you set rhythms without drowning in alerts? Give rhythms only to items with a fixed interval, for example engine oil about every 2 months and the air filter about every 6 months, and just log the rest. The key is that the next time counts from when you actually finished: change the oil a week late and the next one counts from that day instead of piling up drift from the original plan. When a reminder arrives you can tap Done or Snooze without opening the app.

Several vehicles and appliances work the same way. Create separate events such as scooter oil, car service, AC filter and water-filter cartridge, each with its own history and interval insights showing how often you really do it. Attach a photo of the receipt or odometer (photos are compressed) and note the shop and mileage, so comparing prices or switching shops is easy.

What to check before you choose

  • Whether one tap records the service date, with backdating if you forget
  • Whether the next service counts from the actual service day
  • Whether you can attach photos and notes (shop, mileage)
  • Whether reminders offer Done and Snooze directly
  • Whether you can log from Lock Screen or Home Screen widgets
  • Whether it needs an account, shows ads or charges a subscription

The steps that actually work

  1. Create a scooter oil event and backdate the last oil change you remember.
  2. Set an approximate rhythm of about every 2 months (adjust to your shop's advice or mileage).
  3. After the next oil change, tap once at the shop and attach a photo of the receipt.
  4. When the reminder arrives, tap Done or Snooze.
  5. Add gear oil, air filter, car service or appliance filters as needed.

FAQ

How often should scooter oil be changed?

It depends on the model and how you ride; a common guideline is every 1,000 to 1,500 km or two to three months, but follow your shop or the manual. Set that interval as a rhythm and the next time is counted from the day you actually changed it.

Can I track several vehicles?

Yes, as separate events. The free version has one active event; for several vehicles or items, Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited events.

Will I lose my data?

Data stays on your device and needs no account; full backup and JSON export let you back up before switching phones.
