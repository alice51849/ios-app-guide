How to use an iPhone or iPad as a table clock: desk, shelf and bedside

The iPad in a drawer or the iPhone you replaced can work again as a table clock. Just propping it up is not enough, though: the screen goes dark, or glows too bright at night.

Decide where it will sit first, stand it in the orientation that lets the charging cable reach (landscape on a desk, portrait is fine on a shelf or nightstand), and keep the screen on while charging. At the bedside, switch to a dim display at night and choose a face with large digits you can read from across the room.

The first decision is where the clock will live. On a desk it sits a little below eye level, so a large landscape display reads best; on a shelf, full screen digits let you see it from anywhere in the room. At the bedside you look at it lying down, so a low stand that tilts the screen slightly upward helps.

Next come power and brightness. An iPhone or iPad turns its screen off when idle, so a clock needs a spot where it can stay plugged in. In a bedroom, a white screen feels brighter than you expect; showing the time in a quiet color on black at night keeps it out of the way with the lights off.

Stay Clock turns an iPhone or iPad into an always-on table clock. What to check before you choose

  • Full screen, large time in both portrait and landscape
  • A setting that keeps the screen on while charging
  • A dim night display that won't glare in the bedroom
  • Burn-in care if your iPhone has an OLED screen
  • Date or calendar display you can match to the spot

The steps that actually work

  1. Choose desk, shelf or bedside, and check that a charging cable reaches the outlet.
  2. Pick the orientation that reads best on the stand: landscape for a desk, portrait is fine on a shelf or nightstand.
  3. Set the screen to stay on while charging.
  4. At the bedside, schedule a dim display for night hours.
  5. Step back from the spot, check the digits and date are readable, and change the face if they are not.

FAQ

Can an old iPad work as a table clock?

If you have a spot where it can stand plugged in, yes. Check the App Store page to confirm the app supports the iPadOS version on your device.

Portrait or landscape?

On a desk, landscape shows bigger digits; on a shelf or nightstand with limited width, portrait works well too. Let the spot decide.

Can I use Stay Clock in the bedroom?

Yes. A night schedule switches to a dim red face on true black, the screen stays on while charging, and Panel Care shifts pixels gently on OLED iPhones.
