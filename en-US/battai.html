What is BattAI: Battery Health Score?

BattAI: Battery Health Score is an iOS app. Charge Alerts, Heat & Widget

Know how fast your battery is aging and when to replace it. See how long your charge may last, get the full-charge alert on time, and log every charge.

Optimized for iPhone Duo. Built with the latest SDK, the app fills the entire inner display and automatically adjusts its layout whether the device is folded or open.

Settings shows today's maximum capacity. BattAI tells you if it's good and how fast it's falling, and keeps every reading and charge for a battery passport when you sell or repair.

READ YOUR BATTERY IN SECONDS

• Screenshot Settings › Battery › Battery Health, pick it in BattAI, and the numbers are read on your iPhone. You check them before they are saved.

• Where Settings shows them, cycle count and battery dates are read too, and your battery age fills in. Or type the capacity in.

• BattAI can't read maximum capacity by itself. Every number shows its source and date.

AN INSTANT, PLAIN ANSWER

• Great shape, normal range, or below the 80% mark Apple uses for battery service.

• Aging slower than, about the same as, or faster than Apple's design target for your iPhone model. An estimate, not a factory test.

• One sentence on what to do next.

WHAT THE SETTINGS APP DOESN'T SHOW

• Time left: about how many hours until 20% at your recent pace, as a range (estimate).

• Battery care score out of 100, with the habit costing you the most points.

• Bedtime-to-morning drain: how much the battery dropped while you slept, and your recent average.

WHEN TO REPLACE IT

• Your capacity curve, with a line at 80%.

• When it may drop to 80%, as a season or range, never a made-up date. Too little data? It asks for another reading.

• A clear call: no need yet, keep an eye on it over 6–12 months, or worth considering a replacement.

• A monthly reminder to read it again.

CHARGE ALERTS THAT ARRIVE ON TIME

• Full-charge alert at the level you choose (50–100%), plus low battery and running-hot alerts.

• Set up one Shortcuts automation with the guide and the alert arrives the moment you reach your level. Without it, iOS may deliver it late.

EVERY CHARGE, RECORDED

• Add a second automation and every plug-in and unplug is logged, even with the app closed: levels, duration and a speed level (estimate).

• Weekly summary, named chargers and a charger report card.

IS THIS CHARGER FAST?

• Plug in, keep BattAI open a few minutes, and get a speed level and estimated power range.

• Only between 20% and 80% and when not hot. Apple doesn't let apps read charging power, so it's estimated from how fast the level rises.

• Save chargers and compare them side by side.

WIDGETS, COMPANIONS AND APPLE WATCH

• A plain battery widget, plus 10 battery companions whose faces change with your level.

• Medium, large and Lock Screen widgets, and StandBy.

• Apple Watch shows its own battery, plus your iPhone's summary with the unlock.

SHARE AND SELL

• A share card with your model, maximum capacity and aging speed.

• A one-page battery passport (document or long image): every reading with date and source, cycle count, curve, estimated month, battery age, habit score and charges logged. Estimates are labeled.

WITHOUT UNLOCKING, AND WHAT THE ONE-TIME UNLOCK ADDS

Without unlocking: live battery, the capacity reading and answer, time left, care score, bedtime-to-morning drain, all three alerts, one plain widget, the default companion, the basic share card and charger speed level. Data keeps collecting; you see the last 7 days and last 3 charges.

The full version is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, with no automatic charges: full history and curve, the 80% estimate, every charge in detail, estimated charger watts, comparison and report card, care details, all widgets and companions, passport export, the monthly reminder, older analysis pages and the Apple Watch iPhone summary.

PRIVATE AND HONEST

No account, no ads, no servers; your data stays on your iPhone. BattAI doesn't save battery, cool your phone or change the status bar icon. No AI analysis.
