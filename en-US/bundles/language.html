One way to use them together

  1. In Wordmate, pick a topic such as airport, hotel or asking directions and finish a unit.
  2. In DailyMate, open a related everyday topic such as travel and listen to the full phrases with the system voice for that language.
  3. Save the words and lines you want to keep. Both apps track progress separately for each language you study.
  4. On the bus or in a queue, carry on from the widget or your Apple Watch.
