Lumi Learning Bundle: four Lumi Pro apps, one purchase

If you were about to buy the Lumi Zhuyin, phonics, math and routines apps one at a time, the Lumi Learning Bundle gets you all four Pro apps in a single purchase.

Get the Lumi Learning Bundle on the App Store

Who this bundle is for

  • Parents teaching Mandarin Zhuyin (Bopomofo) at home, including Chinese heritage families.
  • Parents who want real phonics for a little learner, not just the ABC song.
  • Kids around 5 to 8 who like picture-based math questions read aloud to them.
  • Parents of children aged about three to seven who want brushing, meals and bedtime to feel less like a fight.

The four apps inside

Lumi Bopomofo Pro: Zhuyin

All 37 Bopomofo sounds through listen-and-match, stroke-order tracing, Bopomofo Fishing and the Star Race, plus blending and tone practice and printable tracing-and-quiz worksheets from the Parent Zone.

Full app overview

Lumi Letters Pro: Kids Phonics

Phonics across 14 planets and 12 question types: kids hear the beginning, middle and end sound of a word, match upper and lower case, trace every letter along stroke guides, and you can print a daily worksheet.

Full app overview

Lumi Math Pro: Kids Numbers

46 picture-based question types, every one read aloud, with weak-spot practice and a 20-question worksheet with an answer key that you can print or save as a PDF.

Full app overview

Lumi Mission Planet Pro

Five daily missions (meals, tooth brushing, sleep time, tidy-up and school prep) plus unlimited custom missions, with a parent dashboard that logs the time of every completion.

Full app overview

What all four have in common

  • No ads and no in-app purchases in any of the four.
  • Progress stays on your device.
  • Each app has a parents' area: Parent Zone in Bopomofo, Letters and Math, a parent dashboard in Mission Planet.

Questions parents ask

Is there an Android version of the bundle?

Google Play has no bundle, but each of the four Pro apps is on Google Play on its own. The links are below.

Where does my child's data go?

All four apps keep progress on the device. Lumi Mission Planet Pro also keeps the optional nickname on the phone.

Do I need all four?

Not necessarily. If you only want one subject, each app is still sold on its own. The bundle makes sense when you want two or more of them.
