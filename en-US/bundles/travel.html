Trip Planner, ID Photo, Budget: three apps for one trip abroad

Going abroad usually means three chores: getting passport or visa photos right, planning the days, and keeping track of money in another currency. This bundle hands each chore to one app.

Get the travel bundle on the App Store

Updated: 2026-11-19

Weeks before: the photo

Snapport: Passport & ID Photos has size templates for passport, visa and ID photos in many regions (US 2×2 and 35×45mm among them), face-alignment guides, background replacement, and print sheets for 4×6, A4 or Letter. Everything is processed on your iPhone. Full app overview

Snapport prepares the photo only. Photo rules differ by country, agency and application type, the agency you submit to decides whether a photo is accepted, and Snapport is not affiliated with any government. Check the requirements of the office you are applying to before you submit.

Before you fly: the plan

TripBee Pro: Trip Planner keeps one day-by-day itinerary that everyone you invite can edit, with boarding passes and booking codes attached to each stop, a smart packing list by days, season and family member, and a countdown to departure. Full app overview

On the trip: the money

G+Money: Spending & Budget lets you log each expense in the local currency and see it in your home currency right away, with a subtotal per trip and a daily limit. 50 currencies are built in, and with no signal it keeps converting with the latest saved rate or one you set. Full app overview

What the three share

  • No ads and no subscription.
  • Usable without a connection: TripBee's core planning, Snapport fully, and G+Money's conversions.
