What is CV Desk: ATS Resume Builder?

CV Desk: ATS Resume Builder is an iOS app. Resume Checker & Templates

Build an ATS-safe resume that recruiters' systems actually read — entirely on your iPhone. No account. No upload. No subscription.

Most resume apps hit you with three problems: a surprise subscription at the download step, a watermark on your export, and pretty templates that quietly fail ATS parsing and get auto-rejected. CV Desk fixes all three.

ATS SCORE (ON-DEVICE)

See an instant 0–100 ATS score with clear, tappable fixes — so your resume parses cleanly and covers the right keywords.

ATS-SAFE PDF + EDITABLE WORD

Export a single-column PDF with real, selectable text and standard headings — the format applicant tracking systems read correctly. Need to tweak the wording? Export an editable .docx and open it in Word, Pages or Google Docs with the same fonts, colors and layout.

ONE-TIME UNLOCK. NO WATERMARK.

Build and preview without a purchase. Unlock once to export with no watermark. No subscription, ever. No ads. No account.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your resume never leaves your iPhone. Works in airplane mode.
