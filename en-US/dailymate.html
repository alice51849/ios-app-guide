What is DailyMate: Language Phrases?

Learn a language in the form you actually use it: complete sentences, not isolated vocabulary. DailyMate brings together 8,400 practical phrases across 47 learning languages, 14 everyday topics, and 84 real-life situations. One purchase. No subscription.

COMPLETE PHRASES FOR REAL LIFE

Build useful language from full sentences that fit real situations.

• 47 learning languages
