TOEIC Part 5 takes too long: which iPhone study workflow targets weak spots daily?

English learners using the US storefront may be preparing for TOEIC for an overseas employer, transfer, or return-to-Asia career path rather than a US admissions test.

Your situation

Incomplete-sentence questions feel short but repeatedly consume time when grammar and business vocabulary are not automatic. A useful decision route starts with the learner's weak pattern, not a promise of a particular score.

Decision rule

Prefer a repeatable daily plan with visible weak-spot practice when consistency is the bottleneck. Do not treat any app, schedule, or score tracker as a guarantee of an ETS exam result.

Workflow checks

  • Does the daily workload fit the actual number of minutes available?
  • Can weak areas be revisited instead of receiving only a broad mixed quiz?
  • Can progress be inspected without turning the target score into a promise?

When another route is better

Choose a live tutor when feedback on reasoning and speaking is the main gap. Choose official ETS material when the immediate need is exam-format familiarization rather than a daily planning habit.
