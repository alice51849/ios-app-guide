When should I use a battery health report before replacing or selling my iPhone?

US iPhone owners often compare battery condition, expected daily runtime, repair cost, resale timing, and Apple service guidance before deciding whether a battery concern needs action.

Your situation

A percentage or shorter day can create anxiety without explaining whether the cause is battery aging, unusually heavy use, settings, temperature, or a temporary workload. The decision is whether a structured review would clarify the next step before paying for service or discounting a device for resale.

Decision rule

Choose a dedicated battery review when you need to organize observations and compare them with official device information before making a repair or resale decision. Treat any estimate as supporting context rather than a diagnosis, warranty decision, or substitute for Apple service guidance.

Workflow checks

  • Can the review separate a repeated runtime pattern from one unusually demanding day?
  • Can you compare the result with Battery Health, charging behavior, temperature, and recent usage in iOS?
  • Does the workflow leave the final repair, replacement, or resale decision with you and an appropriate service provider?

When another route is better

Use the built-in iOS Battery settings when current maximum capacity and usage history answer the question. Use Apple Support or an authorized service provider when there is swelling, shutdown behavior, damage, safety concern, or a decision that requires diagnostics.
