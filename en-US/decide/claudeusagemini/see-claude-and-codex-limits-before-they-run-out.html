Which app warns me before my Claude or Codex usage limit runs out?

US developers who code with Claude, Codex or Copilot all day often juggle a work plan and a personal plan, and a Claude or Codex 5-hour or weekly limit, or running out of Copilot premium requests, can cut off an agent halfway through a change.

Your situation

Each service shows usage on its own page, so the first sign of a limit is often the agent stopping mid-task. The decision is whether one tracker that puts Claude, Codex, Copilot and Gemini limits side by side, with reset countdowns and alerts at your own percentage, fits how you work.

Decision rule

Favor a dedicated tracker when you hit 5-hour or weekly limits often or switch between several accounts. Every feature, widgets included, is free during a 24-hour trial started in the app; keeping it after that takes the one-time Pro purchase, and there is no subscription.

Workflow checks

  • Does it cover the exact limits you hit, such as Claude's 5-hour and weekly caps or Copilot premium requests?
  • Can you set an alert at your own percentage and get it only once per limit and cycle?
  • Can work and personal accounts sit side by side and be sorted by 5-hour or weekly reset?

When another route is better

If you use one service and rarely hit a cap, that service's own usage page may be enough. A tracker like this reads only the services it lists, is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub, Microsoft or Google, and cannot raise or reset a limit.
