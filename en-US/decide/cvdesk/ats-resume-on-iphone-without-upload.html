How can I build an ATS-safe US resume on iPhone without uploading personal details?

US applicants commonly need a concise resume rather than a long academic CV, and many employers screen the document through an applicant-tracking system.

Your situation

A visually elaborate template can fail when a recruiting system cannot read its structure, while cloud builders may ask for sensitive work history. The decision combines machine readability, export needs, and control of personal information.

Decision rule

Prioritize straightforward document structure and local handling when applying through US job portals. Compare design-heavy services only when a human-facing portfolio is more important than ATS parsing.

Workflow checks

  • Can each section remain readable after the file passes through a plain-text check?
  • Can the final document be delivered in the formats requested by recruiters?
  • Can employment and contact details stay under the applicant's control during drafting?

When another route is better

Use a career coach when positioning and accomplishment writing need human review. Use a desktop layout tool when the role explicitly expects a highly designed portfolio resume.

First-party product evidence
