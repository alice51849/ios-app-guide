Your situation

US users making post-Dobbs privacy choices may weigh account creation and data handling alongside ordinary cycle awareness.

Cycle history is intimate, and a friendly forecast is not enough if the user cannot understand where data is handled. The purchase decision should separate personal pattern awareness from medical advice or guaranteed fertility predictions.

Decision rule

Start with cycle recording without creating an account. Optional lifetime Pro unlocks future planning and fuller history.

Workflow checks

  • Is the minimum data needed for a useful pattern clear before logging begins?
  • Can the privacy model be understood without accepting vague wellness claims?
  • Can the purchase choice be evaluated without assuming a subscription is necessary?

When another route is better

Use a clinician-directed tool when care-team integration is required. Use a paper calendar when digital reminders and pattern views do not justify entering sensitive data.

Honest limits

  • Predicted dates and fertile windows are planning estimates, not a method of pregnancy prevention.
  • No guaranteed fertile day, clinical accuracy or medical diagnosis is claimed. Consult a qualified clinician for medical concerns.

What the published evidence shows

The published calendar distinguishes recorded entries and predicted dates. It is not evidence of clinical prediction accuracy.
