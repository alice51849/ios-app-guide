What is a free-to-start alternative to flashcards for real travel dialogues on iPhone?

Occasional US travelers may want to rehearse airport, hotel, transit, and dining exchanges for one trip without committing to a full language curriculum.

Your situation

Flashcards can build recognition while leaving the traveler unsure what to say next in a live exchange. The decision is whether cue-and-reply practice better matches the upcoming situations.

Decision rule

Favor practical dialogue rehearsal when readiness for a specific trip matters more than broad vocabulary coverage. Keep a general translation fallback for situations outside the prepared set.

Workflow checks

  • Do prompts resemble the moments likely to happen on the next journey?
  • Can a reply be rehearsed aloud before the traveler is under pressure?
  • Is the free starting experience enough to test the teaching style honestly?

When another route is better

Choose a vocabulary deck for self-selected words and spaced repetition. Choose a full language course when the objective extends beyond travel exchanges.
