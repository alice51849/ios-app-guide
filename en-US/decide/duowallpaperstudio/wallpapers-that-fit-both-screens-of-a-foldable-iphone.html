How do I get wallpapers that fit both screens of a foldable iPhone?

Many people in the US who move to a foldable or dual-screen iPhone find that a favorite wallpaper looks right on one screen but cropped or stretched on the other, so closing and opening the phone feels like switching between two unrelated pictures.

Your situation

The cover screen is tall and narrow while the inner screen is closer to square, so one picture usually loses its subject on one screen or gets stretched on the other. The decision is whether wallpapers made as pairs, a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen cut from the same original artwork, fit how you use the phone.

Decision rule

Favor a pair-based wallpaper app when you switch between the closed and open screens often and want both to look like one design. It is free to start with 5 sets and every tool, with a small watermark on saved images; a one-time in-app purchase opens the remaining launch sets and removes the watermark.

Workflow checks

  • Is every set a true pair, with a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen?
  • Can you preview the closed and open screens before you save anything?
  • Can you turn your own photo into a pair and drag to set where the focus sits?

When another route is better

If you use a single-screen iPhone and only want a new picture now and then, a general wallpaper gallery may be enough. iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so after saving you still set each screen yourself by following a short setup guide.
