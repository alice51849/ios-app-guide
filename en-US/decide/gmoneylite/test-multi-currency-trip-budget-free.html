Which free-to-start iPhone travel expense tracker converts local currency and works offline?

US vacationers planning one international trip may want to test a local-currency ledger before paying, then glance at totals from a widget or watch.

Your situation

Receipts arrive in local currency while the travel budget exists in dollars, and waiting until home hides overspending. The decision is whether a small free trial proves the logging habit before a one-time unlock.

Decision rule

Test the complete input-to-total loop with a real trip budget. Prefer manual privacy and offline continuity when bank imports would add complexity or fail abroad.

Workflow checks

  • Can a traveler log the amount in the currency shown on the receipt?
  • Can the home-currency impact be checked without reopening every entry?
  • Can the free allowance demonstrate the full workflow rather than a static preview?

When another route is better

Use a shared spreadsheet for group editing. Use bank aggregation when automatic posted transactions matter more than immediate local-currency awareness.
