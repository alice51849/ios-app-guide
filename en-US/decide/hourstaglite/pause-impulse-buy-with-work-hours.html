Can a free iPhone tool convert a price into work hours before I impulse-buy?

US consumers comparing everyday wants across hourly wages, taxes, and side-hustle income may need a fast pre-purchase check rather than a new financial account.

Your situation

The hardest moment is often the few seconds between wanting an item and tapping Buy. The decision is whether a visible time cost creates enough friction without requiring bank access.

Decision rule

Start with a free, low-data pause when mindful spending is the target. Move to a full financial plan if the issue is affordability across rent, debt, savings, and variable income.

Workflow checks

  • Can the price-to-hours result appear before the checkout decision?
  • Can needs, wants, and impulses remain distinguishable over repeated checks?
  • Can the habit work without opening or linking a financial account?

When another route is better

A banking tool is better for transaction history and balances. A browser wish list is simpler when waiting, not calculation, is the only useful intervention.
