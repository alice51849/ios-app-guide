Is there a simple weight tracker that shows a 7-day average and can also keep a log of my GLP-1 shots?

Many people in the United States weigh themselves at home each morning, and some of them are also on GLP-1 shots, often juggling a notes app, a calendar and a separate scale app to see everything together.

Your situation

The number on the scale moves up and down from one day to the next, so a single high reading can feel like a setback even when the overall direction has not changed. The decision is whether a quick daily weight log that leads with a rolling average, with an optional records-only shot log beside it, fits the way you already track.

Decision rule

Favor a weight log built around a 7-day average when daily swings discourage you and you want logging to take seconds on iPhone or Apple Watch. If you are on shots, choose one whose shot card only records what you enter, with no dose tracking, suggestions or interpretation, and keep following your health professional's guidance.

Workflow checks

  • Does it open to today with the last weight filled in so a weigh-in takes only a few seconds?
  • Does it show a 7-day average line next to the daily readings instead of only the latest number?
  • If you log shots, does the shot card only record date, site and notes, without dose tracking or suggestions?

When another route is better

If you want meal-by-meal calorie counting, coaching or medical guidance, a nutrition app or your clinician fits better, since this log is for personal tracking only and does not give health advice. If you only need a number with no trend, the Health app on its own may be enough.
