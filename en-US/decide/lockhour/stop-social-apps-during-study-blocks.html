How do I block distracting social apps during a study or deep-work block on iPhone?

US students and remote workers often study on the same iPhone that delivers social, shopping, and entertainment interruptions.

Your situation

Good intentions compete with apps designed to be reopened automatically. The decision is whether an on-device blocking routine can create a clear boundary without adding ads or another recurring fee.

Decision rule

Choose a focused blocker when access friction is the missing piece. Pair it with a realistic schedule; blocking alone does not decide what work should fill the protected time.

Workflow checks

  • Can the distracting set be defined before concentration begins?
  • Can a focus block be started with less effort than reopening the distraction?
  • Does the routine remain understandable when a legitimate exception is needed?

When another route is better

Use Apple's built-in Screen Time when its controls are sufficient. Use coaching or environmental changes when the problem extends beyond a device boundary.
