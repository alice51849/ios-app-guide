When did I last replace the filter, wash the sheets, or back up photos?

US households juggle filters, smoke-alarm checks, bedding, pet care, photo backups, and family calls whose timing matters but rarely belongs on a fixed weekly checklist.

Your situation

A recurring reminder assumes life happens on schedule, while many maintenance tasks should restart from the day they were actually completed. The decision is whether an event history answers the real question better than another to-do list.

Decision rule

Choose last-done tracking when elapsed time and completion history matter more than a fixed calendar. Use a regulated maintenance system when inspections, warranties, or safety records require formal documentation.

Workflow checks

  • Can completion be recorded quickly enough that the habit survives?
  • Can an overdue rhythm restart from the actual completion date?
  • Can earlier occurrences be corrected without losing the history?

When another route is better

A calendar is better for appointments tied to a fixed date. A shared home-maintenance service is better when multiple people need assignments and vendor records.
