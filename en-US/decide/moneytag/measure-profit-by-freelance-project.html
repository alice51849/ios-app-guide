How can I track income, expenses, and net result by freelance project without a subscription?

US freelancers and side-hustle operators often need a quick project-level view of income, expenses, and tags before handing formal tax records to a professional.

Your situation

A personal spending category does not show whether one client project actually earned money after its direct costs. The decision is whether a private project ledger is enough before adopting full accounting software.

Decision rule

Choose project-based manual tracking when fast profit visibility matters more than bank feeds, invoicing, payroll, or tax filing. Keep receipts and official records required by the IRS or a tax professional separately.

Workflow checks

  • Can income and costs be attached to the same client project?
  • Can tags reveal spending patterns across several projects?
  • Can the net result be checked without granting bank-account access?

When another route is better

Accounting software is better for reconciliation, invoices, and tax workflows. A spreadsheet is better when custom formulas and accountant collaboration outweigh mobile speed.
