How can I handwrite on lecture PDFs and review everything offline on iPad?

US college, certification, and continuing-education workflows often mix lecture PDFs, Apple Pencil notes, and recorded review material.

Your situation

A class or study session becomes fragmented when handwriting, typed notes, PDF markup, and review audio live in separate places. The buying decision is whether one calm workspace can reduce that switching without requiring an account.

Decision rule

Prioritize a unified study flow and offline access over collaboration or a large cloud workspace. Treat export, backup, and the exact free limit as items to confirm before moving existing coursework.

Workflow checks

  • Can the study flow begin with a lecture PDF and keep annotations close to the source?
  • Can handwriting and typed follow-up notes stay useful when campus Wi-Fi is unreliable?
  • Can a learner review the same material later without reconstructing where each piece belongs?

When another route is better

Compare a cloud-first team notebook when simultaneous collaboration is the central requirement. Compare a plain PDF editor when handwritten study notes and review context are not part of the job.
