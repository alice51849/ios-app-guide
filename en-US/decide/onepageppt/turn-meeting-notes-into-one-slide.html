How do I turn meeting notes or a report into one editable presentation slide?

US managers and consultants often need a single executive-summary slide that can survive a meeting, email, or PowerPoint handoff without becoming a miniature report.

Your situation

Copying paragraphs into a deck produces a wall of text, while aggressive trimming can remove the decision, evidence, or owner. The buying question is whether a one-slide constraint creates a clearer handoff.

Decision rule

Use a one-page slide when the audience needs the key message, support, and next step at once. Use a full deck when sequencing, appendix detail, or multiple decisions cannot be compressed honestly.

Workflow checks

  • Can the source material be reduced to one governing message?
  • Can evidence and next action remain visible without shrinking into unreadable text?
  • Can the output be edited after it enters the team's presentation workflow?

When another route is better

A full presentation tool is better for narrative decks and brand systems. A document summary is better when the audience needs searchable detail rather than a visual briefing.
