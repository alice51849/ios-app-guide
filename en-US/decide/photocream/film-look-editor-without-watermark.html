What pay-once iPhone photo editor offers film looks at full resolution without a watermark?

US travel creators may want consistent film-inspired color, grain, halation, and light-leak treatments without turning every edit into a preset subscription.

Your situation

A trendy preset can look convincing on a thumbnail yet produce a low-resolution or watermarked export. The decision is whether a focused film-look editor provides enough variety and output quality for a creator's own style.

Decision rule

Choose a specialized aesthetic tool when film character is the goal and a one-time purchase is preferred. Choose a full editor when masking, compositing, catalog management, or detailed color correction is central.

Workflow checks

  • Can several looks be tested against the same representative travel image?
  • Do grain and light effects support the photo rather than obscure its subject?
  • Can the final result retain the resolution needed for its destination?

When another route is better

A camera profile workflow is better for repeatable professional color. A general editor is better when film styling is only one step in a complex retouch.
