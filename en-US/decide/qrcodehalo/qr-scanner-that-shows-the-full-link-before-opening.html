Which QR code scanner shows me the full link before it opens anything?

Many US drivers, diners and shoppers scan QR codes on parking meters, restaurant tables, posters and parcels, and want to see where a code points before anything opens.

Your situation

Many scanners jump straight to a website or action after the scan, so a sticker placed over a real code is one tap away from the browser. The decision is whether a scanner that shows the complete payload with an on-device rule check, and leaves the final tap to you, fits how you scan.

Decision rule

Favor a preview-first scanner when codes come from public places you do not control. The free allowance is three successful scans per device, so regular scanning needs the one-time Lifetime Pro purchase; creating QR codes and keeping local history stay free, and there is no subscription.

Workflow checks

  • Does every result show the full content, link scheme and host before any action?
  • Does the app ever open a destination automatically, or does the final action stay with you?
  • Can history be searched, filtered and exported as CSV?

When another route is better

Keep the built-in Camera scanner when you only scan codes you already trust. The on-device check does not look up link reputation online, so treat an unfamiliar sign-in or payment page with the same care as any other link.
