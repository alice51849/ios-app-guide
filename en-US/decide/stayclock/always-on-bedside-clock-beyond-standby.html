What is a better always-on bedside clock than iPhone StandBy, without a subscription?

Many US households keep an older iPhone or iPad on a nightstand or desk dock and want the time readable from across the room all night, without a monthly fee for one screen.

Your situation

StandBy only takes over while the phone charges on its side, offers a small set of faces, and on iPhone models without an always-on display it turns off after a short time. The decision is whether a dedicated full-screen clock that stays lit, protects an OLED panel and matches the Lock Screen and watch is worth adding for that one job.

Decision rule

Favor a dedicated always-on clock when a phone or tablet will sit on a desk or nightstand for hours and the time must stay readable from across the room. Start with the one free face and treat the one-time unlock as optional, since there is no subscription.

Workflow checks

  • Does the screen stay lit while charging, with a battery floor for when it is unplugged?
  • Can the same face appear in StandBy, on the Lock Screen, in Home Screen widgets and on an Apple Watch?
  • Does it shift pixels and switch to a dim red face at night to spare an OLED screen?

When another route is better

Keep using the built-in StandBy mode when the phone always charges on its side and the included faces are enough. Choose a physical alarm clock when the goal is to keep the phone out of reach at night.
