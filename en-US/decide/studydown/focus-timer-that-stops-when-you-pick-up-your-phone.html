Which focus timer only counts time while my phone stays face down, without a subscription?

Many US students and remote workers study, read or practice a language with the phone on the desk, and want an honest record of the hours they actually spent without picking it up.

Your situation

A countdown or Pomodoro timer keeps running after the phone is back in hand, so the logged hours can overstate real focus. The decision is whether a timer that pauses when the phone is lifted and logs time per activity gives a more truthful record.

Decision rule

Favor a face-down timer when the goal is an honest log of hours per activity rather than a fixed work and break rhythm. Try the free allowance first, but note it covers up to 3 activities and 5 recorded minutes in total, so regular use needs the one-time Pro purchase.

Workflow checks

  • Does the count pause after a short grace period when the phone is picked up?
  • Can time be logged per activity and compared across today and this week?
  • Can records be exported as CSV so the history is not locked in?

When another route is better

Keep a Pomodoro timer when fixed work and break intervals matter more than a per-activity log. Use Screen Time or a Focus mode when the goal is to block distracting apps rather than measure effort.
