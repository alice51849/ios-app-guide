Your situation

US parents often discover a soft birthday, sports, or school photo only after the moment cannot be recreated, making privacy and realistic expectations important.

A cherished image may be soft from motion, focus, or low light, but no enhancer can recover detail the camera never captured. The decision is whether a private enhancement produces a more usable version without pretending to restore ground truth.

Decision rule

Start by comparing the enhancement preview. Full-resolution, watermark-free export requires a one-time Pro unlock.

Workflow checks

  • Can the original remain available for side-by-side comparison?
  • Does the result look natural at the size where it will be viewed or printed?
  • Can processing stay on the device for a private family image?

When another route is better

A professional retoucher is better for a critical print and nuanced manual work. Keeping the original may be the most honest choice when enhancement introduces distracting artifacts.

Honest limits

  • Enhancement cannot recover information the camera never captured, and some photos will remain soft.
  • Do not use generated detail as identity, forensic or legal evidence. No promise is made to rescue every photo.
