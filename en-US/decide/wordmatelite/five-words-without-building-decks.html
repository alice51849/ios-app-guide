What is a free vocabulary alternative for learning five words a day without making decks?

Busy US adults studying around a commute, coffee break, or school pickup may need a visible one-minute habit rather than the setup work of building flashcard decks.

Your situation

A powerful flashcard system can stall before study begins because choosing, formatting, and maintaining cards becomes the task. The decision is whether a tiny prepared daily trail creates more consistency.

Decision rule

Choose the smallest repeatable habit when initiation is the bottleneck. Choose a custom deck when specialized vocabulary, personal examples, and precise spaced-repetition controls are essential.

Workflow checks

  • Can one session fit honestly into about a minute?
  • Can progress remain visible without requiring deck maintenance?
  • Can study continue offline during a commute or short break?

When another route is better

A custom SRS deck is better for exams and field-specific terminology. Reading with a dictionary is better when learning from rich context is the main preference.
