Which pay-once iPhone app can open encrypted or split ZIP, RAR and 7z archives without ads?

US iPhone and iPad users often receive a password-protected or split archive through email, chat or cloud storage when no desktop computer is nearby.

Your situation

A basic ZIP opener stops being useful when a client, coworker or download sends RAR5, encrypted 7z, ZIP64 or multiple split volumes. The decision is whether one on-device archive tool covers the formats that actually arrive without adding an account, advertising or a recurring payment.

Decision rule

Choose a paid-upfront archive utility when broad extraction support, password handling and predictable ownership matter more than cloud collaboration. Confirm creation separately from extraction because RAR and RAR5 can be opened but not created.

Workflow checks

  • Can the app preview and extract encrypted ZIP or 7z without uploading the archive?
  • Can it join split ZIP, RAR or 7z volumes that arrive as several files?
  • Can it create ZIP or 7z when a smaller outgoing archive is needed?

When another route is better

Apple's Files app is enough for an ordinary ZIP. A desktop archive suite is better for unsupported formats such as standalone Zstandard (.zst) or CAB, or when a large batch needs advanced repair and automation.
