What is Foldable Wallpaper Studio?

Foldable Wallpaper Studio is an iOS app. Dual Screen Pairs & Widgets

Wallpapers made in pairs for the two screens of iPhone Duo. Close it: a close-up on the cover screen. Open it: the whole scene on the inner screen, both cut from the same original artwork.

300+ original pairs in 14 styles · 5 widgets drawn with your wallpaper · your own photos as pairs · no ads, no account, no data collected.

Most wallpapers fit one screen, but a foldable has two, so Foldable Wallpaper Studio makes them in pairs: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen, cut from the same original artwork at the exact pixel sizes of iPhone Duo.

EVERYDAY IDEAS

A quiet night sky for work, a cute 3D scene for the weekend, or a pure black glow that looks great on OLED. Or a travel photo: the close-up on the cover screen, the full landscape inside. Add a matching clock, calendar or text card, and your Home Screen and Lock Screen look like one design.

FEATURES

Closed and open previews in the app, on any iPhone or iPad

Color swatches and one line of your own text, with both previews redrawn instantly

In the Create tab, make a pair from your own photo and drag to set the focus. The system photo picker is used, so the app never reads your whole library

Pixel-exact export: 1398 x 2034 for the cover screen and 2853 x 2007 for the inner screen, saved as two images to Photos with add-only access. On other models, iOS scales the image

Five widgets: My Wallpaper, Art Clock, Calendar, Battery and Text Card, all drawn with the wallpaper you choose, in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes. Battery shows only this iPhone or iPad's charge level and charging state

Setup guide: iOS does not let apps change your wallpaper automatically, so after you save, the Settings tab shows how to set each screen in a few steps

For iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later

PRIVACY

No ads, no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The only network connection is for in-app purchases. Your photos stay on your device.

IN-APP PURCHASE

Without a purchase you can try every tool: HD export, recoloring, text, photo pairs and the basic widget styles, with 5 wallpaper sets ready to use. Images saved from those sets and from your own photos carry a small watermark. All five widgets can be added and work without a purchase. Duo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that opens all the remaining launch sets, removes the watermark and adds extra widget styles, such as more clock and battery styles, along with the color palettes of the paid sets.
