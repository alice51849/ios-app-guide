What is EasyAM: Holiday Alarm Clock?

EasyAM: Holiday Alarm Clock is an iOS app. Rings for work, skips days off

Will your alarm ring tomorrow, and should it? EasyAM decides for you, day by day: it rings on workdays and stays quiet on public holidays and your days off.

Open the app and your next seven days are right there, showing which mornings ring and at what time. No more turning alarms off before a long weekend and back on after it.

What changes with EasyAM

Public and regional holidays are skipped automatically.

Vacations, sick days and trips stay quiet once you add them as time off.

Make-up working days ring as usual in places that have them.

Leaving early one day? Change the time for just that day; the rest stay the same.

Want a slow start tomorrow? One tap skips only the next ring and keeps your routine.

Your next alarm is on your widgets, Lock Screen and Apple Watch.

Made for real routines

Office workers: long weekends take care of themselves, with nothing to switch off the night before.

Students and teachers: ring on class days and add school breaks as time off.

Shift workers: set a shift or alternating-week pattern once, available with the in-app purchase.

Working abroad or moving: change your country or region anytime, with 67 countries and regions built in.

Widgets and Apple Watch

The Next Alarm widget comes in small, medium and large sizes, plus extra large on iPad, and shows your next alarm with holiday and schedule status; medium and large add a one-tap button to sleep in tomorrow. Lock Screen widgets come in three styles, and the small widget also appears in StandBy. Choose an analog dial theme and the Home Screen widget becomes a round clock face.

The Apple Watch app installs with the iPhone app and shows your next alarm and the coming seven days, synced from your iPhone. You can skip tomorrow's ring from your wrist too; it shows done once your iPhone confirms it with iOS. Complications and a Smart Stack card are included. Your iPhone schedules the alarms, so the watch app works with your paired iPhone.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your alarms and settings stay on your device; the only network use is downloading public holiday data files.

Unlimited alarms, shift and alternating-week patterns, a holiday calendar for each alarm and 100 more clock themes are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase.
