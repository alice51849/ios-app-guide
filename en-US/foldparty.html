What is Meow Party: 200 Cat Mini Games?

Meow Party: 200 Cat Mini Games is an iOS app. 2 Player Games: Fold & Play

Most games use your foldable like any other screen, but Meow Party turns the fold itself into the fun. Play 200 original cute cat minigames, most of them solo, plus 54 that two players can enjoy face to face on one phone. On a foldable, the crease becomes the net, the center line or a mirror. Optimized for iPhone Duo foldable screens, it is more fun when you fold, and every game is fully playable on a regular iPhone.

200 original minigames, each starring a cute cat from the Meow Squad.

Most games work solo, and 54 let two players battle or team up face to face on one phone.

Quick rounds of about 30 to 120 seconds, with a gradually rising difficulty, and many games have levels.

Four families of 50 games each: party, fold puzzles, arcade and rhythm, and cozy creations.

Fold moves feel natural on a foldable, and each one has an on-screen alternative on a regular iPhone.

Cute computer rivals let you practice most of the two-player games on your own.

Plays offline, with no ads and no account.

Great for a cafe table, a night on the couch, a long train ride or a few spare minutes. Sit across from a friend, partner or family member with the phone between you, let the crease split the screen into two sides, and play for bragging rights. On your own, warm up against a computer rival or take your time cooking, dressing up and gardening.

What is inside

Party: 50 lively party games where the crease can work as the net, the line or the mirror.

Fold puzzles: 50 puzzles where folding and unfolding is how you solve them.

Arcade and rhythm: 50 games of reflexes, timing and one-thumb skill.

Cozy creations: 50 games of cooking, dress-up, gardening, simple management and wordless puzzles.

Fold tricks: peek at a secret on the outer screen while the phone is closed, then open it to play. Closing the phone can be the move itself, and opening it zooms a tiny world out into a wide field.

Regular iPhone support: every fold action has an on-screen hold-to-close button or a two-finger pinch.

Meow Squad: you always play as a cute cat, and in two-player games both players are cats. Bunny, bear, chick, penguin, fox, frog and mochi friends show up as pals, customers, rivals and props.

The interface comes in 50 languages and follows your system language.

Privacy and purchase

Meow Party has no network features and works fully offline. It needs no account, collects no data, and does not use the camera, location, contacts or photos. Your progress stays on your device.

No ads. No in-app purchases. All 200 games are included.
