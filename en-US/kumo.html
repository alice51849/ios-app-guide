What is Kumo: Weight & GLP-1 Tracker?

Kumo: Weight & GLP-1 Tracker is an iOS app. Shot Log, Fasting & Cute Cloud

The scale jumps around every day, so Kumo turns a 10-second weigh-in into a calm 7-day average, with or without GLP-1 shots. A cute cloud grows every day you show up.

Built for iPhone Duo: open it up and Kumo fills the whole inner screen in two roomy columns.

WHY KUMO

• About 10 seconds a day

• "Didn't weigh today" still keeps your streak

• No account, no ads, no tracking

MADE FOR YOUR DAY

On GLP-1 shots: log each shot and see your weight trend in one place.

Not on shots: just weigh in and watch the trend.

WEIGHT AND TRENDS

• Opens to today with your last weight filled in; adjust by 0.1

• Pounds, kilograms or stones

• Daily dots and a 7-day average line, with the last 30 days of trend and history

• 3-month and all-time trends and older history (Kumo Full)

• Weekly average vs. last week, plus non-scale wins

BODY STATS

• Waist, body composition, hips, chest, thigh, upper arm and neck, each with a trend (1 free, all with Kumo Full)

• An estimated goal date, for reference only

• Before and after photos with faces blurred by default (Kumo Full)

FASTING TIMER

• 12:12, 14:10 and 16:8; 18:6, 20:4, custom and fasting charts with Kumo Full

• One-tap start, editable start time

• Time left on Apple Watch; live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with Kumo Full

• A reminder to eat a proper meal; Kumo caps fasts at 23 hours

• If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, or have eating problems or a chronic condition, talk to a health professional before fasting

SHOT CARD (OPTIONAL)

• Log the date, site and your own notes, with reminders on the schedule you set

• A next-shot countdown, a map of recent sites and how-you-feel check-ins

• Records only: no calculations, no dose tracking or suggestions, no interpretation

WATER AND PROTEIN

• Tap +1 glass of water or +10, 20 or 30 g of protein; rings show progress

• Today's steps from the Health app, shown only and never stored

YOUR CLOUD

• Grows through 5 stages with days logged, not weight

• Basic cloud free; outfits and weather themes (sunny, rainbow, rain, snow, starry night) with Kumo Full

APPLE WATCH, WIDGETS AND SIRI

• Apple Watch: twist the Digital Crown and tap to log, even without your iPhone nearby, plus watch face complications, including fasting

• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, all free: logged today, streak, weekly average, your cloud, plus a quick-log widget

• A Control Center button, and Siri to log weight or water

HEALTH APP AND SHARING

• Optionally write weight and waist to the Health app, or bring in past weight entries

• A weekly card shows your change, never your weight; share easily

• CSV and PDF monthly report export (Kumo Full)

FREE

Weight log with 7-day average and the last 30 days of trend and history; 1 module of your choice (fasting 12:12, 14:10 or 16:8, shot card, or water and protein); 1 body stat; 1 reminder; Apple Watch logging and watch face complications; all Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; the basic cloud; no account; your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, synced between iPhone and Apple Watch.

KUMO FULL

Several modules at once and all body stats; 18:6, 20:4, custom fasting and fasting charts; Lock Screen and Dynamic Island live countdown; 3-month and all-time trends and history beyond 30 days; before and after photos; CSV and PDF export; multiple and water reminders; outfits and weather.

Kumo Full is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.
