What is Lumi Mission Planet Pro?

Lumi Mission Planet Pro is an iOS app. Morning & Bedtime Routines

Getting a small child to brush teeth, finish meals or tidy toys should not take a battle every single day.

Lumi Mission Planet Pro turns five daily routines into gentle missions. Your child taps the screen for every small win, and Lumi, an adorable space bear, jumps and cheers on the spot. Finish a mission and the whole planet celebrates. The feedback is immediate, so the motivation is too.

This is the complete edition. One purchase unlocks everything, forever, for the whole family with Family Sharing.

EVERYTHING INCLUDED

Five daily missions: meals, tooth brushing, sleep time, tidy-up and school prep

Unlimited custom missions with your own icon and scene for each

Six buddy characters: bear, bunny, kitten, panda, dinosaur and fox

One hundred achievement stickers across missions, streaks, perfect days and play

A buddy that greets your child by nickname and grows day after day

Beautiful victory cards to remember every proud moment

Parent dashboard: today at a glance, seven day calendar, full tap-by-tap history

CSV export and one-tap backup so progress moves safely to a new phone

Designed for parents of children aged about three to seven, to be used together.

Bedtime, mealtime and tidy-up can feel like wins for everyone. Let Lumi cheer your child on.
