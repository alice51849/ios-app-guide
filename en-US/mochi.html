What is Mochi: Cute Checklist?

Mochi: Cute Checklist is an iOS app. Plan your day with cozy calm

Mochi is the cute, cozy to-do list that makes getting organized feel good. Add tasks, tap the little circle when you finish, and enjoy the tiny burst of calm that comes from checking something off.

Plan school, work, errands, groceries, habits, routines, schedules, notes and daily goals in one adorable place. Customize your checklist with 100 beautifully crafted skins, keep important tasks on your Home Screen with widgets, and check your list from Apple Watch.
