What is Mochi?

Mochi is an iOS app. Cute, cozy checklists that feel good to finish

Mochi keeps everyday tasks light instead of turning them into another source of pressure. Make a checklist in seconds, keep today's priorities easy to scan, and enjoy a satisfying tap when each item is done.

It works well for study plans, errands, household routines, packing lists, and small goals that do not need a complicated project manager. The calm visual style helps the list stay inviting even on a busy day.

No account or ads are required. Core checklist features are free to use, with an optional one-time purchase for the full experience and no subscription.
