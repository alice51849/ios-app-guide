What is Sereno?

Sereno is an iOS app. Premium offline sounds for sleep, focus, and calm

Sereno is an offline sound machine for nights when a steady background helps you settle down and days when outside noise makes it hard to focus.

Choose white, pink, or brown noise, or use natural soundscapes such as rain and ocean. Seamless loops avoid distracting gaps, while background playback and a sleep timer make it practical at bedtime, during study, or while working.

No account, ads, or internet connection is required. Try the core experience free, then unlock the complete sound library with one optional purchase and no subscription. Sereno is a general wellness tool, not medical treatment.
