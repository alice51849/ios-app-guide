Formula and limits

Subtotal = everything ordered, added up. Tax = subtotal × tax %. Tip = subtotal × tip %. Each person's tax and tip share is their own order ÷ subtotal, and leftover cents go to the largest fractions first, so the shares always add back up to the bill.

Subtotal = 32.40 + 18.90 + 25.00 = 76.30
Tax = 76.30 × 8.25% = 6.29
Tip = 76.30 × 15.00% = 11.45
Person 1: 32.40 + 2.67 + 4.86 = 39.93
Person 2: 18.90 + 1.56 + 2.84 = 23.30
Person 3: 25.00 + 2.06 + 3.75 = 30.81
Bill total = 94.04

Use one currency and enter tax and tip as percentages of the subtotal. No currency conversion, no service-charge rules, no advice on how much to tip and no payments. Display rounds to two decimals; the split itself is calculated in whole cents.

  • What they ordered: 0 ≤ n ≤ 100,000,000
  • Tax % / Tip %: 0 ≤ % ≤ 100
  • Person: 1 ≤ n ≤ 20
