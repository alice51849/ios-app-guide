What is TripBee Pro: Trip Planner?

TripBee Pro: Trip Planner is an iOS app. A private offline itinerary and family packing planner

TripBee keeps a whole trip in one calm, day-by-day plan. Add flights, hotels, activities, notes, and the places your family does not want to miss, then check the itinerary even when you do not have a data connection abroad.

Smart family packing lists help separate shared items from each traveler's essentials. One-tap Apple Maps directions reduce copying between apps when it is time to leave for the next stop.

Travel plans stay on the device with no account required. TripBee is a paid download with no recurring subscription.
