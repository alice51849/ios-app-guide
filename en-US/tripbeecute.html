What is TripBee Cute: Travel Journal?

TripBee Cute: Travel Journal is an iOS app. Vacation Countdown & Planner

Stop digging through emails, screenshots and notes: TripBee Cute turns your whole trip into one cute illustrated booklet, with the countdown, flights, day plans and packing list on a single page.

Each trip sits on your bookshelf as a little booklet with its own illustrated cover. Open it and scroll from the cover art to the countdown, your flights, every day's plan, and finally your packing list and notes.

Why you will love it

See the whole trip on one page instead of jumping between apps.

Tell the days apart at a glance: each day has its own color, and the date filter shows one day or the whole trip.

Let the excitement build with a countdown to departure, also on your Apple Watch.

Keep photos, tickets and notes right next to the plan they belong to.

Plan together by inviting friends through iCloud to edit the same booklet.

Share a web booklet anyone can open in a browser or chat app, even offline.

Made for every kind of trip

A weekend getaway, a family vacation, a road trip, a cruise or a honeymoon. Name the trip, pick a cover, add your flights and fill in each day. If it rains, switch to your rainy-day plan. Prefer spreadsheets? Export the CSV template, fill it in yourself or ask the AI assistant you already use to fill it in, then import it and your booklet is ready.

Features

57 illustrated covers with cities, landmarks, seasons and travel moods (6 included, 51 more with Cute Pass)

Countdown to departure, destination weather and local time

Boarding-pass style flight cards with times, terminals and booking codes

Day plans you can name, decorate with stickers, check off and reorder

Downtime slots and a rainy-day plan you can copy from the main plan

A color for every day and a filter by date

Photos, tickets and notes on each plan, with directions in your maps app

Packing list, plus a smart packing list based on who is coming, the season and trip length

Trip notes and gentle reminders

Plan with friends: invite them through iCloud, edit one booklet together and changes sync to each device (friends need TripBee Cute too)

iCloud sync across your own devices

CSV import that turns a filled-in template into a booklet

Backup export and import: replace everything, or merge to add only what is new without deleting anything

Web booklet: a single web file that opens in any browser, with a countdown that keeps running

Trip files with photos and tickets to share, plus support for trip files from TripBee and TripBee Lite

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that show your next trip on its cover art

Apple Watch app with the countdown and your next plan

Dark Mode, iPad and 50 languages that follow your system or can be changed in the app

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your booklets stay on your device and are saved to your own private iCloud only when you turn on sync. Weather comes from Open-Meteo and uses only your destination.

Cute Pass

Without a purchase you can keep one booklet at a time and use 6 covers, CSV import, the web booklet, flights, day plans, the packing list, widgets and the Apple Watch app. Some features are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase (Cute Pass): unlimited booklets, 51 more covers, smart packing lists, iCloud sync and backup, sharing trip files with photos and tickets, and planning with friends.
