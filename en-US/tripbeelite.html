What is TripBee Lite: Trip Planner?

TripBee Lite: Trip Planner is an iOS app. One Journey, Clearly Planned

Give one upcoming journey your full attention. TripBee Lite is a calm, polished travel planner designed around one active trip—not a crowded archive.

Shape each day on a clear timeline, keep flight and hotel details together, add notes, reminders and ticket references, and see the plan that matters now.

The free app saves one journey at a time. You can edit or replace it whenever your plans change.

A single, non-consumable Premium purchase unlocks unlimited saved journeys, trip sharing, complete backup and restore, and the smart packing list for the lifetime of the app. No subscription.

No account is required. There are no ads, analytics or tracking; your travel plans stay under your control.
