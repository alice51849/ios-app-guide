What is Lumi Trip Planet?

Lumi Trip Planet is an iOS app. Offline travel games and packing fun for ages 4-10

Lumi Trip Planet turns waiting at the airport, long drives, and quiet hotel time into a playful travel adventure for children ages 4 to 10.

Kid-friendly games, packing activities, and discovery prompts help children take part in the journey instead of only asking when it will be over. Activities work offline, making them useful on planes, trains, and roads with weak reception.

There are no ads or social features. Families can start free and use one optional purchase to unlock the complete experience. There is no subscription, and purchases and external links are locked behind a parental gate.
