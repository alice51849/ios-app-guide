What is Trip Planet: Kids Quest?

Trip Planet: Kids Quest is an iOS app. Family Travel Missions & Games

Turn every family trip into a tiny space adventure. Long flights, car rides and hotel stays become missions your child can finish, with collectible rewards and a passport stamp for every completed trip. Two parents can share one trip, each from their own device. No ads, no third-party tracking.

Trip Planet helps parents transform travel moments into magical missions for kids ages 3–10. Create a trip, choose smart Mission Packs, and watch your child’s adventure appear as a glowing constellation across a cinematic nebula. Instead of another checklist, kids get small real-world quests: try a new food, be brave at the airport, say thank you, spot three colors at a museum, take a memory photo, or help pack the hotel bag. Tiny missions. Big smiles.

SAFE BY DESIGN

Trip Planet has no ads, no third-party tracking and no external servers. Purchases and sharing sit behind a parent gate, while child names and photos stay private on-device and in your family’s iCloud.

PAY ONCE. ADVENTURE OFTEN.

Trip Planet is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Unlock it once and make every holiday, road trip, flight and weekend away feel like a story your child helped create.
