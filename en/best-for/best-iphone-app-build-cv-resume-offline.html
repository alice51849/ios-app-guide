Questions & Answers

Does CVDesk upload my personal data to a server?

No. CVDesk works offline. Your resume information stays on your device and is never sent to external servers.

What resume formats does CVDesk export?

CVDesk exports to PDF, the standard format for sending to recruiters and uploading to job platforms.

Can I create multiple resumes in CVDesk?

Yes. You can create and store multiple resume versions within the app — useful if you're applying for different types of roles.

Is CVDesk suitable for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)?

CVDesk produces clean, readable PDF layouts. For best ATS compatibility, avoid excessive formatting and use standard section headings.
