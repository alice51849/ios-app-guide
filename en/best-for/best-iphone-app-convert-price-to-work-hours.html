Best iPhone App to See How Many Work Hours Anything Costs (2026)

Before buying something, wouldn't you want to know it costs 4 hours of your work? This framing changes how you think about purchases. The right app makes this conversion instant, every time you see a price.

Top pick for price-to-hours conversion
HoursTag Lite

HoursTag Lite lets you enter your hourly wage (after tax) and then converts any price into hours of work. It includes a Safari Share Extension so you can convert prices on any website without switching apps. Use it while online shopping to make more deliberate purchase decisions.

Ideal for: Anyone who wants a real-world sense of whether a purchase is worth the cost in their own work hours.

Questions & Answers

Should I enter my gross or net salary into HoursTag Lite?

Net salary (after taxes) gives you the most realistic sense of what each hour of work is actually worth. That makes the conversion more meaningful for everyday purchase decisions.

Does HoursTag Lite work in Safari while shopping online?

Yes. HoursTag Lite has a Share Extension. While browsing in Safari, tap Share → HoursTag Lite to convert the price on any page without leaving the browser.

Can HoursTag Lite handle currencies other than USD?

Yes. You can enter your hourly rate in any currency and convert prices in that same currency.

Is this a subscription app?

No. HoursTag Lite is a one-time purchase with no ongoing subscription fee.
