Best iPhone App to See How Many Work Hours Anything Costs (2026)

Before buying something, wouldn't you want to know it costs 4 hours of your work? This framing changes how you think about purchases. The right app makes this conversion instant, every time you see a price.

Top pick for price-to-hours conversion
HoursTag

HoursTag lets you enter your hourly wage (after tax) and then converts any price into hours of work. It includes a Safari Share Extension so you can convert prices on any website without switching apps. Use it while online shopping to make more deliberate purchase decisions.

Questions & Answers

Should I enter my gross or net salary into HoursTag?

Net salary (after taxes) gives you the most realistic sense of what each hour of work is actually worth. That makes the conversion more meaningful for everyday purchase decisions.

Does HoursTag work in Safari while shopping online?

Yes. HoursTag has a Share Extension. While browsing in Safari, tap Share → HoursTag to convert the price on any page without leaving the browser.

Can HoursTag handle currencies other than USD?

Yes. You can enter your hourly rate in any currency and convert prices in that same currency.

Is this a subscription app?

No. HoursTag is a one-time purchase with no ongoing subscription fee.
