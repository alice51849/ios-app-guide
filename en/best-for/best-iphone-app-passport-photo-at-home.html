Questions & Answers

Will a passport photo taken with Snapport be accepted?

Snapport follows official sizing and background specifications. Acceptance depends on the quality of your photo (lighting, expression, background). Follow the in-app guidance carefully for best results.

What background colour do I need for a passport photo?

Most countries require a white or off-white plain background. Snapport shows the specific requirement for the country you select.

Can I use a photo taken indoors with artificial light?

Yes. Good indoor lighting with no harsh shadows on the face works well. Avoid bright direct light that creates glare or shadows.

How do I print the passport photo after using Snapport?

Download the photo file from Snapport and print at home, at a pharmacy, or upload for an online print service. The file is sized correctly for printing.
