Questions & Answers

What makes an iPhone scanner 'offline'?

An offline scanner processes edge detection, image enhancement, and PDF creation entirely on your iPhone. No data is sent to external servers during or after scanning.

Can I scan multi-page documents into one PDF offline?

Yes. ScanTo Pro lets you scan multiple pages and merge them into a single PDF on-device without any internet connection.

Is offline scanning as accurate as cloud-based scanning?

For most documents, yes. The iPhone camera is high-resolution enough that on-device edge detection and image processing produce professional-quality results.

What formats does offline scanning support?

ScanTo Pro exports to PDF. You can then share it via AirDrop, email it, or save it to the Files app.
