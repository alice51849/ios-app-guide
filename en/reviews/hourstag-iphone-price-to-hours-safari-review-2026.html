HoursTag iPhone Price-to-Hours Safari Extension Review 2026

★★★★☆ (4/5)

Verdict: A simple but surprisingly powerful tool for mindful spending. Seeing prices expressed as hours of your work makes buying decisions feel more real.

This review is written by the app developer. All claims are verifiable — the limitations section describes real drawbacks.

✅ What We Like

  • Safari extension — shows conversion automatically while browsing
  • Immediate psychological impact on purchase decisions
  • One-time purchase, no subscription
  • Set up in under a minute

⚠️ Limitations

  • Safari only — doesn't work in other browsers
  • You manually enter your hourly rate (no automatic updates)
  • No purchase tracking or spending reports

Who It's For

Anyone who wants a simple mental framework for evaluating purchases — especially effective for impulsive online shoppers.

Who It's Not For

Users who don't primarily use Safari, or whose income varies too much to have a consistent hourly rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use HoursTag?

Enter your hourly rate in the app settings, then enable the Safari extension in Settings > Safari > Extensions. Prices on websites will show the hours equivalent automatically.

Does it work on all websites?

It works on most major retail and e-commerce sites. Compatibility depends on how each site displays prices.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. $0.99 one-time, no subscription.
