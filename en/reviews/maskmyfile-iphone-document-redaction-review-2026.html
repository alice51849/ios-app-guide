MaskMyFile iPhone Document Redaction Review 2026

Verdict: A focused, effective tool for permanently redacting personal information before sharing documents. Redaction is permanent and no data leaves your device.

This review is written by the app developer. All claims are verifiable — the limitations section describes real drawbacks.

✅ What We Like

  • Permanent redaction — cannot be reversed or recovered
  • Works completely offline
  • One-time purchase
  • Supports PDF and image files

⚠️ Limitations

  • No OCR verification of redacted text (you need to verify coverage manually)
  • Basic editing only — no advanced annotation features
  • Simple interface without collaboration tools

Who It's For

Anyone who needs to permanently hide SSNs, addresses, bank account numbers, or other PII before sharing documents.

Who It's Not For

Users who need full PDF editing, annotation, or collaboration features — this tool does one thing well.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the redaction be undone?

No. Redaction is permanent. There is no 'undo' — make sure to keep the original file if you need it.

Does it send documents to any server?

No. All processing happens locally on your device.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. $0.99 one-time, no subscription.
