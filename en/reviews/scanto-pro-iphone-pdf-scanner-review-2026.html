ScanTo Pro iPhone PDF Scanner Review 2026

Verdict: An excellent offline scanner for privacy-conscious users. Beats Apple Notes if you don't want your documents automatically syncing to iCloud.

This review is written by the app developer. All claims are verifiable — the limitations section describes real drawbacks.

✅ What We Like

  • Works completely offline — no cloud upload
  • Export PDF directly without creating an account
  • One-time purchase, no subscription
  • Simple, fast to use

⚠️ Limitations

  • OCR is basic compared to expensive premium scanners
  • No multi-device sync (by design)
  • No cloud sharing features

Who It's For

Freelancers, small business owners, and privacy-conscious users who scan documents but don't want them in iCloud.

Who It's Not For

Users who need advanced OCR accuracy or seamless sync across multiple devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ScanTo Pro require internet?

No. It works entirely offline. Documents are saved locally to your iPhone.

How does it compare to the built-in Apple Notes scanner?

Apple Notes scans go to iCloud by default. ScanTo Pro keeps everything on-device. For privacy, ScanTo Pro wins.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. $2.99 one-time, no subscription.
