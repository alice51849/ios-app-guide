Snapport iPhone Passport Photo App Review 2026

Verdict: A genuinely useful tool for taking passport and ID photos at home. Works well for standard documents — saves the trip to a photo shop for most use cases.

This review is written by the app developer. All claims are verifiable — the limitations section describes real drawbacks.

✅ What We Like

  • Auto-crops to official photo sizes for passports and IDs
  • Unlimited retakes after a one-time purchase
  • Completely offline — no photo uploaded anywhere
  • Covers photo requirements for many countries

⚠️ Limitations

  • Result quality depends entirely on the quality of your original photo
  • No artificial background — you need a real white background
  • Some strict government offices may require a certified photographer

Who It's For

Travelers who need passport or visa photos and want to avoid the hassle and cost of a photo shop.

Who It's Not For

Applications to government agencies with extremely strict photo requirements or those requiring a certified photographer's stamp.

Snapport crops to official specifications. Acceptance depends on the quality of the base photo and the specific agency's requirements. Works for most standard use cases.
