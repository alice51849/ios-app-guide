Zafe iPhone Photo Vault Review 2026

Verdict: A clean, privacy-focused photo vault that does exactly what it promises. Best for anyone who wants to store sensitive photos outside the camera roll without a monthly subscription.

This review is written by the app developer. All claims are verifiable — the limitations section describes real drawbacks.

✅ What We Like

  • One-time purchase — no subscription
  • Face ID/Touch ID with encrypted storage
  • No account or cloud upload required
  • Clean, simple interface

⚠️ Limitations

  • No cloud backup — photos in the vault are lost if you don't export before switching iPhone
  • Manual import only (no auto-sync)
  • Doesn't support in-app video recording

Who It's For

Anyone who wants a private place for sensitive photos without paying monthly fees.

Who It's Not For

Users whose primary goal is cloud backup of private photos across devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Zafe actually secure?

Yes. Photos are AES-encrypted and protected by Face ID or PIN. No internet connection required.

Does Zafe sync with iCloud?

No. Everything stays on-device. This is a feature, not a bug — it means your private photos never touch Apple's servers.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. $0.99 one-time, no subscription.
