Cat mini games: 200 cute cat games, one quick round at a time

If you typed “cat mini games” into the App Store, you probably want two things: a screen full of cute cats, and a game you can finish in a couple of minutes.

Plenty of cat games are one idea stretched out, or are packed with ads that interrupt every round. Meow Party takes the opposite approach: 200 original mini games, every one starring a cat from the Meow Squad, with quick rounds of about 30 to 120 seconds.

Below is what to look for in a cat mini game app, how to pick a game for your mood, and how Meow Party plays on your own or with a friend sitting across from you.

Five things to check in a cat mini game app

  • The cats are the stars: in Meow Party you always play as a cute cat from the Meow Squad, in all 200 original mini games.
  • Lots of variety: four families of 50 games each, so you are never stuck with one kind of play: party, puzzles, arcade and rhythm, and cosy creations.
  • Short rounds: about 30 to 120 seconds per round, with gradually rising difficulty, and many games have levels.
  • Room for a second player: most games work solo, and 54 let two players battle or team up face to face on one phone.
  • No interruptions: it plays offline, with no ads and no account. Your progress stays on your device.

Pick a cat mini game for your mood

Want something lively? Try the party family: 50 lively party games. When a friend is around, 54 games across the app let two of you play face to face on one phone.

Want to think? The puzzle family has 50 puzzles to solve.

Want to test your reflexes? Arcade and rhythm has 50 games of reflexes, timing and one-thumb skill.

Want to switch off? Cosy creations has 50 games of cooking, dress-up, gardening, simple management and wordless puzzles.

Not just cats: meet the party guests

You always play as a cat, and in two-player games both players are cats. Bunny, bear, chick, penguin, fox, frog and mochi friends show up as pals, customers, rivals and props, so every round feels like a small animal party.

Meow Party has no network features and works fully offline. It needs no account, collects no data, and does not use the camera, location, contacts or photos. The interface comes in 50 languages and follows your system language.

Your first cat mini game in one minute

  1. Open Meow Party and pick one of the four families.
  2. Playing alone? Start a game, or warm up against a cute computer rival.
  3. Playing with a friend? Put the phone between you and sit face to face.
  4. A round takes about 30 to 120 seconds; when it ends, move on to the next game.
  5. Want to slow down? Head to cosy creations for cooking, dress-up and gardening.

FAQ

Are all the characters cats?

You always play as a cute cat, and in two-player games both players are cats. Bunny, bear, chick, penguin, fox, frog and mochi friends appear as pals, customers, rivals and props.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Most games work solo, and cute computer rivals let you practise most of the two-player games on your own.

Do two players need two phones?

No. 54 of the games let two players battle or team up face to face on one phone. Just put the phone between you.

Does it work offline? Are there ads?

It plays fully offline with no network features and no ads. There is no account, and your progress stays on your device.
