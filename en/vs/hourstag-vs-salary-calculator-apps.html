Questions & Answers

Is HoursTag a salary calculator?

No. HoursTag is a spending awareness tool. You enter your hourly rate once, and then use it to convert prices into hours of work — to help you make more deliberate purchase decisions.

Can I use HoursTag while browsing the web?

Yes. HoursTag has a Safari Share Extension. When you see a price on any website, tap Share → HoursTag to instantly convert it to work hours without leaving Safari.

Does HoursTag calculate taxes or deductions?

No. You enter your net hourly rate (after taxes, as it's most meaningful for spending decisions), and HoursTag uses that as the base for all conversions.

Is HoursTag a one-time purchase?

Yes. One-time purchase with no subscription or ongoing cost.
