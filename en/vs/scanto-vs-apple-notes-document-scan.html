Questions & Answers

Can I create a standalone PDF file using Apple Notes?

Yes, but it takes extra steps — you need to share the note and choose to export as PDF. ScanTo Pro exports directly as a PDF file with one tap.

Do Apple Notes scans upload to iCloud?

If iCloud Notes sync is enabled (which is the default), yes — your scanned documents sync to iCloud and are accessible from icloud.com and other Apple devices.

Does ScanTo Pro work without an Apple ID or iCloud?

Yes. ScanTo Pro doesn't require any account or iCloud sync. Scans are stored locally on your device.

Which is faster for a quick one-page scan?

Both are roughly equal in speed for a one-page scan. Apple Notes has the advantage of being pre-installed; ScanTo Pro has the advantage of saving directly as a PDF without extra steps.
