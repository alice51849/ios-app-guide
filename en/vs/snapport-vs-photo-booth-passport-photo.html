Questions & Answers

Will passport authorities accept a photo taken with Snapport?

Snapport follows official specifications. Acceptance depends on your lighting, background, and expression. Many users successfully use home-taken passport photos — but results vary.

What do I need for a good home passport photo?

A plain white or gray wall, good even lighting (no harsh shadows on face or background), and a neutral expression. Natural window light works well.

How much does it cost per passport photo with Snapport?

After the one-time app purchase, each additional photo set costs nothing — you can retake as many times as you want.

Can I use Snapport for passport renewal?

Yes. Snapport includes the photo specifications for passport applications in many countries, including renewal specifications.
