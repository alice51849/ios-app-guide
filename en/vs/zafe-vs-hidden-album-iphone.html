Questions & Answers

Can someone see my iPhone Hidden Album without my password?

Yes. The Hidden Album in Photos doesn't require Face ID, Touch ID, or a PIN by default. Anyone with physical access to your unlocked iPhone can view the hidden photos by navigating to Albums > Hidden.

Does the iPhone Hidden Album sync to iCloud?

By default, yes. If you use iCloud Photos, your hidden photos also sync to iCloud and can be viewed from icloud.com or another logged-in device.

Does Zafe require Face ID every time you open it?

Yes. Zafe prompts for Face ID or PIN authentication every time the app is opened.

What happens to my Zafe photos if I delete the app?

If you delete Zafe, the photos stored in it are deleted too. Always export any photos you want to keep before deleting the app.
