The iPhone Freelancer Workflow: 4 Apps to Run Your Business from Your Phone (2026)

You don't need a laptop to run a freelance business. This four-app iPhone workflow covers the core needs: time awareness, document management, privacy, and focus.

1
Set your hourly rate in HoursTag Lite

HoursTag Lite converts any price into hours of work. Set your net hourly rate once and use it whenever you're evaluating a tool, subscription, or business expense. Tap Share → HoursTag Lite in Safari to convert prices in any browser.

Get HoursTag Lite → Google Play
2
Scan client documents with ScanTo Pro

When a client sends a paper contract or you receive a receipt, open ScanTo Pro to scan it immediately as a PDF. No internet required. Export to your Files app or share by AirDrop or email.

Get ScanTo Pro →
3
Redact sensitive data before sharing with MaskMyFile

Before forwarding a contract or invoice that contains personal info (bank details, tax IDs), open MaskMyFile, import the file, tap over the sensitive areas, and share the redacted version.

Get Mask My File → Google Play
4
Block distractions during deep work with LockHour Pro

When you need to focus on client work, start a LockHour Pro session. Select the apps to block (social media, news), set the duration, and tap Start. Unblocks automatically when the session ends.

Get LockHour Pro → Google Play

Questions & Answers

Do I need internet for any of these apps?

HoursTag Lite, ScanTo Pro, MaskMyFile, and LockHour Pro all work offline. You only need internet to download the apps initially.

The four apps together cost under $15 USD — a one-time purchase with no subscriptions.

Yes. All four apps are compatible with both iPhone and iPad.
