The iPhone Privacy Toolkit: 3 Apps to Protect Your Data on iPhone (2026)

Your iPhone stores a lot of sensitive information. This three-app system gives you control over where your data goes — and keeps it off the cloud.

1
Move private photos to Zafe

Open Zafe, set up Face ID or PIN, then tap + to import photos from your camera roll. After importing, delete the originals from the camera roll. Your photos now exist only inside Zafe's encrypted vault, protected by Face ID.

Get Zafe →
2
Scan sensitive documents with ScanTo Pro

When you need a digital copy of a passport, medical document, or contract, use ScanTo Pro. It works offline and never uploads your scans. Export to the Files app and keep it local.

Get ScanTo Pro →
3
Redact before sharing with MaskMyFile

Before forwarding any document that contains personal data, open MaskMyFile. Import the file, black out the sensitive parts (SSN, bank account, address), then share the redacted version. The masking is permanent on the shared copy.

Questions & Answers

Does this privacy toolkit require iCloud?

No. Zafe, ScanTo Pro, and MaskMyFile all work without iCloud. Your data stays on your device.

Is this suitable for GDPR compliance?

These apps process data locally without sending it to external servers, which supports GDPR-compliant data handling. Check with a legal adviser for your specific situation.

What if someone gets access to my iPhone?

Zafe requires Face ID or PIN. ScanTo Pro and MaskMyFile don't have their own lock, so your iPhone passcode is the main protection for those files.
