Questions & Answers

Is the Pomodoro technique effective for studying?

Research supports that alternating focused work blocks (25 min) with short breaks (5 min) improves sustained concentration. LockHour Pro is built around this approach.

Can SonoNote record long lectures?

Yes. SonoNote can record for extended sessions limited by your iPhone storage. A standard one-hour lecture uses roughly 30–60 MB of audio storage.

Do these apps have free trials?
