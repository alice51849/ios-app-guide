The iPhone Travel Prep Workflow: 4 Apps Before Your Next Trip (2026)

Four apps that handle the most annoying pre-trip admin tasks: your passport photo, document backups, secure storage, and spending context — all from your phone.

Take your passport photo with Snapport

Open Snapport, select the destination country, and follow the on-screen framing guide. Natural light from a window and a plain white wall work well. Download the photo file when satisfied.

Questions & Answers

How far in advance should I take my passport photo?

Ideally at least a few days before applying, in case you need to retake it. Snapport makes retaking easy since you can do it at home at any time.

Do these apps work in airplane mode?

Yes. Snapport, ScanTo Pro, Zafe, and HoursTag all work offline with no internet connection needed.

Is this workflow suitable for frequent travellers?

Yes. The workflow is designed to be repeatable — update your document scans each time you travel and retake passport photos when they approach expiry.
