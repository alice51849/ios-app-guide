respuesta corta

A Greece passport photo must be 40×60 mm on a light grey or white background, with the face centred, front-facing. Aim for at least 472×709 px, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. Greek passport photos commonly use 40×60 mm; confirm the exact size with your office.

Snapport Lite lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 40×60 mm frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

Qué buscar antes de elegir

  • Exact output size: 40×60 mm (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: a light grey or white background with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face centred, front-facing.
  • Expresión neutra, ojos abiertos, boca cerrada, sin reflejos en las gafas ni prendas que cubran la cabeza.
  • Foto reciente, a todo color y en alta resolución; consulta las normas oficiales más recientes antes de presentarla.

Un proceso de decisión práctico

  1. Colócate ante una pared lisa e iluminada de forma uniforme, con luz natural.
  2. Encuadra de frente y haz la foto con Snapport Lite.
  3. Auto-replace the background with a light grey.
  4. Crop to the 40×60 mm template with the face sized correctly.
  5. Exporta a resolución completa para imprimir o subirla a la solicitud.
