respuesta corta

A Turkish CV / özgeçmiş follows local hiring conventions: written in Turkish with a passport-style photo and a header listing date of birth and marital status, plus — uniquely — a military-service status field for male candidates. Typical length is 1–2 pages. Photo: Yes — a professional photo is expected. Matching these expectations matters as much as the content.

CV Desk te ayuda a montar la estructura adecuada desde el iPhone, mantener un diseño legible para los ATS y exportar un PDF limpio y sin marca de agua. Adapta la redacción a cada oferta antes de enviarlo.

Qué buscar antes de elegir

  • Correct format for a Turkish CV / özgeçmiş: written in Turkish with a passport-style photo and a header listing date of birth and marital status, plus — uniquely — a military-service status field for male candidates.
  • Length: 1–2 pages.
  • Photo: Yes — a professional photo is expected.
  • ATS-readable layout (single column, standard fonts, no text in images).
  • Clean PDF export with no watermark; tailor keywords to each job posting.

Un proceso de decisión práctico

  1. Pick a Turkish CV / özgeçmiş-appropriate template.
  2. Fill in reverse-chronological experience with measurable results.
  3. Apply the local photo rule (Yes).
  4. Mirror keywords from the target job description for ATS.
  5. Export as PDF and check it opens cleanly on desktop.
