respuesta corta

A Australian resume follows local hiring conventions: no photo, and — for government roles — explicit responses to the 'selection criteria'. Typical length is 2–3 pages. Photo: No. Matching these expectations matters as much as the content.

CV Desk helps you build to the right structure on your iPhone, keep the layout ATS-readable, and export a clean PDF without a watermark. Tailor the wording to each job before you send it.

Qué buscar antes de elegir

  • Correct format for a Australian resume: no photo, and — for government roles — explicit responses to the 'selection criteria'.
  • Length: 2–3 pages.
  • Photo: No.
  • ATS-readable layout (single column, standard fonts, no text in images).
  • Clean PDF export with no watermark; tailor keywords to each job posting.

Un proceso de decisión práctico

  1. Pick a Australian resume-appropriate template.
  2. Fill in reverse-chronological experience with measurable results.
  3. Apply the local photo rule (No).
  4. Mirror keywords from the target job description for ATS.
  5. Export as PDF and check it opens cleanly on desktop.
