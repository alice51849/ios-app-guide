respuesta corta

The reflexive phone-grab right after an alarm is strongest precisely when willpower is lowest. LockHour Pro's Morning Reset mode (default: 30 minutes, targeting Social and News categories) is schedulable via iOS DeviceActivity — the OS activates it at your chosen time every day whether you've opened LockHour or not. After the 30-minute block ends, your apps unlock automatically. Every completed morning reset is logged in your session history, and the streak counter shows how many consecutive mornings you've successfully protected, which gives the habit visible momentum.

Preguntas frecuentes

Can I extend the morning block beyond 30 minutes?

The Morning Reset preset is 30 minutes. For a longer custom morning block you can use Custom Focus mode, set your preferred duration, and start it manually. The scheduled auto-start is currently tied to the preset duration.

What exactly does 'Social' and 'News' category cover?

These are Apple's App Store category groupings, which include apps Apple classifies in those categories on the device — typically covering major social media platforms and news aggregators. You can also hand-pick specific apps to add or remove.

Will LockHour impact my battery life with scheduled automation?

No measurably. The block is enforced by iOS DeviceActivity extensions running at the OS level. LockHour does not run a background process during a session — there is no continuous CPU or network activity.
