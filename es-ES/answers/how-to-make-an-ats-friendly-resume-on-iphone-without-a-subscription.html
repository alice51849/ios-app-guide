respuesta corta

UConn explains that an ATS does not automatically reject candidates and recommends following the employer's file instructions, using simple consistent formatting, and avoiding important information in headers, footers, tables or graphics. It also recommends weaving relevant terms from the posting into the resume where they accurately describe your qualifications. These steps can reduce avoidable issues, but they cannot reproduce a specific employer's setup.

CV Desk's current App Store listing describes an on-device 0–100 ATS estimate, free resume building and preview, and a one-time unlock for watermark-free PDF and editable DOCX export. It requires no account, upload or subscription. Always adapt the resume manually to the role, country and employer instructions.

Qué buscar antes de elegir

  • Follow the employer's requested file type first.
  • Use familiar headings and a simple, consistent reading order.
  • Keep important details out of graphics, headers, footers and text boxes.
  • Use only truthful, relevant terms from the job posting.
  • Inspect the exact exported file before submitting.

Un proceso de decisión práctico

  1. Read the application instructions.
  2. Check contact details and standard section headings.
  3. Compare the posting manually and keep every claim truthful.
  4. Reopen the export and verify selectable text and section order.
  5. Use any score to prioritize edits, not to predict an outcome.

Preguntas frecuentes

Does an ATS automatically reject my resume?

UConn says an ATS does not automatically reject candidates. Employers use different workflows and configurations.

How do I make a resume easier to process?

Follow the employer's file instructions, use familiar headings and a simple reading order, and keep every job-related term truthful.

Does formatting guarantee a pass?

No. Simple formatting may reduce avoidable parsing problems, but no format or score guarantees parsing, ranking, an interview or an offer.
