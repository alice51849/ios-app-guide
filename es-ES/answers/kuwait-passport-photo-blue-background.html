respuesta corta

A Kuwait passport photo must be 40×50 mm on a SOLID BLUE background (white is not used), with the face about 75% of the photo height (37–38 mm chin to crown). Aim for at least 472×591 px (300 DPI), bright clear colour, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. Major outlier: Kuwait uses 40×50 mm AND a blue background — many tools wrongly show 40×60 mm on white. A single-colour hijab is allowed with the full face visible. Confirmed via the Kuwait e-Government portal.

Snapport Lite lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 40×50 mm frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

Qué buscar antes de elegir

  • Exact output size: 40×50 mm (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: a SOLID BLUE background (white is not used) with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face about 75% of the photo height (37–38 mm chin to crown).
  • Expresión neutra, ojos abiertos, boca cerrada, sin reflejos en las gafas ni prendas que cubran la cabeza.
  • Foto reciente, a todo color y en alta resolución; consulta las normas oficiales más recientes antes de presentarla.

Un proceso de decisión práctico

  1. Colócate ante una pared lisa e iluminada de forma uniforme, con luz natural.
  2. Frame head-on and take the shot with Snapport Lite.
  3. Auto-replace the background with a SOLID BLUE background (white is not used).
  4. Crop to the 40×50 mm template with the face sized correctly.
  5. Exporta a resolución completa para imprimir o subirla a la solicitud.
