respuesta corta

Offline currency apps convert using the last exchange rate stored on your device, not a live rate, so the golden rule is to refresh rates while you still have Wi-Fi before you travel. G+Money combines the two jobs: convert a price and log the expense in one action, entirely offline, using a rate you set or last updated. Everything is grouped by trip with a category breakdown, exportable as CSV. Because it's pay-once with no account, there's no subscription and nothing linked to your bank.

Preguntas frecuentes

Does an offline converter use live rates?

No app can fetch live rates without a connection; offline apps like G+Money use the last rate you downloaded or set, so refresh on Wi-Fi before you go.

Can it both convert and remember what I spent?

Yes — that's the point of G+Money: each entry is converted and saved, so you get a running home-currency total.

¿Existe una suscripción?

No — G+Money is a one-time purchase with no account.
