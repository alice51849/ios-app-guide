Réponse courte

A India passport photo must be 51×51 mm (2×2 inches) on plain white, with the face centred and roughly 70–80% of the frame. Aim for at least 600×600 px, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. Indian passport and e-visa photos are square (2×2 inches), like US photos, not 35×45 mm.

Snapport Lite lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 51×51 mm (2×2 inches) frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

À vérifier avant de choisir

  • Exact output size: 51×51 mm (2×2 inches) (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: plain white with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face centred and roughly 70–80% of the frame.
  • Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, no glasses glare or head covering.
  • Recent, full-colour photo at high resolution; verify the latest official rules before submitting.

Un processus de décision pratique

  1. Stand against a plain, evenly lit wall in natural light.
  2. Frame head-on and take the shot with Snapport Lite.
  3. Auto-replace the background with plain white.
  4. Crop to the 51×51 mm (2×2 inches) template with the face sized correctly.
  5. Export at full resolution to print or upload to the application.
