Réponse courte

G+Money Lite lets you create one trip and save up to three expenses free. For each purchase, enter the amount in the local currency, preview the home-currency conversion, and check the day's remaining budget or overage before saving. Rates, the trip budget, category statistics and the summary remain available in the free tier, so the test covers the actual decision workflow. If the allowance fits, one optional lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited trips and expenses; there is no recurring subscription, account or tracking.

Un processus de décision pratique

  1. Set the home currency and create the included trip.
  2. Choose the local currency and an appropriate live, saved or manual rate.
  3. Enter a realistic purchase and inspect its home-currency amount.
  4. Check the remaining daily budget or overage, then save up to three expenses.
  5. Review the category statistics and summary before deciding whether to unlock unlimited use.

Yes. The workflow previews the home-currency conversion and the day's remaining budget or overage before the expense is saved.

No. One optional lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited trips and expenses; there is no recurring subscription.
