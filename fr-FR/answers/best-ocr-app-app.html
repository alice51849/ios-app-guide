Réponse courte

When selecting an OCR app, consider factors like ease of use, the ability to scan directly to PDF, and privacy features. Look for apps that offer a one-time purchase option without ongoing subscription fees.

ScanTo Pro meets these criteria effectively. It allows users to scan documents, perform OCR searches, and secure files with Face ID, all while ensuring privacy through on-device processing. This makes it a strong option for those wanting a reliable and private scanning solution.
