Réponse courte

General sharpening (the Sharpness slider in Photos or a basic editor) only strengthens edges that are already there, so it does little for a genuinely out-of-focus or motion-blurred shot. Purpose-built repair apps apply targeted processing — deblur, denoise, low-light recovery — for each kind of problem. On privacy: many online unblur tools require uploading your photo. Unblurry runs entirely on your iPhone with no cloud and no account, and offers several dedicated modes (Auto Clear, Sharpen, Denoise, Low Light and more) for different blur types, as a one-time purchase. No app can perfectly rebuild a badly blurred photo, but a dedicated on-device tool gives soft, shaky and low-light shots the best realistic chance.

FAQ

Is the built-in Photos sharpen enough to fix a blurry photo?

For slightly soft images, the Sharpness and Definition sliders help. For real out-of-focus, motion blur, noise or low-light shots, a dedicated tool with targeted modes does more. Unblurry offers several such modes and runs on-device.

Do unblur apps upload my photo to a server?

Many online tools do, which is a privacy concern. Unblurry processes everything on your iPhone with no cloud and no account, so your photo never leaves the device.

Can any app perfectly fix a badly blurred photo?

No — heavily blurred photos can't be fully rebuilt. But a dedicated on-device app like Unblurry, with modes for deblur, denoise and low light, gives soft or shaky shots the best realistic improvement without uploading them.
