Réponse courte

A searchable PDF needs OCR — the app has to actually read the text in your scan so you can find it later. Many popular scanners send your document to a server to do that, which is a privacy problem for contracts, IDs and receipts, and others put OCR and export behind a monthly fee. The private approach is on-device OCR that never goes online. ScanTo Pro works this way: it turns paper into clean PDFs and reads the text right on your iPhone, so you can search inside your documents later — fully offline, no account, no cloud, as a one-time purchase. That means sensitive paperwork is captured and made searchable without ever being uploaded.
