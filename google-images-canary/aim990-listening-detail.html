How can I practice listening for a missed detail?

Use the real Aim990 workflow shown here: The review targets the listening process rather than memorizing one recording.

Creative: aim990-listening-detail

The problem

Replaying audio without a plan may improve familiarity without fixing why the detail was missed.

The useful result

The review targets the listening process rather than memorizing one recording.

Daily listening and reading plans, weak-spot drills, and progress tracking.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Answer once under normal timing.
  2. Review whether the missed cue was vocabulary, distractor, or attention.
  3. Try a new item with the same listening objective before replaying excessively.
