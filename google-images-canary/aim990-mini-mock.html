When is a mini mock test more useful than a full mock?

Use the real Aim990 workflow shown here: You get a compact performance snapshot with an immediate review step.

Creative: aim990-mini-mock

The problem

A full mock can be too costly when the goal is a quick pacing check or the available time is short.

The useful result

You get a compact performance snapshot with an immediate review step.

Daily listening and reading plans, weak-spot drills, and progress tracking.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Choose the mini mock that fits the available block.
  2. Complete it under uninterrupted test-like conditions.
  3. Review the result by skill and schedule follow-up practice instead of extrapolating a guaranteed score.
