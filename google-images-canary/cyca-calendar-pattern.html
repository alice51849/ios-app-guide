The problem

Remembering dates from several weeks ago is unreliable when looking for a recurring personal pattern.

The useful result

The calendar becomes an organized observation record rather than medical proof.

A warm, private way to understand your cycle.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Open the calendar and review recorded dates across more than one cycle.
  2. Note changes in timing without labelling them normal or abnormal yourself.
  3. Bring the record, not a self-diagnosis, to a clinician if you are concerned.

What this page does not claim

Cycle estimates are informational patterns, not diagnosis, contraception, or a promise about fertility. Record what actually happens and consult a qualified clinician about concerning changes.
