The problem

Forecasts are estimates and become misleading if remembered predictions replace recorded events.

The useful result

You separate predicted context from actual history and can see where they differed.

A warm, private way to understand your cycle.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Review the forecast before the date without treating it as certain.
  2. Record the actual event or feeling when it occurs.
  3. Compare later and rely on the observed record for future discussions.

What this page does not claim

Cycle estimates are informational patterns, not diagnosis, contraception, or a promise about fertility. Record what actually happens and consult a qualified clinician about concerning changes.
