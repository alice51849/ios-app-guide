The problem

Sensitive observations should be recorded only when the storage and sharing choices feel appropriate.

The useful result

You keep a deliberately scoped personal record rather than an uncontrolled data dump.

A warm, private way to understand your cycle.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Review the app's on-device and access settings.
  2. Record only the detail useful to you.
  3. Revisit old entries and remove information you no longer want to retain.

What this page does not claim

Cycle estimates are informational patterns, not diagnosis, contraception, or a promise about fertility. Record what actually happens and consult a qualified clinician about concerning changes.
