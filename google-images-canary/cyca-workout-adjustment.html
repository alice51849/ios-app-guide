The problem

The useful result

The plan stays responsive to real-time condition instead of being controlled by a forecast.

A warm, private way to understand your cycle.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Review your own recent energy and symptom entries.
  2. Choose a flexible intensity range rather than a fixed prediction-based target.
  3. Change or stop the session based on current signs and professional guidance.

What this page does not claim

Cycle estimates are informational patterns, not diagnosis, contraception, or a promise about fertility. Record what actually happens and consult a qualified clinician about concerning changes.
