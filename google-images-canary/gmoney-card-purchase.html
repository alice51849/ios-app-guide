The problem

A card terminal amount is known immediately, but the issuer's final home-currency charge and fees may arrive later.

The useful result

You preserve the transaction context while clearly separating an estimate from the bank's final amount.

Convert and log travel spending in one place, including offline.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Log the local amount and category at purchase time.
  2. Use the displayed conversion as a planning estimate.
  3. Reconcile it against the settled card charge and fee later.

What this page does not claim

G+Money is a manual travel ledger, not a bank feed or accounting system. Exchange-rate totals are planning aids; verify card fees, cash conversions, receipts, and official reimbursement rules.
