The problem

Frequent small meals and rides are difficult to compare from receipts alone.

The useful result

You see a comparable category split while acknowledging that unlogged purchases remain absent.

Convert and log travel spending in one place, including offline.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Use the same food and transport categories throughout the trip.
  2. Log every cash and card purchase manually.
  3. Review both category totals and inspect missing days before drawing a conclusion.

What this page does not claim

G+Money is a manual travel ledger, not a bank feed or accounting system. Exchange-rate totals are planning aids; verify card fees, cash conversions, receipts, and official reimbursement rules.
