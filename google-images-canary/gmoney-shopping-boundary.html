The problem

Optional purchases can consume the remaining trip budget before essential costs are considered.

The useful result

The boundary stays visible at the moment of choice instead of appearing only after the trip.

Convert and log travel spending in one place, including offline.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Reserve expected transport, lodging, and food first.
  2. Set an optional-shopping amount in the trip plan.
  3. Check the running estimate before each nonessential purchase.

What this page does not claim

G+Money is a manual travel ledger, not a bank feed or accounting system. Exchange-rate totals are planning aids; verify card fees, cash conversions, receipts, and official reimbursement rules.
