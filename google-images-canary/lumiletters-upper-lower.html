The problem

A child may know a capital letter but not recognize the lowercase shape in books.

The useful result

The child practices visual equivalence without turning the session into a test.

Playful phonics, tracing, and letter practice for young learners.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Pick one uppercase and lowercase pair from the letter grid.
  2. Say the shared sound while pointing to both forms.
  3. Find the same pair once more, then stop or switch activities.

What this page does not claim

The activities support practice but do not guarantee a reading milestone or replace an educator's assessment. An adult should choose a short session, observe comfort, and stop before frustration.
