The problem

The useful result

You maintain a smaller local working archive with deliberate privacy and retention choices.

Scan, organize, search, and protect documents on the device.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Scan only the documents you genuinely need.
  2. Review on-device processing and lock settings before filing.
  3. Periodically remove expired records according to your own retention obligations.

What this page does not claim

A phone scan is a convenient working copy, not automatically a certified original. Review every crop and OCR result, and follow the recipient's rules for signatures, identity, and retention.
