The problem

A very quiet room can make small household noises feel more noticeable, but louder sound is not automatically better.

The useful result

You create a modest, reversible ambient layer without claiming a sleep treatment.

Layer living sounds for sleep, focus, and calm, including offline.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Choose one neutral sound from the library.
  2. Start at a low, comfortable volume.
  3. Listen for several minutes and lower or stop it if it masks important sounds or feels tiring.

What this page does not claim

Sereno is an ambient-sound tool, not treatment for insomnia, tinnitus, anxiety, or another condition. Keep volume comfortable, preserve awareness when safety matters, and seek professional care for persistent symptoms.
