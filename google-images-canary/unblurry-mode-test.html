The problem

Several enhancement choices are available, but the strongest option is not automatically the most faithful.

The useful result

You select a mode through a repeatable comparison instead of maximizing an abstract setting.

Blurry photo? Make it crystal clear.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Pick one representative detail such as an eye, edge, or sign.
  2. Preview each relevant mode once against that same detail.
  3. Choose the mildest result that improves clarity without halos or plastic texture.

What this page does not claim

Enhancement can make existing edges and texture easier to see, but it cannot recover detail the camera never captured. Inspect faces, text, and fine patterns at full size before keeping the export.
