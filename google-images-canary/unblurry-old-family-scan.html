The problem

A small scan has compression and soft edges, and the goal is a cleaner viewing copy without rewriting faces.

The useful result

You finish with a cautiously reviewed derivative suitable for viewing, while preserving the archival scan.

Blurry photo? Make it crystal clear.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Duplicate the highest-resolution scan.
  2. Try the mode that best matches the amount of softness, then inspect the preview.
  3. Compare eyes, jewelry, fabric, and printed text before exporting.

What this page does not claim

Enhancement can make existing edges and texture easier to see, but it cannot recover detail the camera never captured. Inspect faces, text, and fine patterns at full size before keeping the export.
