The problem

A product image is readable but soft around labels, seams, or surface texture that a buyer may inspect.

The useful result

You obtain a clearer candidate without treating enhancement as permission to misrepresent condition or detail.

Blurry photo? Make it crystal clear.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Open the full-resolution source.
  2. Compare the enhanced and original views around labels and material edges.
  3. Use the result only when it remains faithful to the item being sold.

What this page does not claim

Enhancement can make existing edges and texture easier to see, but it cannot recover detail the camera never captured. Inspect faces, text, and fine patterns at full size before keeping the export.
