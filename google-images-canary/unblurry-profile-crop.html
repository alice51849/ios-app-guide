The problem

Cropping a portrait reduces available pixels and can make the final profile image look soft.

The useful result

The result is a reviewed profile crop that keeps a natural appearance.

Blurry photo? Make it crystal clear.

A practical three-step flow

  1. Crop from the largest original first.
  2. Choose a restrained enhancement mode for the cropped image.
  3. Inspect skin, hair, glasses, and background edges at the final display size.

What this page does not claim

Enhancement can make existing edges and texture easier to see, but it cannot recover detail the camera never captured. Inspect faces, text, and fine patterns at full size before keeping the export.
