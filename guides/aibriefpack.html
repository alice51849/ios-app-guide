Private AI context preparation on iPhone: what to check

When screenshots, PDFs, notes, files and links contain the evidence for an AI task, a useful context builder should turn them into one reviewable brief without silently uploading, deleting or inventing details. A reliable workflow should combine mixed sources, use on-device OCR and PDF reading, and keep each extracted fact connected to its source and confidence so conflicts and open questions remain visible before export.

What to look for

  • Mixed-source import for screenshots, PDFs, files, copied text, notes and links.
  • On-device extraction with source references, confidence and conflict review.
  • Explicit keep, replace or remove choices for detected private details.
  • Structured briefs with templates, version history and Share Extension intake.
  • A free entry point with one lifetime Pro unlock and no subscription, account, ads or tracking.
