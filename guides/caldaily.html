Calculators that keep a searchable history: what to check

A calculator worth keeping should remember more than the last number: what the calculation was for, which tool produced it, and how to find it again a week later. CalDaily saves each result with a name and a type, groups history by day, filters by tool and exports to CSV, so a split dinner bill or a compared loan can be searched for instead of recalculated from memory. Eight advanced tools cover the awkward everyday maths — memory and scientific functions, discount and tax, tip and split, unit conversion, date differences and business days, loan payments, and trip fuel cost — each opening with defaults you set once.

What to look for

  • History entries you can name, type, search and group by day.
  • CSV export when the numbers need to leave the phone.
  • Advanced tools that remember your defaults (tax rate, party size).
  • A Home Screen widget that matches the app's theme.
  • One-time unlock rather than a subscription.
  • On-device only — no account, cloud sync, ads or analytics.
