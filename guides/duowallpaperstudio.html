Wallpapers for a foldable's two screens: what to check

A foldable has two screens with different shapes, so a single wallpaper is usually cropped badly on one of them and looks accidental on both. A wallpaper made for a foldable should be a pair: a close-up that suits the small cover screen and the full scene for the large inner screen, cut from the same artwork so closing and opening the phone feels like one picture zooming in and out. Check that the pair is sized to the real pixel dimensions of each screen rather than scaled afterwards.

What to look for

  • A true pair for each set: a close-up for the cover screen and the full scene for the inner screen.
  • Export sized to each screen's real pixels, so the picture is not stretched or blurred afterwards.
  • Closed and open previews in the app before you save anything.
  • Original artwork, with no characters or works from other brands.
  • No ads, no account and no tracking, with photos saved through add-only access.
