Two-player games on one phone: what to check

Most two-player phone games turn out to be a handful of ideas padded with ads, or they need a second phone and an internet connection before anyone can start. Look for games built for two people facing each other across one screen: each player gets a side, rounds are short enough to swap in a new game often, and nothing interrupts the play. Meow Party has 200 original cat minigames; 54 of them are made for two players on one phone, with rounds of about 30 to 120 seconds, and a Party mode strings 3, 5 or 10 random games together with a running scoreboard.

What to look for

  • Two-player games where each person plays from their own side of one screen.
  • Short rounds of about 30 to 120 seconds, plus solo games with levels that get harder.
  • A Party mode of 3, 5 or 10 random games with a scoreboard.
  • A computer rival for practice when you are on your own.
  • Offline play with no ads, no account and no in-app purchases.
