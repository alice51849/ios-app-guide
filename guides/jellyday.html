A visual daily planner with a timeline: what to check

A plain list says what to do today but not when, or how much of the day each thing will take, so the afternoon quietly runs out. Look for a planner that lays the day out as a timeline. When every item is a block that gets taller the longer it lasts, a crowded morning is obvious before it starts. JellyDay draws each task as a pastel jelly block, shows a "now" line for the current moment, and lets you set a duration from 5 minutes to 2 hours.

What to look for

  • A Today timeline where block height follows how long each task lasts.
  • A "now" line that shows where you are in the day.
  • Drag a block to a new time in 5-minute steps.
  • Push back by 15 minutes or move to tomorrow from one menu.
  • No account, no advertising and no tracking; data stays on your device.
