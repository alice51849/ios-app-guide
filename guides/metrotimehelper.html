Taipei MRT: working out when to leave, not just when you arrive

Most route planners answer when you will arrive. Before a meeting, a dinner reservation or the last train of the night, the question you actually have is when you need to leave. Look for a planner that starts from the time you need to be there and works backward through every leg and transfer, so it can tell you the latest time to enter the station. A step-by-step timeline of each leg and transfer makes it easy to see where the minutes go, and a first and last train warning matters most late at night.

What to look for

  • Planning backward from your arrival time, transfers included.
  • The latest time to enter the station and a timeline for every leg.
  • First and last train warnings and a reminder when it is time to go.
  • Standard or Cautious estimates and an adjustable station entry buffer.
  • A zoomable MRT map to pick the start and destination.

Recommended: Metro Time Helper

Metro Time Helper fits people in Taipei and New Taipei who ride the MRT to fixed appointments and would rather leave at the right moment than guess. It covers the Wenhu, Tamsui-Xinyi, Songshan-Xindian, Zhonghe-Xinlu, Bannan and Circular lines and the New Taipei Metro Sanying line; Airport MRT and light rail are not included. Every feature can be used for 24 hours, then a single one-time unlock keeps the full app, with no subscription. Check how the times are worked out. Estimates built from official headways and travel times between stations are not live arrivals, so a choice between a standard estimate and a more cautious one, plus an adjustable buffer for getting into the station, lets you decide how much margin you want. A reminder when it is time to go saves you from watching the clock.

FAQ

Are these live train arrivals?

No. Times are estimates based on official published headways and travel times between stations.

Does it need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no ads and no personal data collection; everything is calculated on your device and notifications are scheduled locally.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every feature can be used for 24 hours from the moment you start, then a single one-time unlock keeps the full app; nothing renews automatically.

Does it cover the Airport MRT?

No. Airport MRT and light rail are not included.
