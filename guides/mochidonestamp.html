Last-time tracking on iPhone: what to check

For chores and upkeep that do not belong on a rigid calendar, a useful tracker remembers when you actually finished and measures the next interval from that moment. This is different from another to-do list: each tap should add an exact completion time to a durable history, while optional approximate or exact rhythms help with recurring events such as changing sheets, watering plants, replacing filters or backing up photos.

What to look for

  • One-tap completion logging with a clear chronological history.
  • Approximate and exact rhythms recalculated from actual completion.
  • Backdating, editing and deletion for corrections.
  • Local reminders plus Home and Lock Screen widgets.
  • A one-time unlock, on-device core data and a complete backup option.
