A cute trip itinerary you can share with friends: what to check

A trip plan that lives in a spreadsheet or a long chat thread is hard to read on the way to the airport, and nobody wants to open an app they have to install just to see the flight time. The plan that actually gets used is the one companions can open without effort. Look for an itinerary app that turns the trip into a single scrolling page with the countdown, flights, day-by-day plans and packing list together, and that can export that page as a file anyone opens in a normal browser, with no account and no app to install.

What to look for

  • One illustrated page per trip: countdown, flights, daily plans and a packing list.
  • A web booklet saved as one file that opens in any browser, offline.
  • Optional iCloud planning with friends instead of a new account.
  • A layout that works on iPhone and iPad.
  • No subscription and no advertising.
