Free 5-Day Zhuyin Lesson Plan for Heritage Chinese Schools

A five-day starter mini-unit with 30 minutes of instruction per day for weekend Chinese schools, heritage-language families and homeschool groups.

This plan introduces a reusable learning routine and a 12-symbol starter set. It does not claim to teach all 37 Zhuyin symbols in five days.

English / 繁體中文

At a glance

Age
4–8, beginner
Time
5 days × 30 minutes
Group
One child, small group or weekend class
Materials
Symbol cards, pencil, printed grid and a device only if desired

Learning outcomes

  • Recognize and pronounce the 12-symbol starter set.
  • Blend selected initials with ㄚ, ㄧ, ㄨ or ㄩ slowly.
  • Hear basic contrasts among Mandarin tones.
  • Use the same listen → see → move → play → check routine for the remaining symbols.

Five-day plan

Day 1 — Hear and see ㄅ ㄆ ㄇ ㄈ + ㄚ

Objective: Connect five new shapes to their sounds without asking for memorization.

  1. 5 min: teacher models each sound; children watch the mouth and echo once.
  2. 8 min: trace large symbols in the air, then on a printed grid.
  3. 10 min: sound hunt — hold up the matching card when a sound is heard.
  4. 5 min: optional Lumi Bopomofo listen-and-tap practice.
  5. 2 min exit check: identify any two symbols without pressure.

Day 2 — Add ㄉ ㄊ ㄋ ㄌ and review ㄚ

Objective: Notice how tongue placement changes these four initial sounds.

  1. 5 min: quick card review of Day 1.
  2. 8 min: teacher models ㄉ ㄊ ㄋ ㄌ slowly; children echo and compare.
  3. 10 min: sort mixed cards into yesterday/today groups, saying each sound.
  4. 5 min: trace only the two symbols each child finds hardest.
  5. 2 min exit check: hear one sound and point to its symbol.

Day 3 — Add ㄧ ㄨ ㄩ and start blending

Objective: Move from isolated symbols to slow, audible blends such as ㄅㄧ and ㄋㄧ.

  1. 5 min: review ㄅ ㄆ ㄇ ㄈ ㄉ ㄊ ㄋ ㄌ with shuffled cards.
  2. 8 min: introduce ㄧ ㄨ ㄩ with clear mouth-shape contrasts.
  3. 10 min: slide an initial card toward a medial card while blending aloud.
  4. 5 min: optional syllable-train practice in Lumi Bopomofo.
  5. 2 min exit check: blend one teacher-selected pair.

Day 4 — Hear tones with ㄇㄚ

Objective: Hear that pitch changes meaning while the base symbols stay the same.

  1. 5 min: say ㄇㄚ with first, second, third and fourth-tone hand motions.
  2. 8 min: match four spoken tones to arrow cards; add the neutral tone last.
  3. 10 min: tone detective — children identify a tone before seeing its mark.
  4. 5 min: optional Lumi Bopomofo tone-game practice.
  5. 2 min exit check: distinguish two contrasting tones.

Day 5 — Decode, review and celebrate

Objective: Use the starter set to decode a few real words and learn the repeatable routine.

  1. 5 min: child-led review with the full starter card set.
  2. 8 min: decode 爸 ㄅㄚˋ, 媽 ㄇㄚ, 馬 ㄇㄚˇ, 弟 ㄉㄧˋ, 你 ㄋㄧˇ and 他 ㄊㄚ.
  3. 10 min: bingo or memory match using sounds rather than letter names.
  4. 5 min: choose the next four symbols for the following week.
  5. 2 min exit check: name one sound learned and one sound to revisit.

Adapt it to your learners

  • Speaks Mandarin, cannot yet read: Use familiar words first; let oral vocabulary support decoding.
  • Does not hear Mandarin daily: Spend more time listening and echoing; teach fewer symbols per session.
  • Mixed-level class: Beginners match sounds to cards; experienced learners blend and find example words.

End-of-week check

Shuffle the starter cards. Ask each child to identify sounds, blend two teacher-selected combinations and distinguish two tones. Record only what to revisit; do not turn the check into a speed test.

Teacher FAQ

Will children learn all 37 symbols in five days?

No. This is an honest starter mini-unit. Repeat its routine with new symbol groups over following weeks.

Is this an official Ministry of Education lesson plan?

No. It is an independent, reusable plan that links to official Taiwan references for notation and practice.

Do I need the app to teach the lesson?

No. Cards, paper, teacher modeling and games are sufficient. The app is an optional practice layer.
