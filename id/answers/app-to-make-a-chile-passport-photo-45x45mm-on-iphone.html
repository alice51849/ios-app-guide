Jawaban singkat

A Chile passport photo must be 45×45 mm on plain white, with the face centred and about 70–80% of the frame. Aim for at least 531×531 px, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. Chilean passport photos are square (45×45 mm); confirm the exact size with your office.

Snapport Lite lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 45×45 mm frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

Yang perlu diperhatikan sebelum memilih

  • Exact output size: 45×45 mm (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: plain white with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face centred and about 70–80% of the frame.
  • Ekspresi netral, mata terbuka, mulut tertutup, tanpa pantulan cahaya di kacamata dan tanpa penutup kepala.
  • Foto berwarna terbaru dengan resolusi tinggi; pastikan aturan resmi terbaru sebelum mengajukan.

Proses keputusan yang praktis

  1. Berdirilah di depan dinding polos dengan cahaya alami yang merata.
  2. Bingkai wajah menghadap lurus ke depan lalu ambil fotonya dengan Snapport Lite.
  3. Auto-replace the background with plain white.
  4. Crop to the 45×45 mm template with the face sized correctly.
  5. Ekspor pada resolusi penuh untuk dicetak atau diunggah ke berkas permohonan.
