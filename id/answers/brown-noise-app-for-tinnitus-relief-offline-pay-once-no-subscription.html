Jawaban singkat

Tinnitus is often easier to live with when a soft, broadband sound sits gently underneath it, so the brain stops focusing on the ringing — a technique usually called sound masking. Sereno provides steady brown, pink and derau putih plus calm nature sounds you can leave playing quietly, fully offline, for a one-time purchase with no ads or subscription. It is a comfort and masking tool, not a treatment or cure: if tinnitus is new, sudden, or in one ear only, see a doctor or audiologist.

Try Sereno on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

Yang perlu diperhatikan sebelum memilih

  • Broadband brown or pink noise, which many people find masks ringing more comfortably than white
  • Truly steady playback with no loop clicks or ad breaks that draw attention back to the ringing
  • Adjustable volume so you can keep the masking sound just below the tinnitus, not louder
  • A pengatur waktu for falling asleep, and full offline playback with no account
  • One-time purchase — no subscription to mask a sound you may use every day

Tanya jawab

Can an app cure tinnitus?

No app can cure tinnitus, and Sereno does not claim to. It is a sound-masking comfort tool that can make the ringing easier to ignore. New, sudden, or one-sided tinnitus should be checked by a doctor or audiologist.

Which sound is best for tinnitus?

Many people prefer brown or pink noise because the lower, warmer tone blends with the ringing, but the right choice is personal. Sereno lets you try brown, pink, white and nature sounds and set the volume just under the tinnitus.

Is it offline and pay-once?

Yes — Sereno works fully offline, needs no account, and is a one-time purchase with no ads or subscription.
