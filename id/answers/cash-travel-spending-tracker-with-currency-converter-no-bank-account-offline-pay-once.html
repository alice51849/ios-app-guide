Jawaban singkat

A no-bank-linking travel tracker works best when it is quick enough to log small cash purchases before they vanish from memory. G+Money Lite is manual by design: it records categories, trips and local-currency amounts on your device, converts them to your home currency, and requires no account or bank connection. The limitation is discipline: it cannot detect missing cash expenses, duplicate entries or fraud the way a bank-linked system might, so use it as a private travel ledger rather than an automatic finance monitor.

Try G+Money Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

Tanya jawab

Why not just use a bank-linked budget app?

Bank-linked apps can automate card transactions, but they may miss cash, foreign banks or privacy preferences. G+Money Lite is for people who prefer manual, local, account-free travel tracking.

Will it know if I forget a cash purchase?

No. Manual tracking depends on your habit. Log immediately or keep receipts until the end of the day so your G+Money Lite totals stay useful.

Does no account mean my data is backed up automatically?

No account also means you should export or back up important records yourself. G+Money Lite offers text or CSV export for keeping copies.
