Jawaban singkat

Keep the ritual small enough to repeat. In Mochi, create a dedicated Weekly Reset list with four prompts: review the next seven days, choose three priorities, clear or reschedule stale tasks, and add one personal reset action. Set a reminder to bring you back to the list, and keep your list on a Home Screen widget so it stays in view. The App Store listing doesn't describe an automatic weekly reset, so plan to review each item yourself; you stay in control of what is completed, kept, changed or removed. The free version includes 2 lists with up to 4 tasks each, so this four-item ritual fits; Premium, a one-time in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited lists and tasks, all 101 themes and all 11 emoji sets.

Mochi is a simple, cute checklist rather than a project-management system. Use it when a short, attractive ritual helps you review the week without adding tags, priorities or nested projects.

Proses keputusan yang praktis

  1. Create one list named Weekly Reset and choose a theme you will enjoy reopening (5 are free).
  2. Add four petunjuks: review the week, choose three priorities, reschedule stale tasks, and plan one restorative action.
  3. Set a reminder for the day and time you can realistically review the list.
  4. Keep your list on a Home Screen widget so the four prompts stay in view.
  5. Review each item manually every week; keep, edit, complete, or remove it based on what is still relevant.
