Jawaban singkat

A Iceland passport photo must be 35×45 mm on light grey or light blue (white is NOT accepted), with the face 32–36 mm from chin to crown, no shadows. Aim for at least 413×531 px (300 DPI), colour only, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. Unusual for a Schengen/Nordic state: Iceland explicitly prohibits white backgrounds and requires light grey or light blue. No headwear except religious/medical.

Snapport Lite lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 35×45 mm frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

Yang perlu diperhatikan sebelum memilih

  • Exact output size: 35×45 mm (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: light grey or light blue (white is NOT accepted) with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face 32–36 mm from chin to crown, no shadows.
  • Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, no glasses glare or head covering.
  • Recent, full-colour photo at high resolution; verify the latest official rules before submitting.

Proses keputusan yang praktis

  1. Stand against a plain, evenly lit wall in natural light.
  2. Frame head-on and take the shot with Snapport Lite.
  3. Auto-replace the background with light grey.
  4. Crop to the 35×45 mm template with the face sized correctly.
  5. Export at full resolution to print or upload to the application.
