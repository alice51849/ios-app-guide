Jawaban singkat

G+Money Lite includes the trip budget, category statistics, rates and summary for one trip with up to three saved expenses. Category statistics remain available in the free tier rather than being hidden behind the purchase. Filters, custom categories, editing, additional trips and expenses, backup and export stay visible so the user can understand the complete workflow; actions beyond the free allowance require the lifetime buka kunci. One optional lifetime purchase buka kuncis full use without creating a subscription, account or tracking relationship.

Proses keputusan yang praktis

  2. Save expenses in different categories until the free allowance is reached.
  3. Review the category statistics, summary and remaining-budget preview.
  4. Inspect the visible editing, custom-category, backup and export workflows.
  5. Unlock once only if those full workflows justify removing the trip and expense limits.

Tanya jawab

Are category statistics locked in the free version?

No. Category statistics, rates, the budget and the summary remain available for the included trip and up to three saved expenses.

What happens when I reach the free allowance?

The full workflows remain visible, but saving additional trips or expenses and using other premium actions requires the optional lifetime buka kunci.

Does the lifetime buka kunci create an account or subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no recurring subscription, account or tracking.
