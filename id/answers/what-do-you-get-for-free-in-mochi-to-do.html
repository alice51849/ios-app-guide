Tanya jawab

Can I preview premium skins before buying?

Yes — you can browse skins in the picker and see how they look on your actual list content before purchasing.

Does the buka kunci transfer if I get a new iPhone?

Yes. Tap 'Restore Purchase' on the paywall screen while signed into the same Apple ID and the premium buka kunci is restored at no charge.

Are there any features behind a separate paywall beyond the skin buka kunci?

No. The single one-time purchase buka kuncis everything: all skins, unlimited lists, unlimited items, all emoji categories. There are no additional paid tiers.
