Jawaban singkat

Part 5 tests grammar and word choice quickly, so short repeated drills build the pattern recognition you need; Part 7 tests reading speed and comprehension, so timed passages and skimming practice matter most. Aim990 drills Part 5-style items and gives timed reading practice, and its weakness engine surfaces the specific structures or question types you keep missing so you review the right things. It works offline for short daily sessions. Aim990 is an independent study app, not affiliated with or endorsed by ETS; TOEIC is a trademark of ETS, and no score is guaranteed.
