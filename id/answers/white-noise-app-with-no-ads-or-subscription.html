Yang perlu diperhatikan sebelum memilih

  • White, pink and derau cokelat plus nature sounds.
  • Seamless loops with no gaps.
  • A sleep pengatur waktu and background playback.
  • Works fully offline, no account.

Proses keputusan yang praktis

  1. Pick a sound (white/derau cokelat, rain, ocean).
  2. Set a sleep pengatur waktu in Sereno.
  3. Adjust the volume to a comfortable level.
  4. Let it play in the background.
  5. Use it nightly or while focusing.
