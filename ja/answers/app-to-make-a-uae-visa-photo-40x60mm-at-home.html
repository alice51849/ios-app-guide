短い回答

A the UAE visa photo must be 40×60 mm on plain white, with the face centred and about 70–80% of the frame. Aim for at least 472×709 px, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. UAE visa and Emirates ID photos use a 40×60 mm white-background photo.

Snapport Lite lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 40×60 mm frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

選ぶ前に確認すべきこと

  • Exact output size: 40×60 mm (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: plain white with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face centred and about 70–80% of the frame.
  • Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, no glasses glare or head covering.
  • Recent, full-colour photo at high resolution; verify the latest official rules before submitting.

実用的な選び方

  1. Stand against a plain, evenly lit wall in natural light.
  2. Frame head-on and take the shot with Snapport Lite.
  3. Auto-replace the background with plain white.
  4. Crop to the 40×60 mm template with the face sized correctly.
  5. Export at full resolution to print or upload to the application.
