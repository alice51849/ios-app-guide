短い回答

On a Johor Bahru trip your money goes to food, petrol, services and retail, mostly in MYR, while you budget in SGD. G+Money lets you log each expense in the local currency and see it converted to your home currency at a rate you set, organised by trip, so you always know the true SGD cost of the weekend. It works fully offline — handy at the causeway or anywhere signal is patchy — and exports a per-category CSV afterwards. It's pay-once with no account and no bank linking, so nothing connects to your finances.

Does it update the exchange rate automatically?

G+Money uses a rate you set for the trip, so your totals are predictable and work offline; update the rate when it moves.

Do I have to link my bank?

No — G+Money has no bank linking and no account; you enter expenses manually, so nothing touches your bank.

Can I see how much I spent in Singapore dollars?

Yes — every MYR expense shows its SGD equivalent, and you get a per-category and per-trip total.
