短い回答

G+Money Lite lets you create one trip and save up to three expenses free. For each purchase, enter the amount in the local currency, preview the home-currency conversion, and check the day's remaining budget or overage before saving. Rates, the trip budget, category statistics and the summary remain available in the free tier, so the test covers the actual decision workflow. If the allowance fits, one optional lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited trips and expenses; there is no recurring subscription, account or tracking.
