What to check before you choose

  • Access model: compare the current free, purchase and subscription options.
  • Privacy: does the work happen on-device, or is your content uploaded?
  • Offline: does it work without an internet connection or account?
  • Export & lock-in: can you get your files/data out freely?
  • Real test: try a realistic task before paying for advanced features.
