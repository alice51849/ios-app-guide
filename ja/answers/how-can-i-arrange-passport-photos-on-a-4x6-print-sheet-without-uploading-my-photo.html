短い回答

Choose the U.S. 2×2 inch, UK 35×45mm or Canada 50×70mm size, load a photo, adjust its zoom and position, then download a 300 DPI PNG. When the layout has white margin or gap space, non-overprinting cut marks stay entirely outside every photo. The photo is decoded and laid out only inside the current browser tab; the tool has no upload, account, storage or analytics.

Before printing, compare the selected dimensions and the original photo with the issuing authority's current rules. Print at 100% or Actual Size and measure one finished crop with a ruler. The UK requires printed photos to meet additional professional-standard rules, while Canada requires passport photos to be taken by a commercial photographer, so this independent layout cannot guarantee acceptance.

実用的な選び方

  1. Open the current official photo rules for the exact passport or application.
  2. Choose the matching country size and the paper loaded in your printer.
  3. Load the photo locally, then adjust zoom and position inside the crop.
  4. Download the PNG and print at 100% or Actual Size with auto-fit disabled.
  5. Measure one printed crop with a ruler and recheck every non-size requirement before use.

よくある質問

Does the free print-sheet maker upload or save my passport photo?

No. The file stays in the current browser tab and is not uploaded, stored, tracked or analysed. Closing or reloading the tab clears it.

プリンターの自動フィットを使ってよいですか？

No. Auto-fit, Fit to Page and some borderless modes can change the physical crop size. Print at 100% or Actual Size, then measure one crop with a ruler.

印刷シートなら必ず受理されますか？

No. The tool arranges physical dimensions only. Head size, background, expression, lighting, recency, print quality and country-specific capture rules still apply.
