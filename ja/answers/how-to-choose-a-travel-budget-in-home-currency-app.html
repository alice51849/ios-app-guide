短い回答

G+Money Lite は、旅行 1 件と支出 3 件までを無料で使えます。為替レート、予算、カテゴリ別の統計、まとめの表示も含まれます。支払いは現地通貨で入力すると、リアルタイム・保存済み・手動設定のいずれかのレートで自国通貨に換算して表示。保存したレートを使えばオフラインでもそのまま使えます。

If you need more entries or trips, one lifetime purchase removes those limits. There is no subscription, account, or tracking, and the App also supports a Home Screen widget and Apple Watch.

選ぶ前に確認すべきこと

  • A useful free allowance that includes one trip and three expenses.
  • Local-currency entry with immediate home-currency totals.
  • Budget, category statistics, and summary available before purchase.
  • Saved or manual exchange rates for offline use.
  • One lifetime unlock with no subscription, account, or tracking.

実用的な選び方

  1. Set your home currency and create the free trip.
  2. Choose a live, saved, or manual rate for the local currency.
  3. Log up to three expenses while testing the workflow.
  4. Review the budget, category statistics, and home-currency summary.
  5. Unlock unlimited expenses and trips once only if the free allowance fits your travel workflow.

よくある質問

What can I use without paying?

You can use rates, a budget, category statistics, and a summary for one trip with up to three expenses.

Will it still work without data abroad?

Yes — saved or manually set rates keep expense entry and conversion available offline. An internet connection is needed only when fetching updated public exchange rates.

Is the full version a subscription?

No — one optional lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited expenses and trips.
