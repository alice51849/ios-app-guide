短い回答

For tones, pair each with a movement so pitch becomes physical: a flat hand held high for the 1st (level) tone, a rising hand for the 2nd, a dip-and-rise for the 3rd, and a sharp chop down for the 4th. Drill tone pairs on the same sound — ma1, ma2, ma3, ma4 — and use minimal pairs your child can picture, like ma1 (media, mom) versus ma3 (horse), so a wrong tone visibly changes meaning. For blending, treat it like phonics: say the initial, medial and final separately, tap each one, then slide them together (for example the initial plus a final), first slowly then faster. Practise with real words and silly nonsense syllables, and read simple Zhuyin books together while pointing at each symbol. Keep sessions to 5-10 minutes, rotate songs, cards and games to stay fresh, and praise effort over accuracy. How do I teach the four Mandarin tones to a young child?

Pair each tone with a movement (flat hand high for 1st, rising for 2nd, dip-and-rise for 3rd, sharp chop for 4th), drill the same sound across all four, and use minimal pairs your child can picture. Keep it short and playful. Lumi Bopomofo has a dedicated tone game for exactly this.

What is the easiest way to teach Zhuyin blending?

Treat it like phonics: say the initial, medial and final separately, tap each symbol, then slide them together — slowly first, then faster. Practise with real and nonsense syllables. Lumi Bopomofo builds blending into play so children get lots of low-pressure repetition.

How long should each practice session be?

For a 4-7 year old, 5-10 minutes is plenty. Short, frequent, playful sessions beat long ones. Lumi Bopomofo is designed in short game loops, pay once with no ads or subscription.
