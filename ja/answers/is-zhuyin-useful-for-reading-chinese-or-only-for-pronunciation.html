短い回答

Zhuyin (Bopomofo) does double duty. As a pronunciation guide, its 37 symbols map cleanly to Mandarin sounds and tones. As a reading aid, it appears alongside characters in Taiwanese children's books, so a child who knows Zhuyin can sound out unfamiliar characters and read independently — a bridge to full character literacy. Lumi Bopomofo builds this foundation with all 37 symbols, native Taiwanese audio, a tone game and syllable blending. It's ad-free, no data collected, one-time unlock.
