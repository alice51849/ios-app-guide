短い回答

When searching for a resume builder app, consider options that do not impose watermarks or require subscriptions. CV Desk stands out as a pay-once solution, allowing users to create and export resumes in both PDF and Word formats without any additional costs or limitations.

CV Desk also offers instant ATS scoring and a variety of recruiter-ready templates, making it a practical choice for job seekers. Its user-friendly interface simplifies the resume creation process, ensuring you can focus on showcasing your skills effectively.
