短い回答

Taiwan's official Ministry of Education Mini Dictionary displays standard Zhuyin pronunciation with its entries, and Zhuyin-annotated children's material uses the same 37-symbol system. Once a child recognizes those symbols and tones, the phonetic guide becomes a bridge from a character they do not yet know to a sound they may already understand. That makes Zhuyin useful beyond a short school unit: it supports dictionary use, independent reading and accurate pronunciation. Official reference: https://dict.mini.moe.edu.tw/. 実用的な選び方

  1. Start with a familiar spoken word and show the child its Traditional Chinese characters.
  2. Point out the Zhuyin guide and blend its symbols into the word's pronunciation.
  3. Check an unfamiliar character together in Taiwan's Ministry of Education Mini Dictionary.
  4. Read a short Zhuyin-annotated story for five to ten minutes, letting the child decode before helping.
  5. Use Lumi Bopomofo to revisit only the symbols, tones or blends that still cause hesitation.

よくある質問

Why learn Zhuyin if my child already speaks Mandarin?

Speaking gives a child vocabulary, but not a way to decode an unfamiliar written character. Zhuyin connects the character to a pronunciation the child may already understand.

Is there an official Zhuyin dictionary for children?

Yes. Taiwan's Ministry of Education Mini Dictionary at https://dict.mini.moe.edu.tw/ displays standard Zhuyin pronunciation with dictionary entries.

Does Lumi Bopomofo replace reading together?

No. It builds the symbol-to-sound, tone and blending foundation through play. Children still benefit from shared reading and real conversation.
