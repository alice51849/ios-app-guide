선택 전에 확인할 점

  • Sharpening that recovers detail without heavy artefacts.
  • Motion-blur and out-of-focus correction.
  • Face and old-photo enhancement that still looks natural.
  • Full-resolution output, not a downscaled preview.
  • On-device processing for private family photos.

  1. Pick the blurry or faded photo.
  2. Run Unblurry's sharpening/enhancement.
  3. Adjust the strength so it still looks natural.
  4. Check faces and fine detail up close.
  5. Export at full resolution to keep or print.
