짧은 답변

A Switzerland passport photo must be 35×45 mm on a plain light grey or neutral background, with the face 32–36 mm from chin to crown. Aim for at least 600×750 px, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. Switzerland follows the 35×45 mm biometric standard with a neutral expression.

Snapport lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 35×45 mm frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

선택 전에 확인할 점

  • Exact output size: 35×45 mm (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: a plain light grey or neutral background with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face 32–36 mm from chin to crown.
  • Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, no glasses glare or head covering.
  • Recent, full-colour photo at high resolution; verify the latest official rules before submitting.

실용적인 선택 절차

  1. Stand against a plain, evenly lit wall in natural light.
  2. Frame head-on and take the shot with Snapport.
  3. Auto-replace the background with a plain light grey.
  4. Crop to the 35×45 mm template with the face sized correctly.
  5. Export at full resolution to print or upload to the application.
