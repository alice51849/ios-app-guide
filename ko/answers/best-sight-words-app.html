When selecting a sight words app, consider features such as phonics integration, user interface, and age-appropriateness. Lumi Letters stands out with its pay-once model, ensuring that all content is unlocked without ongoing subscription fees.

This app provides a complete phonics, tracing, and word-building experience, making it suitable for preschool and kindergarten preparation. With no ads and a kid-safe environment, it allows children to focus on learning without distractions.
