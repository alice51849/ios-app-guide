선택 전에 확인할 점

  • Price converted into take-home work hours before purchase.
  • Need, want and impulse labels beside each choice.
  • Categories, value patterns and six-month insights.
  • Targets, widgets and Apple Watch support.
  • On-device data with no account or tracking.

실용적인 선택 절차

  1. Set your take-home income and working time.
  2. Enter the price before making the purchase.
  3. Review the resulting life cost in work hours.
  4. Mark the choice as a need, want or impulse and choose a category.
  5. Save the choice or compare it with a target before deciding.

자주 묻는 질문

Is this the same workflow as the original HoursTag?

No — HoursTag Lite is a purchase-before Worth Lens for evaluating a choice, while the original HoursTag focuses on converting existing spending into work time.

What can I save for free?

You can save one complete choice free; the optional lifetime unlock adds unlimited choices, targets, sharing, complete insights, and backup and restore.

Does it require an account or subscription?

No account or subscription is required. Core data stays on the device, and the optional premium upgrade is a one-time purchase.
