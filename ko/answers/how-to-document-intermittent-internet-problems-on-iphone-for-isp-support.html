짧은 답변

Run WiFi Aid's Deep Check when the problem is happening and again when the connection appears healthy. Its stability result uses a bounded series of TCP samples and variation; it is not ICMP packet-loss measurement, Wi-Fi RSSI, channel congestion or a complete speed test. Keep the DNS, TCP, TLS, TTFB, HTTP, numeric-IP TCP Direct IP, IPv4 and IPv6 observations in the private history stored on the device. Note what you were doing and the affected destination separately, then use the repeated evidence to ask the ISP a focused question without claiming that the app can see router logs, other devices or the provider's network.

Check the current App Store listing before buying; WiFi Aid is a paid download with no free trial. After purchase, collect the same bounded observations during a failure and a healthy period before contacting support.

선택 전에 확인할 점

  • Capture a Deep Check while the intermittent problem is visible
  • Repeat the same bounded checks during a healthy period
  • Keep protocol timing and IP-version observations in private on-device history
  • Describe stability as bounded TCP samples and variation, not packet loss or RSSI
  • Avoid claims about router logs, channel congestion, mesh topology or other devices

실용적인 선택 절차

  1. Record the time, affected task and destination outside the diagnostic result.
  2. Run Deep Check during the failure without repeatedly changing settings.
  3. Repeat the same check after the connection appears healthy.
  4. Compare DNS, TCP, TLS, TTFB, HTTP, numeric-IP TCP Direct IP and IP-version observations.
  5. Use the dated on-device history to ask the ISP about the specific repeated layer or timing change.
