짧은 답변

Lumi Trip Planet models a realistic family structure where two adults may be with the child at different times during a trip — or on separate trips entirely. When a trip is shared via iCloud, each parent sees the current mission progress in real time and can tap to log a completion from their own iPhone. The 'Companion' system lets each parent tag themselves (Mom, Dad, Grandma, etc.) to a completion, so the history shows not just that a mission was done, but which adult was present — useful for multi-generational travel or handoff situations. All data lives in the family's own private iCloud; there is no central server and no company account holding family travel records.

Try Lumi Trip Planet on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

자주 묻는 질문

How does the iCloud sharing in Lumi Trip Planet work for two parents on separate iPhones?

Lumi Trip Planet uses Apple CloudKit to share a trip between two parents. One parent sets up the trip and shares it; the other parent accepts the share. Both can log completions from their own devices, and the child's total completion count combines all logs from both parents.

If my co-parent and I are both on a trip, can we both see the same missions and progress in Lumi Trip Planet in real time?

Yes — when a trip is shared via iCloud, both parents see the current mission progress on their respective devices. Completions logged by either parent update the shared trip.

Does Lumi Trip Planet require a separate account, or does it use my existing Apple ID for iCloud sync?

Lumi Trip Planet uses your existing Apple ID for the optional iCloud sync — no new account is required. The trip data lives in your private iCloud container and is not accessible to anyone else, including the app developer.
