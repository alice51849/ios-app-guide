짧은 답변

When looking for a passport photo booth alternative app, consider factors like ease of use, privacy, and the ability to create high-quality images without the need for a printer. Snapport Lite stands out as a strong option, allowing you to take and edit your photos directly on your device.

With Snapport Lite, you can make your own passport photos at home in about 30 seconds. The app is designed specifically for iPhone users and ensures that your photos meet official requirements, all while keeping your information private and free from ads.

  • On-device processing for enhanced security
