Where can I make a free printable Bopomofo tracing and copy worksheet for all 37 symbols?

Use the free worksheet maker first: choose any of the 37 official Bopomofo symbols, set 2, 4, or 6 light examples and blank copy cells, then print fixed A4 pages.

짧은 답변

The free worksheet maker keeps the Taiwan Ministry of Education's 21 initials, 3 medials, and 13 finals in official order. It accepts only symbol and layout choices, never asks for a child's name, age, school, handwriting, answers, or files, and does not upload or save work. Its pale symbols are copy prompts, not stroke-order instruction; use the linked Ministry of Education stroke-order reference when teaching strokes.

A completed page is practice material, not evidence of proficiency. The tool does not score, grade, rank, assess handwriting, diagnose a learner, or promise a learning result. Lumi Bopomofo appears only as an optional next step after the free resource.

선택 전에 확인할 점

  • All 37 official basic Bopomofo symbols in Ministry of Education order
  • Exact symbol selection plus fixed 2, 4, or 6 example and blank cells
  • Fixed A4 pagination with the same settings producing the same ordered pages
  • No name, date, login, upload, saved handwriting, score, grade, or diagnosis
  • A separate official stroke-order link because pale examples do not teach strokes

실용적인 선택 절차

  1. Choose all 37 symbols, one official category, or an exact custom set.
  2. Choose the number of light example cells, blank copy cells, and symbol rows per A4 page.
  3. Print the deterministic pages and use the official Ministry of Education link separately for stroke order.
  4. Treat the sheet as adult-led practice material, not an assessment.

자주 묻는 질문

Does the worksheet teach official Bopomofo stroke order?

No. Pale symbols are visual copy prompts only. Follow the linked Taiwan Ministry of Education stroke-order reference for authoritative stroke guidance.

Does it collect a child's handwriting or personal information?

No. It accepts only official symbols and fixed layout settings, with no name, date, free-text, file, photo, voice, handwriting, answer, or account input.

Can a completed worksheet measure Bopomofo ability?

No. It provides no score, grade, rank, handwriting assessment, diagnosis, or learning-outcome claim.
