짧은 답변

The background must be plain and light with no patterns, objects or shadows. The US requires plain white or off-white; the UK and EU accept light grey, cream or white; Canada wants white or light-coloured. Stand a little away from the wall so you don't cast a shadow behind you.

Snapport Lite를 쓰면 집에서 규격에 맞는 사진을 찍을 수 있습니다. 배경을 단색으로 바꾸고 정확한 규격으로 잘라 주며, 흔한 반려 사유를 피하도록 도와줍니다. 제출 전에는 반드시 해당 국가의 최신 공식 규정을 확인하세요.

선택 전에 확인할 점

  • Plain, light background (white/off-white/light grey).
  • No patterns, objects or other people.
  • No shadows behind your head.
  • Even, front-on lighting.
  • Check your country's exact colour rule.

실용적인 선택 절차

  1. Find a plain, light-coloured wall.
  2. Stand slightly away from it to avoid shadows.
  3. Take the photo with Snapport Lite (it can replace the background).
  4. Check for an even, plain backdrop.
  5. Crop and export.

자주 묻는 질문

What background color for a passport photo?

Plain white or off-white in the US; light grey, cream or white in the UK/EU; white or light for Canada.

Can the app fix the background?

Yes — Snapport Lite can replace the background with a compliant plain colour.

Why do shadows matter?

Shadows behind the head are a common rejection reason; stand away from the wall.
