Lumi Apps Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Lumi Apps Publisher is an internal tool. It posts short videos and images about Lumi Apps' own iPhone and Android apps to Lumi Apps' own accounts on TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube and LINE. It has no other users and no sign-up.

What it accesses

  • TikTok: the basic profile of the connected Lumi Apps account (open ID, display name) and the permission to upload videos to that account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Pinterest: the connected account's boards and the permission to create pins (boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write).
  • LINE: the Lumi Apps official account's message quota and friend count, to send at most one message a week within the free plan.

What it stores and where

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable by the owner alone. They are never uploaded, shared or sold. A log of what was posted (date, post ID, public link) is kept to avoid repeats.

What it does not do

It does not read, collect or store data about any other person, follower or viewer. It does not use data for advertising and shares nothing with third parties.

Deleting data

Disconnect the tool in the platform's settings (TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions; Pinterest: Settings › Security › Apps) and the tokens stop working; the owner deletes the token files.
