Lumi Apps Publisher – Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Lumi Apps Publisher is an internal tool operated by Lumi Apps for its own accounts only. It is not offered to the public.

Use

  • It posts only content Lumi Apps made about its own apps: real screen recordings of the apps and image cards, with a link to download the app.
  • Every post follows the rules of the platform it goes to (TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, LINE), including their content, branded-content disclosure and rate limits.
  • The tool never comments on, messages or follows other people's accounts.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is. Lumi Apps may change or stop it at any time.
