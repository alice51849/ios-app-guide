Jawapan ringkas

For an authentic film look, presets should reproduce real film stocks — colour shifts, butiran filem, halasi and kebocoran cahayas — rather than a flat Instagram-style filter, and export at full resolution with no tanda air.

PhotoCream is built for exactly this, with Pay once, No tanda air, Full-res. Test it on a real example before relying on it, and check the current App Store listing for pricing.

Apa yang perlu dipertimbangkan sebelum memilih

  • Real film-stock emulation, not a generic filter.
  • Authentic butiran filem, halasi and kebocoran cahaya options.
  • Full-resolution export with no tanda air.
  • Fine control over strength and tone.
  • A pay-once model instead of a subscription.

Proses membuat keputusan yang praktikal

  1. Import the photo you want to style.
  2. Pick a film look in PhotoCream.
  3. Dial in butiran filem, halasi and kebocoran cahayas.
  4. Fine-tune the colour and strength.
  5. Export at full resolution, tanda air-free.
