Jawapan ringkas

A multi-country perjalanan means juggling several currencies with wildly different scales (a bowl of noodles can be 50 baht or 30,000 dong). G+Money Lite lets you log each expense in the local currency and converts everything to one home currency at rates you set per currency, so a single running total actually means something. It groups spending by perjalanan and category, works luar talian between SIMs and border crossings, and exports CSV at the end. Pay-once, no account, no bank linking.

Try G+Money Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

Soalan Lazim

Can it handle more than two currencies on one perjalanan?

Yes — G+Money Lite lets you log any number of currencies and converts each to your home currency, so the perjalanan total stays meaningful.

What if I can't get a local SIM right away?

G+Money Lite is fully luar talian; set each currency's rate once and keep logging without any connection.

Will big-number currencies be awkward?

No — you enter the local amount as-is (e.g. dong), and G+Money Lite shows the home-currency equivalent.
