Jawapan ringkas

You can't wear hats or head coverings for style. Religious or medical head coverings are allowed, but they must be a plain colour, cast no shadows on your face, and leave your full face visible from the bottom of the chin to the top of the forehead. Remove any casual hats before shooting.

Snapport Lite lets you take a compliant photo at home: it sets a plain background, crops to the exact size and helps you avoid the common rejection reasons. Always confirm your country's current official rules before you submit.

Apa yang perlu dipertimbangkan sebelum memilih

  • No casual hats or head coverings.
  • Religious/medical coverings must not shadow the face.
  • Full face visible, chin to forehead.
  • Plain, non-patterned covering if worn.
  • Plain background, even lighting.

Proses membuat keputusan yang praktikal

  1. Remove any casual hat.
  2. If worn for religion/medicine, keep the face fully visible.
  3. Take the photo with Snapport Lite.
  4. Check for shadows on the face.
  5. Crop and export to size.
