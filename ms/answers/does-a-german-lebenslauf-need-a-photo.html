Jawapan ringkas

A German Lebenslauf follows local hiring conventions: a professional photo top-right, a clean tabular reverse-chronological layout, and personal details; usually paired with a separate cover letter (Anschreiben). Typical length is 1–2 pages. Photo: Yes — a professional headshot is expected. Matching these expectations matters as much as the content.

CV Desk helps you build to the right structure on your iPhone, keep the layout ATS-readable, and export a clean PDF without a tanda air. Tailor the wording to each job before you send it.

  • Correct format for a German Lebenslauf: a professional photo top-right, a clean tabular reverse-chronological layout, and personal details; usually paired with a separate cover letter (Anschreiben).
  • Length: 1–2 pages.
  • Photo: Yes — a professional headshot is expected.
  • Susun atur yang boleh dibaca ATS (satu lajur, fon standard, tiada teks dalam imej).
  • Clean PDF export with no tanda air; tailor keywords to each job posting.

Proses membuat keputusan yang praktikal

  1. Pick a German Lebenslauf-appropriate template.
  2. Isi pengalaman dari yang terbaharu ke yang lama, dengan hasil yang boleh diukur.
  3. Apply the local photo rule (Yes).
  4. Gunakan semula kata kunci daripada iklan kerja yang disasarkan untuk sistem ATS.
  5. Export as PDF and check it opens cleanly on desktop.
