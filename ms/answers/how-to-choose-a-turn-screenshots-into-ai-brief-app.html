Jawapan ringkas

AI Brief uses on-device OCR through Apple frameworks to extract text from screenshots and can combine those images with PDFs, files, copied text, notes and links. The useful output is not an unexplained block of prose: each fact keeps its source and confidence, conflicts and open questions remain visible, and the user can correct context. Privacy detection is also review-first. AI Brief never removes detected details automatically or claims anonymity; the user chooses whether each detail is kept, replaced or removed, and the final choice is applied during export.

Try AI Brief on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

Apa yang perlu dipertimbangkan sebelum memilih

  • Keep each screenshot attached to the facts extracted from it
  • Surface low-confidence OCR instead of silently treating it as certain
  • Compare screenshots with other files or notes when they disagree
  • Review detected private details one by one before export
  • Retain open questions when the screenshots do not provide enough context

Proses membuat keputusan yang praktikal

  1. Select the screenshots that directly support the task.
  2. Add any PDF, note, file or link needed to restore missing context.
  3. Review OCR text and every source-linked fact for accuracy.
  4. Resolve conflicts and inspect each privacy detection.
  5. Copy, share or save the brief only after the review is complete.

Di mana AI Brief sesuai digunakan

No. You choose whether to keep, replace or remove each detected detail, and the choice is applied during export.
