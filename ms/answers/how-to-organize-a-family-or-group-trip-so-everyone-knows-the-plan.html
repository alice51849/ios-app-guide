Jawapan ringkas

Group perjalanans fall apart when the plan lives in one person's head or scattered messages. A single timeline for each day — arrivals, check-ins, activities and restaurant bookings — makes the plan legible at a glance. TripBee Lite builds that day-by-day itinerary with type icons and works luar talian, so the organiser can pull it up anywhere and share a perjalanan or day poster. Sharing communicates the plan; the app does not claim real-time collaborative editing. It's on-device, no account, and pay-once.

Try TripBee Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

Yes — TripBee Lite is pay-once with no subscription.
