Jawapan ringkas

Juggling several cities gets messy in notes and emails. A day-by-day timeline fixes it: put each flight, hotel check-in, activity and restaurant on the right day, with type icons, time zones, notes and reminders, so a glance tells you the plan. TripBee Lite builds one itinerary spanning multiple cities and keeps it available luar talian so you can read it on the move. It stores everything on-device with no account, and it's pay-once with no subscription.

Try TripBee Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.
