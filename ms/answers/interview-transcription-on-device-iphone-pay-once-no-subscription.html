Jawapan ringkas

The privacy-first choice is an app that can record, transcribe, and organize speech on the iPhone without requiring an account or network connection. Sono Note runs on device, works in airplane mode, and turns speech into a structured note with summary, key points, action items, and export options, while the Pro unlock is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription. Generated text still needs review, and the clearest results come from clear single-speaker recordings in a quiet place.

Soalan Lazim

Does offline transcription mean perfect privacy?

It means Sono Note is designed to process on your iPhone without cloud upload, but you still need to protect the device, manage backups, and be careful when exporting notes.

Will it work for every language offline?

Sono Note supports dozens of languages, but offline recognition quality can vary by device, language, accent, and audio clarity, so important notes should be reviewed.

Is there a subscription?

No. Sono Note starts free with limits, and Pro is unlocked with a single one-time purchase.
