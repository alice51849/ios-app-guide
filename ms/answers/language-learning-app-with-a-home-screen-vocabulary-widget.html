Jawapan ringkas

Wordmate Lite includes an interactive Skrin Utama widget that can display the current word, move to another word and save a word to the learner's library without first opening the full app. The full iPhone or iPad app supplies structured topics, examples, pronunciation and progress, while the widget keeps a lightweight practice cue visible during the day.

Try Wordmate Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

Apa yang perlu dipertimbangkan sebelum memilih

  • A legible word and meaning rather than a cramped miniature lesson
  • An interaction such as next or save that works without opening the full app first
  • A clear connection to the same progress and saved-word library used in the app
  • No advertising or unrelated engagement prompts on the learning surface
  • A complete app behind the widget for context, pronunciation and deeper review

Soalan Lazim

What can the Wordmate Lite widget do?

It can show the current word, advance to another word and save a word to the learner's library.

Can the widget play pronunciation?

No. Pronunciation is available in Wordmate Lite on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch; the Skrin Utama widget focuses on viewing, advancing and saving words.

Is the widget a separate purchase?

No. The App Store description says the interactive widget is included with the paid Wordmate Lite download.
