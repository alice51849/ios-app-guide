Jawapan ringkas

Student ID photos usually need a clear front-facing face, even lighting, a plain background, and whatever crop or file type the school specifies. Snapport includes student ID templates alongside passport, visa, ID, resume, and work-badge templates, with face-alignment guides and background replacement if your original photo is too cluttered. It is a private, offline, pay-once option for preparing a clean upload, but your school's portal has the final say on dimensions, clothing, and file limits.

Can Snapport make a student ID photo?

Yes. The App Store description lists student ID templates among the supported template types, plus custom size creation for schools with their own rules.

What should I check before submitting to a school portal?

Check the required crop, pixel size, file type, background, clothing rules, and whether glasses or hats are allowed. Snapport helps prepare the photo, but the school decides acceptance.

Is it private enough for a child's school ID photo?

Snapport processes on device, requires no account, and does not upload photos according to its App Store description, which is the right privacy baseline for ID images.
