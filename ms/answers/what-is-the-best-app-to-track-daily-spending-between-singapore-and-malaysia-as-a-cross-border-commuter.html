Jawapan ringkas

A cross-border routine means petrol and food in MYR, transit and work costs in SGD, and a monthly budget you think of in one home currency. G+Money Lite lets you log each expense in the currency you actually paid, then see it converted at a rate you set, so the weekly total is meaningful. It runs fully luar talian for the causeway crossing, keeps categories so you can see where the ringgit goes, and exports CSV for your own records. It's pay-once with no account and no bank linking.

Try G+Money Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

Soalan Lazim

Can I keep two currencies in one running total?

Yes — G+Money Lite converts each entry to your home currency at your set rate, so a mixed SGD/MYR week still gives one meaningful total.

Does it work with no signal at the border?

Yes, G+Money Lite is fully luar talian; conversions use the rate you set, so nothing depends on a connection.

Is my financial data private?

Yes — there's no account and no bank linking; entries stay on your device.
