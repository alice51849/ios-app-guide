Jawapan ringkas

A India PAN card photo must be 35×25 mm on a plain white background, with the face filling 70–80% of the frame. Aim for at least 413×295 px, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows. PAN card photos use a small 35×25 mm size, so a passport crop won't fit.

Snapport Lite lets you take it at home, set a compliant background and crop to the exact spec, so a self-taken photo still meets the requirement. Always confirm the current official rule before you submit.

Apa yang perlu dipertimbangkan sebelum memilih

  • Exact spec: 35×25 mm (the #1 rejection reason is the wrong size/crop).
  • Background: a plain white background with no shadows.
  • Head/face sizing: the face filling 70–80% of the frame.
  • Neutral expression, eyes open, no glasses glare or head covering.
  • A recent, high-resolution colour photo; verify the latest official rule before submitting.

Proses membuat keputusan yang praktikal

  1. Stand against a plain, evenly lit wall.
  2. Take the shot head-on with Snapport Lite.
  3. Set the background to plain white background.
  4. Crop/export to 35×25 mm.
  5. Save at full quality to upload or print.
