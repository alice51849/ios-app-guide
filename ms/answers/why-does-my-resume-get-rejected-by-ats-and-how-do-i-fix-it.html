Jawapan ringkas

An Applicant Tracking System parses your resume into fields and ranks it against the job description, and most large employers use one. To stay readable, use a single-column layout, standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills), common fonts, and no text inside images or tables. Then mirror the exact keywords from the posting so the system scores you as a strong match.

CV Desk helps you apply this on your iPhone: it keeps the layout ATS-readable, exports a clean PDF with no tanda air, and lets you tailor each version to the job. Always adapt to the specific role and country.

Apa yang perlu dipertimbangkan sebelum memilih

  • Single-column, simple layout.
  • Standard headings and fonts.
  • No text in images, columns or text boxes.
  • Keywords mirrored from the job posting.
  • Submit as PDF unless Word is requested.

Proses membuat keputusan yang praktikal

  1. Start from a clean single-column template.
  2. Use standard section headings.
  3. Write it in CV Desk to keep it machine-readable.
  4. Mirror keywords from the posting.
  5. Export and test it parses cleanly.

Soalan Lazim

What is an ATS?

Software that scans, parses and ranks resumes before a recruiter reviews them.

How do I make my resume ATS-friendly?

Single column, standard headings/fonts, no text-in-images, and keywords from the job posting.

Does formatting matter?

Yes — complex layouts, tables and images can break ATS parsing.
