Frequently asked questions

What app converts prices to hours of work before I buy?

HoursTag converts any price into hours of your own time based on your hourly wage. HoursTag Lite is the free version with the core conversion feature.

How do I track daily habits and spending together?

DailyMate Lite helps you log daily habits and recurring tasks, while HoursTag shows the time cost of each purchase. Used together they give you a complete view of how you spend your time and money.

Is there a free travel budget app?

G+Money Lite tracks trip expenses with live currency conversion for free. G+Money adds offline rates and more detailed reporting.
