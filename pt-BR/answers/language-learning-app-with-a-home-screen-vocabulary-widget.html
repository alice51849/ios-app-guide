Resposta curta

Wordmate: Learn 44 Languages includes an interactive Home Screen widget that can display the current word, move to another word and save a word to the learner's library without first opening the full app. The full iPhone or iPad app supplies structured topics, examples, pronunciation and progress, while the widget keeps a lightweight practice cue visible during the day.

O que observar antes de escolher

  • O que observar antes de escolher
  • A legible word and meaning rather than a cramped miniature lesson
  • An interaction such as next or save that works without opening the full app first
  • A clear connection to the same progress and saved-word library used in the app
  • No advertising or unrelated engagement prompts on the learning surface
