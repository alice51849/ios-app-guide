O que verificar antes de escolher

  • Counting and number recognition first.
  • Number bonds and simple addition through play.
  • Short, colourful, game-based activities.
  • No ads or pressure to buy.

Um processo de decisão prático

  1. Practise counting to ten, then twenty.
  2. Match numbers to quantities.
  3. Introduce simple number bonds through games.
  4. Keep each session short and playful.
  5. Praise effort to build confidence.
