The Photos app's built-in Edit mode includes Sharpness and Definition sliders (tap Edit, then scroll the adjustment dial at the bottom to find them) that can visibly improve a mildly soft or slightly out-of-focus photo — apply modest increases and tap the photo to compare before/after, since over-sharpening adds visible halos and noise. Perguntas frequentes

What's the difference between Sharpness and Definition in iPhone Photos?

Sharpness increases edge contrast — it makes boundaries between areas appear crisper, but can introduce halos if overdone. Definition improves midtone contrast and local detail, which tends to look more natural for soft portraits and landscapes. Using both at moderate levels usually gives the best results.

Can I undo changes after editing in iPhone Photos?

Yes — iPhone Photos edits are entirely non-destructive. Tap Edit on any photo and then tap 'Revert' at the bottom right to restore the original at any time, even months later. There is no need to duplicate a photo before editing.

Should I use a third-party AI app for blurry photos?

For mild motion blur or slight softness, yes — AI apps can produce meaningfully better results than the native sliders. For heavy blur or serious defocus, manage expectations: even the best AI tools produce improvements in appearance rather than true data recovery, and the generated sharpness may not be accurate to the original scene.
