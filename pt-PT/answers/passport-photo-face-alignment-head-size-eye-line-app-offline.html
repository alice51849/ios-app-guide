Resposta curta

Official photo rules commonly specify a centered, front-facing head, visible eyes, neutral expression, and a head-size range; the U.S. example is roughly 1 to 1 3/8 inches from chin to crown in a 2×2 inch photo. Snapport Lite uses on-device Apple Vision face detection with head-top, eye-line, and chin guides, plus pinch, drag, rotate, and auto-center tools so you can fit the face to the selected template. It is a guide, not an official compliance checker, so compare the result with the issuing authority before submitting.

Because it's on-device, your photo isn't uploaded anywhere, and a single export gives you a file ready to drop into a CV or a LinkedIn/ID upload.

Perguntas frequentes

Does Snapport Lite check passport head size?

Snapport Lite provides head-top, eye-line, and chin guides using on-device face detection, which helps you align the photo to a template. It does not certify or guarantee government acceptance.

Why does eye line matter?

Many official rules define where the eyes or face should sit in the final photo. A face that is centered poorly can be rejected even if the photo is sharp and the background is plain.

Can I fix a tilted face in Snapport Lite?

You can pinch, drag, rotate, auto-center, and reset the photo. If the original pose is strongly turned or not front-facing, retaking the photo is usually safer than trying to fix it.
