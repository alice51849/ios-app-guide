Resposta curta

There is no rule that a preschooler must learn Zhuyin first — Taiwan's early-years guidance favours play, stories and songs over formal drilling, and first-grade teachers do teach all 37 symbols from the start over roughly the first ten weeks. If your child is curious, a gentle, playful introduction (games, picture books with Zhuyin) can ease that start without pressure; if your child isn't interested, pushing can backfire and sour them on reading. Lumi Bopomofo fits the gentle route — it teaches the 37 symbols through play, pay once, no ads, with a parent gate — as light exposure, not a bootcamp.

Perguntas frequentes

Does my child have to learn Zhuyin before first grade?

No. First-grade teachers introduce all 37 symbols from the start (about ten weeks in Taiwan). Early exposure can ease the transition, but it isn't required, and forcing an uninterested child can backfire. Lumi Bopomofo is for gentle, playful exposure.

Won't early drilling put my child off?

It can, if it's heavy or joyless. Early-years guidance favours play and stories. Keep it light — games, songs, Zhuyin picture books — which is how Lumi Bopomofo is designed.

Is Lumi Bopomofo pay-once?

Yes — one-time purchase, no ads, no subscription, offline, with a parent gate.
