Short answer

Subscription fatigue often comes from the accumulation of many small automatic charges that no longer feel like active choices. HoursTag Lite can turn each recurring price into work time, tag it as Need, Want or Impulse, and show category patterns in a monthly breakdown so you can decide which services still earn their hours. It does not cancel subscriptions for you or inspect bank statements; you must enter the prices and cancel through the service or App Store yourself.

Try HoursTag Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

FAQ

Can HoursTag Lite find all my subscriptions automatically?

No. HoursTag Lite does not connect to banks or scan statements. Enter the recurring prices you want to judge, then use the work-hour result for your own review.

Should I look at monthly or yearly hours?

Both help. Monthly hours show the immediate burden; yearly hours reveal the true annual tradeoff of keeping a service.

Can it cancel a subscription?

No. HoursTag Lite is a calculator and spending-awareness tool. Cancel through the App Store, the service website or your payment provider.
