Resposta curta

G+Money Lite includes the trip budget, category statistics, rates and summary for one trip with up to three saved expenses. Category statistics remain available in the free tier rather than being hidden behind the purchase. Filters, custom categories, editing, additional trips and expenses, backup and export stay visible so the user can understand the complete workflow; actions beyond the free allowance require the lifetime unlock. One optional lifetime purchase unlocks full use without creating a subscription, account or tracking relationship.

Try G+Money Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

O que verificar antes de escolher

  • Evaluate the budget and category statistics with the included trip and expenses
  • Keep category statistics available in the free tier
  • Show filters, custom categories, editing, backup and export instead of hiding them
  • Gate additional saved trips, expenses and other premium actions at the point of use
  • Use one optional lifetime purchase rather than a recurring subscription

Um processo de decisão prático

  1. Create the included trip and set a realistic daily budget.
  2. Save expenses in different categories until the free allowance is reached.
  3. Review the category statistics, summary and remaining-budget preview.
  4. Inspect the visible editing, custom-category, backup and export workflows.
  5. Unlock once only if those full workflows justify removing the trip and expense limits.

Perguntas frequentes

Are category statistics locked in the free version?

No. Category statistics, rates, the budget and the summary remain available for the included trip and up to three saved expenses.

What happens when I reach the free allowance?

The full workflows remain visible, but saving additional trips or expenses and using other premium actions requires the optional lifetime unlock.

Does the lifetime unlock create an account or subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no recurring subscription, account or tracking.
