Medials

Three glide symbols that can appear between an initial and a final.

ㄧ — i / yi

Hanyu Pinyin
i / yi
Broad IPA
/i/
Unicode
U+3127

Example: 一 (yī) — one

Hanyu Pinyin

Broad IPA
/u/

Example: 五 (wǔ) — five
