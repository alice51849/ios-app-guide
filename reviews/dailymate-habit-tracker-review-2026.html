DailyMate Habit Tracker Review 2026 — Simple Daily Routines Without Subscription

4/5 Free

DailyMate is deliberately simple — it logs recurring daily tasks and habits without adding streaks, leaderboards or subscription pressure. Best for users who want an uncomplicated tool.

✓ What it does well

  • Clean, clutter-free habit logging
  • No subscription, no streak-based pressure
  • One-time purchase
  • Works offline, no account required

✗ Real limitations

  • No gamification or streaks for users who are motivated by those features
  • No Apple Watch companion app
  • No data export to health apps or spreadsheets

Who it's for: People who want simple daily task and habit logging without the complexity or subscription cost of apps like Streaks or Habitica.

Who should skip it: Users who are motivated by streaks, social features or detailed analytics dashboards.

Frequently asked questions

Does DailyMate sync habits across devices?

DailyMate stores habit data on-device. If you use multiple iPhones, data does not sync between them automatically.

Can DailyMate send reminders for habits?

Yes. DailyMate supports local notifications to remind you to complete daily habits at set times.

Is DailyMate better than Streaks?

DailyMate is simpler and cheaper (one-time purchase vs. Streaks subscription). Streaks has more polish, Apple Watch integration and streak features. The right choice depends on whether you need those extras.

Does DailyMate track mood or health metrics?

No. DailyMate focuses on task and habit completion logging. For mood or health metric tracking, a dedicated app like Daylio is more appropriate.
