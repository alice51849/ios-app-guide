HoursTag Review 2026 — Does Converting Prices to Hours of Work Actually Help?

4/5 Free

HoursTag works best as a friction-adding tool before impulse purchases. The conversion from dollars to hours is a genuinely useful mental reframe — if you use it consistently.

✓ What it does well

  • Simple, effective concept: price → hours of your life
  • One-time purchase (free lite version available)
  • No subscription, no account, works offline
  • Share prices via widget or action extension

✗ Real limitations

  • Requires self-discipline to actually open before purchases
  • Only as useful as the discipline of the person using it
  • Some users prefer the friction of a subscription tracker instead

Who it's for: Anyone trying to curb impulse buying, freelancers evaluating tool costs against their billable rate, students on tight budgets.

Who should skip it: Users looking for full budget-tracking or expense categorisation — HoursTag only converts prices, it doesn't track spending history.

Frequently asked questions

What does HoursTag actually do?

You enter your hourly wage, and HoursTag converts any price you type into the number of hours you need to work to afford it. It reframes purchasing decisions in terms of time rather than money.

Is there a free version of HoursTag?

Yes. HoursTag is free and includes the core conversion feature. The full HoursTag app adds additional features as a one-time purchase.

Does HoursTag track my spending history?

No. HoursTag converts prices in the moment — it is not a budgeting or expense-tracking app. If you need spending history, use a separate finance app.

Can I use HoursTag from the iPhone Share Sheet?

Yes. HoursTag includes a Share Sheet extension so you can convert prices you see in Safari or other apps without opening HoursTag separately.
