Lumi Letters Star Planet Review — Best ABC Phonics App for Kids iPhone?

★★★★½ 4.6/5
Lumi Letters Star Planet is a polished phonics app for ages 4–8 set in an enchanted forest. It covers all 26 English letters with sounds and simple words, works completely offline, and uses a one-time purchase model with no subscriptions or in-app ads.

Pros

  • Full 26-letter phonics coverage with sounds and example words
  • Charming enchanted-forest visual style that engages young children
  • No ads, no subscriptions — one-time purchase
  • Fully offline — ideal for travel and no-Wi-Fi situations
  • Available in Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean interfaces

Cons

  • Phonics only — no sight words or beginner reading practice
  • Limited content for children who already know the alphabet

Who is it for?

Children aged 4–8 who are learning the English alphabet and letter sounds for the first time. Great for bilingual families and international schools.

Who is it NOT for?

Children who can already read simple sentences in English.

Frequently asked questions

What age is Lumi Letters Star Planet for?

Lumi Letters is designed for children aged 4 to 8 who are at the beginning stages of learning English letters and phonics.

Does Lumi Letters teach letter sounds or just the alphabet?

Both. Each letter has a dedicated sound activity that teaches the phonetic sound (not just the alphabet name), plus an example word and illustration.

Is there an Android version of Lumi Letters?

Lumi Letters is currently iOS only and is available on iPhone and iPad.

Can a 3-year-old use Lumi Letters?

Some 3-year-olds may enjoy the visuals with parent guidance, but the app is optimised for ages 4 and up.
