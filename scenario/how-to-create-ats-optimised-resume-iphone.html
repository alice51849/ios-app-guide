How to Create an ATS-Optimised Resume on iPhone

1
Start from a template or import your existing resume

Open CV Desk. Choose a clean template or import your existing CV file.

2
Complete all sections thoroughly

Fill in every section: work experience with dates, education, skills with keywords, and full contact details. Gaps reduce your ATS score.

3
Run the ATS scan

Tap Scan to run the ATS analysis. CV Desk checks for formatting issues, keyword density, and common ATS filter triggers.

4
Fix flagged issues

Address each flagged item. Common problems: tables, text boxes, unusual fonts, missing keywords for your target role.

5
Add job-specific keywords

Read the job description and add relevant keywords from it to your skills and experience sections. Re-scan to see the score improve.

6
Export and submit

When your score is above 80, tap Export to generate a clean PDF ready for submission.

Frequently asked questions

What ATS score in CV Desk means my resume is ready?

A score above 80 suggests your resume is unlikely to be filtered by common ATS systems. Below 60 usually means significant formatting or keyword problems.

Should I have a different resume for each job?

Yes. Adding keywords from the specific job description significantly improves your ATS score for that role. CV Desk makes this fast to re-scan and re-export.

Do graphics and tables hurt ATS scores?

Yes. Most ATS systems struggle to read tables and graphics. CV Desk flags these and recommends plain text formatting instead.
