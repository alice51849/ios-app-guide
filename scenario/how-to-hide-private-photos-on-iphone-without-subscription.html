How to Keep Private Photos Hidden on iPhone Without a Subscription

Apple's built-in 'Hidden' album is not encrypted and is easy to find. If you want photos genuinely inaccessible to someone who picks up your phone, you need a separate vault app.

Step by step

  1. Download Zafe: Private Photo Vault (one-time purchase)

    Zafe uses Face ID or Touch ID to lock access. There is no subscription. Pay once, use it for as long as your iPhone runs.

  2. Move photos in from your Camera Roll

    Select photos from your Camera Roll and move them into Zafe. The originals are removed from the standard camera roll.

  3. Set up a strong Face ID or PIN lock

    Zafe locks automatically when you leave the app. Choose the strictest lock option for maximum security.

  4. Back up carefully if needed

    Photos inside Zafe are not in your iCloud Photo Library. If you want a backup, export them manually to a secure location.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zafe really hidden from the home screen?

Zafe appears on your home screen like any app, but the contents require Face ID or PIN to view. You can also use Screen Time restrictions to hide the app itself from casual viewing.

What happens if I delete Zafe?

Photos stored inside Zafe are deleted with it if not exported first. Back up any important photos before uninstalling.

Can someone recover the photos after I move them?

Once moved into Zafe and deleted from the camera roll, standard recovery tools should not be able to retrieve them from the camera roll. However, if iCloud Photos was syncing at the time of the original capture, a copy may still exist in iCloud.
