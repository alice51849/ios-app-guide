How to Track Travel Expenses in Multiple Currencies on iPhone

1
Set your home currency

Open G+Money Lite and choose your home currency (e.g. TWD, USD, AUD). All totals will be converted to this currency.

2
Create a new trip

Tap + to create a trip. Name it (e.g. 'Japan 2026') and set the travel dates.

3
Log each expense in local currency

Each time you pay, tap + Expense. Enter the amount and select the local currency. G+Money Lite converts to your home currency automatically.

4
Check your running total

The trip overview shows your cumulative spending in your home currency so you always know where you stand.

5
Review by category after the trip

When you return, review the category breakdown (food, transport, accommodation, shopping) to see where you spent most.

Frequently asked questions

Does G+Money Lite work offline when travelling?

G+Money Lite includes offline exchange rates so you can log expenses without internet. Rates update when you next connect.

Can I track multiple trips at the same time?

Yes. G+Money Lite supports multiple separate trips, each with its own expense log.

What if I pay in a mix of currencies on the same day?

Log each transaction separately in its own currency. G+Money Lite handles mixed currencies within the same trip.
