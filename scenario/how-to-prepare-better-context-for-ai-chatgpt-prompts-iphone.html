How to Get Better AI Answers by Preparing Context First (iPhone)

The quality of an AI answer is almost entirely determined by the quality of the context you provide. Pasting a vague question gets a vague answer. Providing a structured brief with relevant screenshots, documents and notes gets a useful, specific answer.

Step by step

  1. Collect your context in AI Brief

    Take screenshots of the relevant screens, attach the relevant files or paste in your notes. AI Brief keeps them together as a project.

  2. Let AI Brief organise it into a structured brief

    The app turns your collection of context into a clean, readable brief that you can copy with one tap.

  3. Paste the brief into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI chat

    The model now has the structured context it needs. Your question can be short and specific because the brief provides all the background.

  4. Iterate with the same brief

    If the first answer is not what you needed, keep the brief open and refine only your question — not the entire context again.

Frequently asked questions

Does AI Brief send my files to any AI model?

No. AI Brief is a context organiser, not an AI model. It helps you prepare the brief to copy and paste. It does not send your content anywhere unless you share it yourself.

Does it work with Claude, Gemini and other AI tools?

Yes. AI Brief produces a text brief that you copy and paste into any AI chat tool you use — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or anything else.
