How to Scan Documents Offline on iPhone (No Adobe Account, No Cloud Upload)

Adobe Scan requires an account and uploads your scans to Adobe's cloud. If you're scanning sensitive documents — contracts, passports, medical records — you may want everything to stay on your device.

Step by step

  1. Open ScanTo Pro and position your document

    Place your document on a flat, well-lit surface. ScanTo Pro auto-detects the edges and suggests a crop.

  2. Tap to capture and apply auto-enhancement

    The app sharpens text, removes shadows and corrects perspective automatically. No manual adjustment is usually needed for printed documents.

  3. Export to PDF or image

    Save to Files or share directly. Everything happens on-device — no internet connection required, no account, no cloud upload.

  4. Add OCR text recognition (optional)

    ScanTo Pro includes offline OCR so you can search the text within your scans later, without sending the content to any server.

Frequently asked questions

Does ScanTo Pro upload my scans to any server?

No. ScanTo Pro is fully offline. All processing happens on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to any server unless you actively choose to share via AirDrop, email or Files.

Does it support multi-page PDFs?

Yes. You can scan multiple pages in one session and export them as a single PDF.

Is there a free version?

ScanTo Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. There is no ongoing fee for any feature including OCR.
