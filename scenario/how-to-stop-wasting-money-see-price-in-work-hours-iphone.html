How to Stop Impulse Buying: See Any Price as Hours of Your Own Work

The most effective way to reduce impulse purchases is to convert prices from abstract dollars into concrete time. If a jacket costs three hours of your work, you feel it differently than if it costs $90.

Step by step

  1. Set your hourly wage in HoursTag

    Enter your hourly take-home pay (after tax). HoursTag converts it automatically for every calculation.

  2. Next time you're about to buy something, open HoursTag

    Type the price. HoursTag shows you exactly how many hours of your time that purchase costs.

  3. Pause for 10 seconds

    Seeing '2.5 hours of work' instead of '$60' triggers a different part of your decision-making. Many impulse purchases feel less urgent after the conversion.

  4. Track which purchases passed the test

    You don't need to refuse every purchase. The goal is to make spending conscious, not to eliminate it.

Frequently asked questions

Is HoursTag only for high earners?

No. The conversion works at any wage. A $5 coffee might be 20 minutes of work at one salary and 40 minutes at another. The question is whether 20 or 40 minutes feels worth it to you personally.

Is there a free version?

HoursTag Lite is a free version with the core conversion feature and no subscription. HoursTag adds tracking, categories and deeper reporting.

Does it replace a budget app?

They serve different purposes. HoursTag is a decision tool — it's most useful at the moment of considering a purchase. A budget app tracks what you spent after the fact.
