How to Take a Passport Photo at Home with Your iPhone

Most people only need a passport photo a few times in their lives, so paying a pharmacy $15–20 each time adds up. With the right app and a plain background, you can take a compliant photo in minutes.

Step by step

  1. Find a plain white or off-white wall

    Avoid textured walls, shadows and coloured backgrounds. Natural daylight from a window is ideal — avoid harsh flash.

  2. Position yourself correctly

    Face the camera directly. Keep your expression neutral. Remove glasses. Ensure the full face and top of shoulders are visible.

  3. Use Snapport Lite to auto-crop and check compliance

    Snapport Lite guides you through official size requirements for passports, visas and ID cards, checks biometric rules and lets you download or print the result.

  4. Review the output before printing

    Check the white balance, that there are no shadows on your face, and that the background is uniform. Retake if the lighting is uneven.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really use an iPhone photo for a real passport?

Yes. Many government passport services now accept digital photos taken on a smartphone, provided they meet biometric size and background requirements. Snapport Lite checks these for you.

What background colour do I need for a passport photo?

Most countries require a plain white or off-white background. Some countries allow light grey. Snapport Lite shows you the specific requirements for your target document.

How much does it cost compared to a pharmacy?

Snapport Lite is a one-time purchase. A typical pharmacy charges $10–20 per set of prints. If you need passport photos more than once, the app pays for itself quickly.
