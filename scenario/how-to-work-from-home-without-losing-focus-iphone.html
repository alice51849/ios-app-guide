How to Work From Home Without Losing Focus — iPhone Approach

1
Set a defined start time and workspace

Start work at the same time every day. Your brain associates location and time with 'work mode'. A dedicated desk helps.

2
Plan your day's top 3 priorities in the morning

Before opening your inbox, write your 3 most important tasks for the day using Sono Note's voice-to-text.

3
Use LockHour Pro to start a focused work block

Launch LockHour Pro, block social media and messaging apps, and set a 45–90 minute deep-work block.

4
Work without interruption until the block ends

During the block, blocked apps show a Screen Time restriction. Don't override it — the friction is the point.

5
Take a proper break between blocks

After each block, take a 10–15 minute break away from your desk. Don't check social media during the break either.

6
Use Scanto to process any physical documents

If you receive any physical documents during the day, scan them immediately with Scanto rather than letting paper pile up.

Frequently asked questions

How many deep-work blocks should I do per day when working from home?

3–4 blocks of 45–90 minutes per day is typical for knowledge work. Total deep-work time of 3–5 hours per day is sustainable long-term.

What if I need to check messages during a work block?

Schedule specific windows for checking messages — e.g. 9am, 12pm, 4pm. Outside those windows, keep the blocks uninterrupted.

How do I handle video calls during a deep-work block?

Schedule calls at the boundaries of work blocks (start or end), not in the middle. Breaking a deep-work block to join a call resets your concentration.
