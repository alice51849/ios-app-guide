What iPhone app helps students study for TOEIC?

Aim990 starts with an 8-minute TOEIC L&R diagnosis that finds your 3 weakest skills, drills them daily and rechecks them on Day 30. Aim990 Plus is a separate paid download with 630 original questions, 15-minute Pressure Sets, Mistake Replay and mini, half or full mocks.

Is there a free resume app for iPhone?

CV Desk provides an instant ATS score and exports a clean PDF — a one-time purchase with no recurring fee.
