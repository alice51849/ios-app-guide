คำตอบสั้น

Young children learn numbers best through repeated, concrete experiences: seeing quantities, hearing number words, comparing and solving tiny problems. Lumi Math Planet is built as a space adventure for early math, with voice-guided questions, number operations, patterns, logic, geometry and real-life application, plus progress tracking across five skill areas. It is especially honest to frame it as a kids numbers and early math app for about ages 5–8, playable by pre-readers with voice guidance, with no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection, offline use and one-time purchase access.

Try Lumi Math Planet on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

FAQ

Is this mainly for toddlers?

The official description says it is perfect for ages 5–8 and playable before children can read. Younger children may enjoy it with a parent, but it is not just a baby counting toy.

Does it only teach 1-10?

No. Lumi Math Planet covers broader early math: operations, patterns, sequences, logic, geometry, story problems, place value and more.

Is it safe for kids?

The App Store description promises zero ads, zero in-app purchases, zero data collected and a parent-check gate for the Parent Zone.
