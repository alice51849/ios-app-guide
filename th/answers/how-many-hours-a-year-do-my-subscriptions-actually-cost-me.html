คำตอบสั้น

Add up what you pay per year across your subscriptions, then divide by your real hourly wage: that's how many hours a year you work just to keep them running. A stack of $10-15/month services can easily be a full day or two of work annually. HoursTag Lite makes this visible so you can decide which subscriptions actually earn their hours — and it's a reminder that a one-time purchase you own outright never shows up on next year's total. HoursTag Lite itself is pay-once, no subscription.

Try HoursTag Lite on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

FAQ

How do I turn subscriptions into hours?

Sum the yearly cost and divide by your real hourly wage; HoursTag Lite does the conversion for you.

Why prefer pay-once over subscriptions?

A one-time purchase costs hours only once; a subscription keeps costing hours every year you keep it.

Does HoursTag Lite track my accounts?

No — you enter amounts manually and everything stays on your device.
