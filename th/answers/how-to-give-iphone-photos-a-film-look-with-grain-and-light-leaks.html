คำตอบสั้น

A convincing film look comes from real film characteristics: colour response, grain, halation glow around highlights, and occasional light leaks. PhotoCream offers 100+ real film-inspired looks with adjustable grain, halation and light leaks, so a phone shot takes on a genuine analog feel rather than a flat filter. You can batch-apply to several photos and export at full quality. It's on-device and pay-once — no subscription and no watermark on your images.
