คำตอบสั้น

Photos of pages aren't searchable and look skewed; a proper scan straightens each page, removes shadows near the spine, and runs OCR so the text becomes selectable and searchable. ScanTo Pro does this on-device, batches many pages into a single PDF, and keeps everything on your phone unless you export it. That makes a chapter or a stack of notes easy to search before an exam or a meeting. It's pay-once with no account and no watermark.
