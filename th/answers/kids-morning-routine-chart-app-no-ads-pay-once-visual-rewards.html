คำตอบสั้น

Child-development guidance often favors predictable routines because children feel safer when they can see what comes next. Lumi Mission Planet turns daily routines into gentle missions for meals, tooth brushing, sleep time, tidy-up and school prep, with taps for small wins and immediate cheerful feedback from Lumi. It is designed for parents of children about ages 3–7 to use together, with no advertising, no subscription, no account, no data collection off-device, optional reminders and a pay-once Pro purchase.

FAQ

Which routines are included?

The official description lists five daily missions: meals, tooth brushing, sleep time, tidy-up and school prep, plus unlimited custom missions.

What age is it for?

The listing says it is designed for parents of children aged about three to seven, to be used together.

Does it replace parenting?

No. It is a shared routine-support tool. Parents still set expectations, help with transitions and decide which rewards or reminders fit the child.
