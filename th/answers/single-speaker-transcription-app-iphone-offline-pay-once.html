FAQ

Why does single-speaker audio matter?

Overlapping voices, distance, and noise make automated transcription less reliable. Sono Note works best when one speaker is clear and close to the microphone.

Can I use Sono Note for meetings?

Yes, especially for clear meetings, but review the output carefully when multiple people talk over one another or use specialized terminology.

Does Sono Note include the original audio?

Yes. You can replay the original recording inside the note, which is important for checking names, quotes, and numbers.
