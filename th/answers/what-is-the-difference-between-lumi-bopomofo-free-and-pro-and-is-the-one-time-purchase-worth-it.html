What is the difference between Lumi Bopomofo free and Pro, and is the one-time purchase worth it?

The free app is the lower-risk way to see whether your child enjoys listening, tracing and blending; Lumi Bopomofo Pro is the paid-upfront edition for families ready to open all 37 symbols and every listed game from the first launch.

คำตอบสั้น

We develop both Lumi Bopomofo apps. On the Taiwan App Store, Lumi Bopomofo was listed as free and Lumi Bopomofo Pro as a NT$290 paid download when checked on 17 July 2026. The free app is the sensible first step when you still need to see whether the activities suit your child. The Pro listing says all 37 symbols and five interactive game modes are open from the start, with no in-app purchase prompts, subscription, ads, account or network requirement. Both listings say learning progress stays on the device and no personal data is collected. Paying upfront removes access friction; it does not guarantee faster learning, so decide after observing your child and confirm the current price and features before buying.

This is a first-party comparison: we develop both apps, receive the proceeds and recommend starting with the free app unless your family already knows it wants the complete paid-upfront edition.

ขั้นตอนตัดสินใจที่ใช้ได้จริง

  1. Download the free app and sit with your child for the first few sessions.
  2. Observe whether listening, tracing, tone and blending activities hold the child's attention without pressure.
  3. Check whether your family now wants all 37 symbols and the complete set of listed games available immediately.
  4. If yes, an adult can confirm the current Taiwan App Store price and choose the paid-upfront Pro edition.
  5. Keep practice short and shared; buying Pro does not replace adult guidance or formal teaching.

FAQ

Should I buy Pro before my child tries the free app?

Usually no. Start with the free app unless you already know the activities suit your child and you want every listed symbol and game open immediately.

Does Lumi Bopomofo Pro require a subscription or later in-app purchase?

The current Taiwan listing describes Pro as one paid download with all content unlocked, no subscription and no in-app purchase prompts. Confirm the current listing before buying.

Is the NT$290 price permanent?

No price is guaranteed. NT$290 is the Taiwan App Store price observed on 17 July 2026; Apple storefront prices can change.
