Full screen flip clock

This flip clock runs in your browser tab: press Full screen and the whole display becomes a large clock you can read from across the room. Choose 12 or 24 hour time, show or hide seconds, and switch between a dark, a light and a dim red night theme. Nothing is installed and no account is needed.

Runs in your browser; only download-button clicks are countedShows your device's local time

This browser cannot show a web page in full screen; turn the phone sideways for a larger clock.

Shortcut: press F for full screen, Esc to leave. While in full screen, the page asks the browser to keep the screen awake.

On iPhone or iPad? Stay Clock: Flip Desk Clock is a full screen clock that stays on while charging, with a dim red night face on true black and gentle pixel shifting for OLED screens. View Stay Clock on the App Store

How to use it

  • Open this page on a computer or tablet and press Full screen.
  • Set 12 or 24 hour time and seconds; the page remembers your choice in this browser.
  • At night, the dim red theme keeps a dark room dark.

Want the same flip clock on your nightstand? Stay Clock: Flip Desk Clock turns your iPhone or iPad into an always-on desk clock you can read from across the room, and the face you pick also appears in StandBy, on the Lock Screen and on Apple Watch.
