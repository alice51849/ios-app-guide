Your message

Type it, pick a colour and speed, then go full screen.

Browser preview only. Brightness depends on your screen; nothing you type is stored.

How it works

  1. Your text is drawn once into a 16-pixel-high image, and every lit pixel becomes one round LED on the board.
  2. Scrolling moves that bitmap one LED column at a time, timed by the browser’s animation clock so speed stays steady on fast and slow screens.
  3. Mirror flips the board left to right so the message reads correctly from outside a window or in a rear-view mirror.
  4. Full screen uses the browser’s own full-screen mode; press Esc or the button again to leave.

What it will never do

  • It is a browser preview, not a phone app: there are no saved boards, fonts or effect packs.
  • It does not keep the screen awake on every device — check your display settings for long use.
  • No account, no tracking, nothing uploaded.

Questions

Can I use emoji or Chinese characters?

Yes. Any character your browser can draw is rasterised onto the board; very detailed characters are simplified by the 16-LED height.

How do I make it bigger?

Use Full screen, or turn a phone sideways in full screen to give the letters more height.

Why does my message look mirrored?

The Mirror option is on. Turn it off unless you are showing the board through a window.
