Common questions

Can you wear glasses in a passport photo?

Not for a U.S. passport: glasses are not allowed unless you cannot remove them for medical reasons, in which case a signed statement from your doctor is needed.

Can you smile in a passport photo?

Yes, within limits: face the camera directly with both eyes open and a neutral expression or a natural smile.

Why do passport photos get rejected?

Usually because one rule is broken: the wrong photo or head size, shadows or a patterned background, glasses, a blurry, grainy or out-of-date photo (it must be taken in the last 6 months), a selfie, or any filter or digital edit.

Source: U.S. Department of State, passport photo requirements (rules as recorded on 2026-10-16). Check the official page before you apply.

Before you print

  • Use a recent photo with a plain light background, even lighting and a neutral expression.
  • The guides show the head-height range for the chosen size; they are not an acceptance check.
  • Requirements change; confirm the current rules on the official website before you apply.
