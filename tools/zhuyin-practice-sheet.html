What this sheet does—and does not do

It places selected official symbols into light example cells and blank crosshair cells for adult-led pencil practice. It does not demonstrate stroke order, inspect handwriting, score a learner, diagnose a need, or promise better writing.

How the fixed worksheet is built

  1. Choose a preset or tap the exact official symbols needed.
  2. Symbols appear once each in Taiwan Ministry of Education order, never in a live random sequence.
  3. Choose 2, 4, or 6 light and blank cells plus 4–6 rows per page; the same inputs always reproduce the same pages.

A responsible way to use the grids

  1. Check the official Ministry of Education stroke-order portal before modeling an unfamiliar symbol.
  2. An adult can demonstrate on separate paper; the learner may trace, copy, point, or stop without a score.
  3. Treat the sheet as optional practice, not proof of readiness, mastery, motor ability, or a learning difficulty.

Bopomofo practice-sheet questions

Does the sheet teach official stroke order?

No. The light glyph is only a visual copying cue. Use the linked Taiwan Ministry of Education portal to check official strokes.

Does the page collect a child's writing or personal details?

No. It accepts only official symbols and layout settings in the current page; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Does completing a page measure Zhuyin or handwriting ability?

No. The sheet has no score, grade, ranking, assessment, diagnosis, or claim that completion proves mastery.
