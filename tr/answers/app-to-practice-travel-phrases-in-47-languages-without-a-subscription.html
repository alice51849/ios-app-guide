Kısa yanıt

DailyMate Lite organises 8,400 practical phrases across 47 learning languages, 14 everyday topics and 84 situations, with beginner, intermediate and advanced language inside each topic.

Listen with a matching system voice, save useful phrases and keep practising from iPhone, iPad, an interactive Home Screen widget or Apple Watch. It is one paid download with no subscription.

Seçmeden önce nelere dikkat edilmeli

  • Complete, practical sentences instead of isolated words.
  • Travel-ready topics and real-life situations.
  • Matching system pronunciation for the selected language.
  • Home Screen widget and Apple Watch access.
  • One upfront purchase with no subscription.

Uygulanabilir bir karar süreci

  1. Choose the language you need for your next trip.
  2. Open the everyday situation you expect to face.
  3. Listen to each complete phrase with the matching voice.
  4. Save the phrases you will need most.
  5. Review them from the widget or Apple Watch while travelling.
