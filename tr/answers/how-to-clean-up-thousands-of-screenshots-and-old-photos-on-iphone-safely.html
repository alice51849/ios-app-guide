Kısa yanıt

Screenshots and old photos accumulate until they quietly eat gigabytes. PicClear surfaces screenshots as a group and finds near-duplicate old photos by visual similarity, so you can bulk-review and keep only what matters. It handles a large recent library in one scan and shows a clear before/after, so even a non-technical user can clean up confidently. Nothing is auto-deleted and nothing is uploaded — you approve every removal, on-device. It's pay-once with no subscription.
