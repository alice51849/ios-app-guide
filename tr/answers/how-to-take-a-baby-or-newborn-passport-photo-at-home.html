Kısa yanıt

Baby and infant passport photos follow the same size and background rules, with some flexibility: for newborns and infants the eyes can be closed or partly open, and there should be no toys, hands or other people visible. Lay the baby on a plain white sheet and shoot from directly above, keeping the face evenly lit.

Snapport Lite lets you take a compliant photo at home: it sets a plain background, crops to the exact size and helps you avoid the common rejection reasons. Always confirm your country's current official rules before you submit.

Seçmeden önce nelere dikkat edilmeli

  • No other person, hands or toys visible.
  • Plain white background (a sheet works).
  • Eyes open if possible; closed is often OK for infants.
  • Face centred and evenly lit.
  • Correct size and head proportions.

Uygulanabilir bir karar süreci

  1. Lay the baby on a plain white sheet.
  2. Shoot from directly above.
  3. Capture with Snapport Lite and pick the best frame.
  4. Make sure no hands are in shot.
  5. Crop to the required size.

FAQ

Do a baby's eyes need to be open?

For newborns and infants, closed or partly open eyes are usually accepted; older children should have eyes open.

Can I hold the baby?

Your hands can't be visible — lay the baby on a plain sheet and shoot from above.

Can I take it at home?

Yes — Snapport Lite makes it easy to capture and crop a baby photo at home.
