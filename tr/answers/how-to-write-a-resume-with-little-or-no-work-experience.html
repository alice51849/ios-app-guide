Kısa yanıt

You don't need years of jobs to have a solid resume. Lead with your education, then coursework projects, internships, volunteering, part-time work and transferable skills, described with concrete outcomes. The key for a first resume is still keyword alignment: match the posting's required skills so an ATS scores you in range. CV Desk gives an on-device ATS score and a keyword matcher that shows what's missing, and exports a clean, ATS-safe PDF with no watermark — all on-device, so your details aren't uploaded. It's pay-once.

Try CV Desk on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

FAQ

What do I put if I have no jobs yet?

Education, coursework projects, internships, volunteering, part-time roles and transferable skills — described with concrete results.

Will it still pass ATS?

Yes if you mirror the posting's keywords; CV Desk's matcher shows which required skills are missing so you can add the genuine ones.

Is my data uploaded?

No — CV Desk scores and matches on-device; it's pay-once with no account.
