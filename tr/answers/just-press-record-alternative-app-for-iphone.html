Kısa yanıt

Just Press Record is a well-known voice notes app. If you'd rather pay once than subscribe, Sono Note is a one-time-purchase voice notes app for iPhone — Just talk — get clean notes, a summary & to-dos. Compare the current features and pricing on the App Store before you switch, since apps change over time.

Sono Note's listed strengths include Private, No subscription, On-device. It's independent and not affiliated with Just Press Record; names and trademarks belong to their owners.
