Kısa yanıt

Passport and ID photos are often printed on standard photo paper, then cut to the exact final size; poor scaling at print time can ruin an otherwise good photo. Snapport Lite creates print-ready layouts for 4×6, A4, or Letter sheets with multiple copies and cut guides, after you align the face and choose the correct template. It does not replace official rules or professional judgment, but it helps avoid the common mistake of printing a correct crop at the wrong physical size.

Because it's on-device, your photo isn't uploaded anywhere, and a single export gives you a file ready to drop into a CV or a LinkedIn/ID upload.

Seçmeden önce nelere dikkat edilmeli

  • Print sheet layouts for the paper you actually use: 4×6 photo paper, A4, or Letter
  • Multiple copies on one sheet so you can cut extras for forms, backups, or mistakes
  • Cut guides that preserve the final physical photo dimensions after printing
  • Export to PDF or image formats so you can print at home, save to Files, or share to a print shop
  • No subscription, no account, and offline processing for a one-time document-photo task

FAQ

Can Snapport Lite make a 4×6 passport photo print sheet?

Yes. Snapport Lite supports 4×6 print sheets, plus A4 and Letter layouts with multiple copies and cut guides.

Should I let the printer auto-fit the sheet?

Be careful. Auto-fit or borderless scaling can change the physical photo size. Use the exported layout at the intended scale and check the printed dimensions with a ruler.

Does a print sheet guarantee acceptance?

No. It helps with physical size and cutting, but the photo still must meet the official rules for head size, background, expression, lighting, and recency.
