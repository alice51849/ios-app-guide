Kısa yanıt

Many popular scanners route your documents through their cloud or require an account for basic features, which is a poor fit for anything confidential. The privacy-first alternative captures, straightens, runs OCR and exports without ever transmitting the document. ScanTo Pro works fully on-device, batches pages into searchable PDFs, and can lock files — all pay-once with no subscription and no account. You keep the convenience of a phone scanner without the data trade-off.

FAQ

Why choose an on-device scanner?

Confidential documents (tax, ID, contracts) shouldn't be uploaded to a third party; an on-device scanner keeps them on your phone.

Does ScanTo Pro need internet?

No — scanning, OCR and export work offline; nothing is uploaded unless you export it.

Is it really pay-once?

Yes — ScanTo Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no account.
