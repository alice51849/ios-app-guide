FAQ

How is this different from the built-in weather app?

Lumi Weather translates the forecast into practical parent guidance — what to wear, whether to go out, what to do — rather than just raw numbers.

Is it for kids or parents?

It's a tool for parents to plan the day around young children, with kid-friendly presentation.

Does it replace a full weather app?

It focuses on family planning; use a full meteorology app if you need detailed radar and data.
