How to Use G+Money for Travel Budget Tracking on iPhone

1
Set your home currency

Open G+Money and set your home currency (e.g., TWD, USD, JPY). All totals are shown in this currency.

2
Create a trip

Tap + to create a new trip. Name it and set the start and end dates.

3
Add expenses as you spend

Each time you pay for something, tap + Add Expense, enter the amount and select the local currency. G+Money converts to your home currency automatically.

4
View your running total

The trip overview shows your total spend in your home currency so you can see where your budget stands at any moment.

5
Review by category after the trip

Use the category breakdown to see how much you spent on food, transport, accommodation and activities.

Frequently asked questions

Does G+Money work without an internet connection?

G+Money includes offline exchange rates. You can log expenses without internet and rates update the next time you connect.

Can I track expenses for multiple trips at the same time?

Yes. G+Money lets you create and manage separate trips, each with its own expense log and totals.

What is the difference between G+Money and G+Money?

G+Money is free with core expense tracking. G+Money adds offline exchange rates and more detailed reporting.
