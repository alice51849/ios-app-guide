How to Use ScanTo Pro PDF Scanner on iPhone — Quick Start Guide

1
Open ScanTo Pro and position your document

Open the app and point your iPhone camera at the document. The app auto-detects the edges.

2
Tap capture when edges are aligned

When the highlighted edges match the document, tap the shutter button to capture the page.

3
Add more pages if needed

Tap + to add more pages to the same PDF. Repeat for each page of your document.

4
Review and adjust the scan

After scanning, adjust brightness, rotation or crop if needed. Tap Done when satisfied.

5
Export or share the PDF

Tap the share icon to send the PDF via email, AirDrop, Files app or any other share sheet destination.

Frequently asked questions

Does ScanTo Pro work without internet?

Yes. All scanning and PDF creation happens on-device. You do not need an internet connection at any point.

How do I scan a multi-page document into one PDF?

After scanning the first page, tap + to add the next page to the same document. Repeat until all pages are scanned, then export as a single PDF.

Can I rename the PDF before sharing?

Yes. Tap on the file name in the review screen to rename it before exporting.
