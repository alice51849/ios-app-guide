How to Use Sono Note for Voice Notes and Transcription on iPhone

1
Open Sono Note and tap Record

Open the app and tap the record button to start capturing audio. The recording indicator appears.

2
Speak clearly at a normal distance

Hold the iPhone naturally — you don't need to hold it close to your mouth. Clear speech in a quiet environment gives the best transcription.

3
Stop and wait for transcription

Tap Stop when done. Sono Note processes the audio on-device and produces a text transcript.

4
Review and edit the transcript

Read through the transcript and correct any errors. Proper nouns and technical terms may need manual correction.

5
Export or share the notes

Tap Share to copy the transcript as text, send via email, or save to Files. The audio file can also be shared separately.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is Sono Note's transcription?

Accuracy is highest for clear speech in quiet conditions. Accented speech, fast speech and background noise reduce accuracy. On-device transcription is somewhat less accurate than cloud-based AI services.

Does Sono Note support multiple speakers?

Sono Note transcribes audio as a single stream — it does not identify or label different speakers.

Can I use Sono Note in a noisy café?

It works but accuracy drops significantly in noisy environments. A quiet room gives the best results.
