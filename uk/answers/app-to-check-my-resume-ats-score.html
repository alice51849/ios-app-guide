When selecting an app to check your resume's ATS score, consider features like ease of use, instant scoring, and the availability of templates. Look for options that allow you to export your resume without watermarks and offer both PDF and Word formats.

Так, CV Desk дозволяє експортувати ваше резюме у форматах PDF та Word без водяних знаків.
