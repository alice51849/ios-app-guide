Câu trả lời ngắn gọn

Visual schedules are commonly recommended for helping children anticipate transitions and build independence, especially when tasks are broken into small visible steps. Lumi Mission Planet provides routine missions, custom missions with icons and scenes, buddy characters, stickers, victory cards and a parent dashboard so the family can make routines concrete. It is not a clinical therapy app, but it can be a calm home visual schedule with no ads, no subscription, no account, on-device progress and optional local backup/export.

Try Lumi Mission Planet on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

  • Custom missions with icons and scenes for family-specific routines

FAQ

Is Lumi Mission Planet a visual schedule app?

It can be used that way at home: parents create or use missions, children tap completed steps, and the app gives visual feedback and progress history.

Is it a therapy or autism treatment app?

No. It is a routine and habit-support app for families. For clinical needs, parents should work with qualified therapists or educators.

Can routines be customized?

Yes. The Pro description says it includes unlimited custom missions with your own icon and scene for each.
