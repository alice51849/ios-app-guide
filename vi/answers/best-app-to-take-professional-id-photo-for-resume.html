Câu trả lời ngắn gọn

A resume-photo template crops to the right proportions and lets you swap a messy background for a plain one, so a phone snap becomes a tidy headshot. Shoot in soft, even light facing a window and keep a neutral, approachable expression.

Because it's on-device, your photo isn't uploaded anywhere, and a single export gives you a file ready to drop into a CV or a LinkedIn/ID upload.

Cần xem những gì trước khi chọn

  • Resume / ID headshot template with correct proportions.
  • Background replacement to a clean, plain colour.
  • On-device processing (photo isn't uploaded).
  • Single-photo export ready for a CV or profile.
  • Guidance on framing and expression.

Quy trình quyết định thực tế

  1. Face a window in soft, even light.
  2. Use the resume/ID template to frame the crop.
  3. Swap the background for a plain colour.
  4. Keep a neutral, approachable expression.
  5. Export and place it on your CV or profile.

FAQ

Do I need a studio backdrop?

No — background replacement gives you a clean plain background from any setting.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

On-device processing keeps the photo on your phone unless you export it.

What size should a resume photo be?

There's no single standard; a square or 3:4 headshot works for most CVs and profiles — the template keeps proportions right.
