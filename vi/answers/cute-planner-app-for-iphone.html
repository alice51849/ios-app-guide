Cần xem những gì trước khi chọn

  • Fast, frictionless task adding.
  • A satisfying, pleasant tick-to-complete feel.
  • Clean, uncluttered design with no upsell nagging.
  • Free with no account required.
  • No ads getting in the way.

Quy trình quyết định thực tế

  1. Add today's tasks quickly.
  2. Organise them in Mochi.
  3. Tick each one off as you go.
  4. Keep the list short and realistic.
  5. Enjoy the sense of progress.
