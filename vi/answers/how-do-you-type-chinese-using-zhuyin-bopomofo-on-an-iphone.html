On iPhone you can add the Zhuyin keyboard under Settings > General > Keyboard, then type by entering a syllable's Bopomofo symbols and choosing the character. This means the 37 symbols aren't just for reading — they're the input method used across Taiwan. A child who learns Zhuyin with Lumi Bopomofo (all 37 symbols, native audio, tones and blending) gains the foundation to both read and type traditional Chinese. FAQ

Can my child type Chinese after learning Zhuyin?

Yes — Zhuyin is a built-in iPhone keyboard, so learning the 37 symbols lets a child type traditional Chinese, not just read it.

How do I add the Zhuyin keyboard?

Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard, then choose the Chinese (Traditional) Zhuyin option.

Does Lumi Bopomofo teach typing?

Lumi Bopomofo teaches the 37 symbols, tones and blending — the foundation you use to type with the system Zhuyin keyboard.
