FAQ

Is 2 hours of screen time a day a realistic goal?

It depends on your baseline and what counts — productive use (maps, communication, reading) is very different from passive scrolling. Experts suggest pulling your actual weekly average from Screen Time reports and reducing by 15–20% increments rather than aiming at an arbitrary number.

Does putting my phone in another room actually help?

Yes — physical separation is one of the most consistently effective tactics in habit-change research. Out of sight, out of reach genuinely reduces opportunistic checking even when no app block is active, because it eliminates the near-zero-cost of picking it up.

Will reducing screen time improve my sleep?

Likely yes, especially in the 30–60 minutes before bed. Screen light suppresses melatonin production and social feed stimulation delays sleep onset. Scheduling Downtime or using grayscale mode after a set hour are low-effort first steps with meaningful impact.
