FAQ

Does Lumi Letters have any subscription fees after the initial purchase?

No. Lumi Letters is a one-time purchase — there are no subscriptions, no monthly fees, and no additional in-app purchases. Buy once and your child can use the full app indefinitely.

Does Lumi Letters show ads to my child?

Lumi Letters contains absolutely no advertising — no banners, no video ads, no sponsored characters. The app is completely ad-free.

Does Lumi Letters collect any data about my child?

Lumi Letters collects no personal data. No account is required, no information is sent to external servers, and your child's progress is stored only on your device.
