Câu trả lời ngắn gọn

The core mechanic of Lumi Mission Planet is immediate positive feedback: when a child finishes brushing their teeth, they open the app and tap once to feed their buddy, who jumps and cheers. This instant reward is deliberately separate from a sticker-at-end-of-week model, because young children's motivation responds better to immediate feedback. Over time, a buddy character grows through five stages from Baby to Star, collectible stickers unlock (with rarity reveals), and a streak counter grows — all without ads, external links, or any mechanic designed to create anxiety about missing a day. The Lite version includes four free missions (meals, brushing, sleep, tidy-up); a one-time in-app purchase adds school prep, unlimited custom tasks, all six buddy characters, and the full sticker collection.

Try Lumi Mission Planet on a real example first, and check the current App Store listing for exact features and pricing before you decide.

FAQ

How does Lumi Mission Planet actually motivate a toddler to brush their teeth or tidy up?

Lumi Mission Planet gives immediate feedback: after completing a real-life task, the child taps the screen to feed their buddy, who visibly jumps and cheers right away. Young children respond to immediate rewards far more than delayed ones, which is why Lumi Mission Planet rewards the instant the task is done rather than at week's end.

Is there a subscription for Lumi Mission Planet, or is it a one-time purchase?

The four core missions are free with no subscription. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks school prep, unlimited custom missions, all six buddy characters, and the full 100-sticker collection permanently. There are no monthly fees and no subscription of any kind.

Does Lumi Mission Planet track and show parents how consistently their child is completing routines?

Yes — Lumi Mission Planet includes a Parent Dashboard behind a long-press gate with a 7-day streak calendar, total completions, stars and stickers earned at a glance, and a full completion log where every tap is recorded with the exact local time. The full history can be exported as a CSV file.
