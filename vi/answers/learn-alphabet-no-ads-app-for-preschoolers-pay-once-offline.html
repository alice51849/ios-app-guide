FAQ

What should parents avoid in alphabet apps?

Avoid apps with ads, external links, confusing upsells or long sessions that overwhelm preschoolers. A safer choice keeps learning focused and lets adults control purchases and links.

Can Lumi Letters be used ngoại tuyến?

Yes. The App Store description states that progress is stored on-device and the app works fully ngoại tuyến, which is useful for travel and short practice sessions.

Does it collect child data?

The official description says no data is collected and progress stays on the device. Parents should still verify the current App Store privacy label before purchase.
