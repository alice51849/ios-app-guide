Short answer

Official photo rules commonly specify a centered, front-facing head, visible eyes, neutral expression, and a head-size range; the U.S. example is roughly 1 to 1 3/8 inches from chin to crown in a 2×2 inch photo. Snapport uses on-device Apple Vision face detection with head-top, eye-line, and chin guides, plus pinch, drag, rotate, and auto-center tools so you can fit the face to the selected template. It is a guide, not an official compliance checker, so compare the result with the issuing authority before submitting.

What to look for before choosing

FAQ

Does Snapport check passport head size?

Snapport provides head-top, eye-line, and chin guides using on-device face detection, which helps you align the photo to a template. It does not certify or guarantee government acceptance.

Why does eye line matter?

Many official rules define where the eyes or face should sit in the final photo. A face that is centered poorly can be rejected even if the photo is sharp and the background is plain.

Can I fix a tilted face in Snapport?

You can pinch, drag, rotate, auto-center, and reset the photo. If the original pose is strongly turned or not front-facing, retaking the photo is usually safer than trying to fix it.
