Cần xem những gì trước khi chọn

  • Auto edge-detection and de-skew for crumpled receipts.

  • Batch multiple receipts into one PDF.

Quy trình quyết định thực tế

  1. Lay the receipt on a contrasting surface.
  2. Scan it with ScanTo Pro and let edges auto-detect.
  3. Enable OCR to capture the total and date.
  4. Tag by expense category.
  5. Export the PDF to your bookkeeping folder.
