Câu trả lời ngắn gọn

G+Money Lite includes one trip and up to three expenses free, together with rates, a budget, category statistics, and a summary. Enter each purchase in the local currency and see it in your home currency using a live, saved, or manually set rate; saved rates keep the workflow usable ngoại tuyến.

If you need more entries or trips, one lifetime purchase removes those limits. There is no subscription, account, or tracking, and the App also supports a Home Screen tiện ích and Apple Watch.

Cần xem những gì trước khi chọn

  • A useful free allowance that includes one trip and three expenses.
  • Local-currency entry with immediate home-currency totals.
  • Budget, category statistics, and summary available before purchase.
  • Saved or manual exchange rates for ngoại tuyến use.
  • One one-time unlock with no subscription, account, or tracking.

Quy trình quyết định thực tế

  1. Set your home currency and create the free trip.
  2. Choose a live, saved, or manual rate for the local currency.
  3. Log up to three expenses while testing the workflow.
  4. Review the budget, category statistics, and home-currency summary.
  5. Unlock unlimited expenses and trips once only if the free allowance fits your travel workflow.

FAQ

What can I use without paying?

You can use rates, a budget, category statistics, and a summary for one trip with up to three expenses.

Will it still work without data abroad?

Yes — saved or manually set rates keep expense entry and conversion available ngoại tuyến. An internet connection is needed only when fetching updated public exchange rates.

Is the full version a subscription?

No — one optional lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited expenses and trips.
