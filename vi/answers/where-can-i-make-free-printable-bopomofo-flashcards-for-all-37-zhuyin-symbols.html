Short answer

The free generator linked above presents the 21 initials, 3 medials, and 13 finals in the order documented by Taiwan's Ministry of Education. Choose 4, 6, or 8 cards per A4 page and whether to show the category label. The tool deliberately omits unverified one-to-one Pinyin and example-word answers: it prints only the official symbol set and links to Ministry of Education and Unicode sources. It does not play or record audio, save selections, score pronunciation, assess readiness, diagnose a learning need, or promise a learning result.

Start with the free cards and an official pronunciation source. Lumi Bopomofo is optional if a family later wants guided symbol, sound, tracing, tone, or blending activities; verify the current App Store listing before deciding.
