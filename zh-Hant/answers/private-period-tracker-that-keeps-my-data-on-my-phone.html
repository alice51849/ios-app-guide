The privacy question comes down to a few concrete things: does the app work without an account, does it store data locally instead of on a company server, does it avoid third-party ad and analytics trackers, and is its business model a subscription or data model rather than a simple one-time purchase? Look for explicit on-device-only language and no login requirement. Cyca is built this way — everything stays on your iPhone with no account and no data selling, and instead of a cold log it gives a gentle Daily Body Forecast and a calm calendar for your next period, fertile window and ovulation. It is a one-time purchase, so a private tracker is not a monthly bill or a data trade.
