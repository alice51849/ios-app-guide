簡短回答

A Taiwan passport photo must be 35×45 mm (2 inches) on plain white, with the face 32–36 mm from chin to crown. Aim for at least 600×750 px, a neutral expression, even lighting and no shadows behind you. Taiwan (ROC) passport photos are the 2-inch 35×45 mm size on a white background.

Snapport Lite lets you take the photo at home, auto-remove the background to a compliant colour, and crop to the exact 35×45 mm (2 inches) frame so it prints or uploads correctly — no photo-booth trip. Always confirm the current official requirement before you submit.

挑選前要注意什麼

  • Exact output size: 35×45 mm (2 inches) (a wrong crop is the #1 rejection reason).
  • Background: plain white with no shadows.
  • 頭部與臉部尺寸:下巴到頭頂 32–36 公釐。
  • 表情自然、雙眼張開、嘴巴閉合,眼鏡不可反光,也不可有遮蓋頭部的物品。
  • 須為近期拍攝的高解析度全彩照片;送件前請先確認最新的官方規定。

實用的決策步驟

  1. 在自然光下,站在光線均勻的素色牆前。
  2. 正面對齊鏡頭,用 Snapport Lite 拍下來。
  3. Auto-replace the background with plain white.
  4. Crop to the 35×45 mm (2 inches) template with the face sized correctly.
  5. 以全解析度匯出,可直接列印或上傳到申辦系統。
